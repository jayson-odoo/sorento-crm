# Getting started for new users

A zero-knowledge orientation to the Sorento CRM: how to sign in, what each part of the sidebar is for, where to find each kind of information, and how to ask the AI assistant for help. Read this first if you have never used the system before.

## 1. Sign in

1. Open the CRM in your browser.
2. On the **Sign In** page, enter your work email and password, then click **Sign In**. (If your organisation uses Google sign-in, click **Sign in with Google** instead.)
3. After signing in you land on **Dashboards** — the home page. The left-hand **sidebar** is your main way to move around; the top bar holds search, notifications, your profile, and the AI assistant.

If you forget your password, use the **Reset Password** link on the sign-in page. If a page says you don't have access, your account hasn't been granted that area yet — ask your administrator.

## 2. The sidebar map

Each entry below is a top-level group in the left sidebar. Click a group to expand its pages. You will only see the groups your account has access to, so your sidebar may be shorter than this list.

* **Dashboards** — the landing page with at-a-glance summaries and your tasks.
* **User Management** — people and access: administrative users, **Roles**, **Permissions**, **Teams**, **AI Agents**, and your own **Account**.
* **Commercial** — the sales pipeline: **Leads**, **Projects**, **Tenders**, **Quotations**, and sales-order progression.
* **Activity Plans** — planned work: **Activities**, **Activity Tasks**, and **Project Tasks**.
* **Delivery Order Management** — outgoing customer deliveries: **Delivery Orders**, **Delivery Order Status**, and **Customers**.
* **Complaint Management** — customer **Complaints** plus their **Root Causes** and **Resolutions**.
* **SLA Management** — service-level tracking and your team's queue: **SLA Policies**, **Conversation SLA Tracking**, **Form SLA Tracking**, **My Team Tasks**, and the **KPI Dashboard**.
* **Product Management** — the product catalogue: **All Products**, **Product Attachments**, **Product Categories**, **Brands**, and **Units of Measure**.
* **Procurement** — buying and inbound goods: **Suppliers**, **Packing Lists**, **SPO Allocations**, **GRN**, **Picking Lines**, and **Stock Inquiries**.
* **Project Sales Admin** — request forms you raise and review: **Purchase Requests** and **Sponsorship Forms**.
* **Inventory Management** — stock on hand: **Warehouses**, **Storage Zones**, **Stock**, **Stock Batches**, and **Stock Ledger**.
* **Marketing Management** — campaigns and offers: **Promotions**, **Promotion Products**, and **Campaigns**.
* **Forms Management** — downloadable application and marketing **Forms**.
* **Workflow Forms** — multi-step form **Definitions** that drive approval workflows.
* **Resource Management** — your document library: **Files**, **Trash**, and **Attachment Types**.
* **System Management** — administration: **App Store** (modules), import and integration logs, email and messaging settings, and **AI Assistant** settings. Most users won't need this group.

## 3. Where each kind of information lives

| You're looking for… | Go to |
|----|----|
| A product, its price, brand, or dimensions | **Product Management → All Products** |
| A brochure, datasheet, or product photo | **Product Management → Product Attachments** |
| The product catalogue PDF, price list, or any uploaded file | **Resource Management → Files** |
| How much stock you have right now | **Inventory Management → Stock** |
| Batches and expiry dates | **Inventory Management → Stock Batches** |
| The history of stock movements | **Inventory Management → Stock Ledger** |
| Stock that is still arriving | **Procurement → Packing Lists** |
| A customer's delivery orders and their status | **Delivery Order Management → Delivery Orders** |
| Active or past promotions | **Marketing Management → Promotions** |
| An application form to download | **Forms Management → Forms** |
| A customer complaint | **Complaint Management → Complaints** |
| A purchase request you raised | **Project Sales Admin → Purchase Requests** |
| Your outstanding tasks and SLA deadlines | **SLA Management → My Team Tasks** |

## 4. How to ask the AI assistant

The AI assistant answers questions in plain language and points you to the right page. Open it from the **AI assistant button** (the chat bubble) shown on every page, type your question, and press **Enter**.

It reads only the data you're allowed to see, and it always identifies things by their real names (product codes, order numbers, customer names) — never internal IDs.

Things you can ask:

* *"What can you do?"* — get a guided overview of everything the assistant can help with.
* *"List products in category Beverages."* — find catalogue items.
* *"Which items are low on stock right now?"* — check inventory.
* *"What is the status of order ORD-12345?"* — track a delivery order.
* *"How do I file a complaint?"* — get step-by-step instructions for a task.

If you're not sure how to phrase something, just describe what you want in your own words — the assistant will ask a follow-up if it needs more detail.
