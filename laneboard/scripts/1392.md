# Hand test #1392 (EMAIL-HANDOVER-QTY)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Sign in as a user who can open Supply Chain > Project Demand > Fulfilment Planning, Procurement > Supply Chain > Order Inquiries, and System Management > Email Templates and Email Outbox.
2. Pick a confirmed sales order line whose Order Inquiries row reads On PO/SPO (placed) with no document linked, or one that has two open ORDER rows. SO402757 line SRTWT6808 on the dev DB is the original case if it still has a placed row; any line with the same shape works. Note the row's quantity (call it Q) and its delivery date (D).
3. Emails on the test copy go to the outbox only; nothing is sent. Do not press Retry on any outbox row.

## Steps

1. From the sidebar open System Management > Email Templates and open "Order Inquiry Handover to Purchasing (default)".
2. Read the Width field under Preheader, then look at the preview's Desktop pane and its line table.
3. Click Edit, open the Width select, read its two options, then Cancel.
4. Re-upload the outstanding sales order book (the same upload that raises a planning change) with the line from Setup step 2 changed to a larger quantity (Q + 30) and an earlier required date (D minus 7 days).
5. From the sidebar open Supply Chain > Project Demand > Fulfilment Planning, open the order's Changed badge, and press Confirm on the changed line.
6. Open Procurement > Supply Chain > Order Inquiries, Lines view, and find the line's rows.
7. Open System Management > Email Outbox, find the newest row whose subject starts with "OI:" for that sales order, open its detail and read the Body preview.
8. Repeat steps 4 and 5 with the same line changed only in quantity (Q + 30 to Q + 40, date unchanged), then read the newest OI: outbox row again.
9. Open any other email template (for example Order Inquiry Undone) and read its Width field and preview.

## Expected

1. The template page opens.
2. Width reads "Wide (900px)". The Desktop preview is 900 wide; the sample line table shows every column on one line with no wrapped dates or quantities, and only REMARK wraps.
3. The Width select lists "Standard (600px)" and "Wide (900px)"; Cancel leaves the template unchanged.
4. The upload completes and the order carries a Changed badge.
5. Confirm succeeds.
6. The placed row still reads its original quantity Q with the new date (D minus 7); a fresh ORDER row for 30 sits beside it on the new date. Nothing about the worklist rows differs from before this change.
7. The body is the wide card. The line table holds ONE row for the line: QTY Q, QTY CHANGE TO Q + 30, DELIVERY DATE D, DELIVERY DATE CHANGE TO D minus 7, REMARK "ADVANCE, ORDER 30". There is no separate bare ORDER row for the same line. The headline names ADVANCE and ORDER. The QTY CHANGE TO and DELIVERY DATE CHANGE TO columns are present, and no date or quantity wraps inside its cell.
8. ONE row for the line: QTY Q + 30, QTY CHANGE TO Q + 40, DELIVERY DATE D minus 7, REMARK "ORDER 10"; the DELIVERY DATE CHANGE TO column is absent from this email because no line moved a date.
9. Width reads "Standard (600px)" and the Desktop preview is 600 wide, unchanged.
