# Hand test: PR #1934 CONFIRM-DOUBLE-ROWS (confirming a line again never holds what was short)

Owner, 28 Oct: "Fix double confirm". A Reserve that one Confirm recorded short was written in full by the next Confirm
of the same line (each press then kept a full Reserve row).

What a hand test can and cannot show:

- The board will not re-save an UNCHANGED decision on a confirmed line ("Save decision" stays disabled). So three
  identical presses only arrive from outside the board (a replayed or retried request).
- On fresh data, the board offers exactly what the Confirm then accepts. A Reserve is recorded short at a later
  Confirm only when the stock picture changed in between, for example an AutoCount sync added an earlier order for
  the same stock. That cannot be set up on demand from the board.
- The bug path is therefore proven by `tests/scm/test_confirm_double_rows.py` (12 tests, 9 fail on main) and by the
  sandbox replay in the PR comments. This hand test checks that Confirm otherwise works exactly as before.

## Setup
1. The test copy runs on the shared dev DB (prod copy). No migration.
2. Sign in as a user who can confirm on Supply Chain > Project Demand > Fulfilment Planning.
3. Pick an undecided order line whose own location shows free stock of at least the line's open quantity Q on the
   board. Note the SO number, the line, Q and the own location's code (LOC).

## Steps
1. From the home page, open the sidebar: Supply Chain > Project Demand > Fulfilment Planning.
2. Open the order and click "Change decision" on the line. Turn off "Buy the whole line", Reserve Q at LOC, give a
   reason, then Save decision, Confirm, Confirm 1 order.
3. Open the order's Project Sales SO page and read the line in the Allocation panel.
4. Back on the board: "Change decision", Amend, set Reserve 0 and Buy Q, Save decision, Confirm.
5. Read the Allocation panel again.
6. Back on the board: "Change decision", Amend, set Reserve Q at LOC and Buy 0, Save decision, Confirm.
7. Read the Allocation panel again.
8. Click "Change decision", Amend, and change nothing.
9. From the sidebar open Supply Chain > Stock Debt, search the product, open the line's month and find the line.

## Expected
- Step 3: one Reserve Q at LOC.
- Step 5: Buy Q only. The Reserve is gone, not added to.
- Step 7: one Reserve Q at LOC again. There is one Reserve row, never two, and never 2Q.
- Step 8: "Save decision" stays disabled.
- Step 9: the line reads as it did before step 2, by date: covered if nothing earlier needs LOC's stock, otherwise
  short. A Reserve holds no stock since #1919.
- No error at any step. The order's "Decided rev N" rises by exactly one at each of steps 2, 4 and 6.
