# Hand test: PR #1934 CONFIRM-DOUBLE-ROWS (pressing Confirm again changes nothing)

Owner, 28 Oct: "Fix double confirm". The bug: on the Fulfilment Planning board, a Reserve that the first Confirm
recorded short was written in full when the same decision was confirmed again. Each later press then kept a full
Reserve row.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
1. The test copy runs on the shared dev DB (prod copy). No migration.
2. Sign in as a user who can confirm on Supply Chain > Project Demand > Fulfilment Planning.
3. Pick any undecided project line whose own location has LESS free stock than the line's open quantity. The
   board's stock detail for the product shows that location's Free figure. Example: the line is open for 36 and its
   own location shows Free 10 or less. Note the line's SO number, line number, open quantity (Q) and the Free
   figure (F).

## Steps
1. From the home page, open the sidebar: Supply Chain > Project Demand > Fulfilment Planning.
2. Find the order picked in Setup and open the line.
3. Change its plan to Reserve Q from its own location, with nothing else on the line, and press Confirm.
4. Read the notice the Confirm shows.
5. Without changing anything, press Confirm on the same line again.
6. Read the notice again.
7. Press Confirm a third time, unchanged, and read the notice.
8. From the sidebar open Supply Chain > Stock Debt, search the product and open the line's month; find the line.
9. Open the order's Project Sales SO page and read the line in the Allocation panel.
10. Back on the board, change the line to Buy Q and press Confirm, then read the Allocation panel again.

## Expected
- Step 4: "Reserve Q at <location> confirmed as decided; only X free at <location> when confirmed, S short", with
  X at most F. X can be lower than F when an earlier-dated order needs the same stock (date order, #1919). Note X
  and S.
- Steps 6 and 7: the SAME notice, with the same X and S. Before this fix, the second press showed no notice and
  wrote the full Q.
- Step 8: the line's Stock Debt status and figures are the same after the third press as after the first.
- Step 9: the line shows ONE Reserve of X at its own location (none at all if X is 0). It never shows Q, and never
  two Reserve rows.
- Step 10: the line shows Buy Q only; the Reserve is gone, not added to.
