## Setup

1. Sign in as Teh Jayson (the E2E admin account).
2. No migration and no data step. Read-only: open and look only, never press Save all suggested, Decide or Confirm.
3. Pick two outstanding project sales orders on the Fulfilment Planning worklist (any two rows).

## Steps

1. From `/`, open Supply Chain > Project Demand > Fulfilment Planning from the sidebar.
2. Tick the two sales orders, press Plan together.
3. Look at the header row beside "Planning 2 sales orders together" (the board opens in List).
4. Press Grid.
5. Set Rows to Customer.
6. Set the date selector to By day.
7. Press List.
8. Press Grid.
9. Narrow the window to phone width (about 375px).
10. Press List.
11. Press Grid.

## Expected

- Step 3: the header row shows only the Grid | List toggle (List highlighted). No "Rows" label, no Product select, no By date select, no Earlier days / Later days.
- Step 4: the row now shows Rows [Product] [By date] to the left of Grid | List; Grid highlighted.
- Step 6: Earlier days and Later days appear at the left of the row; Rows reads Customer, the date select reads By day.
- Step 7: the row is back to only Grid | List; nothing else in the header moved.
- Step 8: Earlier days, Later days, Rows [Customer] and [By day] are back with the same choices.
- Step 9: Grid at 375px: Earlier days / Later days on one line, then Rows [Customer] full width, then [By day] full width, then Grid | List. No sideways scroll.
- Step 10: List at 375px: under the title only the Grid | List toggle, then straight to the summary bar. No sideways scroll.
- Step 11: the Grid controls come back with Customer and By day still selected.
