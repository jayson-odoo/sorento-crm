# Hand test: PR #2130 STOCKPOS-FILTER-MULTI (Stock position Supplier, Brand, Warehouse take several values)

Before this fix a second Brand pick replaced the first. Now each of the three filters keeps every
pick (OR inside a filter, AND across filters), the Excel export reads the same selection, and Back
from Stock status returns to the same filtered view.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Signed in as an admin, one browser tab.
- The copy's data holds items of at least two brands and at least two suppliers with stock (the
  prod copy does).

## Steps
1. From `/`, open the sidebar Inventory > Stock position.
2. Note the record count under the grid (call it ALL).
3. Click Filters, open Brand, tick one brand (call it B1). Note the count (N1).
4. In the same Brand list tick a second brand (B2). Close the list. Note the count (N12).
5. Open Supplier, tick two suppliers that appear in the Supplier column.
6. Open Warehouse, tick two warehouse codes.
7. Close Filters. Click the "Total stock" column header to sort it.
8. Click Export to Excel and open the downloaded workbook.
9. Click any item code in the grid (opens Stock status), then press the browser Back button.
10. Click Filters, then Clear filters.

## Expected
- Step 3: Brand shows B1; only B1 rows remain; Filters badge reads 1.
- Step 4: Brand shows "B1, B2" (both kept, B1 not replaced); rows are B1 or B2 only; N12 = N1 + the B2 count; the badge still reads 1 (it counts filters, not values).
- Step 5: rows are (B1 or B2) AND one of the two suppliers; badge reads 2; the trigger names the suppliers, never an id.
- Step 6: rows also hold stock in at least one of the two ticked warehouses; badge reads 3; the page is back to page 1.
- Step 7: the filtered rows sort by Total stock on the server; the filter selection is unchanged.
- Step 8: the workbook holds exactly the rows the grid counts (same count, same order).
- Step 9: Stock position comes back with the same three filters, the same sort and the same page (the address bar shows `brand_id=...,...` comma-joined).
- Step 10: every row is back; the count equals ALL; the badge is gone.
