# Hand test: PR #2194 PROPOSAL-HEADER-LAYOUT (proposal / quotation header like Order Inquiry; lines on the Header tab)

The proposal and quotation page (one page) now opens with the Order Inquiry record card: number,
status and version on one line, the title and any hint on their own lines under it, and on the
right the pager, gear, printer and the one main button. The customer line and the "The customer
holds this version. Edit opens the next one." hint are gone. The Lines tab is gone: the lines sit
on the Header tab under the letterhead, with no area row (area name, outcome, series, Record
outcome) and no version row (v1 pill, Recheck alerts, Revise to v2) above them. Recheck alerts and
Revise to vN are in the gear. Record outcome is no longer on this page (per-project outcome is
lane PROJECT-OUTCOME-SINGLE). Order Inquiry detail uses the same card, unchanged to the eye.

## Setup
- Signed in as a user who can edit projects (project sales rep or admin), window about 1440 px
  wide.
- Proposal PRJQ-2026-0005 "test" (status Accepted, Our Ref PRJQ-2026-0005-R2, total RM 350.00)
  on its project. Any sent proposal with at least one area works the same way.
- A second proposal on the same project, for step 9.
- A proposal with two versions (revised at least once), for steps 10 and 11. If none exists, skip
  those two steps (confirming step 13 on a TEST proposal would make one).
- Any order inquiry, for example OI-2609-0002.

## Steps
1. From `/`, open the sidebar Project Sales > Projects and open the project of PRJQ-2026-0005.
2. Open the Proposals tab and click PRJQ-2026-0005.
3. Look at the card at the top.
4. Look at the tabs under the card.
5. On the Header tab, scroll down below the letterhead card.
6. Click the gear.
7. Press Escape. Hover the printer button, then click it.
8. Close the downloads window. Click Send to Customer R3 (do not pick Email or WhatsApp), then press Escape.
9. Click > in the card's pager (top right), then click < to come back.
10. Open a proposal that has two versions and click the version chip next to the status.
11. Pick the older version (vN (frozen)), then click the chip and pick the current one.
12. Open the Customer feedback tab and, if it lists a note, click Show in Lines.
13. On a draft proposal (or a test one), click the gear, then Revise to vN, then Cancel.
14. Type `/lines` at the end of the address bar of the proposal and press Enter.
15. Narrow the window to 375 px (or open the page on a phone) and scroll the page sideways.
16. From `/`, open the sidebar Procurement > Supply Chain > Order Inquiries and click OI-2609-0002.
17. Back on a DRAFT proposal (or QT-2026-0001), click the gear, then Edit proposal (Edit quotation).
18. Look at the top of the edit page and at its tabs.
19. On the Header tab, scroll below the letterhead fields.
20. Click Cancel.
