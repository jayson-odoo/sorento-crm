# Hand test: PR #2186 TARGET-TIER-ADDITIONAL (tier Additional % or RM, reached tier only)

Each tier row now has its own Additional (a % or a fixed RM). Only the tier the period reaches
pays its Additional; lower tiers' Additionals no longer stack on top. Periods shows the rate as
"A/C I 5.00% + 1.00%". Old "+ Additional" rows keep paying the old way until they are folded
onto their tier (by hand below, or by `scripts/fold_tier_additionals.py --apply --expect N`
after the owner approves its dry run). Figures below are TGT-000008 A CRAFT IDEA SDN BHD on the
test copy's database (Q1 achieved 61,351.64, Q2 64,740.40).

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- The `crew-migration:` SQL from PR #2186 applied to the test copy's database.
- Signed in as an admin, one browser tab, window about 1280 px wide.
- Do NOT run `fold_tier_additionals.py --apply` on any shared database.

## Steps
1. From `/`, open the sidebar Sales > Targets, open the Dealers tab, open TGT-000008 A CRAFT IDEA SDN BHD (A/C I, 300-A077).
2. Look at the page header and the commission strip above the tabs.
3. Open the Details tab.
4. Open the Periods tab.
5. Click "Tier 1" on the 01/01/2026 - 31/03/2026 row.
6. Close the popup. Click Columns, untick "Pays to", then tick it again.
7. Open the Commission tab. Click Edit.
8. On each Q1 tier (60,000, 75,000, 85,000) type 1 in Additional and leave it on "%".
9. Delete the three Q1 "+ Additional" rows (Remove tier button on each), then Save.
10. Open the Periods tab again.
11. Open the Commission tab. In the Period filter pick 01/01/2026 - 31/03/2026.
12. Click Edit, on the Q1 85,000 tier switch Additional to "RM" and type 250, Save.
13. Clear the Period filter.
14. Open the Accounts tab and click the 01/01/2026 - 31/03/2026 period.
