# Hand test #1385 (ASKS-UX)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Sign in to the portal as a WhatsApp contact linked to a sales agent whose customers have asks (Jayden Loo's contact, the one behind the 30 Sep screenshots, is the reference). The Customer asks tab must be switched on for that contact (Contact page > Portal forms).
2. Have at least one open ask asked BEFORE today by a contact whose WhatsApp thread has more than 50 messages after it (Jayson's Hanlim asks qualify), and at least one ask in the done history.
3. For the CRM half, sign in to the CRM as the user linked to the same agent's contact (or any user with sales.customer_asks.view_all).

## Steps

1. Open the portal, Customer asks tab, Cards view. Press Filter.
2. In the popover, open the Contact select and pick "Jayson". Close the popover.
3. Press Filter again and press Clear all.
4. With Cards view still chosen, press Show done.
5. Press the Reopen button on one done card (not the card body).
6. Switch to List view with Show done still open.
7. Switch back to Cards view and press Hide done.
8. Tap one of Jayson's asks asked before today (the card body).
9. In the drawer, scroll the conversation up past the top of what is loaded, then keep scrolling.
10. Press the arrow button at the bottom right of the conversation.
11. Press Jump to message in the Asked / Answered block.
12. Press the magnifier in the conversation header, type a word from an old message (for example "hanlim"), and use the arrows to step through the matches.
13. In the CRM, from the sidebar open Sales > Customer asks and repeat steps 1, 2, 8, 11.

## Expected

1. The popover lists Customer, Contact, Product, Answer, Created, State (Contact is new, second).
2. The Contact select offers only the contact names present in the loaded asks; picking Jayson leaves only Jayson's cards in Needs attention, Today and Done today, and the Filter button shows a 1 badge.
3. Every card is back and the badge is gone.
4. A "Done" heading appears under the to-do with the done asks as CARDS (same shape as the to-do cards, a Reopen button top right, "Done by ..." line), no table. With no done ask the section reads "No done asks yet". More than 20 done asks: a pager under the cards.
5. That ask leaves the Done section and appears in the to-do above (Needs attention or Today); no drawer opens.
6. The done history becomes the table (Asked at, Customer, Contact, Product, Qty, Branch, Answer, Notified, State, Done by, Note), same rows.
7. The history is gone; the button reads Show done again.
8. The drawer opens with the WhatsApp-style thread (contact name and phone in the chat header, bubbles left for the customer and right for us, a date pill per day). The thread is centred on the ask's answer bubble, which carries a "This enquiry" tag, NOT on today's messages. There is no "Show the whole day" button.
9. Older messages load in pages as you scroll up until "Beginning of this conversation" shows; nothing is limited to one day.
10. The thread lands on the newest message of the whole conversation (today's), the arrow disappears while you are at the bottom.
11. The thread scrolls back to the "This enquiry" bubble and flashes it, loading the page around it if needed.
12. Matches are counted ("1 of N"), the active one is ringed and scrolled into view, the word is highlighted in the bubbles; closing search returns to the newest messages.
13. Same as the portal, plus "Open in Conversations" under the thread opens the Conversations inbox on that contact.
