# PR 2366 hand test: price tag request, tick several items from one search

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- No migration. Sign in as a salesperson whose contact has the Price Tag Request form (a segment that grants it, or a per-contact override). Admins create price tag requests from the staff pages, not this form.
- The catalogue has several products whose code contains `DIY` (for example `SRT6536-DIY`). Any shared prefix or suffix works; replace `DIY` below if the copy has none.

## Steps
1. From `/`, open MY REQUESTS > Price Tag Request in the sidebar (or Dealer Kit > Room Designer > Price Tag Requests if that is what your sidebar shows) and click Create.
2. Pick a customer, open the Price section (or the lines table), and on line 1 click "Search a set or product...".
3. Type `DIY`.
4. Click one result (anywhere on its row).
5. Without reopening the list, click two more results, one after the other.
6. Click the second of the three results again, then look at the lines table and the list.
7. Click that result once more, then press Esc.
8. Look at the lines table.
9. Click line 1's picker again and type `DIY`.
10. Look at the three items you added.
11. Click "Select all N loaded" at the top of the list, then press Esc.
12. Look at the lines table.
13. On line 1, click the clear button next to its item.
14. Click "+ Add line", open the new line's picker, and type `D`.
15. Press Esc.
16. Shrink the window to phone width (375 px), start a new request, and repeat steps 2 to 7. Then return to the first request at full width.
17. Click Save Draft, then reopen the draft from the list.
18. Click "+ Add line", type `test` in the new line's Remarks without picking an item, and click Save Draft.
19. Clear that line's remarks and click Save Draft again.

## Expected
- Step 3: each result is one line showing only the product code (no name, no price); a set shows a grey "Set" hint, a discontinued product a grey "Discontinued" hint.
- Step 4: the list stays open, `DIY` stays in the search box, the clicked row shows a tick, and line 1 now holds that item.
- Step 5: the list stays open the whole time; each click adds its own line right under the previous one, so lines 1 to 3 hold the three items in the order you clicked them. No empty line is left.
- Step 6: the second item's line is gone; the other two keep their order; its row in the list is unticked.
- Step 7: the item is added back on a new line under the last one; Esc closes the list and the cursor is back on line 1's item box.
- Step 8: each line's Item shows the item's CODE, for a set exactly as for a product (hover it to see the name), Qty (tags) 1, empty remarks, and a product line its list price (a set line shows no list price, as today).
- Step 10: the three items show ticked, greyed, "Already added", and clicking them does nothing.
- Step 11: every shown item not yet on the request is added, each on its own line, in the order the list shows them, directly under line 1 (the line whose picker you opened).
- Step 13: line 1's item is emptied; "Search a set or product..." shows again.
- Step 14: results that are already on the request are greyed with "Already added".
- Step 16: the list fits the screen, you can click three results in a row without it closing, and the page never scrolls sideways.
- Step 17: "Draft saved". The reopened draft shows the same items in the same order, each Item showing its code, without the two empty lines (line 1 cleared in step 13 and the line added in step 14): an empty line is simply left out of a draft.
- Step 18: nothing is saved, and that line shows "Pick a set or a product for this line." under it.
- Step 19: "Draft saved"; the empty line is left out.
