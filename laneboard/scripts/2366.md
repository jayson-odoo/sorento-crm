# PR 2366 hand test: price tag request, tick several items from one search

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- No migration. Sign in as a salesperson whose contact has the Price Tag Request form (a segment that grants it, or a per-contact override). Admins create price tag requests from the staff pages, not this form.
- The catalogue has several products whose code contains `DIY` (for example `SRT6536-DIY`). Any shared prefix or suffix works; replace `DIY` below if the copy has none.

## Steps
1. From `/`, open MY REQUESTS > Price Tag Request in the sidebar and click Create.
2. Pick a customer, open the Price section (or the lines table), and on line 1 click "Search a set or product...".
3. Type `DIY`.
4. Click one result (anywhere on its row).
5. Without reopening the list, click two more results, one after the other.
6. Look at the list, then press Esc.
7. Look at the lines table.
8. Click line 1's picker again and type `DIY`.
9. Look at the three items you added.
10. Click "Select all N loaded" at the top of the list, then press Esc.
11. Look at the lines table.
12. On line 1, click the clear button next to its item.
13. Click "+ Add line", open the new line's picker, and type `D`.
14. Press Esc.
15. Shrink the window to phone width (375 px), start a new request, and repeat steps 2 to 7.
16. Click Save Draft, then reopen the draft from the list.

## Expected
- Step 3: each result is one line showing only the product code (no name, no price); a set shows a grey "Set" hint, a discontinued product a grey "Discontinued" hint.
- Step 4: the list stays open, `DIY` stays in the search box, the clicked row shows a tick and "Already added", and line 1 now holds that item.
- Step 5: the list stays open the whole time; each click adds its own line right under the previous one, so lines 1 to 3 hold the three items in the order you clicked them. No empty line is left.
- Step 6: all three clicked rows are ticked, greyed and read "Already added"; Esc closes the list.
- Step 7: each line's Item shows the product's name (as when you pick one), Qty (tags) 1, empty remarks, and a product line its list price (a set line shows no list price, as today).
- Step 9: the three items show ticked, greyed, "Already added", and clicking them does nothing.
- Step 10: every shown item not yet on the request is added, each on its own line under the last one you added, in the order the list shows them.
- Step 12: line 1's item is emptied; "Search a set or product..." shows again.
- Step 13: results that are already on the request are greyed with "Already added".
- Step 15: the list fits the screen, you can click three results in a row without it closing, and the page never scrolls sideways.
- Step 16: the reopened draft shows the same lines in the same order.
