# Hand test #1433 (DO-ASK-SIMPLIFY)

## URL
crew test copy (local, real dev data, LIVE parser)

## Setup

1. Apply the migration's seed on the test copy DB. It is the same statement as migration `do_ask_0001_reveals`: it switches EVERY DO field ON for every existing contact, and re-running it changes nothing.
   `INSERT INTO contact_field_reveals (id, respond_contact_id, field_key, granted, created_by) SELECT gen_random_uuid(), c.id, k.key, true, 'migration:do_ask_0001_reveals' FROM respond_contacts c CROSS JOIN (VALUES ('delivery_orders.status'), ('delivery_orders.pickup_time'), ('delivery_orders.transporter'), ('delivery_orders.driver'), ('delivery_orders.lorry_plate'), ('delivery_orders.order_number'), ('delivery_orders.customer'), ('delivery_orders.order_date'), ('delivery_orders.delivery_date'), ('delivery_orders.warehouse'), ('delivery_orders.products')) AS k(key) ON CONFLICT (respond_contact_id, field_key) DO UPDATE SET granted = true;`
2. Sign in as an admin.
3. EXISTING contact: any contact that existed before step 1 and is either linked to no customer or holds a "Sorento Office" access type (an office/internal contact).
4. NEW contact: create one (or use any contact created after step 1) with no Field reveals saved.
5. DEALER contact (for steps 7 to 13): linked to the HANLIM TRADING ledgers on its contact page, with NO "Sorento Office" / "Cabana Office" / "Mocha Office" access type.

## Steps

1. From the sidebar open **Users & Access > People > Internal Users**, open the EXISTING contact, open its **Access** tab and read the **Field reveals** card.
2. Do the same for the NEW contact.
3. From the sidebar open **System > Messaging > Chatbot Console** and pick the EXISTING contact.
4. Send: `delivery to hanlim`
5. Send: `this month`
6. Send: `delivery to hanlim january to june`
7. Open the DEALER contact's **Access** tab (same path as step 1) > **Field reveals**. Turn OFF: DO status, DO pickup time, DO transporter, DO driver, DO lorry plate and DO warehouse. There is no Save button: each switch asks "Hide ...?"; click **Hide**, and the switch saves.
8. In the Chatbot Console pick the DEALER contact (Reset first).
9. Send: `delivery to hanlim`
10. Send: `this month`
11. Send: `delivery to hanlim january to june`
12. Send: `delivery to hanlim in 2026`
13. Send: `delivery to hanlim 15 sep to 10 oct`

## Expected

1. Steps 1 and 2: one switch per DO field, ALL ON for BOTH the existing and the NEW contact (owner rule, 4 Oct: every DO field defaults ON; the owner turns off what he wants): DO customer, DO delivery date, DO driver, DO lorry plate, DO order date, DO order number, DO pickup time, DO products, DO status, DO transporter, DO warehouse. Non-DO switches (e.g. Last purchase cost, PO supplier) stay OFF for the new contact.
2. The DO range rules apply to EVERY contact, office/internal ones included (owner ruling, 4 Oct, replacing "dealers only"). Step 4 (office contact): no list. The reply reads:
   - `Which period for HANLIM TRADING SDN BHD?`
   - `- This month (Oct 2026)`
   - `- Last month (Sep 2026)`
   - `Or type a month (e.g. August) or dates (e.g. 15 Sep to 10 Oct).`
3. Step 5: the DO list for 01/10/2026 to 31/10/2026 for HANLIM only (not every customer).
   - The header reads `Customer: HANLIM TRADING SDN BHD`: the group name only, with no account count, no "and N more" and no "[A/C ...]". Each row still shows its own full ledger name.
   - Every row shows Status, Pickup Time, Transporter, Driver, Lorry Plate and Warehouse (all on for this contact).
4. Step 6: no list. The reply reads `That is 6 months (01/01/2026 to 30/06/2026). I can show up to 31 days of delivery orders at a time:`, then `- Jun 2026`, `- Jan 2026`, `Or type a month or dates.`
5. Step 7: the six switches read off, and the other five stay on.
6. Step 9: the same period question as step 4.
7. Step 10: this month's DO list for the SAME HANLIM accounts, not every customer the dealer is linked to.
   - Rows show Order Number, Customer, Order Date, Actual Delivery Date and Products.
   - Rows show NO Status, Pickup Time, Transporter, Driver, Lorry Plate or Warehouse.
8. Step 11: the same 6 months refusal as step 6.
9. Step 12: no list. The reply reads `That is 12 months (01/01/2026 to 31/12/2026). I can show up to 31 days of delivery orders at a time:`, then `- Oct 2026`, `- Jan 2026`, `Or type a month or dates.`
10. Step 13: the DO list for 15/09/2026 to 10/10/2026, with no question and no Warehouse line.
11. In the DO header and the period question a customer company is named by its customer GROUP name, once; a ledger in no group prints its own full name. Never `(N accounts)` or `and N more`. The "Sorry, that isn't under your account. I can only check on ..." line groups by the name rule since SO-NUMBER-ASK (#1435): grouped ledgers print the group name once, and an ungrouped ledger prints without its account marker (e.g. `ZZT OTHER SDN BHD`, not `ZZT OTHER SDN BHD [A/C I]`).
12. No period question when the ask names a DO/SO/order number, asks for a quantity, or asks for outstanding orders.

Not a hand step: the new-contact default is pinned by `tests/chatbot/test_do_reveals_default_on.py`; the migration's seed for existing contacts by `tests/test_migration_do_ask_0001_reveals.py`.
