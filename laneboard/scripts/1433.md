# Hand test #1433 (DO-ASK-SIMPLIFY)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Apply the migration's seed on the test copy DB (the same statement as migration
   `do_ask_0001_reveals`; it switches EVERY DO field ON for every existing contact, and
   re-running it changes nothing):
   `INSERT INTO contact_field_reveals (id, respond_contact_id, field_key, granted, created_by) SELECT gen_random_uuid(), c.id, k.key, true, 'migration:do_ask_0001_reveals' FROM respond_contacts c CROSS JOIN (VALUES ('delivery_orders.status'), ('delivery_orders.pickup_time'), ('delivery_orders.transporter'), ('delivery_orders.driver'), ('delivery_orders.lorry_plate'), ('delivery_orders.order_number'), ('delivery_orders.customer'), ('delivery_orders.order_date'), ('delivery_orders.delivery_date'), ('delivery_orders.warehouse'), ('delivery_orders.products')) AS k(key) ON CONFLICT (respond_contact_id, field_key) DO UPDATE SET granted = true;`
2. Sign in as an admin.
3. EXISTING contact: any contact that existed before step 1 (e.g. the owner's own), not
   linked to customers, or holding a "Sorento Office" access type.
4. DEALER contact (for steps 6 to 10): linked to the HANLIM TRADING ledgers on its contact
   page, with NO "Sorento Office" / "Cabana Office" / "Mocha Office" access type.

## Steps

1. From the sidebar open User Management > Contacts, open the EXISTING contact, then its
   Access tab, and read the Field reveals card.
2. From the sidebar open System Management > Chatbot Console and pick the EXISTING contact.
3. Send: delivery to hanlim this month
4. Send: delivery to hanlim january to june
5. Back on the DEALER contact's Access tab > Field reveals, untick DO status, DO pickup time,
   DO transporter, DO driver, DO lorry plate and DO warehouse, then Save.
6. In the Chatbot Console pick the DEALER contact.
7. Send: delivery to hanlim
8. Send: this month
9. Send: delivery to hanlim january to june
10. Send: delivery to hanlim 15 sep to 10 oct

## Expected

1. Step 1: one switch per DO field, ALL TICKED for the existing contact: DO customer,
   DO delivery date, DO driver, DO lorry plate, DO order date, DO order number, DO pickup time,
   DO products, DO status, DO transporter, DO warehouse.
2. Step 3 (EXISTING contact, not a dealer, so never asked for a period or capped: owner
   ruling Q2 (b), 2 Oct): the DO list for 01/10/2026 to 31/10/2026; the header reads
   `Customer: HANLIM TRADING SDN BHD` (the group name only: no account count, no
   "and N more", no "[A/C ...]" marker; each row still shows its own full ledger name); every row shows Status, Pickup Time, Transporter, Driver and Lorry Plate, exactly
   as before this change.
3. Step 4: the DO list for January to June, with no refusal (only dealers are capped).
4. Step 7: no list; the reply reads `Which period for HANLIM TRADING SDN BHD?`,
   `- This month (Oct 2026)`, `- Last month (Sep 2026)`,
   `Or type a month (e.g. August) or dates (e.g. 15 Sep to 10 Oct).`
5. Step 8: the DO list for this month for the SAME HANLIM accounts (not every linked
   customer); rows show Order Number, Customer, Order Date, Actual Delivery Date and
   Products, and NO Status, Pickup Time, Transporter, Driver, Lorry Plate or Warehouse.
6. Step 9: no list; `That is 6 months (01/01/2026 to 30/06/2026). I can show up to 31 days
   of delivery orders at a time:`, `- Jun 2026`, `- Jan 2026`, `Or type a month or dates.`
7. Step 10: the DO list for 15/09/2026 to 10/10/2026, no question.
8. Anywhere a customer company is named in these replies (header, period question, the
   "Sorry, that isn't under your account. I can only check on ..." line): its customer GROUP
   name, once, e.g. `HANLIM TRADING SDN BHD` (Master Data > Customer Groups; the HANLIM
   ledgers are one group there); a ledger in no group prints its own full name (CUSTOMER-GROUP
   ruling (b), #1441). Never `(6 accounts)`, `(2 accounts)` or `and N more`.

Not a hand step: a contact created AFTER the migration starts with every DO field hidden,
pinned by `sorento_crm_backend/tests/test_migration_do_ask_0001_reveals.py::test_a_contact_created_after_the_migration_has_no_do_field`;
an existing contact ends with every field on, pinned by
`::test_an_existing_contact_ends_with_every_do_field_switched_on`.
