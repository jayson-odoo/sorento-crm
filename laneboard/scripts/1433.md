# Hand test #1433 (DO-ASK-SIMPLIFY)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Apply the migration's seed on the test copy DB (the same statement as migration
   `do_ask_0001_reveals`; it switches the five DO fields ON for every existing contact, and
   re-running it changes nothing):
   `INSERT INTO contact_field_reveals (id, respond_contact_id, field_key, granted, created_by) SELECT gen_random_uuid(), c.id, k.key, true, 'migration:do_ask_0001_reveals' FROM respond_contacts c CROSS JOIN (VALUES ('delivery_orders.status'), ('delivery_orders.pickup_time'), ('delivery_orders.transporter'), ('delivery_orders.driver'), ('delivery_orders.lorry_plate')) AS k(key) ON CONFLICT (respond_contact_id, field_key) DO UPDATE SET granted = true;`
2. Sign in as an admin.
3. EXISTING contact: any contact that existed before step 1 (e.g. the owner's own), not
   linked to customers, or holding a "Sorento Office" access type.
4. DEALER contact (for steps 6 to 10): linked to the HANLIM TRADING ledgers on its contact
   page, with NO "Sorento Office" / "Cabana Office" / "Mocha Office" access type.

## Steps

1. From the sidebar open User Management > Contacts, open the EXISTING contact, then its
   Access tab, and read the Field reveals card.
2. From the sidebar open System Management > Chatbot Console and pick the EXISTING contact.
3. Send: delivery to hanlim this month
4. Send: delivery to hanlim january to june
5. Back on the DEALER contact's Access tab > Field reveals, untick DO status, DO pickup time,
   DO transporter, DO driver and DO lorry plate, then Save.
6. In the Chatbot Console pick the DEALER contact.
7. Send: delivery to hanlim
8. Send: this month
9. Send: delivery to hanlim january to june
10. Send: delivery to hanlim 15 sep to 10 oct

## Expected

1. Step 1: DO status, DO pickup time, DO transporter, DO driver and DO lorry plate are all
   TICKED for the existing contact.
2. Step 3: the DO list for 01/10/2026 to 31/10/2026; the header reads
   `Customer: HANLIM TRADING SDN BHD (N accounts)` (one ledger prints its full name, no
   count); every row shows Status, Pickup Time, Transporter, Driver and Lorry Plate, exactly
   as before this change.
3. Step 4: the DO list for January to June, with no refusal (only dealers are capped).
4. Step 7: no list; the reply reads `Which period for HANLIM TRADING SDN BHD (N accounts)?`,
   `- This month (Oct 2026)`, `- Last month (Sep 2026)`,
   `Or type a month (e.g. August) or dates (e.g. 15 Sep to 10 Oct).`
5. Step 8: the DO list for this month for the SAME HANLIM accounts (not every linked
   customer); rows show Order Number, Customer, Order Date, Actual Delivery Date, Warehouse
   and Products, and NO Status, Pickup Time, Transporter, Driver or Lorry Plate.
6. Step 9: no list; `That is 6 months (01/01/2026 to 30/06/2026). I can show up to 31 days
   of delivery orders at a time:`, `- Jun 2026`, `- Jan 2026`, `Or type a month or dates.`
7. Step 10: the DO list for 15/09/2026 to 10/10/2026, no question.

Not a hand step: a contact created AFTER the migration starts with all five DO fields hidden,
pinned by `sorento_crm_backend/tests/test_migration_do_ask_0001_reveals.py::test_a_contact_created_after_the_migration_has_no_do_field`;
an existing contact ends with every field on, pinned by
`::test_an_existing_contact_ends_with_every_do_field_switched_on`.
