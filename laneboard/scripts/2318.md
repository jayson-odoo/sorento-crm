# PR 2318 hand test: price tag request, customers first, one Price tags step, Preview then Approve, Edit as create

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- The test copy's DB needs `ALTER TABLE price_tag_requests ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS customers JSONB;` (the crew-migration on the PR).
- Sign in as admin, then use View as a salesperson (for example Eric Ng) who has at least three customers in the Customers list.
- Use a product WITH a tag template: SRT6544-DIY, or the SRTKS7061 package and pick 2 taps. (SRTKS7061-BL, SRTSC07 and SRTWT5830-BL show "No tag template yet".) Have one sales order PDF or photo handy.
- PT-202611-0006 (approved by crew) and PT-202611-0007 (Approved) exist already and can be used for the Edit steps.

## Steps
1. From `/`, open MY REQUESTS > Price Tag Request in the sidebar and click Create.
2. In Customers tick two customers.
3. In Price tags click "Upload a file", drop the sales order and use the extract action on its tile as today.
4. Click "Type the items instead", click Add line and pick SRT6544-DIY.
5. Click Preview, then Approve in the popup.
6. Open the request you just approved from the list.
7. Click Preview at the top of the request page, look, then close the popup.
8. Click Edit.
9. Add a third customer, change the quantity of the line to 3.
10. Click Preview, then Approve in the popup.
11. Open the request again and click the History tab.
12. Click Edit, then Cancel.
13. Shrink the browser to phone width (375px) and repeat steps 6 to 8.

## Expected
- Step 2-4: the form has three sections (Customers, Price tags, Additional information); Price tags asks "How do you want to add the items?"; the upload path shows only the upload box with "Type the items instead" at the right and no "Sales Order" heading; the typed path shows the table and Add line with "Upload a file instead" at the right and no "Lines" heading.
- Step 5: toast "Tag approved" and you are back on the list.
- Step 6: the request page is compact: Status, then Customers, Price tags, Additional information. No design is drawn on the page. At the top right: Preview, a blue Edit, and the gear. The Price tags card has no "Sales Order" and no "Lines (1)" heading; with no file it says "No files attached.".
- Step 7: the design opens in the popup (zoom, Download PDF once approved); closing it returns to the compact page.
- Step 8: the SAME form as Create opens, titled "Edit PT-...": no Reason box, no "Submit revision"; at the bottom Cancel and a blue Preview.
- Step 10: the popup opens with "Back to edit" and a blue Approve; Approve gives toast "Tag approved" and you are back on the list.
- Step 11: the tab is called History (not Revisions); the newest entry reads "Edit 1" with the customer and quantity changes; nothing on the page says "revision".
- Step 12: Cancel returns to the request page unchanged, nothing saved.
- Step 13: everything fits at phone width with no sideways scrolling; Preview and Edit stay visible at the top.
