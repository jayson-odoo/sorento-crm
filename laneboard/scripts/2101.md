# Hand test #2101 (PRICETAG-SELF-APPROVAL)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Have a portal contact (salesperson) who can see Price Tag Request, and the portal link for it.
2. Have one product with exactly one published tag template linked to it (Products > the product > tag
   templates, from #2100). Note its code and list price.
3. In SLA > Form SLA, have an active config for Price Tag Request on the submit event whose tier-1 team has
   one member (the marketing assignee, Akmal on dev). Note that person's name.
4. Sign in to the CRM in a second window as that marketing assignee.

## Steps

1. In the portal, open Price Tag Request > New. Pick a dealer, Printing "I print myself", add the product
   from Setup 2, and press Submit.
2. Open the request from the portal list.
3. Look at the Design card.
4. In the CRM window, open Dealer Kit > Room Designer > Price Tag Requests and open the same request.
5. Back in the portal, press Approve in the Design card header.
6. Reopen the request and open the gear menu.
7. Press Download PDF and open the file.
8. Open the gear menu again and press Edit. Change the line quantity to 2, type a reason, and send the
   revision.
9. Open the gear menu.
10. Press Approve again, then Download PDF.
11. Open the Revisions tab.
12. Open the gear menu and press Escalate to marketing. Type "Photo is the old model" and press Send to
    marketing.
13. In the CRM window, reload the request.
14. In the CRM, open Design tags.
15. In the portal, reopen the request and open the gear menu.
16. Open Settings > Portal revisions in the CRM.

## Expected

1. Submit succeeds; the portal returns to the list; a WhatsApp reads "<doc> received. Your tag is ready for
   you to check and approve."
2. Status reads Design Ready.
3. The tag for the product is drawn with the template from Setup 2. Buttons: Approve (header), Edit and
   Escalate to marketing (under the tag).
4. Status Design Ready, nobody assigned, no Claim and no Design tags button (Void only). Marketing has nothing to do.
5. Toast "Tag approved"; status Approved.
6. Gear: Download PDF (or Preparing your PDF), Duplicate, Edit, Escalate to marketing.
7. The PDF shows the same tag as step 3, with the product's current price.
8. The revision is accepted with no "revisions left" count and no cap message.
9. Status is Design Ready again; Download PDF reads "Available after approval".
10. Approved again; the new PDF shows the edited request.
11. Two rows: the original and revision 1 with the reason from step 8.
12. Toast "Sent to marketing"; a WhatsApp reads "<doc> sent to marketing with your note.", then "<doc> is
    being designed by <assignee from Setup 3>".
13. Status Designing, assigned to the person from Setup 3; the change request list shows "Photo is the old
    model".
14. The designer opens; marketing can draw and Mark design ready as before.
15. While marketing holds it, the gear has no Edit and no Escalate to marketing; the header reads
    "Marketing is working on this request. Wait for their proof."
16. The list has no Price Tag Request row.
