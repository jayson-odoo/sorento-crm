# Hand test #2101 (PRICETAG-SELF-APPROVAL)

Owner ruling 3 Nov 14:10: the New Price Tag Request screen has no Submit. It ends in **Approve**
(created AND approved in one step, from the preview) or **Escalate to marketing** (note required).
Save Draft stays.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Have a portal contact (salesperson) who can see Price Tag Request, and the portal link for it.
2. Products (#2100 is on main; templates are linked under Products > the product > tag templates):
   - SRTKS7061, with exactly one published tag template linked. Note its list price.
   - one product with NO linked template, for steps 16-17;
   - one product with TWO linked templates, for step 18;
   - one Cabana product (a brand whose promotions carry no selling price, #2104), for step 19. On dev
     CKS821 is Cabana but has NO linked template: link one published template to it first (Products >
     CKS821 > tag templates), or it can only be escalated.
3. In SLA > Form SLA, have an active config for Price Tag Request on the submit event. Escalation goes
   round-robin across its tier-1 team (three members on dev, Akmal is one), so the request may land on any
   of them. Note their names. For Akmal every time, leave him as the team's only member.
4. Sign in to the CRM in a second window as an admin or one of those members.

## Steps

1. In the portal, open Price Tag Request > New. Pick a dealer and add SRTKS7061. Look at the Preview and
   at the buttons at the bottom of the form.
2. Press Approve.
3. Open the request from the portal list and open the gear menu.
4. In the CRM window, open Dealer Kit > Room Designer > Price Tag Requests and open the same request.
5. In the portal, press Download PDF and open the file.
6. Open the gear menu and press Edit. Change the line quantity to 2, type a reason, and send the revision.
7. Look at the request and its gear menu.
8. Press Approve in the Design card header, then Download PDF.
9. Open the Revisions tab.
10. Open the gear menu and press Escalate to marketing. Type "Photo is the old model" and press Send to
    marketing.
11. In the CRM window, reload the request, then open Design tags.
12. In the portal, reopen the request and open the gear menu.
13. Open Settings > Portal revisions in the CRM.

Saved draft:
14. New request: pick a dealer, add SRTKS7061, press Save Draft, go back to the list and reopen the draft.
15. Press Approve.

No template:
16. New request: pick a dealer, add SRTKS7061 AND the product with no linked template. Look at the
    bottom of the form.
17. Press Escalate to marketing, type a note, and press Send to marketing. Reload the CRM list.

Two templates:
18. New request: add the product with two linked templates. Press Approve before picking a template; then
    open the template picker, preview both, pick the second, and press Approve.

Cabana line:
19. New request: switch the price to Selling, add the Cabana product, check the Preview, press Approve.

## Expected

1. The Preview draws the SRTKS7061 tag with its template and price. The bottom of the form shows Save
   Draft, Escalate to marketing and Approve. There is no Submit button.
2. Toast "Tag approved"; the portal returns to the list; one WhatsApp reads "You approved <doc>. The PDF is
   being prepared." (no separate "received" message).
3. Status Approved. Gear: Download PDF (or Preparing your PDF), Duplicate, Edit, Escalate to marketing.
4. Status Approved, nobody assigned; marketing was never notified and no SLA stage exists.
5. The PDF shows the same tag as the step 1 Preview, with the product's current price.
6. The revision is accepted with no "revisions left" count and no cap message.
7. Status Design Ready. The Design card draws the tag (2 copies) with Approve in its header and Edit /
   Escalate to marketing under it; Download PDF reads "Available after approval". In the CRM the card still
   shows the last APPROVED design (from step 2) until he approves again, and only Void is offered.
8. Approved again; the new PDF shows 2 tags.
9. Two rows: "Original (reconstructed)" and revision 1 with the reason from step 6.
10. Toast "Sent to marketing"; a WhatsApp reads "<doc> sent to marketing with your note.", then "<doc> is
    being designed by <one of the members from Setup 3>".
11. Status Designing, assigned to one of the members from Setup 3; the change request list shows "Photo is
    the old model". The designer opens; marketing can draw and Mark design ready as before.
12. While marketing holds it, the gear has no Edit and no Escalate to marketing; the header reads
    "Marketing is working on this request. Wait for their proof."
13. The list has no Price Tag Request row.
14. The reopened draft shows the same Preview and the same three buttons: Save Draft, Escalate to
    marketing, Approve.
15. As step 2: created and approved in one step.
16. Approve is disabled. Right beside the buttons: "No tag template yet for <code of the unlinked product>.
    Escalate it to marketing." Hovering the greyed Approve shows the same sentence.
17. Toast "Sent to marketing"; the request arrives at marketing (Designing under a Setup 3 member) with the
    note in its change requests.
18. The first Approve is refused with the template picker named on the line. After picking the second
    template the Preview draws it, and Approve creates and approves the request; its PDF uses the
    second template.
19. The Cabana line carries no promotion selling price (#2104): the tag shows its selling price equal to its
    list price with no promo price; Approve works as in step 2.
