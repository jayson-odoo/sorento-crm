# Hand test #2101 (PRICETAG-SELF-APPROVAL)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Have a portal contact (salesperson) who can see Price Tag Request, and the portal link for it.
2. Have one product with exactly one published tag template linked to it (Products > the product > tag
   templates; #2100 is on main). On dev use SRTKS7061. Note its list price. Also note:
   - one product with NO linked template, for step 22;
   - one product with TWO linked templates, for step 23;
   - one Cabana product (a brand whose promotions carry no selling price, #2104; on dev CKS821), for
     step 24.
3. In SLA > Form SLA, have an active config for Price Tag Request on the submit event. Escalation goes
   round-robin across its tier-1 team (three members on dev, Akmal is one), so the request may land on any
   of them. Note their names. For Akmal every time, leave him as the team's only member.
4. Sign in to the CRM in a second window as an admin or one of those members.

## Steps

1. In the portal, open Price Tag Request > New. Pick a dealer, add the product from Setup 2, and press
   Submit.
2. Open the request from the portal list.
3. Look at the Design card.
4. In the CRM window, open Dealer Kit > Room Designer > Price Tag Requests and open the same request.
5. Back in the portal, press Approve in the Design card header.
6. Reopen the request and open the gear menu.
7. Press Download PDF and open the file.
8. Open the gear menu again and press Edit. Change the line quantity to 2, type a reason, and send the
   revision.
9. Open the gear menu.
10. Press Approve again, then Download PDF.
11. Open the Revisions tab.
12. Open the gear menu and press Escalate to marketing. Type "Photo is the old model" and press Send to
    marketing.
13. In the CRM window, reload the request.
14. In the CRM, open Design tags.
15. In the portal, reopen the request and open the gear menu.
16. Open Settings > Portal revisions in the CRM.

Optional, ready for collection (a portal request reaches it only when the office sets the printing):
17. Submit a second request as in step 1 and leave it at Design Ready.
18. In the CRM, open it, gear > Edit request, set Printing to Office prints, and save.
19. In the portal, Approve it.
20. In the CRM, press Mark ready for collection.
21. In the portal, reopen it and open the gear menu.

No template:
22. Submit a request with the product from Setup 2 AND the product with no linked template, and open it.
    Press Escalate to marketing under the tag (it is there at Design Ready), type a note, and send.

Two templates:
23. New request: add the product with two linked templates. On its line, open the template picker,
    preview both, pick the second, and Submit. Open the request.

Cabana line:
24. New request: switch the price to Selling, add the Cabana product, and Submit. Open the request.

## Expected

1. Submit succeeds; the portal returns to the list; a WhatsApp reads "<doc> received. Your tag is ready for
   you to check and approve."
2. Status reads Design Ready.
3. The tag for the product is drawn with the template from Setup 2. Buttons: Approve (header), Edit and
   Escalate to marketing (under the tag).
4. Status Design Ready, nobody assigned, no Claim and no Design tags button (Void only); the Design card reads
   "No design yet" with the hint "With the salesperson for approval." Marketing has nothing to do.
5. Toast "Tag approved"; status Approved.
6. Gear: Download PDF (or Preparing your PDF), Duplicate, Edit, Escalate to marketing.
7. The PDF shows the same tag as step 3, with the product's current price.
8. The revision is accepted with no "revisions left" count and no cap message.
9. Status is Design Ready again; Download PDF reads "Available after approval".
10. Approved again; the new PDF shows the edited request.
11. Two rows: the original and revision 1 with the reason from step 8.
12. Toast "Sent to marketing"; a WhatsApp reads "<doc> sent to marketing with your note.", then "<doc> is
    being designed by <one of the members from Setup 3>".
13. Status Designing, assigned to one of the members from Setup 3; the change request list shows "Photo is the old
    model".
14. The designer opens; marketing can draw and Mark design ready as before.
15. While marketing holds it, the gear has no Edit and no Escalate to marketing; the header reads
    "Marketing is working on this request. Wait for their proof."
16. The list has no Price Tag Request row.
17. Design Ready, as step 2.
18. The request shows Printing: Office prints.
19. Approved; the CRM now offers Mark ready for collection.
20. Status Ready for collection.
21. The gear shows the PDF item and Duplicate only: no Edit and no Escalate (the tags are printed).
22. Above the tag, "No tag template yet for <code of the unlinked product>. Escalate it to marketing.";
    the linked product's tag is drawn; Approve is disabled and hovering it shows the same sentence. The
    escalation then behaves as in steps 12-13.
23. The form refuses Submit until a template is picked. The generated tag is drawn on the template you
    picked, not the first one; Approve works as in step 5.
24. The Cabana line carries no promotion selling price (#2104). The tag shows its selling price equal to its
    list price, with no promo price; Approve works as in step 5.
