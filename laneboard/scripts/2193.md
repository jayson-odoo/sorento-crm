# Hand test: PR #2193 SPEC-SHEET-EDITOR-UX (spec sheet editor full screen, folding panels, Apply to, Templates)

The spec sheet page editor gets a full screen toggle and folding Pages / Inspector panels (the
price tag designer's own controls), "Apply to" becomes "This page | Every page", and the gear
gets Templates to switch the sheet's templates. A switch redraws every unedited page without a
reload and saves NO page edit; switching back restores the original pages.

crew test copy

## Setup
- Dev database `sorento_prod_1104`. Its only spec sheet is SS1 on quotation PRJQ-2026-0007-D:
  a cover and one item page. The item page prints from the sheet's **Single item** template
  slot. The published Spec sheet page templates there are "Project Page Default Template" and
  "Project Quotation Page Template".
- Signed in as a user who can edit project quotations. Window about 1440 px wide.
- Before step 12, make sure neither page of SS1 shows the amber edited dot in the Pages rail (if
  one does, hover that page and click "Reset to template", wait 10 s). Note which template the
  item page header names after the product code (call it A; the other one is B).

## Steps
1. From `/`, open the sidebar Project Sales > Projects, open the project of PRJQ-2026-0007-D, then the quotation > Spec sheets > SS1.
2. Click the full screen icon in the editor toolbar (just right of "Fit width").
3. Press Esc.
4. Click the left arrow next to "Pages" above the page thumbnails.
5. Click the right arrow button left in its place.
6. Click a text layer on the item page (for example the product code).
7. Look at the top of the Inspector on the right.
8. Click the right arrow next to "Inspector".
9. Click the left arrow button left in its place.
10. Click "Every page" in "Apply to", then click "Every page" again.
11. Click "This page", then click an empty grey area beside the pages to deselect.
12. Click the gear (Spec sheet actions) in the header, then "Templates".
13. In "Single item template" pick B, click Save.
14. Wait 5 seconds without reloading. Look at the Pages rail and the toolbar's save state.
15. Reload the page.
16. Repeat steps 12 to 14, picking A back.
17. Reload the page.
18. Resize the window to 375 px wide (phone) and click the full screen icon, then the Exit full screen icon.

## Expected
- Step 2: the sidebar, top bar and page header disappear; toolbar, Pages rail, page stack and Inspector fill the window; "Press Esc to exit full screen" shows bottom right.
- Step 3: back to the normal page, nothing reloaded.
- Step 4: the rail is gone, one arrow button remains, the pages widen. Step 5: the rail is back.
- Step 7: "Apply to" with two joined options "This page" | "Every page", "This page" picked; no "(each becomes an edited page)" text.
- Step 8: the Inspector is gone, the pages widen. Step 9: it is back with the same layer selected.
- Step 10: "Every page" stays picked after the second click. Step 11: "This page" picked.
- Step 12: "Pick templates" dialog, Cover / Page / Single item templates prefilled with the sheet's current ones (Single item = A).
- Step 14: the item page draws B's layout and its header names B; the cover is unchanged. NO amber edited dot on either page in the rail, and the toolbar save state stays empty (never "Saving..." / "Saved"). No pages/*/edit request in the network log.
- Step 15: the same as step 14 after the reload (B, no amber dots).
- Step 16: the item page draws A's layout again and its header names A; no amber dots, no save.
- Step 17: A after the reload, no amber dots.
- Step 18: no blank rows under the toolbar buttons; no Pages / Inspector arrows; nothing clipped. On a touch phone there is no "Press Esc" hint (a desktop browser resized to 375 px still shows it).
