# PR 2345 hand test: project stage buttons open their own form directly

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Sign in as an admin (the owner's own login).
- Pick a non-admin salesperson whose user is linked to a contact (called S below; note the
  CONTACT's name, called J). Admins cannot be impersonated.
- Make two fresh projects, both owned by S, because existing projects are used up and their codes
  differ between copies: open Project Sales > Projects in the sidebar, click Start > Register a
  project, register one with an address and an estimated sales value, and Approve it if it waits
  for approval. That is P. Register a second the same way with a delivery address on it: that is Q.
  P and Q must be two projects: P ends at PO Received (step 12), which has no Back in the gear.
- Both start at Registered with no sponsorship forms. P and Q have no customer, so Customer name
  and PIC start empty; Project title, Registered project, Delivery address and Total project value
  come from the project.
- No migration and no SQL is needed.

## Steps
1. From the home page, open Project Sales > Projects in the sidebar and open P.
2. Click the header button until it reads "Sample submitted" (Spec In opens New proposal; click
   Back in the browser to return to P).
3. Click "Sample submitted".
4. Click Cancel at the bottom of the New Sample Request form.
5. Click "Final negotiation".
6. Click "Sponsorship".
7. Look at the form, then click Cancel at the bottom of the form.
8. Open the gear and click "Back to final negotiation", then click "Sponsorship" again.
9. Pick a Sponsor subject, click "Add item", type an item code, quantity 2, unit price 10. Open
   "Expected delivery date" and pick a day.
10. Click "Submit sponsorship form", then "Submit sponsorship form" in the confirm box.
11. On P's Sponsorship Form tab, click the new form's row, look at it and at its Audit Trail, then
    click "Back to project".
12. Click "PO received".
13. Click Cancel in the "Upload a customer PO" box.
14. Click "Upload a PO document".
15. Close the box.
16. Open Users & Access > People > Administrative Users in the sidebar, open S's row menu and
    click "Impersonate user". Then open Projects under My work and open Q.
17. Click the header button until it reads "Sample submitted" (Spec In opens New proposal; click
    Back in the browser to return to Q), then click it.
18. Look at the form (and its breadcrumb), then click Cancel at the bottom of the form.
19. Click "Final negotiation", then "Sponsorship". Look at the form, then click Cancel at the
    bottom of the form.
20. Open the gear and click "Back to final negotiation", then click "Sponsorship" again. Fill an
    item as in step 9 and submit as in step 10.
21. On Q's Sponsorship Form tab, click the new form's row, look at it, then click "Back to project".
22. Stop impersonating S.

## Expected
- Step 3: the New Sample Request form opens with P already on it; Request Delivery Date is the
  DD/MM/YYYY picker (a calendar opens from its icon; not the browser's own date box). The
  breadcrumb reads Dashboards > Projects > Project > New Sample Request.
- Step 4: you are back on P, Samples Request tab.
- Step 5: the button changes to "Sponsorship"; no page opens.
- Step 6: "New Sponsorship Form" opens (breadcrumb Dashboards > Projects > Project > New
  Sponsorship Form) and it is the salesperson's form: Customer name search box, PIC, Delivery
  address, Project title, Registered project, Total project value, Sponsor subject, Sales type,
  Expected delivery date, Requested by, Items, Attachments, then Cancel / Save as draft / Submit
  sponsorship form. Not the old form with "Registered project (optional)" and "Purpose
  (showroom/mock up/others)". No "AI Extract" button.
- Step 7: before Cancel, Project title = P's title, Registered project = P's code, Delivery address
  = P's address, Total project value = P's estimated value, Sales type = Project, Requested by = J
  (the contact's name, not the user's); Customer name and PIC empty. After Cancel: back on P,
  Sponsorship Form tab.
- Step 9: Expected delivery date shows the day as DD/MM/YYYY.
- Step 10: you land on P, Sponsorship Form tab; the new form is listed with a number, status
  "Submitted", Requested = today's date, Value RM 20.00, and the total above the table reads
  "RM 20.00 across 1 form".
- Step 11: the saved form shows Requested by = J (the same name as in step 7) and Registered
  project = P's code; its Audit Trail shows one Create for the form and its line, and no "Line ...
  removed"; "Back to project" returns to P's Sponsorship Form tab.
- Step 12: P's PO tab shows with the "Upload a customer PO" box already open (drop zone + PO number).
- Step 13: the box closes and you stay on P's PO tab.
- Step 14: the same "Upload a customer PO" box opens.
- Step 18: S's sample request form opens with Q on it; Request Delivery Date is the DD/MM/YYYY
  picker; the breadcrumb's "Project" leads to Q (never to a page saying sample requests are not
  available). After Cancel: back on Q, Samples Request tab. (Filing it needs Sample Request switched
  on for S's contact; see the PR's evidence note.)
- Step 19: the same form as step 6, Registered project = Q's code, Sales type = Project, no AI
  Extract, Requested by = J, Delivery address = Q's address (filled from the project). After Cancel: back on Q, Sponsorship Form tab.
- Step 20: you land on Q, Sponsorship Form tab, and the new form is listed straight away (no
  reload) as "Submitted", Requested = today's date, Value RM 20.00, total "RM 20.00 across 1 form".
- Step 21: the saved form shows Requested by = J and Registered project = Q's code; "Back to
  project" returns to Q's Sponsorship Form tab.
