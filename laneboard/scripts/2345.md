# PR 2345 hand test: project stage buttons open their own form directly

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Sign in as an admin (the owner's own login).
- Pick one approved project whose header button reads "Final negotiation" or earlier, owned by a
  salesperson whose user is linked to a contact (Settings > Users, the contact on their profile).
  Call it P and its salesperson S.
- No migration and no SQL is needed.

## Steps
1. From the home page, open Project Sales > Projects in the sidebar and open P.
2. Click the header button until it reads "Sample submitted" (Spec In opens New proposal; click
   Back in the browser to return to P).
3. Click "Sample submitted".
4. Click Cancel at the bottom of the New Sample Request form.
5. Click "Final negotiation".
6. Click "Sponsorship".
7. Look at the form, then click Cancel at the top right.
8. Click "Sponsorship Form" tab, then go back to Overview, then open the gear and click "Back to
   final negotiation", then click "Sponsorship" again.
9. Pick a Sponsor subject, click "Add item", type an item code, quantity 2, unit price 10.
10. Click "Submit sponsorship form", then "Submit sponsorship form" in the confirm box.
11. Click "PO received".
12. Click Cancel in the "Upload a customer PO" box.
13. Click "Upload a PO document".

## Expected
- Step 3: the New Sample Request form opens with P already on it.
- Step 4: you are back on P, Samples Request tab.
- Step 5: the button changes to "Sponsorship"; no page opens.
- Step 6: "New Sponsorship Form" opens and it is the salesperson's form (the same one S sees: Customer
  name search box, PIC, Delivery address, Project title, Registered project, Total project value,
  Sponsor subject, Sales type, Expected delivery date, Requested by, Items, Attachments, then Cancel /
  Save as draft / Submit sponsorship form). Not the old one with "Registered project (optional)" and
  "Purpose (showroom/mock up/others)". No "AI Extract" button.
- Step 7: before Cancel, Project title = P's title, Registered project = P's code, Delivery address =
  P's address, Total project value = P's estimated value, Customer name and PIC = P's customer and its
  attn + phone (empty when P has no customer), Requested by = S. After Cancel: back on P, Sponsorship
  Form tab.
- Step 10: you land on P, Sponsorship Form tab, and the new form is listed with a number and status
  "Submitted"; opening it shows Requested by = S.
- Step 11: P's PO tab shows with the "Upload a customer PO" box already open (drop zone + PO number).
- Step 12: the box closes and you stay on P's PO tab.
- Step 13: the same "Upload a customer PO" box opens.
