# PR 2345 hand test: project stage buttons open their own form directly

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Sign in as an admin (the owner's own login).
- P = project PPF2611012 (its header button reads "Final negotiation" or earlier). Its owner is a
  salesperson whose user is linked to a contact; note that CONTACT's name, called S below.
- Q = a project owned by the non-admin salesperson Justin, whose header button reads "Final
  negotiation" or earlier (admins cannot be viewed as, so the salesperson part uses Justin). Note
  Justin's contact name, called J below.
- P has no customer, so Customer name, PIC and Delivery address start empty; Project title,
  Registered project and Total project value come from the project.
- No migration and no SQL is needed.

## Steps
1. From the home page, open Project Sales > Projects in the sidebar and open P.
2. Click the header button until it reads "Sample submitted" (Spec In opens New proposal; click
   Back in the browser to return to P).
3. Click "Sample submitted".
4. Click Cancel at the bottom of the New Sample Request form.
5. Click "Final negotiation".
6. Click "Sponsorship".
7. Look at the form, then click Cancel at the bottom of the form.
8. Open the gear and click "Back to final negotiation", then click "Sponsorship" again.
9. Pick a Sponsor subject, click "Add item", type an item code, quantity 2, unit price 10. Open
   "Expected delivery date" and pick a day.
10. Click "Submit sponsorship form", then "Submit sponsorship form" in the confirm box.
11. On P's Sponsorship Form tab, click the new form's row, then click "Back to project".
12. Click "PO received".
13. Click Cancel in the "Upload a customer PO" box.
14. Click "Upload a PO document".
15. Close the box.
16. Open the avatar menu and use "View as" on Justin, then open Project Sales > Projects in the
    sidebar and open Q.
17. Click the header button until it reads "Sample submitted", then click it.
18. Look at the form, then click Cancel at the bottom of the form.
19. Click "Final negotiation", then "Sponsorship". Look at the form, then click Cancel at the
    bottom of the form.
20. Open the gear and click "Back to final negotiation", then click "Sponsorship" again. Fill an
    item as in step 9 and submit as in step 10.
21. On Q's Sponsorship Form tab, click the new form's row, look at it, then click "Back to project".
22. Stop viewing as Justin.

## Expected
- Step 3: the New Sample Request form opens with P already on it; Request Delivery Date is the
  DD/MM/YYYY picker (a calendar opens from its icon; not the browser's own date box).
- Step 4: you are back on P, Samples Request tab.
- Step 5: the button changes to "Sponsorship"; no page opens.
- Step 6: "New Sponsorship Form" opens and it is the salesperson's form: Customer name search box,
  PIC, Delivery address, Project title, Registered project, Total project value, Sponsor subject,
  Sales type, Expected delivery date, Requested by, Items, Attachments, then Cancel / Save as draft /
  Submit sponsorship form. Not the old form with "Registered project (optional)" and "Purpose
  (showroom/mock up/others)". No "AI Extract" button.
- Step 7: before Cancel, Project title = P's title, Registered project = P's code, Total project
  value = P's estimated value, Sales type = Project, Requested by = S (the contact's name, not the
  user's); Customer name, PIC and Delivery address empty. After Cancel: back on P, Sponsorship Form
  tab.
- Step 9: Expected delivery date shows the day as DD/MM/YYYY.
- Step 10: you land on P, Sponsorship Form tab; the new form is listed with a number, status
  "Submitted", Requested = today's date, Value RM 20.00, and the total above the table reads
  "RM 20.00 across 1 form".
- Step 11: the saved form shows Requested by = S (the same name as in step 7) and Registered
  project = P's code; "Back to project" returns to P's Sponsorship Form tab.
- Step 12: P's PO tab shows with the "Upload a customer PO" box already open (drop zone + PO number).
- Step 13: the box closes and you stay on P's PO tab.
- Step 14: the same "Upload a customer PO" box opens.
- Step 17: Justin's sample request form opens with Q on it; Request Delivery Date is the DD/MM/YYYY
  picker.
- Step 18: back on Q, Samples Request tab (not the Sample Requests list, never "not available for
  your account").
- Step 19: the same form as step 6 with the same start as step 7 (Registered project shown, Sales
  type = Project, no AI Extract), Requested by = J. After Cancel: back on Q, Sponsorship Form tab.
- Step 20: you land on Q, Sponsorship Form tab; the new form listed as "Submitted", Requested =
  today's date, Value RM 20.00.
- Step 21: the saved form shows Requested by = J and Registered project = Q's code; "Back to
  project" returns to Q's Sponsorship Form tab.
