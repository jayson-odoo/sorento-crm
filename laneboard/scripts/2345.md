# PR 2345 hand test: project stage buttons open their own form directly

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Sign in as an admin (the owner's own login).
- Use project PPF2611003 (call it P) for steps 1 to 15 and PPF2611009 (call it Q) for steps 16 to 22;
  PPF2611012 is a spare. Each reads "Final negotiation" or earlier on its header button. Their
  owner is a salesperson S whose user is linked to a contact; note S's contact name.
- These projects have no customer, so Customer name and PIC start empty and the Delivery address
  is empty too; Project title, Registered project and Total project value come from the project.
- No migration and no SQL is needed.

## Steps
1. From the home page, open Project Sales > Projects in the sidebar and open P.
2. Click the header button until it reads "Sample submitted" (Spec In opens New proposal; click
   Back in the browser to return to P).
3. Click "Sample submitted".
4. Click Cancel at the bottom of the New Sample Request form.
5. Click "Final negotiation".
6. Click "Sponsorship".
7. Look at the form, then click Cancel at the bottom of the form.
8. Open the gear and click "Back to final negotiation", then click "Sponsorship" again.
9. Pick a Sponsor subject, click "Add item", type an item code, quantity 2, unit price 10. Open
   "Expected delivery date" and pick a day.
10. Click "Submit sponsorship form", then "Submit sponsorship form" in the confirm box.
11. On P's Sponsorship Form tab, click the new form's row, then click "Back to project".
12. Click "PO received".
13. Click Cancel in the "Upload a customer PO" box.
14. Click "Upload a PO document".
15. Close the box.
16. Open the avatar menu and use "View as" on S (or sign in as S), then open Project Sales >
    Projects in the sidebar and open Q.
17. Click the header button until it reads "Sponsorship" (as in steps 2 to 5), then click it.
18. Look at the form.
19. Click Cancel at the bottom of the form.
20. Click "Sponsorship" again, fill an item as in step 9 and submit as in step 10.
21. On Q's Sponsorship Form tab, click the new form's row, then click "Back to project".
22. Stop viewing as S.

## Expected
- Step 3: the New Sample Request form opens with P already on it.
- Step 4: you are back on P, Samples Request tab.
- Step 5: the button changes to "Sponsorship"; no page opens.
- Step 6: "New Sponsorship Form" opens and it is the salesperson's form: Customer name search box,
  PIC, Delivery address, Project title, Registered project, Total project value, Sponsor subject,
  Sales type, Expected delivery date, Requested by, Items, Attachments, then Cancel / Save as draft /
  Submit sponsorship form. Not the old form with "Registered project (optional)" and "Purpose
  (showroom/mock up/others)". No "AI Extract" button.
- Step 7: before Cancel, Project title = P's title, Registered project = P's code, Total project
  value = P's estimated value, Sales type = Project, Requested by = S's contact name; Customer name,
  PIC and Delivery address empty. After Cancel: back on P, Sponsorship Form tab.
- Step 9: Expected delivery date shows the day as DD/MM/YYYY (a calendar opens from its icon; not
  the browser's own date box).
- Step 10: you land on P, Sponsorship Form tab; the new form is listed with a number, status
  "Submitted", Value RM 20.00, and the total above the table reads "RM 20.00 across 1 form" (the
  sponsored amount, not P's value).
- Step 11: the form opens with Requested by = S's contact name; "Back to project" returns to P's
  Sponsorship Form tab.
- Step 12: P's PO tab shows with the "Upload a customer PO" box already open (drop zone + PO number).
- Step 13: the box closes and you stay on P's PO tab.
- Step 14: the same "Upload a customer PO" box opens.
- Step 18: the same form as step 6 with the same start as step 7 (Registered project shown, Sales
  type = Project, no AI Extract), Requested by = S.
- Step 19: back on Q, Sponsorship Form tab (not the Sponsorship Forms list).
- Step 20: you land on Q, Sponsorship Form tab, the new form listed as "Submitted", Value RM 20.00.
- Step 21: "Back to project" returns to Q's Sponsorship Form tab.
