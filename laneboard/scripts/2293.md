## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Signed in as a Purchasing or admin user on the crew test copy (prod-copy data).
- No migration.

## Steps
1. Start a low stock report for location BRW only (Reorder Planning, Start Plan, locations: BRW; or ask the chatbot for the BRW low stock report). Wait for the plan to complete.
2. Open the plan's Low stock report (Reorder Planning, Actions, Low stock report), choose split None, sheet All.
3. Search SRTWT167-QT.
4. Read BRW on hand, BRW incoming qty, Last in qty, Last in date on that row.
5. Click Download, open the workbook from My downloads, sheet All, find SRTWT167-QT.
6. In the same workbook find SRTWT167-DIY and check the row below it.
7. Back in Reorder Planning, start a plan for two locations (for example BRW and BRW-IB) and open its low stock report; pick any item whose last SPO line was for a third location.

- Step 4: BRW incoming qty `121 KMTU9395907 - 121`; Last in qty `KMTU9395907 - 121` (not `- 99`); Last in date `25/10/2026`; BRW on hand counts BRW stock only.
- Step 5: the same values in the Excel row.
- Step 6: SRTWT167-DIY is ONE row; its BRW PO qty cell holds all of `800`, `202609-S0079 - 300`, `202610-S0158 - 500` on separate lines inside the cell; the next row is the next item, not a blank row.
- Step 7: Last in, BRW on hand, BRW PO qty and BRW incoming qty only reflect the two chosen locations; an item whose only SPO lines are at other locations shows Last in blank.
