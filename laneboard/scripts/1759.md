## Setup

1. Sign in as Teh Jayson (the E2E admin account).
2. No migration and no data step. Read-only: open folders only, never upload, rename, move or delete real files.
3. Pick a real folder path 3 deep with a second subfolder in the first one, e.g. Marketing (A) > Sorento Product Photo (B) > Kitchen Sink (C), and another subfolder of Marketing such as Price Tag (D). Pick one more top-level folder, e.g. Purchasing (E).

## Steps

1. From `/`, open Resources > Files from the sidebar. Note the address bar.
2. In the LEFT folder tree, click A (at phone width: tap the folder button above the list first, then A in the drawer).
3. In the right pane click B, then C. Note the address bar and the breadcrumb.
4. Press the browser Back button once.
5. Press Back again.
6. Press Back again.
7. Press the browser Forward button three times.
8. Reload the page (Cmd+R). Look at the left folder tree.
9. Press Back twice.
10. In the right pane click D.
11. Press Back.
12. Press Forward.
13. Copy the address bar, open a new tab, paste it, press Enter. Close that tab.
14. In the breadcrumb, click A.
15. Press Back.
16. In the left folder tree, click E, then press Back.
17. Type something in the search box, then press Back.
18. Edit the address bar: replace the `directoryId` value with `not-a-real-folder`, press Enter.
19. Click the Home crumb ("All files").
20. Narrow the window to phone width (about 375px) and repeat steps 2 to 12.
