# Hand test #1382 (LOWSTOCK-SHOW-ALL)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Sign in as a purchasing user or an admin (someone who can open Supply Chain > Reorder planning).
2. Have on the dev DB a completed reorder plan that planned products on the manual reorder-level basis, some of them covered with net above their level. Run dd28049a (18 Sep) is one: 1,393 planned products, 413 of which were hidden before this change. If it is gone, run a fresh plan over the whole product range from Reorder planning > Run plan and use that; the exact numbers below then differ but the relationships hold.
3. Note the run's planned product count from the plans list (Products column) before opening it.

## Steps

1. From the sidebar open Supply Chain > Reorder planning and open the plan from Setup step 2.
2. On the Lines tab, with no search, no status filter and no Filters condition, read the row count in the grid footer.
3. Open Filters and add a condition Rec type is Covered by stock. Read the row count. Remove the condition.
4. Read the Decisions tile total ("N of Total made") and the run's Lines / Products figures on the plans list.
5. Open Actions > Order sheet Excel, wait for My Downloads to show Ready, open the file and count its data rows.
6. Open Actions > Low stock report Excel. The Low stock report page opens. Set Split to None and clear any supplier or category filter, then read the "N rows, 2 sheets" line above the preview.
7. Press Download, wait for My Downloads to show Ready, and open the file.
8. Count the data rows on the All sheet and on the Low stock sheet. On the Low stock sheet check every row has BRW on hand < Reorder level. Find a product on All with BRW on hand below Reorder level and confirm it is also on Low stock. Find a product on All with BRW on hand equal to Reorder level and confirm it is not on Low stock.
9. Back on the page, switch Split to Supplier and category and read the "N rows" line again.
10. Ask the WhatsApp bot "low stock report" from a contact holding the Low stock report over chat reveal, and read the counts in its reply.

## Expected

1. The plan opens as before.
2. The footer count equals the run's planned product count from Setup step 3. For dd28049a: 1,393, not 980. Products that used to be missing (covered, net above level) are in the list with Suggestion "Covered by stock".
3. The count goes DOWN to the covered rows only while the condition is on, and back to the full count when it is removed. Nothing extra appears at any point: the condition narrows, it no longer reveals.
4. The Decisions tile total and the plans list Lines / Products figures equal the same planned count (1,393 for dd28049a), not the old narrowed figure (980).
5. The order sheet has one data row per planned product (1,393 for dd28049a).
6. The line reads "1,393 rows, 2 sheets" for dd28049a (was 980).
7. The file lands in My Downloads and opens with two sheets, Low stock first, then All.
8. All sheet rows = 1,393; Low stock rows = 798 for dd28049a (was 604). Every Low stock row has BRW on hand strictly below Reorder level. A product at exactly its level is on All only. No "covered by PO/SPO" column exists; the 16 columns are unchanged.
9. Still "1,393 rows" for dd28049a; only the sheet count changes.
10. The bot's reply names All = every product of its fresh plan and Low = the ones with on hand below level; the workbook it sends has the same two sheets and the same membership rule as step 8.
