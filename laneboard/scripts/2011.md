# Hand test: PR #2011 AGENTIC-P3-PR-DECISION (the agent approves / rejects purchase requests)

The Agent tab can now approve or reject a purchase request, the way the Approve / Reject buttons on the request
do. It proposes first; nothing happens until you confirm. Rejecting needs a reason. Confirming goes through the
same approval as the screen, so the requester is notified and, when an approval grace window is configured,
the decision waits out the window and can be cancelled before it commits.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
1. The copy runs the PR head with a Gemini key in the AI assistant settings. No migration in this PR.
2. Sign in as the owner (admin).
3. Crew names Contact S (Agent mode on, linked to a user who holds Approve on purchase requests), and two
   purchase requests waiting for approval that S may see: PR-1 and PR-2 (crew gives their numbers).
4. Crew sets an approval grace window of 60 seconds for purchase requests (System Settings, form action grace),
   or says it is off.

## Steps
1. CRM sidebar: System > Messaging > Chatbot Console. Click the `Agent` tab and pick Contact S.
2. Send: `which purchase requests are waiting for my approval?`
3. Send: `approve <PR-1 number>`
4. Open Procurement > Purchase Requests > PR-1 in another tab.
5. Back in the console, click `Confirm change`.
6. Refresh PR-1 straight away, then again after the grace window.
7. Send: `reject <PR-2 number>`
8. Answer the agent's question with `price too high, ask for a new quote`, then click `Confirm change`.
9. Within the grace window, send `cancel that` and click `Confirm change`.
10. Refresh PR-2.

## Expected
1. The Agent tab with Contact S's conversation.
2. A list with PR-1 and PR-2 by number and customer; no ids.
3. A proposal line `<PR-1 number> (<customer>): approve (notifies the requester)` with Confirm / Cancel.
4. PR-1 is still waiting for approval.
5. With a grace window: a block `Approval: Pending -> Approved (commits at <HH:MM>)`; without one: `Done` and
   `Approval: Pending -> Approved`.
6. With a grace window PR-1 shows the pending countdown first, then Approved after the window; without one it
   is Approved at once. The requester gets the usual approval notification once.
7. The agent asks for a reason; it does not propose a rejection without one.
8. A proposal line `<PR-2 number> (<customer>): reject: "price too high, ask for a new quote" (notifies the
   requester)`; after Confirm, the parked block with the commit time (or Done without a grace window).
9. A proposal to cancel the waiting decision on PR-2; after Confirm, a Done block for the cancel.
10. PR-2 is still waiting for approval; no rejection notification was sent.
