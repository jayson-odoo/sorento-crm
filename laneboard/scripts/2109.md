# Hand test: PR #2109 STOCK-POS-DYNAMIC-TOTAL (Stock position totals follow the visible columns)

Owner 2 Nov: the total of incoming and stock on hand must add up only the columns shown; hide a location such as
BMW-RSV and the total recalculates. On screen that total is **Total stock** (shown location columns + Incoming
BRW); **Total incoming** (Incoming BRW + Incoming (Project)) follows the same rule. Sort and Export to Excel read
the same visible set.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
Sign in as a user who can open Inventory > Stock position. Start with every column shown: if you hid columns
before, open the Columns menu and use Reset columns.

## Steps
1. Open Inventory > Stock position.
2. Pick any row that has stock in two or more location columns and Incoming BRW above 0. Write down its location figures, Incoming BRW, Incoming (Project), Total stock and Total incoming.
3. Hover over the Total stock header.
4. Open the Columns menu and untick one location column that has stock on that row (for example BMW-RSV).
5. Look at the same row's Total stock and Total incoming, and hover over the Total stock header again.
6. Click the Total stock header to sort by it, then click it again for descending.
7. In the Columns menu untick Incoming BRW.
8. Look at the same row's Total stock and Total incoming, and hover over the Total incoming header.
9. Click Export to Excel and open the workbook.
10. Reload the page.
11. In the Columns menu tick the two columns back on.

## Expected
1. The grid loads with one column per location, then Incoming BRW, Total stock, Incoming (Project), Total incoming and the rest.
2. Total stock = the sum of that row's location figures + Incoming BRW; Total incoming = Incoming BRW + Incoming (Project).
3. The tooltip reads "Sum of " followed by every location code, then "Incoming BRW".
4. The column disappears from the grid and the list goes back to page 1.
5. Total stock is lower by exactly that location's figure on that row (and on every other row); Total incoming is unchanged; the tooltip no longer names that location.
6. The list sorts by the reduced Total stock across all pages (the first row ascending has the smallest visible total).
7. Incoming BRW disappears.
8. Total stock is lower again by the Incoming BRW figure; Total incoming equals Incoming (Project) only; the Total incoming tooltip reads "Sum of Incoming (Project)".
9. The workbook has no column for the hidden location and no Incoming BRW column; each row's Total stock and Total incoming match the screen.
10. The two columns stay hidden after the reload and the totals are still the reduced ones.
11. Both columns come back and every Total stock and Total incoming is back to the figures written down in step 2.
