# Hand test: PR #2010 AGENTIC-P2-JEV-ROUTER (console Auto mode: Jev decides bot or agent)

The Chatbot Console gets a third tab, Auto. Each message you send there is routed by Jev: plain lookups go to
today's bot, analysis and changes go to the agent. A small badge under each reply says which side answered and,
when something other than Jev decided it, why (routing off, low confidence, daily cap, router failed, not
eligible). WhatsApp is unchanged: routing works in the console only, behind a switch that starts off.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
1. The copy runs the PR head with the migration applied (crew-migration comment), a Gemini key in the AI
   assistant settings, and the owner's Jev key entered by the owner on the AI assistant settings screen (crew
   never handles the key value).
2. Sign in as the owner (admin).
3. Crew names Contact S: AI replies on, Agent mode on, linked to a salesperson user.

## Steps
1. CRM sidebar: System > AI Assistant settings. Find the Jev API key field and paste your key; save.
2. Reload the page.
3. CRM sidebar: Users & Access > Settings > Chatbot Media. Find the Auto routing card.
4. Leave the switch off. CRM sidebar: System > Messaging > Chatbot Console, click the `Auto` tab, pick
   Contact S, send `stock of <a product code you know>`.
5. Back on the Auto routing card, switch it on, keep the confidence at 70%, save.
6. In the console Auto tab send `stock of <the same code>`.
7. Send `compare my sales this month with last month by customer and tell me who dropped the most`.
8. Send `mark my oldest open customer ask done, note: checked`, then click `Cancel`.
9. Open Contact Details for Contact S > Chatbot, set `Agent turns per day` to 1, save, and send another
   analysis question in the Auto tab.
10. Clear `Agent turns per day` again.

## Expected
1. Saved.
2. The key shows only as a mask (stars and the last 4 characters).
3. A switch for the console Auto mode (off), a confidence of 70% and a daily cap of 50.
4. The bot answers with the stock; the badge says `Bot` and `routing off`.
5. Saved.
6. The bot answers; the badge says `Bot` (no reason words: Jev chose the bot).
7. The agent answers (steps and figures as in the Agent tab); the badge says `Agent`.
8. The agent proposes the change with Confirm / Cancel; after Cancel nothing changed.
9. The bot answers instead; the badge says `Bot` and `daily cap`.
10. The field is empty again (the global 50 applies).
