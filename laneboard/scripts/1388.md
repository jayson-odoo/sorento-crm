# Hand test #1388 (SPO-RECEIVED-PIN)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Sign in as a user who can open Supply Chain > Stock Debt (`projects.stock_debt.view`, or an admin).
2. The case is SRTSS8710 with its order inquiry placement on SPO-2026/05-0001. Confirm on the dev DB that the SPO line is out of incoming supply: Procurement > Shipping Orders > SPO-2026/05-0001, the SRTSS8710 line shows Received equal to Qty (or the shipment shows an arrival date). If the line is still open on the dev copy, pick any product whose order inquiry placement sits on a fully received SPO line instead; the relationships below hold for it too.
3. Note the bin the SPO line was received into (its Warehouse column) and that bin's on hand for SRTSS8710 (Inventory > Stock, or the Supply tab in step 5).
4. Note the sales order line the placement belongs to (the OI row's S/O) and its month; call it SO-A. Note any other open sales order line of SRTSS8710 at the same bin due in the same or a later month; call it SO-B.

## Steps

1. From the sidebar open Supply Chain > Stock Debt.
2. Search `SRTSS8710`. Read the row's month cells.
3. Click SO-A's month cell to open the drawer. On the Demand tab find SO-A and read Status, Assigned and Covered by.
4. On the same Demand tab find SO-B (if it is in this month) and read Status, Assigned and Covered by.
5. Switch to the Supply tab for the current month cell (close the drawer, click the current month cell, open Supply). Read every row: Kind, Document, Bin, Qty, Free, Assigned to.
6. Still on the Supply tab of SO-A's month, look for a row naming SPO-2026/05-0001.
7. Open Supply Chain > Fulfilment Planning, plan SO-A's sales order, and read SO-A's line.

## Expected

1. The Stock Debt list renders as before.
2. The row is present. Its month balances add up to on hand plus open SPO and PO outstanding less open demand; the received SPO no longer inflates a month.
3. SO-A reads Status `Pinned`, Assigned = min(placement qty, what landed, what the bin still holds), Covered by `On hand <bin>` (the bin from Setup step 3). It does NOT read `Covered by SPO-2026/05-0001`.
4. SO-B reads whatever the bin's REMAINING free stock allows: `Covered` only if on hand less SO-A's pin still covers it, otherwise `Short` with the shortfall in Short. Before this change SO-B read `Covered` off the same units SO-A was pinned to.
5. The current month's Supply tab has ONE on-hand row for that bin: Qty = the bin's on hand, Assigned to names SO-A for its pinned quantity, Free = on hand less every assignment. There is no `SPO` row for SPO-2026/05-0001 in any month.
6. No row. A received SPO is not supply, and no stand-in row dated today appears for it.
7. The board reads the same: SO-A's line is covered from the bin's stock (its own goods), not from SPO-2026/05-0001, and no other line of SRTSS8710 at that bin is offered the units SO-A holds.
