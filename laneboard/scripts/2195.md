# Hand test: PR #2195 PROJECTS-BOARD-VIEW-PREF (no hover underline on board cards; Projects remembers your view)

Board cards on Projects no longer underline the code and title on hover; the card tints like a
grid row does. Projects now remembers Board or Grid per user, on the server, so it follows you
to any browser or device. A user who never picked still opens on Board.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Two users who can open Projects (projects.projects.view): you, and a second test user.
- Window about 1280 px wide. At least one project on the board.

## Steps
1. From `/`, open the sidebar Dashboards > Project Sales > Projects.
2. In Board view, hover a card's code (e.g. PPF2611003) and then its title.
3. Hover a long title and wait for the tooltip.
4. Click the card's title.
5. Go back to Projects; click the Grid view icon in the header.
6. Reload the page.
7. Open another sidebar page, then open Projects again from the sidebar.
8. Open the copy in a different browser (or a private window), sign in as yourself, open Projects.
9. Sign out; sign in as the second user; open Projects.
10. As the second user, click Board view; sign out, sign back in as yourself, open Projects.
11. Narrow the window to 375 px and look at the header toggle and a board card (switch to Board to check, then back to Grid).

## Expected
- Step 2: no underline on code or title; the whole card gets a light grey tint, as a grid row does on hover.
- Step 3: the full title shows in the tooltip.
- Step 4: the project's detail page opens.
- Step 5: the Grid icon is highlighted and the list shows.
- Steps 6, 7 and 8: Projects opens straight in Grid, no Board flash first.
- Step 9: the second user (never picked) opens on Board.
- Step 10: you still open on Grid.
- Step 11: toggle and Start fit the header; the card is not clipped.
