# Hand test: PR #2228 PRODUCT-FIELD-CHANGE-NOTIFY (list price change notification)

Staff who tick "Email on list price changes" (or the WhatsApp one) on their user get one digest per company when
product list prices change: each product's old and new price, the % change, who changed it, today's date and a link
to just those products. The discontinued notification works exactly as before.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
1. Sign in as Jayson Foundryx (its first role is Admin; Teh Jayson is Salesperson-first and cannot open Scheduled Tasks).
2. Pick one active product you can edit and note its code, name and current list price (e.g. RM 100.00).
3. Email will not arrive on the dev copy (SMTP is off). WhatsApp uses the same switch and the same rules as "WhatsApp on products discontinued": it needs a linked WhatsApp contact on your user and the `product_list_price_changed` template mapped in WhatsApp templates (not mapped yet, so out of the 24h window it is skipped, exactly like an unmapped Product Discontinued). Check the bell.

## Steps
1. From the sidebar open Users & Access > People > Administrative Users and open your own user (Jayson Foundryx).
2. Click Edit user.
3. Tick "Email on list price changes" and "WhatsApp on list price changes" (they sit right under the two "products discontinued" boxes).
4. Check the "Product notification scope" box shows one all companies / all brands row (it fills in by itself the first time the box is ticked); save.
5. From the sidebar open Products > Products > All Products and open the product you picked.
6. Edit it, open the Pricing tab, change the list price from RM 100.00 to RM 110.00, and click Update Product.
7. From the sidebar open System > Operations > Scheduled Tasks, click the "Product List Price Change Notification" row, and click Run now on its page.
8. Open the notification bell.
9. Read the new notification, then click its "View the list" link.
10. Back in System > Operations > Scheduled Tasks, open "Product List Price Change Notification" again and click Run now.
11. Open "Product Discontinued Notification" and click Run now.

## Expected
- Step 4: after saving, your roles and companies are still the same set. Note: saving Edit user re-creates them (pre-existing behaviour on main, not this PR), so which role is shown first can change.
- Step 8: one new notification titled "List price changed for 1 product".
- Step 9: the body shows each part on its own line: a header "List price changed for 1 product (<today as dd/mm/yyyy>):", then the line "- <code> <name>: RM 100.00 -> RM 110.00 (+10.0%), by <your name>", then "View the list: <link>". The link opens the product list showing only that product, with the banner "Showing only the products from a recent "list price changed" notification." and a Clear filter button.
- Step 10: no new notification (that change was already reported).
- Step 11: no new notification unless a product was newly discontinued (discontinued behaves exactly as before).
- WhatsApp: no message is expected on the dev copy (template not mapped, contact window rules as for Product Discontinued). The same switch and code path send it once the owner maps the template.
