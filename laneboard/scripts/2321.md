crew test copy

## Setup
- The dev DB has the PR's `crew-migration:` SQL applied (crew test copy on a dev DB copy).
- Sign in as an admin (admin passes every permission check). Have a salesperson login ready for step 18.
- Dev data already left by crew: a CNY rate 1.6 effective 01/11/2026, an RMB row 1.6 effective 01/11/2026 (free text from before the currency pick-list; it matches no supplier cost, so it is never used), the transport rule CB-WB = Add 8, and a Bath Plus costing profile at 8% with 0 products.
- The dev DB's `deferred_action_seconds` is 1, so the removal countdown toast in step 14 lasts about 1 second.
- Figures below are the dev data's: CGB9032B (WB 245) primary supplier cost CNY 34.00, list price 100.00.

## Steps
1. From `/`, open the sidebar: Products > Products > Costing.
2. Click Settings, then the Exchange rates tab. Open the Currency field.
3. In Settings open "SST and method" and read it. Then open "Transport by category" and go to page 2: find CB-WB (this tab has no search) and read its rule. Close Settings.
4. Search "CGB9032B" and read the row.
5. Click the row's expand control.
6. Clear the search. Open Filters, turn "Has cost" on, then off.
7. In the party picker type "304-R017" and pick RELATED - BATHPLUS SDN BHD.
8. Read the header chips. Only if the header shows "Set up costing": click it, enter Party mark-up 8, leave Cost method as it is (it already shows "Company default"), Save.
9. Open the "Actions" menu (top right of the grid), choose "Add products", tick CGB9032B, add it.
10. Click Edit, type 10 into the CGB9032B Mark-up, Save.
11. Open the CGB9032B row menu, choose History.
12. Click Edit, clear the CGB9032B Mark-up, Save.
13. Open the "Actions" menu, choose "Export", open the downloaded xlsx.
14. CGB9032B row menu, "Remove from party". Do nothing while the toast counts down (about 1 s on this DB).
15. Reload the page.
16. Click "Clear party" (the X beside the picker). Open the browser console.
17. Settings, Exchange rates: pick Currency CNY, Effective from the 1st of next month, "Units per MYR 1", Rate 1.63, Add rate.
18. Sign out. Sign in as the salesperson. Look at the sidebar, then open `/master-data-management/costing` directly.
