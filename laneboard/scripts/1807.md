## Setup

1. Branch `claude/chat-index-viz-3ladv2` (stacks on #1809). Backend, worker and frontend from it. No migration beyond #1809's.
2. Crew sets Closed lists to Index on Settings > Chatbot and runs the console on the parser version with the CLOSED LISTS section (v77). No SQL for the owner.

## Steps

1. From `/`, sidebar: System Management > Chatbot Console.
2. Send a fresh message: `stock <a product code that has a -FRG variant> frg`
3. On the bot's reply, click "trace". The Chat History turn drawer opens; click "Index match".
4. Look at the `frg` row, then press "Show details", then look at "What the bot looked up".
5. Back in the console, send: `send the drawing`
6. Open its trace and "Index match".
7. In the console, send: `phtos for <a product code>`
8. Open its trace and "Index match".
9. Sidebar: System Management > AI Assistant > Prompts > `chatbot_semantic_parser`. In Version history pick v77, then click "Architecture".
10. Open another prompt key (e.g. `chatbot_clarifier`).
11. Repeat steps 3 and 9 at phone width (375px).
