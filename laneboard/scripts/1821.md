## Setup

1. Sign in as Teh Jayson (the E2E admin account).
2. No migration and no data step. The RQ worker must be running (the downloads are made by it, like every My Downloads file).
3. The copy holds OI-2609-0165. Nothing in this script sends an email: never press Send, Confirm or "Send to purchasing".

## Steps

1. From `/`, open Procurement > Supply Chain > Order Inquiries from the sidebar.
2. Search `OI-2609-0165` and open it.
3. Note the header card's S/O no, SO date, customer and project, and the Lines tab's rows.
4. Open the gear menu next to Confirm and press "Print / Download PDF".
5. Open My downloads (the download icon in the top bar) and open the new PDF.
6. Open the gear menu again and press "Export Excel"; open that file from My downloads.
7. Go back to the list and switch to Lines. If the saved "To confirm" chip shows above the grid, clear it with its x first (otherwise every download below is limited to the To confirm rows). Type `OI-2609-0165` in the search box and press Enter.
8. Open Actions and press "Print / Download PDF"; open the new PDF from My downloads.
9. Open Actions and press "Export Excel"; open that file from My downloads.
10. Resize the window to phone width (375px) and repeat steps 4 and 8 once.
