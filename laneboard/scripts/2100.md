# Hand test: PR #2100 PRICETAG-TEMPLATE-PER-PRODUCT (templates per product, auto-pick or choose, live preview)

Owner rulings 2 Nov: a product can carry one or many price tag templates; with one it is used
automatically, with many the salesperson chooses. Owner on the mock (21:30): the request form shows
the design on the fly as soon as the first item is added. Product sets keep today's family rule
(decided from the ruling's wording, card Q4).

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
Run the PR's `crew-migration` SQL on the copy first. Two users, because no single user can run it:
MARKETING (holds Tag Templates manage) for steps 1-7, and a SALESPERSON (view own price tag
requests, no `view`) for steps 8-14 (`my_requests.py:216-226`: only a view-own user can use the
form). Templates on the copy: "Bathroom Furniture" (98 x 140 mm, prints list price only) and
"Kitchen Sink SP" (140 x 98 mm, prints the selling price). Pick product A = a basin, B = a kitchen
sink with a live promotion. Product C = any product with no link; the copy has no mirror family,
and C has no template at all. Use the tailnet URL (plain
http) for at least steps 3 and 6.

## Steps
1. As marketing, open Master Data > Products, open product A, stay on Overview.
2. In the "Price tag templates" card, pick "Bathroom Furniture".
3. Open product B; in the same card pick "Bathroom Furniture" and "Kitchen Sink SP", then untick "Bathroom Furniture" and tick it again after the countdown.
4. Open product C and look at the card.
5. Open Dealer Kit > Room Designer > Tag Templates and click the "Assign to products" icon on "Kitchen Sink SP".
6. Without picking anything, look at the buttons; then pick product B's brand under Brands and click "Remove from N", then Cancel on the countdown toast.
7. Reopen the dialog and click Cancel.
8. As the salesperson, open Dealer Kit > Room Designer > Price Tag Requests and click Create. Pick a dealer, then add a line with product A.
9. Add a second line with product B.
10. Click Submit without touching the Tag template select.
11. On line 2 pick "Kitchen Sink SP" in the Tag template select.
12. Add a third line with product C.
13. Switch Price to Selling price, then back to List price.
14. Narrow the browser to phone width (375px) and scroll the form.

## Expected
1. The product opens on Overview; a "Price tag templates" card sits under Combos.
2. A toast says "Price tag templates saved"; the card holds "Bathroom Furniture".
3. The card holds both templates; the untick shows an "Unlinking ... in Ns" toast with Cancel and then commits (also on the tailnet URL); re-ticking saves again.
4. The card reads "No price tag templates" with the hint "Requests for this product use the template that matches its code." and no button.
5. A dialog titled "Assign Kitchen Sink SP to products" opens with Brands, Categories, Item classes and Model families.
6. Empty filter: the text "Pick at least one brand, category, item class or model family." and both "Remove from 0" and "Add to 0" are disabled. With the brand picked: "N products match, M already have this template." Remove shows a "Removing ... in Ns" toast (also on the tailnet URL); Cancel leaves every link in place.
7. The dialog closes; nothing changed.
8. Line 1 shows "Template: Bathroom Furniture" under the item. A "Preview" card appears under the lines within a few seconds, drawing one tag for product A.
9. Line 2 shows a "Tag template" select with both templates and their sizes. The preview now draws two tags.
10. Submit is refused: line 2 shows "Pick a tag template for this line." and the page scrolls so that message is on screen (1280 and 375). Nothing is submitted.
11. The message under line 2 goes away; the preview redraws line 2's tag at 140 x 98 mm.
12. Line 3 has no template field and is NOT drawn; under the preview: "No tag template yet for <C's code>."
13. The preview redraws after each switch. In Selling price mode line 2's tag (Kitchen Sink SP) shows the promotion price; Bathroom Furniture tags print list price in both modes; a Cabana line shows list price only (once #2104 is merged).
14. Lines, the Tag template select, the line message and the Preview are usable and not clipped at 375px.
