# Hand test: PR #2100 PRICETAG-TEMPLATE-PER-PRODUCT (templates per product, auto-pick or choose, live preview)

Owner rulings 2 Nov: a product can carry one or many price tag templates; with one it is used
automatically, with many the salesperson chooses. Owner on the mock (21:30): the request form shows
the design on the fly as soon as the first item is added. Product sets keep today's family rule
(decided from the ruling's wording, card Q4).

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
Run the PR's `crew-migration` SQL on the copy first. Sign in as a user with Tag Templates manage
(marketing or an admin). Pick three products on the copy and note their codes:
A = a basin (code with WB), B = a kitchen sink (code with KS), C = a mirror (code with MR).
Have at least two LIVE tag templates of different sizes, for example "Basin A5" and "Sink A4".

## Steps
1. Open Master Data > Products, open product A, stay on Overview.
2. In the "Price tag templates" card, pick "Basin A5".
3. Open product B; in the same card pick "Basin A5" and "Sink A4".
4. Open product C and look at the card.
5. Open Dealer Kit > Room Designer > Tag Templates and click the "Assign to products" icon on "Sink A4".
6. Without picking anything, look at the buttons; then pick product B's brand under Brands.
7. Click Cancel.
8. Open My Requests > Price Tag Request > New. Pick a dealer, then add a line with product A.
9. Add a second line with product B.
10. Click Submit without touching the Tag template select.
11. On line 2 pick "Sink A4" in the Tag template select.
12. Add a third line with product C.
13. Switch Price to Selling price, then back to List price.
14. Narrow the browser to phone width (375px) and scroll the form.

## Expected
1. The product opens on Overview; a "Price tag templates" card sits under Combos.
2. A toast says "Price tag templates saved"; the card holds "Basin A5".
3. The card holds both templates.
4. The card reads "No price tag templates" with the hint "Requests for this product use the template that matches its code." and no button.
5. A dialog titled "Assign Sink A4 to products" opens with Brands, Categories, Item classes and Model families.
6. Empty filter: the text "Pick at least one brand, category, item class or model family." and both "Remove from 0" and "Add to 0" are disabled. With the brand picked: "N products match, M already have this template." (M is at least 1, product B).
7. The dialog closes; nothing changed.
8. Line 1 shows "Template: Basin A5" under the item. A "Preview" card appears under the lines within about a second, drawing one tag for product A.
9. Line 2 shows a "Tag template" select with both templates and their sizes. The preview now draws two tags.
10. Submit is refused: line 2 shows "Pick a tag template for this line." and the page scrolls to it. Nothing is submitted.
11. The message under line 2 goes away; the preview redraws line 2's tag at the Sink A4 size.
12. Line 3 has no template field; the preview draws a third tag (the mirror family's template).
13. The preview redraws after each switch; in Selling price mode a line with a promotion shows the promotion price, a Cabana line shows list price only (once #2104 is merged).
14. Lines, the Tag template select and the Preview are usable and not clipped at 375px.
