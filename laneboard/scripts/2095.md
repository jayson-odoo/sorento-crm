# Hand test: PR #2095 AGENTIC-WA-S2 (warranty / complaint claim with chat photos on WhatsApp)

A dealer on WhatsApp files a warranty / complaint claim through the agent, with the photos sent in the chat, through
the portal's own complaint flow. Same propose -> yes -> number -> undo pattern as the W2 stock inquiry (#2072).

Prompts: agent system prompt `chatbot_agent` (published label; the claim paragraph is appended by code, no new
version), router `chatbot_router` v1 (published, floor 0.70). No migration.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Branch head of #2095. No migration. Worker running (photos are read and kept by the worker). Respond SENDS blocked;
  inbound webhooks and the respond.io media download must still reach the copy, or the photos cannot be kept (see
  the fallback under Steps).
- Test contact (crew picks and names it in the handtest comment; the W2 test contact works if it has all of these):
  - **AI replies allowed** and **Agent mode (auto)** on; no linked CRM user; one contact row for its respond.io id;
  - photo reading turned on for its number with allowance left this month;
  - the Complaint portal form left on (the default);
  - at least one customer account linked on **Contact Details**, and on that account one delivery order DO with
    product CODE on it. To pick them: open **Contact Details**, open a linked customer account, open any of its
    delivery orders; DO = that order's number exactly as shown, CODE = the item code of one of its lines. Crew writes
    DO and CODE into the comment. OTHER_DO = the number of a delivery order of a customer NOT linked to the contact
    (any other customer's order in **Delivery Orders**).
- Lookup sets `complaints_within_warranty` / `_complaint_type` / `_defects_discovered` / `_customer_type` as on dev.

## Steps
WhatsApp part (test contact):
1. Send 2 photos of the product (any product photo; one with CODE readable on it), no caption.
2. Send: `CODE from DO is leaking after 3 months, want to claim warranty`
3. Open the contact's **Chat History** and read the reply to step 2 and its route.
4. If the agent asked about the warranty, reply: `yes still under warranty` (otherwise skip this step).
5. Read the reply.
6. Send: `yes`
7. Open **Complaints** from the sidebar and open the newest complaint.
8. Look at its files.
9. Within 10 minutes, send: `undo that complaint please`, then `yes`.
10. Open **Complaints** again and search for DO.
11. Repeat steps 1, 2, 4 and 6 (a new claim; a second claim on the same DO goes through, the owner deferred duplicates), wait 11 minutes, then send `undo that complaint please`.
12. Send one more photo, then: `add this photo to my complaint`, then `yes`.
13. Open that complaint's files.
14. Send: `OTHER_DO CODE cracked, please claim`

Console part (a console turn is a test, nothing is written; console photo uploads are not chat photos):

15. Open **System > Messaging > Chatbot Console**, pick the test contact, press **New conversation**, switch **Agent** on.
16. Send: `CODE from DO leaking, under warranty, please file a claim`. Answer any choice the agent asks for with one of
    the options it lists. When the confirm block shows, press **Confirm change** (in the console a plain `yes` is
    routed to the bot, because a console test proposal never holds the route).
17. Open **Complaints** and count the test contact's complaints.

Fallback: if the photos of step 1 are not kept (the respond.io download is blocked on the copy), step 3's confirm
block reads `no photos`. Record steps 1, 8, 12 and 13 as **NOT RUN: media download blocked** and go on.
If the bot answers step 2 instead of the agent (route `chat`, no confirm block), send step 2 once more; if the bot
answers again record **NOT RUN: router (published v1)**.

## Expected
- 3: Route **agent**. Either the agent asks whether it is within warranty, or it shows the confirm block of step 5 with
  "within warranty: Yes": step 2 talks about claiming warranty, which counts as the dealer's answer (AC C-7: within
  warranty is taken from the dealer speaking about the warranty, never from a default). Either is a PASS; if it asks,
  step 4 answers it, otherwise skip step 4.
- 4/5: If complaint type, defects discovered or customer type has no default on this copy, the agent asks for each,
  listing only the portal form's options; answer with one of them. It never fills one you did not give.
- 5: The reply contains (each dropdown shows the option you answered in step 4/5, or the copy's default where one is
  set; the agent may repeat DO and CODE in its question above the block, shown whole, never "(not verified)"):
  > I will make these changes when you confirm:
  > 1. File a warranty / complaint claim: DO DO; CODE; "leaking after 3 months"; within warranty: Yes; complaint type: <your choice, or the set default>; defects discovered: <your choice, or the set default>; customer type: <your choice, or the set default>; customer <DO debtor name>; contact <contact name> <contact WhatsApp number>; date <today, YYYY-MM-DD>; salesperson <contact name>; photo 1 (<day> <time>): <what it shows, e.g. CODE>; photo 2 (<day> <time>): <what it shows or "could not be read">.
  > Reply yes to go ahead, or tell me what to change.
- 6: The reply contains "Complaint <number> is with the team. Reply undo within 10 minutes to withdraw it."
- 7: Status Submitted; DO DO; product CODE; customer the DO's debtor; contact person the test contact; within
  warranty Yes; defect description "leaking after 3 months" (or the dealer's words as sent).
- 8: The 2 photos from step 1 (also one that "could not be read"), uploaded by the test contact.
- 9: The agent proposes "Withdraw complaint <number> (sent N minutes ago). It is deleted, with its photos, and the team
  no longer sees it."; after `yes`, done. 10: the complaint from step 7 is gone.
- 11: The new claim carries only the 2 photos sent in step 11, never step 1's (photos from before the last claim
  action are not offered). After 11 minutes: "It is with the team now, so I can't withdraw it. I can add a note to it instead."
- 12: A confirm block "Add 1 photo to your complaint <number>: photo 1 (<day> <time>): ..."; after `yes`, done.
  13: the claim from step 11 now has 3 files.
- 14: "Delivery order OTHER_DO not found." No confirm block, no "I could not confirm that nothing exists" line, and
  the reply names no other customer.
- Every proposal / confirm reply: the confirm block, and "Complaint <number> is with the team ...", appear exactly once.
- 16: Any question lists only options that exist on the portal form (no invented ones), and no confirm block shows
  until each is answered. Then the confirm block as in 5 with "no photos"; after **Confirm change** the reply ends with
  "Test turn: nothing was sent."
- 17: Same count as before step 16.
- Throughout: the words "agent mode" never appear; nothing is sent to Respond beyond the blocked egress.
