# Hand test #1465 (MULTI-CODE-DYM)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Sign in as an admin who can open System Management > Chatbot Console.
2. Pick a customer contact: not staff (chatbot profile tier is not `office`), not a dealer (stock visibility is not "Availability only"), escalation allowed, and allowed to see stock quantities.
3. The codes come from the owner's 4 Oct transcript. SRT5764's did-you-mean is SRT57-CR, SRT5713 and SRT5732. Their scores are tied, so they print in the database's collation order (AVAIL-MODE S47). Below, `A`, `B` and `C` are the three codes in the order step 2 prints them, and the numbers are the ones printed next to them.
4. `zzq123` is any code-shaped word with no product near it.

## Steps

1. From the sidebar open System Management > Chatbot Console and pick the contact.
2. Send `srtwc286 , srtwc6022  srt5764  stock`. Read the reply.
3. Send `4`. Read the reply.
4. Send `srtwc286 , srtwc6022  srt5764  stock` again, then `3 and 5`. Read the second reply.
5. Send `srtwc286 , srtwc6022  srt5764  stock` again, then `4 and srtwc6022?`. Read the second reply.
6. Send `srtwc286 , srtwc6022  srt5764  stock` again, then `the srt57 one`. Read the second reply.
7. Send `srtwc286 zzq123 stock`. Read the reply.
8. Send `yes`. Read the reply.
9. Send `srt5764 zzq123 stock`. Read the reply.
10. Send `srt5764 srtwc6022x stock`. Read the reply.
11. Switch to a staff contact (tier `office`) and send `srtwc286 , srtwc6022  srt5764  stock`. Read the reply.

## Expected

1. The console opens with the contact selected.
2. Stock blocks `1. *Product Code:* SRTWC286 ...` and `2. *Product Code:* SRTWC6022 ...`. After them comes `Couldn't find "srt5764" (product). Did you mean:` with `3. A`, `4. B`, `5. C`. The last line is `Reply with a code to continue, or would you like me to escalate to warehouse team?`. The reply has no `I could not find srt5764.` line and no number printed twice.
3. The stock block for B (printed at 4) only.
4. The stock blocks for A and C (printed at 3 and 5), in one reply.
5. The stock blocks for B AND SRTWC6022. The question mark does not drop SRTWC6022.
6. The stock block for SRT57-CR only. No family list of SRT57 codes, and no second fetch.
7. The SRTWC286 stock block, then `I could not find zzq123.`, then `Would you like me to escalate to warehouse team?`.
8. The escalation to the warehouse team goes out.
9. `Couldn't find "srt5764" (product). Did you mean` with A, B and C (numbered with has-stock stamps, or one line `Did you mean A, B, or C?`). Then `I could not find zzq123.`, then `Reply with a code to continue, or would you like me to escalate to warehouse team?`.
10. One paragraph per code, each opening `Couldn't find "<code>" (product). Did you mean:`, with the numbers running on from the first list into the second (1-3 then 4 onward). Then one closing line. There is no `Couldn't find some items:` header and there are no indented sub-lists. If srtwc6022x has no neighbour, it reads `I could not find srtwc6022x.` above the closing line instead.
11. The same lists as step 2, closing with `Reply with a code to continue.` and no escalation offer.
