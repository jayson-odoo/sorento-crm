# Hand test: PR #1634 DO-BRANCH-NAME (DO branch name in the chatbot DO list and the DO screens)

The AutoCount branch name synced onto each delivery order now shows in the chatbot's DO list as
its own line `*Branch:* <name>`, switched per contact like every other DO field ("DO branch",
on by default), and in the CRM's DO list, detail and edit screens so the owner can cross-check.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
Crew prepares the copy. The owner runs no commands.

1. Branch names are filled on the copy's DB (done 13 Oct: 2,334 branch rows, 705/705 coded DOs
   named). Example customer: **Grand Waterhouse**, last month (September 2026): 27 DOs, branch
   **KULAI BRANCH**.
2. Sign in to the CRM as the owner.
3. Pick an internal test contact that has chatted with the bot before (the console borrows its
   last conversation). The chatbot is used ONLY in the CRM's own Chatbot Console, which is a dry
   run: nothing is sent through Respond.io or WhatsApp.

## Steps
1. CRM sidebar: Delivery Orders > Delivery Orders. Search `Grand Waterhouse`.
2. Look at the Branch column for its September 2026 DOs.
3. Click one of those rows to open the DO.
4. Open the `Delivery & tracking` tab.
5. Click `Edit`.
6. Go back to the list and open a DO of any customer that shows `-` in the Branch column.
7. CRM sidebar: System > Messaging > Chatbot Console. Pick the test contact; leave Prompt version
   on `Live (production label)`.
8. Send: `DO for Grand Waterhouse last month`
9. Note one Actual Delivery Date from that reply, then send: `only the ones delivered on <that date>`
10. Send: `check stock SRTWC8383-RL-UF`
11. CRM sidebar: Users & Access > People > Internal Users. Open the same test contact, open the
    `Access` tab, find the `Field reveals` card.
12. Switch off `DO branch`; in the `Hide do branch?` dialog click `Hide`.
13. Back in the Chatbot Console (same contact), click `Reset`, then send: `DO for Grand Waterhouse last month`
14. In the contact's `Field reveals` card switch `DO branch` back on.
15. In the Chatbot Console click `Reset`, then send: `DO for Grand Waterhouse last month`

## Expected
1. The list shows a `Branch` column right after `Debtor Name`.
2. Grand Waterhouse's September DOs show `KULAI BRANCH`.
3. The `Delivery order information` tab shows `Branch: KULAI BRANCH` under Debtor Code.
4. `Branch: KULAI BRANCH` shows here too.
5. `Branch` is a greyed input holding `KULAI BRANCH`, with `From AutoCount` under it; it cannot be edited.
6. Branch shows `-` on the detail page.
7. The console opens with the contact picked.
8. Each DO prints its own line `*Branch:* KULAI BRANCH` directly under `*Customer:*`, one fact per
   line, no ` · ` joins; the value matches the CRM screen. (The bot may first ask which period:
   answer `Sep 2026`.)
9. Only the DOs delivered on that date, each still with `*Branch:* KULAI BRANCH`.
10. A stock answer: no Branch line, no DO carried over.
11. `DO branch` is in the Field reveals list and is switched on (default for every contact).
12. The switch turns off and stays off after a page refresh.
13. The same DO list as step 8 with NO Branch line on any DO; every other field unchanged.
14. The switch is on again.
15. The Branch lines are back, exactly as in step 8.
