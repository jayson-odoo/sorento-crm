## Setup
A contact with Agent mode on and a linked user. A product code you know.
No settings change needed: the agent now defaults to Low reasoning effort.

## Steps
1. Sidebar: System > AI Assistant > Prompts.
2. Open chatbot_agent.
3. In Runs on, set Reasoning effort to None. Click Save.
4. Sidebar: System > Messaging > Chatbot Console. Open the Agent tab. Pick the contact.
5. Send: stock of <code>?
6. Click Steps under the reply. Write down the Tokens line (A).
7. Back to Prompts > chatbot_agent. Set Reasoning effort to Low. Click Save.
8. Back to the console. Click New conversation. Send the same question.
9. Click Steps. Write down the Tokens line (B).
10. Click Thinking (round 1).
11. Reload the page, pick the contact, click Steps.
12. Narrow the window to phone width.

## Expected
- Step 3: Save works; a v1 "Built-in text, published to configure the model" appears in Version history if there was none.
- Step 6: few or no Thinking items (effort None).
- Step 9: Thinking (round 1), (round 2)... collapsed, each above that round's tool lines.
- Step 10: the model's thinking in plain words; no long ids. The reply bubble has none of it.
- A vs B: the token cost of Low effort for one turn (post both lines on #2022).
- Step 11: same Thinking items after reload.
- Step 12: text wraps, no sideways scroll.
- Leave Reasoning effort on Low (the owner's choice), or clear it (Default (low) is the same).
