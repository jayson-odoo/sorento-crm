# Hand test #1386 (REFER-SALESMAN)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Sign in as an admin (someone who can open System Management > Chatbot Console, Sales > Customer asks, and a customer's Asks tab).
2. Pick a dealer contact: a Respond contact on the "Availability only" stock policy (User Management > Settings > Stock visibility), linked to a customer that has a sales agent. The contact whose WhatsApp transcript raised this (the SRTWC286-SH asks of 30 Sep) is one; its customer's agent is Jayson.
3. Know three products: one with stock and incoming shipments (SRTWC286-SH-NEW had an ETA of 2026-10-19 in the transcript), one with no incoming shipment at all, and a code that does not exist but is one character off a real one (e.g. the real code with its last character changed).
4. Note the row count on Sales > Customer asks (Open) before starting.

## Steps

1. From the sidebar open System Management > Chatbot Console, pick the dealer contact in Contact.
2. Send `incoming SRTWC286-SH-NEW` (the product with shipments). Read the reply.
3. Send `check stock SRTWC286-SH`, then answer the quantity question with `8`. Read the reply.
4. Send `check stock SRTWB8003-BL 6000` (a quantity above the category cap). Read the reply.
5. Send `incoming <the product with no shipments>`. Read the reply.
6. Send `check stock <the one-character-off code> 10`. The bot asks "Did you mean <real code>?". Reply `no`. Read the reply.
7. From the sidebar open Sales > Customer asks. Read the Open list.
8. Open the row from step 2 (the Incoming ETA one). Read Asked, Answered, the branch badge and the conversation panel.
9. From the sidebar open Order Management > Customers, open the dealer's customer, open the Asks tab. Read the Qty and Branch columns.
10. Sign in to the portal as the customer's sales agent and open Customer asks.

## Expected

1. The console opens with the contact selected.
2. Reply is exactly the product code on one line, `ETA: 2026-10-19` (or the current padded ETA list) on the next, a blank line, then `Please refer to your salesman.` No name, no "salesperson".
3. Reply is `SRTWC286-SH x 8: yes, we have stock. Please refer to your salesman.` (no "to proceed").
4. Reply is `SRTWB8003-BL x 6000: the quantity is more than what I can confirm here. Please refer to your salesman.`
5. Reply names the product, says no incoming matched, and ends with `Please refer to your salesman.` No offer to escalate to purchasing.
6. Reply is exactly `Please refer to your salesman.` and nothing is left open (a following `hi` is a fresh turn).
7. Five new Open rows, all marked Console, one per step 2 to 6, newest first: step 2 shows Product `SRTWC286-SH-NEW` (no quantity), branch `Incoming ETA`, Answer `ETA: 2026-10-19. Please refer to your salesman.`; step 3 shows `SRTWC286-SH x 8`, `In stock`, Answer `Yes, we have stock. Please refer to your salesman.`; step 4 shows `SRTWB8003-BL x 6000`, `Too big`; step 5 shows the product code alone, `Referred`, Answer = the reply text; step 6 shows the typed (wrong) code `x 10`, `Referred`, Answer `Please refer to your salesman.` Notified reads `Not sent` on the Incoming ETA and Referred rows with the title "This kind of answer does not notify the salesman".
8. Asked is the product code alone (no "x null"), Answered is `ETA: 2026-10-19. Please refer to your salesman.`, the badge reads `Incoming ETA`, and the conversation panel highlights the bot's ETA message.
9. Qty shows `-` on the Incoming ETA and Referred rows and the number on the others; Branch shows `Incoming ETA` / `Referred` beside the existing labels. Nothing shows `null` or a UUID.
10. The portal list shows the same five rows with the same Asked / Answered text; Done and Reopen still work on the new rows.
