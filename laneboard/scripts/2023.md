# Hand test: PR #2023 QUOTE-MEDIA-CHAT (drawing and photo requests use the chat escalation flow)

Follow-up to #1983. Escalating a missing drawing or photo from a quotation line now works like a
chat escalation: the salesperson gets a WhatsApp acknowledgement, the PIC sees that WhatsApp
conversation in the same side panel as chat tickets and replies in it (the reply is the Respond),
and Resolve ticket closes it the chat way. Each salesperson gets their own request. A salesperson
with no linked WhatsApp contact keeps the #1983 panel (Respond button, in-app and email notices).

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
Crew keeps the copy's Respond.io and SMTP credentials pointed at nothing, so nothing is delivered:
WhatsApp messages are queued and fail, and a reply in the panel shows a send error instead of
going out. Everything else is visible.

Pick a project quotation in draft with its lines SAVED, with a line whose product has no drawing
and a line whose product has no photo. Note:
- salesperson A, whose user has a linked WhatsApp contact (User Management, the user's WhatsApp
  contact field; most active salespeople already have one);
- salesperson B, who can open the same quotation and has NO linked WhatsApp contact;
- a member of the marketing product team (the PIC; their role holds the normal SLA resolve
  permission, as for any SLA ticket).

## Steps
1. As salesperson A, open the quotation (Project Sales > the project > Quotations > the quotation), click the `Lines` tab and click `Escalate` in the Drawing column of the line with no drawing. Hover the tile.
2. As salesperson B, open the same quotation and look at that line's Drawing tile. Click it.
3. Sign in as the PIC (the assignee of A's request; if B's request went to another member, sign in as that member for step 6). Open the home Dashboards page, "My pending tasks", and click A's `Product drawing` row.
4. In the side panel, type a short reply in the message box and send it.
5. Close the panel and open A's row again. Click `Resolve ticket`, then `Confirm`.
6. Open B's `Product drawing` row on "My pending tasks".
7. Sign in as salesperson A and open the notification bell. Then open the quotation's Lines tab again.

## Expected
1. A toast `Escalated: you will be told when the request is resolved`; the tile reads `Escalated` with today's date; the hover reads `Escalated <date and time>. Due <date>.` (no "by N people"). Crew can see one queued WhatsApp to A reading `Your project request: technical drawing for <product code> (<quotation no>). Routed to your PIC, they will reply shortly.` (it fails on the copy, nothing is sent).
2. B's tile reads `Escalate` (A's request does not hide it). Clicking gives the same toast and opens B's own request; no WhatsApp is queued for B.
3. A side panel like the chat one: the title `Product drawing · <code>`, the line `From <salesperson A> · With <PIC>`, the product link, `Upload in Resources`, `Waiting on this` with the quotation, and below them A's WhatsApp conversation with a message box. There is no `Respond` button and no Following list.
4. On the copy the send shows an error (no Respond.io key), so the row stays on `Respond`. On a live system the message goes to A's WhatsApp and the row moves to `Resolve`, as on a chat ticket.
5. `Resolve ticket` asks to confirm; the panel then shows `Resolved` and the row leaves "My pending tasks". Crew can see the standard "your enquiry ... has been resolved" WhatsApp and the conversation close queued for A (both fail on the copy).
6. Today's #1983 panel: the facts, `From <salesperson B> · With <PIC>`, a `Respond` button, and no conversation or message box.
7. A notice `Product drawing request for <product code> resolved` (in-app; email queued; no WhatsApp notice, the WhatsApp resolved message replaces it). The line's Drawing tile reads `Escalate` again for A.
