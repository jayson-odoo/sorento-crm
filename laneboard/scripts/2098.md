# Hand test: PR #2098 PS-SAMPLE-TRACKING (sample request date answer + consignment delivery)

## URL

crew test copy

## Setup

1. Apply the `crew-migration:` SQL from the PR (the second block, with `consignment_order_id`).
2. Sign in as a user with **Process sample requests** (the sample team), e.g. an admin.
3. Have one sample request in **Submitted** with Request Delivery Date **1 Dec 2026** (create one
   from **Project Sales > Sample Requests > Add**, submit it). Call it SR-A.
4. Pick two live consignments on the copy, both in the copy's own company:
   * CG-T: one that HAS an Overall Tracking row (`orders.order_number` = the CG number with an
     `actual_delivery_date`), e.g. any CG dated after 29 Apr 2026. Note its tracking date.
   * CG-N: one with NO tracking row, e.g. a CG dated before 29 Apr 2026. Note its CG date.

## Steps

1. Open SR-A. Click **Change to processing**.
2. In the dialog, leave both options unticked. Look at the **Change to processing** button.
3. Tick **No, propose a new date**. Set **Proposed date** to yesterday.
4. Set **Proposed date** to 9 Dec 2026, type `Stock from KL` in **Note**, click **Change to processing**.
5. Look at the line under **Request Delivery Date**.
6. Go to **Project Sales > Sample Requests**. Find SR-A's row and look at its **Delivery** cell.
7. Click the **Delivery** column header to sort ascending.
8. Open **Filters**, set **Stage** to **Processing** only.
9. Open SR-A, gear menu, **Change date answer**. Tick **Yes, we can meet it**, click **Save**.
10. Gear menu, **Link consignment**. Search the CG-N number, pick it, click **Link**.
11. Look at the status, **Consignment No**, **Delivered** and **Return due**.
12. Look at the action buttons in the header.
13. Gear menu, **Link consignment** again. Search the CG-T number, pick it, click **Link**.
14. Look at **Delivered** and **Return due**.
15. Back on the list, **Filters > Stage**: choose **Delivery** only.
16. Sign in as SR-A's salesperson and open SR-A.

## Expected

2. The button is disabled.
3. A red line reads *The proposed date cannot be in the past.* and the button stays disabled.
4. The dialog closes; the status pill reads **Processing**.
5. **Sample team**: **Proposes 9 Dec 2026** with a yellow **New date** badge; under it *Stock from KL*.
6. **Delivery** shows **9 Dec 2026** with a **New date** badge (not 1 Dec 2026).
7. SR-A sorts by 9 Dec 2026, i.e. after any open request due before 9 Dec.
8. SR-A is listed; Draft, Delivered and Cancelled requests are not.
9. Toast *Date answer saved*; the line reads **Can meet the requested date**, no badge.
10. Toast *Consignment linked*.
11. Status **Delivered**; **Consignment No** = `<CG-N> - <CG-N date>`; **Delivered** =
    `<CG-N date> (consignment date)`; **Return due** = CG-N date + 2 months (the default loan).
12. No **Mark delivered** button (and no Change to processing / Customer confirmed).
13. Toast *Consignment linked*.
14. **Delivered** = `<CG-T tracking date> (Order Tracking)`; **Return due** = that date + 2
    months; **Consignment No** = `<CG-T> - <CG-T date>`.
15. SR-A is listed under Delivery.
16. The salesperson sees **Sample team: Can meet the requested date** under Request Delivery
    Date (in the staff detail, or in the portal-style form for a salesperson limited to their own
    requests) and no **Link consignment** or **Change date answer** anywhere.
