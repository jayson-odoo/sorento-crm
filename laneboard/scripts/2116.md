# Hand test: PR #2116 CONFIRM-REMARK-FIX (SO remark and line remark reach the purchasing email; one dry run per change)

Follow-up to #2091. Three things to see:

1. The SO **Remark** typed on the Confirm page is printed in the purchasing email's SO table. Before
   this fix it came out blank whenever the Confirm answered a pending AutoCount change (SO394876,
   3 Nov).
2. A line's own **Remark** typed in the guide prints in the popup, in the email and in its Excel,
   not the generated words (`DELAY`, `DELAY, CHANGED: ...`).
3. The guide checks the email words once per change, for the order on screen only, so a save is not
   followed by two slow waits.

The copy sends no real mail: the email is queued and stays in Email Outbox, which is where it is
read.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- A sales order on Fulfilment Planning with a pending AutoCount change on one line (for example a
  date move) and that line's decision already saved as a Buy. On 3 Nov SO425045 line 176 (delivered
  25 of 25, AutoCount moved 15/06/2026 to 12/10/2026, saved Buy 25) was such an order; any order in
  the same state works. Write down its S/O number and the line's item code.
- The copy's database has active users with a purchasing role (it does on the prod copy). The
  purchasing email only goes out when an enabled "Order inquiry to purchasing" automation exists, as
  on prod.
- One browser tab only, signed in as an admin.

## Steps
1. From `/`, open the sidebar: Supply Chain > Project Demand > Fulfilment Planning, and open the order from Setup.
2. Click Confirm.
3. Wait until the page shows the lines and Next is on.
4. Read the line's REMARK cell.
5. Type `Planner line words` into that line's Remark, then click the page's Save.
6. Watch the page until Next is on again.
7. Read the line's REMARK cell again.
8. In the Remark field in the order's header (beside the S/O number) clear it and type `Hand test SO remark`.
9. Click Next to reach the Confirm step.
10. Click the Confirm button and wait for the result toast.
11. From `/`, open the sidebar: System > Messaging > Outgoing Mails > Email Outbox.
12. Open the newest email whose subject is `OI: <location> @ <S/O number>` for the order from Setup.
13. Read the REMARK cell of the S/O NO table (the first table).
14. Read the REMARK cell of the line row (the second table).
15. Open the email's Excel attachment (OI-....xlsx) and read the line's REMARK cell.
16. Compare the line row (LOCATION, ITEM CODE, QTY, NEW QTY, DELIVERY, NEW DATE, REMARK) with what the popup showed in step 7.

## Expected
1. The board opens on the order.
2. The guide opens on the order's page with a loading state.
3. The lines show; for the moved line DELIVERY 15/06/2026 and NEW DATE 12/10/2026 (or your order's dates).
4. The email's own words for the line, for example `DELAY`.
5. "Remark saved".
6. The sheet stays up the whole time; only Next waits, and it waits once (crew: one email dry run after the board re-read, not two).
7. `Planner line words`.
8. The field reads `Hand test SO remark`.
9. The Confirm step lists the order.
10. The toast reports the line confirmed (for example "1 line confirmed").
11. The Email Outbox list shows.
12. The email opens. On the copy it stays queued, never sent.
13. `Hand test SO remark` (before this fix it was blank).
14. `Planner line words` (before this fix the generated words, for example `DELAY`).
15. `Planner line words` (the Excel has no SO remark column; the line REMARK is the one to compare).
16. Every cell matches the popup: popup = email = Excel.
