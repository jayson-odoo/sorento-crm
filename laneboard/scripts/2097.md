# Hand test: PR #2097 PS-PROPOSAL-QUOTATION (proposals convert to a quotation; PO tallies with the quotation)

Owner rulings 2 Nov: today's project quotations are PROPOSALS (revisions A, B, C); a price change
between proposal revisions alerts management; a Won project turns one proposal revision into its
QUOTATION (own number QT-...); a further discount on the quotation needs approval; POs are checked
against the quotation and ordering more than quoted asks for a reason (never a block).

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
Crew applies migration `ppq_0001_proposal_quotation` to the test copy first (the crew-migration SQL
on the PR, once the owner has said yes to its backfill of `revision_label`); every proposal and PO
screen answers 500 until it is applied. Sign in as a project sales user with edit rights who also holds `projects.projects.manage` (so the alerts land in your
own bell). Pick any project you own whose Proposals tab is empty, or register a new one, e.g.
"PPQ hand test". Have one catalogue product handy, e.g. 32MM TAIL PIECE COUPLING. Also note one
existing project whose quotation was already sent before this change (dev copy: PRJQ-2026-0015, issued
R1 and R2) and download its PDF once BEFORE the migration, to compare in step 21.

## Steps
1. Open Project Sales > Projects and open the project.
2. Click the `Proposals` tab.
3. Click `Add a proposal`, add a scope "Townhouse" with one line: 32MM TAIL PIECE COUPLING, qty 10, unit price 300.00, and save.
4. Sign it from the actions menu (`Sign proposal`), then send it (Issue).
5. Edit it, change the unit price to 280.00, save, sign again and send it again.
6. Open the bell (notifications).
7. Edit it again, change only the quantity to 12, save, sign and send.
8. Open the bell again.
9. On the project, mark the scope "Townhouse" Won (scope outcome Won).
10. Open the proposal again and open its actions menu.
11. Click `Make quotation`, pick revision B, and click `Make quotation`.
12. Look at the header of the page that opens.
13. Edit the quotation, set the unit price to 270.00, save.
14. Look at the primary button and the banner above the lines.
15. Set the unit price back to 280.00, save, sign, and click `Send to Customer R1`.
16. Go to the project's Purchase Orders tab and record PO "PPQ-PO-1", checked against the quotation, with one line: 32MM TAIL PIECE COUPLING qty 8 at 280.00. Save.
17. Record PO "PPQ-PO-2" against the quotation with the same product qty 5 at 280.00. Save.
18. Type "Variation order VO-3" in Reason and click `Save with reason`.
19. Look at the Purchase Orders list and hover the badge on PPQ-PO-2.
20. Download the proposal's PDF (revision C) and the quotation's PDF (R1).
21. Open the existing project's document PRJQ-2026-0015 (sent as R1 and R2 before this change), look at its header and revision history, and download the R2 PDF.

## Expected
1. The project opens on its detail page.
2. The tab reads `Proposals`; the list has a `Kind` column.
3. The proposal is saved with a number like `PRJQ-2026-00NN`; Kind reads `Proposal`.
4. The first revision is `PRJQ-2026-00NN-A`.
5. The second revision is `PRJQ-2026-00NN-B`.
6. One notification "...: proposal price changed (PRJQ-2026-00NN-B)" listing `32MM TAIL PIECE COUPLING RM 300.00 -> RM 280.00`.
7. Revision C goes out (`PRJQ-2026-00NN-C`).
8. No new price-changed notification for revision C (quantity only).
9. The scope reads Won and the project outcome is Won.
10. The menu shows `Make quotation`.
11. The dialog lists C, B, A (newest first, C preselected); after picking B and confirming, a toast "Quotation QT-2026-NNNN made" and the quotation page opens.
12. Title `Quotation QT-2026-NNNN`; the header shows `From PRJQ-2026-00NN-B` as a link; the line reads qty 10 at 280.00 (revision B's line).
13. Saved.
14. The primary button reads `Send for approval` (no `Send to Customer`); the banner reads "One line is priced below the proposal, so this quotation needs a manager's approval before it can be sent to the customer."
15. Sent as `QT-2026-NNNN-R1`.
16. PPQ-PO-1 saves with no question (8 of 10).
17. A dialog `Quantity over the quotation` lists 32MM TAIL PIECE COUPLING, All POs 13, Quoted 10, with a Reason box; `Save with reason` is disabled until something is typed.
18. The PO saves; a notification "...: PO PPQ-PO-2 orders more than the quotation" reaches management.
19. PPQ-PO-2 shows a `Qty over quote` badge; hovering shows "Variation order VO-3". Its "Against" column starts with the quotation number.
20. The proposal PDF is named `proposal-PRJQ-2026-00NN-C.pdf` and prints "Proposal No. PRJQ-2026-00NN-C" (cover title PROPOSAL); the quotation PDF is named `quotation-QT-2026-NNNN-R1.pdf` and prints "Quotation No. QT-2026-NNNN-R1" (cover title QUOTATION).
21. PRJQ-2026-0015 opens, Kind reads `Proposal`, its revisions still read `PRJQ-2026-0015-R1` and `-R2` (no letters), its signatures and won scopes are unchanged, and its next send button reads `Send to Customer R3`. The R2 PDF prints the same number, lines, totals and signatures as the one downloaded before the migration; the only differences are the words "Proposal No." in place of "Quotation No." (and PROPOSAL on the cover title) and the file name starting `proposal-` instead of `quotation-`.
