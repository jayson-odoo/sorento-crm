## Setup
1. No migration and no SQL. Restart nothing beyond the usual test copy boot.
2. From `sorento_crm_backend/` on the test copy: `venv/bin/python scripts/repair_oi_reserve_used_rows.py` (dry run, writes nothing).
3. Sign in as an admin. "Borrow from another order" stays OFF (Users & Access > Settings) until step 13.

## Steps
1. Read the dry run output from Setup step 2.
2. Sidebar: Procurement > Supply Chain > Order Inquiries. Press Lines. Clear the "To order" chip.
3. Type SRTSA830-PJ in the search box.
4. Look at the Qty column of every row.
5. Open Filters, set Reserve: Needs reserve, apply. Note the OI numbers listed.
6. Press Documents. Press All. Open Filters, set Reserve: Needs reserve. Compare the OI numbers with step 5.
7. In Documents, search OI-2609-0257 and read its Lines figure. Open OI-2609-0257 and count the lines on its Lines tab.
8. Sidebar: Procurement > Supply Chain > Stock Debt. Press Save snapshot, then Save snapshot.
9. Press Snapshots, open the snapshot just saved, search SRTSA830-PJ, press the product name.
10. In the OI-2609-0257 block, read the OI row and Now cells of the SO396347 row.
11. On any block that shows "Request CS to reserve", press it and read the rows offered.
12. Back on the live Stock Debt page, look through the Covered by and Status columns of a few drills (press a month cell).
13. Users & Access > Settings: switch "Borrow from another order" ON, save. Reload Stock Debt and open the drill of a product that showed "order back" before this lane (any line with landed goods due far out).
14. Switch "Borrow from another order" back OFF and save.
