# Hand test: PR #2233 NPA-FROM-SI (New Product Application from a Stock Inquiry)

Purchasing flags "NPA needed" on its stock inquiry reply; the salesperson raises the application in the
portal from that inquiry, prefilled; purchasing answers it in the stock inquiry's own "Edit purchasing
response" dialog, with Save only or Update & Reply. Update & Reply goes through the stock inquiry's
conversation with the application's OWN WhatsApp use case, "New Product Application", and its View link opens
the application's portal page.

## URL
crew test copy (CRM and the portal)

## Setup
1. Apply the latest `crew-migration:` on the PR first. It adds `purchasing_response` and widens
   `new_product_applications.project_name` to TEXT.
2. Accounts:
   - Admin (CRM): holds every NPA permission; plays purchasing and the approver.
   - Salesperson A (PORTAL): contact 678da08e, which holds the New Product Application form. Every
     application in this script is raised by Salesperson A in the portal (owner 8 Nov: staff do not raise
     NPAs), so it is source = portal and carries that contact. Use the portal sign-in, or the contact's
     "Impersonate in portal" from the CRM; never "New" on the CRM's New Product Applications list.
3. SI-A = SI26-0191, a stock inquiry of contact 678da08e with a Respond.io conversation (as any stock
   inquiry that shows "Update & Reply").
4. SI-LONG = SI26-0126 (project name of 586 characters, no application yet). SI26-0139 / SI26-0140 already
   have live applications, so they do not offer Create. If SI26-0126 is not contact 678da08e's, use any
   Responded SI of that contact and paste a long project name into PROJECT in step 11.
5. NEWCODE: any active code except SRTWB1633, SRTWT8207, CB313, SRTWT2215, SRTWB7099 or SRTSA831.
6. MOCHACUST = 300-1002 "1 LIVING DEPOT SDN BHD - [CERAMIC]" (Mocha). SORCUST: a Sorento customer.
7. WhatsApp template (optional, owner): once the owner's "New Product Application" template is approved in
   Respond.io, Admin presses Sync on Settings > Integrations > WhatsApp Templates and maps the
   "New Product Application" use case. Without it, step 10 checks the in-window text only.

## Steps
1. CRM as Admin: open SI-A, press "Edit purchasing response". Note the dialog: its description line, fields
   and buttons. Tick "Salesperson must create a New Product Application", type any response, press
   "Save only".
2. Portal as Salesperson A: open SI-A, press "Create new product application". In CUSTOMER type
   "LIVING DEPOT", pick MOCHACUST, press "Save as draft".
3. Change CUSTOMER to SORCUST, press "Save as draft", then Submit.
4. CRM as Admin: open the application (New Product Applications), press Approve. Look at the header buttons.
5. Press "Edit purchasing response" on the application. Compare it with step 1's dialog.
6. Leave "Purchasing response" empty and look at the two save buttons.
7. Type "Supplier can cut 600 x 900.", choose "Link an existing product code", pick NEWCODE, set Lead time
   "8 weeks" and Cost (MYR) "210", press "Save only".
8. Look at the application, and at Salesperson A's bell (CRM user linked to contact 678da08e).
9. Press "Edit purchasing response" again, change the text to "Code opened, please quote.", press
   "Update & Reply".
10. Open SI-A's conversation (Respond.io, or the SI's chat) and read the message. Then open its link (in-window
    text) or press View (template) three ways, and compare each with SI-A's own View link opened the same way:
    a. in a browser already signed in to the portal as Salesperson A;
    b. in a private window (no portal session), entering the OTP code;
    c. in a browser signed in to the portal as ANOTHER contact (for example via "Impersonate in portal" of a
       different contact).
11. CRM as Admin: open SI-LONG, press "Edit purchasing response", tick "Salesperson must create a New Product
    Application", press "Save only" (without the tick there is no Create button). Portal as Salesperson A:
    open SI-LONG, press "Create new product application", press "Save as draft", then Submit.
12. CRM as Admin: open that application.
13. CRM as Admin, on SI-A's own page press "Reject" with any reason (the SI has no Close button; Reject itself
    queues the SI's WhatsApp update, as always). Open the step 2 application and press "Edit purchasing
    response". Change the text and press "Save only". Then go back to SI-A and press "Reopen to pending
    purchasing" to restore it.
14. Repeat steps 1, 5, 9 and 12 at 375 px wide.

## Expected
- Step 1: the SI dialog, in order: description "Save updates the record only. Update & Reply saves your text
  and sends it to the contact.", "Purchasing response" text, the NPA checkbox, Attachments, then Cancel /
  "Save only" / "Update & Reply".
- Step 2: the CUSTOMER list equals the SI's, Mocha rows included; it saves as text
  "300-1002 - 1 LIVING DEPOT SDN BHD - [CERAMIC]". No product table.
- Step 3: SORCUST links with its code. Submitted shows no "Waiting for approval / Locked" banner.
- Step 4: Revise, "Edit purchasing response" and the red-outline "Reject" (the SI's button).
- Step 5: the same dialog as the SI's, with the same description line. "Purchasing response" text first,
  then Link an existing product code / New product code, Lead time, Cost (MYR), then Cancel / "Save only" /
  "Update & Reply". No "Not opening", no "No answer yet".
- Step 6: "Save only" and "Update & Reply" are both disabled while the text is empty, as on the SI.
- Step 7: saves, the dialog closes. Nothing is sent to the conversation.
- Step 8: Completed; NEW PRODUCT CODE = NEWCODE, LEADTIME 8 weeks, COST MYR 210.00, PURCHASING RESPONSE
  "Supplier can cut 600 x 900.". Salesperson A's bell shows "NPA-xxxxxx is done: the codes are open": the
  application's lifecycle notice, already on main (#1769) for EVERY completion, not a reply. Save only sends
  no message.
- Step 9: saves; PURCHASING RESPONSE reads the new text. No new bell (the application was already Completed).
- Step 10: the message never says "Stock Inquiry"; it is sent with the "New Product Application" use case, on
  SI-A's conversation (the Respond outbox / integration log row is SI-A's, as for the SI's own reply).
  - Template mapped (setup 7), outside the 24 h window: the owner's template, filled with the mapped values
    (NPA no, SI no SI-A, customer SORCUST, current codes, NEWCODE, 8 weeks, "Code opened, please quote.");
    a blank value shows "-". View opens the application's portal page.
  - Inside the 24 h window: the template's body as text when mapped; unmapped, the text "There is a response
    to your new product application:" / "NPA-xxxxxx: Code opened, please quote." / "New product code:
    NEWCODE" / "Lead time: 8 weeks", then the link.
  - Unmapped and outside the window: nothing is delivered and the outbox row is failed, exactly as an
    unmapped stock inquiry update.
  - 10a: the link / View opens the application straight away.
  - 10b: it first lands on the OTP verify page (/portal/c/<slug>/verify), exactly as the SI's link; after the
    code it opens the application itself, exactly where the SI's link lands for its SI: a contact with a CRM
    user (Salesperson A) on the CRM page /procurement-management/new-product-applications/<id> (the SI:
    /procurement-management/stock-inquiries/<id>), a contact without one on the portal page
    /portal/c/<slug>/new_product_application/<id>. Never the applications LIST.
  - 10c: the owner's verify card ("log in with that number"), exactly as the SI's link in the same session;
    never "Application not found". After the owner's code it opens the application as in 10b.
- Step 11: saves and submits (no "String should have at most 255 characters").
- Step 12: on the submitted application (read-only), PROJECT shows two lines, cut off, with an eye button that
  opens the full 586-character name.
- Step 13: while SI-A is Rejected (outside its response stage), the application's dialog has NO "Update &
  Reply" (only Cancel / "Save only"), like the SI's own; Save only still saves the text and sends nothing from
  the application. After Reopen, SI-A is back at its status before the reject (Responded) and the dialog
  shows "Update & Reply" again.
- Step 14: both dialogs fit the phone with the same footer stacking; the clamped PROJECT stays two lines;
  nothing scrolls sideways.
