# Hand test #1384 (SALES-CONVO)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Pick a salesperson who uses the sales portal: a `respond_contacts` row that a `sales_agents` row points at (`sales_agents.contact_id`), with at least two customers assigned to that agent (`customers.sales_agent_id`) whose WhatsApp contacts are linked on the customer (Contact page > Customers, i.e. `respond_contact_customers`) and have chatted with the business number (rows in `chat_histories`). On the dev DB the agent behind Customer asks hand test #1378 qualifies; if none does, link one customer's WhatsApp contact from the CRM Contact page first.
2. In the CRM open User Management > Contacts, open that salesperson's contact, Portal forms section: switch **Conversation** on (it is off by default, exactly like Customer asks). Leave Customer asks on too.
3. Note one of the agent's customers whose contact wrote the LAST message (the customer's turn), and one where the business wrote last.
4. Open the portal as that salesperson on a phone-sized window (375 wide) and on a desktop window: `/portal/c/<slug>`, verify with the OTP as usual.

## Steps

1. On "Welcome, <name>", open the view dropdown and read its entries.
2. Pick **Conversation**.
3. Read the cards: order, the tinted ones, the "Customer wrote last" label, the arrow before the last message, the time at the top right.
4. Tap the card of the customer from Setup step 3 who wrote last.
5. In the sheet, scroll up to the top of the thread; then tap the magnifier in the header, type a word that appears in an earlier message, use the down / up arrows.
6. Look for any way to type a message in the sheet.
7. Close the sheet (swipe down or tap outside). Tap the Sort button, pick Customer; tap it again.
8. Tap Filter, set "Last message from" to Customer.
9. Clear the filter. Type part of a customer's name in the landing search box, then part of a contact's phone number, then a word from a last message, then nonsense.
10. Clear the search. Switch to the list icon (List view). Click a row.
11. On the desktop window repeat steps 2, 4 and 10.
12. In the CRM, switch Conversation OFF on the contact's Portal forms, then reload the portal landing.
13. Open the portal as a contact who is NOT a sales agent (a dealer) with Conversation switched on for them.

## Expected

1. The dropdown lists Conversation after Customer asks, with a count badge = the number of the agent's customer contacts that have a chat. Only when Setup step 2 is on.
2. The body shows one card per customer contact with a chat, no New button, one toolbar (Filter, Sort "Last message", cards / list toggle). Customers of OTHER agents never appear. A customer with no linked WhatsApp contact, or whose contact never chatted, has no card.
3. Latest message first. A card whose last message came from the customer is tinted blue and says "Customer wrote last" with an arrow pointing in; one where the business wrote last is plain with an arrow pointing out. The time reads like "12 minutes ago" / "Yesterday"; hovering (desktop) shows the full Malaysia date-time. No ids anywhere.
4. A bottom sheet opens with the SAME chat look as the CRM ticket drawer: green avatar initial, "<customer> · <contact>" and the phone in the header, date pills, white incoming bubbles, green outgoing bubbles with the sender label and ticks. No internal notes: the CRM team's notes on the contact stay in the CRM (security ruling 30 Sep).
5. Older messages load as you scroll up until "Beginning of this conversation". The search bar shows "n / m", highlights every match, the arrows step between them and the active match gets a ring; a match outside the loaded window still scrolls into place. A "jump to latest" round button appears when scrolled up.
6. None: no text box, no Reply / Comment switch, no Send, no note. Long-press or right-click on a bubble offers Copy only.
7. Sorted A to Z by customer; the second tap flips to Z to A. Reload the page: the choice is still applied (remembered per contact).
8. Only the tinted cards remain; the Filter button shows "1".
9. Each term narrows the list to the matching customers; nonsense shows "No conversation matches "<term>"" with a hint line.
10. The DataGrid has the columns Customer, Contact, Last message, When, latest first (the When header carries the arrow; clicking Customer sorts by it). A row click opens the same sheet as step 4.
11. Same as on the phone; nothing clipped at 1280.
12. Conversation is gone from the dropdown; a bookmarked `?type=conversation` link falls back to the first visible kind.
13. The dealer does not see Conversation at all (the switch alone is not enough: the contact must be linked to a sales agent).
