# Hand test #1435 (SO-NUMBER-ASK)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Sign in as an admin who can open System Management > Chatbot Console and a contact's Access tab.
2. Pick the HANLIM dealer contact the PR #1433 tester used (linked to the HANLIM TRADING SDN BHD accounts, A/C I to IV among them). On its Access > Field reveals card, make sure `Sales order outstanding` (`sales_orders.outstanding`) is ON.
3. Find one cancelled HANLIM SO and one SO of a customer this contact is NOT linked to, on the test copy DB:
   `SELECT so_number, debtor_name FROM sales_orders WHERE status = 'cancelled' AND debtor_name ILIKE 'HANLIM%' ORDER BY order_date DESC LIMIT 1;`
   `SELECT so_number, debtor_name FROM sales_orders WHERE status = 'open' AND debtor_name NOT ILIKE 'HANLIM%' ORDER BY order_date DESC LIMIT 1;`
   Note them as CANCELLED_SO and FOREIGN_SO.

## Steps

1. From the sidebar open System Management > Chatbot Console and pick the HANLIM contact.
2. Send `status of SO422056`. Read the reply.
3. Send `status of SO421624`. Read the reply.
4. Send `status of SO422057`. Read the reply.
5. Send `status of SO422095`. Read the reply.
6. Send `status of CANCELLED_SO` (the number from setup 3). Read the reply.
7. Send `status of FOREIGN_SO` (the number from setup 3). Read the reply.
8. Send `status of SO421624 SO999999 SO422057`. Read the reply.
9. Send `status of SO999999`. Read the reply.
10. On the contact's Access > Field reveals card, switch `Sales order outstanding` OFF. Back in the console, send `status of SO421624`. Read the reply. Switch the key back ON afterwards.

## Expected

1. The console opens with the contact selected.
2. Three lines, no period question, no DO list:
   `*SO422056* - <its debtor name, HANLIM TRADING SDN BHD [A/C III]>`
   `Ordered <its order date, e.g. 21 Sep 2026> - Closed`
   `Delivery: fully delivered` (or `partly delivered` if its lines were closed short).
3. `*SO421624* - HANLIM TRADING SDN BHD [A/C I]` / `Ordered 15 Sep 2026 - Open` / `Delivery: partly delivered`.
4. `*SO422057* - HANLIM TRADING SDN BHD [A/C IV]` / `Ordered 21 Sep 2026 - Open` / `Delivery: not delivered yet`.
5. `*SO422095* - HANLIM TRADING SDN BHD [A/C I]` / `Ordered 21 Sep 2026 - Closed` / `Delivery: fully delivered`.
6. `*CANCELLED_SO* - <debtor name>` / `Ordered <date> - ❗ Cancelled`, and no Delivery line.
7. Exactly `Sorry, that isn't under your account. I can only check on <the contact's linked customers>.` Nothing about FOREIGN_SO: no status, no customer name.
8. The SO421624 card, a blank line, the SO422057 card, a blank line, then `I could not find SO999999.` No "Which one do you mean?" list.
9. Exactly `I could not find SO999999.` No DO rows, no customer names, no escalate offer.
10. Exactly `Sales order figures are not enabled for your account.`

No reply anywhere lists DO numbers. The SO to DO link is empty in the data (0 of 37,996 orders rows), so the card shows delivery progress from the SO lines instead.
