# Hand test: PR #2120 SUPPLIER-ASK side rule (the parser decides a both-sides name) + near-floor supplier match

Owner on prod, 3 Nov 2026: a name that is both a customer and a supplier was asked "customer or supplier?" even on a
plain sales question. Now the parser reads the side from the whole turn (`party_side`): a sales question is the
customer, a purchase question is the supplier, and only a message that says neither is asked. Also from crew's
re-check: a supplier found only by a weak meaning match (exactly at the floor, alone) is never taken as the answer.

## URL
crew test copy, Chatbot Console (staff)

## Setup
1. The copy runs head of PR #2120 or later.
2. Crew publishes the candidate parser version from `documentation/plans/chatbot/PROMPT-party-side-3nov.md`, UNLABELLED. It is combined with #2105's section if crew lands both in one version. Crew pins it in the Chatbot Console and names its version number and md5 on the PR. Without the `== PARTY_SIDE ...` section the bot behaves as prod does today and every step 1 to 4 asks "customer or supplier?".
3. Contact: the office-access staff contact with the supplier reveal (`purchase_orders.supplier`) and the sales report grant, as in the #2066 hand test.
4. Data: hanlim and fullshun each exist as a customer AND a supplier on the copy (the same as prod, where the owner saw the question).
5. A new conversation for every step (Console: new conversation).

## Steps
1. Send: `delivery for hanlim`
2. Send: `dealer fullshun oct sale`
3. Send: `customer fullshun only`
4. Send: `fullshun PO outstanding`
5. Send: `fullshun`
6. Send: `Outstanding for 金百川`. Repeat in 5 new conversations.
7. Reply `all` to the step 6 question each time it asks one.

## Expected
1. No "Which one do you mean?", no "(customer)" / "(supplier)" lines, no "couldn't find a supplier". The sales side answers for the hanlim CUSTOMER: either "Which period for <the hanlim customer name>?" with the month options, or its deliveries. No purchase orders. The turn trace shows `party_side` customer.
2. No customer-or-supplier question. The October sales of the fullshun CUSTOMER (dealer channel), or the sales side's own question ("Invoiced, delivered, ordered or outstanding?"). Never a PO list.
3. No customer-or-supplier question, even though the message says "customer": the sales side for the fullshun customer (its report, or the sales side's own period or basis question).
4. No customer-or-supplier question. The open purchase orders of the fullshun SUPPLIER: the PO tool runs with that supplier's id only (no customer filter). With none open, "No outstanding purchase orders found" naming the supplier. Never a customer sales report.
5. "Which one do you mean?" with two lines, `fullshun (customer)` and `fullshun (supplier)` (order may vary). Nothing is listed yet.
6. Each run is one of these:
   - the JINBAICHUAN supplier question, then (step 7) its open PO lines;
   - a "Which one do you mean?" over several near suppliers (e.g. KIM HOH beside another KIM supplier);
   - "Sorry, I couldn't find a supplier called "金百川"" or the not-found line.

   It is NEVER a PO list (or "no outstanding purchase orders") for KIM HOH HARDWARE alone, and never a list with no supplier filter.
7. After a supplier question: the open POs of exactly the suppliers that question listed, never the whole PO book.
