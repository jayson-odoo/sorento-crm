# Hand test: PR #2294 DEALER-ACCESS-SCREEN (a salesperson grants, changes and removes dealer access on a screen)

MY WORK > Dealer access lists the people linked to the salesperson's OWN customers (their sales agent's accounts),
one row per person and company, with company as a filter. Add people, Edit and Unlink go through the same service
as the #2268 agent. Business Owner = the company's "Business Owner" tag; Employee = no tag. Another salesperson's
accounts are never shown or named.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
1. The copy runs the FINAL head of PR #2294 (the head named in the latest crew-note on the PR), migrated. This PR adds
   no migration (#2268's `dap_0001_dealer_access` gives the salesperson role `user_management.contacts.edit_own`).
2. No message or contact may reach the live Respond.io: a grant enqueues a Respond lookup/create job. Keep the copy's
   egress blocked (on the crew copy the dead proxy set in `.env` is what blocks it; the key alone is not the guard),
   or stop the worker's `respond_io` queue.
3. If the owner tags are missing on the copy (dev has none), create them on Users > Contact tags
   (`/user-management/contact-tags`), active, with EXACTLY these access types: "Sorento Business Owner" = dealer,
   cabana_dealer; "Mocha Business Owner" = mocha_dealer.
4. Set Settings > Change countdown (seconds) (`/user-management/settings`) to 5 (dev has 1), so step 12 can be
   cancelled.
5. Pick a salesperson user SP1 granted BOTH Sorento and Mocha whose agent has accounts in both companies. Note:
   D1 = an SP1 Sorento dealer with 2 or more accounts (codes C1, C2), and M1 = one SP1 Mocha account.
6. Pick a contact P_OTHER linked to one of SP1's Sorento accounts AND to an account of ANOTHER salesperson (any
   company). Note the other salesperson's account code (X1). Also note one SP1 row that shows "Employee" but holds the
   dealer access type without the owner tag (a legacy row, P_LEGACY; most real rows are like this).
7. Invented phones P1, P2 (+6011 numbers, never a real dealer's). SP1's own phone is S1.
8. As admin, open Users, find SP1 and use Impersonate to sign in as SP1. Header company switcher on Sorento.

## Steps
1. From the sidebar, click MY WORK > Dealer access.
2. Open Filter, set Company = Sorento, then Access = Business Owner, then Clear all.
2a. Click the P_LEGACY row, set Access to Business Owner, Save. Then set it back to Employee, Save.
3. Search for X1.
4. Click Add people. Company Sorento, My accounts C1 and C2.
5. Row 1: name "Test One", phone P1, Business Owner. Click "+ Add person"; row 2: "Test Two", phone S1, Employee.
6. Click Save.
7. Remove row 2 with its trash button. Click Save.
8. Click the Test One row. Untick C2, set Access to Employee, Save.
9. Click the P_OTHER row. Set Access to Business Owner, Save.
10. Click Add people. Company Mocha, My accounts M1, row "Test Two", phone P2, Employee. Save.
11. With the header still on Sorento, open the Test Two row menu > Unlink from M1. Wait 6 seconds.
12. Open the Test One row menu > Unlink from C1, then click Cancel within the 5 second countdown.
13. Narrow the browser to phone width (375px).
14. Stop impersonating. As admin, open Test One and P_OTHER on Contacts (their Tags and Customers), then open
    System > Audit Logs (`/system-management/audit-logs`) and find the entries for Test One's contact.

- Step 6: refused, nothing saved: "This number belongs to a staff member." under row 2; the form stays open.
