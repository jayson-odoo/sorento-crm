# PR 2386 hand test: price tag request line can be ala carte (product only) even when the product has a combo

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Migration `ptag_0023_line_package` applied (crew-migration note on the PR: drops the one-line-per-product rule; deletes no data).
- Catalogue shapes (dev data has all three):
  - Product A: a basin with exactly ONE combo named like "<code> + ADD ON" (the owner's case), with a choice group of taps.
  - Product B: a product with TWO or more combos (e.g. a bathroom furniture set with two mirror-cabinet combos).
  - Product C: a product with no combo.
- Sign in as a salesperson whose contact has the Price Tag Request form (staff list pages have no Create for price tag requests). Steps 15 needs the AI reader and the worker running on the copy.

## Steps
1. From `/`, open MY REQUESTS > Price Tag Request in the sidebar (portal) and click Create ("New Price Tag Request").
2. Pick a customer. On line 1 click "Search a set or product...", type product A's code and click it.
3. Look at line 1.
4. Click line 1's eye icon (tag preview), then close it.
5. Open line 1's Package select and choose the combo.
6. Click "+ Add line", search product A again and click it.
7. Click "+ Add line", add product A a third time and switch that line to SP.
8. On line 1, clear the Package select (its clear button).
9. Open line 1's Package select again and choose the combo.
10. On a new line, type a shared part of several codes (e.g. A's prefix), tick product A and one other product, then press Esc.
11. Add product B on a new line and open its Package select.
12. Add product C on a new line.
13. Click Save Draft, then reopen the draft from the list.
14. Open Preview, then Approve.
15. Start another new Price Tag Request, pick a customer, choose "Upload a file instead" and drop a sales order file listing product A twice: once alone, once joined to one of its combo's parts with "+". Extract with AI, then Confirm and prefill.
16. Save Draft, then on the same request add a line of a bathroom furniture or kitchen sink product that has combos, leave its Package empty, and look at the row.
17. Approve the request, then click Edit, change the line that is on the combo to an empty Package (Ala carte), and save.
18. Open the request's History tab.
19. Shrink the window to 375 px wide and repeat steps 2, 3, 5 and 8 on a new request.

## Expected
- Step 3: line 1 holds A with a Package select that is EMPTY and reads "Ala carte"; no part rows; the LP is A's own list price. There is no "already on this request" message anywhere.
- Step 4: the preview shows A on its own: A's own price, template and accessories, never the combo's.
- Step 5: the combo's part rows appear under line 1 (open tap group reads "Not sure, any of N"); the price is the combo's (or "-" if the combo has no list price).
- Step 6: line 2 holds A, Package reads "Ala carte", no parts, no refusal.
- Step 7: line 3 holds A on SP; three lines of A sit on the request.
- Step 8: line 1's parts disappear and Package reads "Ala carte".
- Step 9: the combo and its parts come back on line 1.
- Step 10: product A is NOT greyed or "Already added" in the list; the two ticks add two new lines (A a fourth time, plus the other product), both on "Ala carte".
- Step 11: B's line starts on "Ala carte"; its Package list shows B's combos by name (no "Ala carte" option, empty = ala carte).
- Step 12: C's line shows no Package select.
- Step 13: "Draft saved". The reopened draft shows every line in the same order with the same package: line 1 on the combo with its parts, every other A line still "Ala carte" (nothing switched itself to the combo).
- Step 14: one tag per line; the ala carte A tags show A's own price, the combo line shows the combo tag. No error about repeated products.
- Step 15: two lines of A: the bare row on "Ala carte" with no parts, the "+" row on the combo. They are not merged into one line.
- Step 16: the ala carte line shows NO "Package warning" pill (no "No package chosen"). A product in that class with no combo at all still shows "No package defined".
- Step 17: the edit saves without any "appears twice" refusal.
- Step 18: History shows the edit with "Product line N package", the old value <combo name> struck through, an arrow, then "Ala carte".
- Step 19: the Package select fits on the line, the page never scrolls sideways, and the steps behave as at full width.
