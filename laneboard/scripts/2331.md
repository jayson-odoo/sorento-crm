## Setup
Sign in as a user who can edit project sales. Use a project quotation document that already has a
Draft spec sheet (e.g. SS1) with at least two product pages, and a price tag template you can edit.
Have a screenshot ready on your OS clipboard for step 9.

## Steps
1. Open Project Sales > a project > the quotation document > Spec sheets > SS1.
2. Hover a product page header and open its "Add accessory" dropdown.
3. Drag the grip between the Pages rail and the pages to the right, then drag the grip left of the Inspector to the left.
4. Reload the page.
5. Click an image on a page to select it, then press Cmd+C (Ctrl+C on Windows), then Cmd+V.
6. With the pasted copy selected, press Cmd+D, then Cmd+Z.
7. Select a text layer and press Cmd+X, then click an empty spot on ANOTHER page's canvas and press Cmd+V.
8. Right-click an empty spot of a page and choose Paste.
9. Take a screenshot (Cmd+Ctrl+Shift+4 on Mac copies it), click a page, press Cmd+V.
10. Select a text layer, open Insert field in the Inspector, type "Description" in the search.
11. Open Dealer Kit > Tag templates > any template, right-click an empty spot on the canvas.
12. In the same template designer, drag the Inspector grip, then go back to the spec sheet.

- Step 3: both side panels resize while dragging; the pages re-fit to the new middle width.
- Step 4: the widths you dragged are kept.
- Step 5: a copy of the image appears 5 mm right and down from the original, on the same page only; no other page changes.
- Step 6: Cmd+D adds a second copy; Cmd+Z removes it (the toolbar Undo state matches).
- Step 7: the text leaves the first page and lands on the page you clicked, at the same x/y.
- Step 8: Paste is enabled and pastes the last copied layer (or the clipboard picture/text if you copied one since). The browser may ask once to allow clipboard access.
- Step 9: the screenshot is uploaded and placed centred on the clicked page as an image layer.
- Step 10: "Description" is listed under Product (beside Price tag description); inserted, the page prints the product's catalogue description.
- Step 11: Paste is enabled even with nothing copied in the designer, and pastes what is on the OS clipboard.
- Step 12: the spec sheet's Inspector has the width you dragged in the template designer (one shared setting).
