## Setup
Sign in as a user who can edit project sales. Open the project "ZZT outcome 2197 B" (PPF2611006)
> Proposals > PRJQ-2026-0027-A > Spec sheet > SS1 (3 product pages). Have a screenshot ready on
your OS clipboard for step 10.

## Steps
1. Open the project "ZZT outcome 2197 B" (PPF2611006) > Proposals > PRJQ-2026-0027-A > Spec sheet > SS1.
2. Hover a product page header and open its "Add accessory" dropdown.
3. Drag the grip between the Pages rail and the pages to the right, then drag the grip left of the Inspector to the left.
4. Reload the page.
5. Click an image on a page to select it, then press Cmd+C (Ctrl+C on Windows), then Cmd+V.
6. With the pasted copy selected, press Cmd+D, then Cmd+Z, then drag the image a little, then Cmd+Z again, then reload.
7. On page 3 drag a box to a new spot. Click page 4. Click page 3 again. Drag another box on page 3. Wait 2 seconds and reload.
8. Select a text layer and press Cmd+X, then click an empty spot on ANOTHER page's canvas and press Cmd+V.
9. Click page 4 first, then right-click an empty spot on page 4 and choose Paste.
10. Take a screenshot (Cmd+Ctrl+Shift+4 on Mac copies it), click an empty spot on a page, press Cmd+V.
11. Click a text layer on a page, then in the Inspector click Insert field, search "Project title", click it, Done. Reload.
12. Open Dealer Kit > Room Designer > Tag Templates > "Project Quotation Page Template", select a text layer, click Insert field in the Inspector, click "Search fields" first, then type "Description".
13. In the same template designer, right-click an empty spot on the canvas and choose Paste.
14. In the same template designer, drag the Inspector grip, then go back to the spec sheet.
