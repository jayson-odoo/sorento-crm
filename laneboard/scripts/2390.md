## Setup
- Sign in as a staff user who can open price tag requests (Marketing or admin).
- Pick a price tag request that the salesperson edited at least once on the portal and that Marketing has worked on (claimed, design saved, status moved). A request in Approved, Ready for collection or Collected usually has all of these.

## Steps
1. From the home page, open Dealer Kit in the sidebar, then Price Tag Requests.
2. Open the chosen request.
3. Look at the tab row under the header.
4. Click History.
5. Read the first three entries.
6. Find an entry that starts with "Edit" and click "Show N lines" on it (if it shows only one line, read that line).
7. Find a "Status:" entry.
8. Find an entry made on the portal by the salesperson (Submitted, Edit, Changes requested).
9. If the list has more than 10 entries, type "Status" in "Search history".
10. Click Claim (or any header action the request offers) and open History again.
11. Open the same request on a phone width (375 px) and click History.
12. Open a brand-new request nobody has touched since it was submitted, then History.

## Expected
- Step 3: tabs read Request, Lines, Files, History; History is last.
- Step 5: newest first; each entry is one sentence, then "dd/mm/yyyy, h:mm am/pm · by <name>" (seconds only where two entries share a minute).
- Step 6: lines read "<field>: <old> → <new>" (for example "Product line 2 quantity: 4 → 6", dates as dd/mm/yyyy); a cleared or newly set field shows "-" on its empty side; the title reads "Edit N" (plus ": <reason>" when one was given), the same word the portal's History tab uses.
- Step 7: "Status: <old> → <new>" with the words the status pill uses (New, Designing, Design Ready, Changes Requested, Approved, Ready for collection, Collected, Rejected, Void).
- Step 8: the salesperson shows by name, never blank and never "You".
- Step 9: only entries containing "Status" remain.
- Step 10: the new action appears at the top of History without reloading the page.
- Step 11: the list is readable with no sideways page scroll (the tab strip itself may scroll sideways to reach History, as on other pages with four tabs).
- Step 12: either one "Submitted" entry or "No history yet" with "Changes to this record appear here."
- Nowhere: an id, UUID, IP address or raw code.
