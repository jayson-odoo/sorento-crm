# PR 2350 hand test: Order Inquiry lines link to purchase orders with the Stock Debt link function (OI-PO-LINK)

Run in the same session as PR 2332, after its steps (both PRs are on the same db3 stack).

## URL
db3 demo stack (AutoCount demo, db3 test book only) for every step. On the crew test copy (shared dev DB) the switch stays OFF, so only step 14 can run there.

## Setup
- Sign in as a user holding "Link PO lines in AutoCount (Stock Debt, Order Inquiries)" (`projects.stock_debt.link_po`), the Order Inquiry view, confirm and action grants (`projects.order_inquiry.action`), and `projects.projects.view`.
- On the db3 demo stack: User Management > Settings, switch "AutoCount PO links (Stock Debt, Order Inquiries)" ON.
- Pick ONE product (call it P) that has, in the db3 book:
  - two Order Inquiry rows whose sales order lines are in AutoCount (rows R1 and R2, on two different sales order lines, outstanding Q1 and Q2);
  - one Order Inquiry row of P whose sales order line is NOT in AutoCount yet (row R3);
  - PO line A of P: open, not linked to any sales order, free at least Q1 + Q2;
  - PO line B of P: open, not linked, free at least Q1.
- One Order Inquiry row of a DIFFERENT product (row R4).

## Steps
1. From the sidebar open Procurement > Supply Chain > Order Inquiries and filter to product P.
2. Tick R1, R2 and R3.
3. Open Actions.
4. Choose "Link to PO... (2 of 3)".
5. In the popup, on R1's line pick PO A and type a qty 1 lower than the default.
6. On R2's line pick PO A as well and keep the default qty.
7. Press Link.
8. Read the result cards, press Done.
9. Read R1 and R2 in the list without reloading.
10. Right-click R1, choose "Change PO...", in "Change to" pick PO B, press Change.
11. Read the two result cards, press Done, read R1.
12. Right-click R2, choose "Unlink from PO...", press Unlink, read the result, press Done, read R2.
13. Tick R2 and R4, open Actions.
14. Switch "AutoCount PO links (Stock Debt, Order Inquiries)" OFF in Settings and reload Order Inquiries; open Actions; right-click a row.
15. Narrow the window to 375 px wide, switch ON again, tick R1, open Actions, choose "Link to PO...", then Cancel.

## Expected
3. Actions shows "Link to PO... (2 of 3)", "Change PO... (2 of 3)", "Unlink from PO... (2 of 3)", "Recheck in AutoCount (3)" and "Remove link in CRM". There is no "Link selected" or "Unlink selected".
4. The popup lists 2 lines, one per sales order line, each with sales order, line, Location and Delivery date (dd/mm/yyyy). R3 is named under the grid with "Not in AutoCount yet". Each qty defaults to what the line still needs, capped at the PO line's free qty.
7. Two result cards: "Linked: <SO> line <n> now covered by PO A (Q1 - 1)." and "Linked: <SO> line <n> now covered by PO A (Q2)."
8. In AutoCount PO A is split: one row linked to R1's sales order row with Q1 - 1, one row linked to R2's with Q2, and the rest stays as a free row with no sales order. Nothing on PO A is deleted and its total qty is unchanged.
9. R1 and R2 show PO A in their PO column without pressing anything else.
10. Two result cards: "Unlinked: <SO> line <n> is no longer covered by PO A (Q1 - 1)." then "Linked: <SO> line <n> now covered by PO B (Q1 - 1)."
11. R1 shows PO B. In AutoCount PO A's row for R1 keeps its qty and is free again (no sales order).
12. One result card "Unlinked: ...". R2 no longer shows PO A. In AutoCount that PO A row has no sales order (merged into PO A's free row when they match).
13. The three PO items are greyed with "Tick rows of one product."
14. The three PO items are gone from Actions and from the right-click menu; everything else on the screen is as before.
15. The page does not scroll sideways; the popup fits the screen and its Link and Cancel buttons are on screen.

Any line AutoCount refuses or skips reads "Not linked" with a plain reason naming the sales order and line (never a code) and a "Try again" button that sends only that line again; the other lines stay linked.
