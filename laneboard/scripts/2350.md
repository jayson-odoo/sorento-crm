# PR 2350 hand test: Order Inquiry lines link to purchase orders with the Stock Debt link function (OI-PO-LINK)

Run in the same session as PR 2332, after its steps (both PRs are on the same db3 stack).
Owner rulings in force: ONE "Link PO" item on every screen; inside its popup the user links, changes or
unlinks (13 Nov 18:55); "Choose document" is gone; leftover qty stays unlinked.

## URL
db3 demo stack (AutoCount demo, db3 test book only) for every step. On the crew test copy (shared dev DB) the switch stays OFF, so only step 12 can run there.

## Setup
- Sign in as a user holding "Link PO lines in AutoCount (Stock Debt, Order Inquiries)" (`projects.stock_debt.link_po`), the Order Inquiry view, confirm and action grants (`projects.order_inquiry.action`), and `projects.projects.view`.
- On the db3 demo stack: User Management > Settings, switch "AutoCount PO links (Stock Debt, Order Inquiries)" ON.
- Pick ONE product (call it P) that has, in the db3 book:
  - two Order Inquiry rows whose sales order lines are in AutoCount (rows R1 and R2, on two different sales order lines, outstanding Q1 and Q2);
  - one Order Inquiry row of P whose sales order line is NOT in AutoCount yet (row R3);
  - PO line A of P: open, not linked to any sales order, free at least Q1 + Q2;
  - PO line B of P: open, not linked, free at least Q1.
- One Order Inquiry row of a DIFFERENT product (row R4).

## Steps
1. From the sidebar open Procurement > Supply Chain > Order Inquiries and filter to product P.
2. Tick R1, R2 and R3, open Actions.
3. Choose "Link PO... (2 of 3)".
4. On R1's row pick PO A and type a qty 1 lower than the default; on R2's row pick PO A and keep the default qty.
5. Press Save, read the result cards, press Done.
6. Read R1 and R2 in the list without reloading.
7. Right-click R1, choose "Link PO...". On R1's PO A row set Action to "Change to" PO B. Press Save, read the cards, press Done, read R1.
8. Right-click R2, choose "Link PO...". On R2's PO A row set Action to "Unlink". Press Save, read the card, press Done, read R2.
9. Right-click R1, choose "Link PO...". Link PO A again to R1 on its new row (so R1 has PO B and PO A). Press Save, Done.
10. Right-click R1, "Link PO...": on the PO A row choose "Change to" any other open PO line of P with free qty; leave the PO B row on Keep. Press Save, Done, read R1.
11. Tick R2 and R4, open Actions.
12. Switch "AutoCount PO links (Stock Debt, Order Inquiries)" OFF in Settings and reload Order Inquiries; tick R1, open Actions; right-click a row.
13. Narrow the window to 375 px wide, switch ON again, tick R1, Actions > "Link PO...", then Cancel.
14. At full width, open the Order Inquiry document that holds R1 and R2 (click its number), tick R1, open the gear.
15. Gear > "Link PO...", then Cancel.
16. Tick R3 (not in AutoCount), gear > "Link PO...".
17. Open Stock Debt, open a snapshot drill for product P, tick R1's sales order line, open Decide.

## Expected
2. Actions shows "Link PO... (2 of 3)", "Recheck in AutoCount (3)" and "Remove link in CRM". There is no "Link to PO...", "Change PO...", "Unlink from PO...", "Link selected", "Unlink selected" or "Choose document".
3. The "Link PO" popup lists one new row per sales order line (R1, R2), each with sales order, Location and Delivery date (dd/mm/yyyy); there is no Line column. R3 is named under the grid with "Not in AutoCount yet". Each qty defaults to what the line still needs, capped at the PO line's free qty. One main button "Save (n)".
5. Two result cards: "Linked: <SO> line <n> now covered by PO A (Q1 - 1)." and "Linked: <SO> line <n> now covered by PO A (Q2)." In AutoCount PO A is split: one row linked to R1's sales order row with Q1 - 1, one row linked to R2's with Q2, and the rest stays as a free row. Nothing on PO A is deleted and its total qty is unchanged.
6. R1 and R2 show PO A in their PO column without pressing anything else; R1 has no "+1" (the 1 unit it still needs stays unlinked).
7. The popup shows R1's PO A row with an Action picker (Keep / Unlink / Change to ...). Cards: "Unlinked: ... no longer covered by PO A (Q1 - 1)." then "Linked: ... now covered by PO B (Q1 - 1)." R1 shows PO B only (no "+1"). Exactly those two cards, both "Unlinked"/"Linked" (no "Not linked", no "Could not reach AutoCount", no "Try again"), and Save finishes in a few seconds, not about a minute. The Unlinked qty is R1's own (Q1 - 1), never the size of PO A's free row after the merge. In AutoCount PO A's row for R1 keeps its qty and is free again.
8. One card "Unlinked: ... no longer covered by PO A (Q2)." (R2's own qty, not PO A's free row total). R2 no longer shows PO A. In AutoCount that PO A row has no sales order (merged into PO A's free row when they match).
9. R1 shows PO B and PO A.
10. Only the PO A row changes (unlinked, then the new PO line linked); PO B stays linked to R1. No "+1". Cards "Unlinked ... (1)" and "Linked ... (1)" only: no "Not linked" / "Try again", and Save finishes in a few seconds.
11. "Link PO..." is greyed with "Tick rows of one product."
12. "Link PO..." stays (Actions and right-click) and opens the CRM link popup offering PO lines only, nothing pre-filled, no PO line AutoCount ties to another sales order; nothing is written to AutoCount. Everything else on the screen is as before.
13. The page does not scroll sideways; the popup fits the screen (its grid scrolls inside it) and Save and Cancel are on screen.
14. The gear shows Auto link, "Link PO... (1)" and "Remove link in CRM"; there is NO "Link to PO...", "Change PO...", "Unlink...", "Choose document", "Link selected" or "Unlink selected".
15. The same "Link PO" popup as the lines view, with R1's PO rows and their Action pickers.
16. The CRM link popup opens, PO lines only (no SPO), nothing pre-filled; linking makes a CRM-only link (nothing written to AutoCount).
17. Decide shows "Link PO…" and "Move PO to another sales order…"; there is no separate "Link to PO…", "Change PO…" or "Unlink from PO…". "Link PO…" opens the same popup.

Any line AutoCount refuses or skips reads "Not linked" (or "Not unlinked") with a plain reason naming the sales order and line (never a code) and a "Try again" button that sends only that line again; the other lines stay as they are.
