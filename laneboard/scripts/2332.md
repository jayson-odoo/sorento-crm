# PR 2332 hand test: Stock Debt snapshot keeps quantities frozen, cover live; Change PO (STOCKDEBT-LIVE-ACTIONS)

## URL
crew test copy for steps 1 to 3 (read side). db3 demo stack (AutoCount demo, db3 test book only) for steps 4 to 13.

## Setup
- Sign in as a user holding Stock Debt view, `projects.stock_debt.link_po` and `projects.projects.view`.
- Steps 4 to 13 only: on the db3 demo stack, User Management > Settings, switch "AutoCount links from Stock Debt" ON. Leave it OFF on the crew test copy (shared dev DB): there the drill has no tick column and no Decide, so steps 4 to 13 cannot run.
- Pick a product that has open PO lines: one open AutoCount sales order line that is short (call its outstanding Q), plus open PO lines of the same product in the same AutoCount book.
- PO line A and PO line B of that product, both open and unlinked, each with at least Q free.
- PO line C of that product, open and unlinked, with LESS than Q free.
- On the crew test copy any product with a short line will do for steps 1 to 3 (no PO lines needed there).

## Steps
1. From the sidebar open Procurement > Supply Chain > Stock Debt, filter to the product, press Save snapshot, then Save snapshot in the popup.
2. Press Snapshots, open the new snapshot, press the product's month cell that holds the short line.
3. Write down the line's Ordered, Delivered, Outstanding, Assigned, Covered by, Status and Short.
4. (db3 from here on) Tick the line, press Decide, choose Link to PO..., pick PO line A, keep Qty Q, press Link 1 line. (UNVERIFIED, from the headless db3 run: if the first click on a PO option closes the Link popup, open it again and pick the PO again, or pick it with the keyboard.)
5. Press Done and read the line without reloading.
6. Reload the page and open the same cell again.
7. Tick the line and press Decide.
8. Choose Change PO..., in "Change to" pick PO line C, press Change 1 line.
9. In "Change to" pick PO line B instead, press Change 1 line.
10. Read the two result cards, press Done.
11. Read the line.
12. Tick the line, press Decide, choose Change PO..., open the "Change to" picker (do not send), then Cancel.
13. Narrow the window to 375 px wide, tick the line, press Decide, choose Change PO..., then Cancel.

## Expected
1. The snapshot saves and shows in Snapshots.
2. The drill opens headed "As frozen <time>".
3. Before any link: Assigned 0, Covered by "-", Status Short, Short Q; Outstanding Q.
4. One result card: "Linked: <SO> line <n> now covered by PO A (Q)."
5. Status Pinned, Assigned Q, Short 0, Covered by names PO A, Now shows "PO A (Q)". Ordered, Delivered and Outstanding exactly as in step 3.
6. Same as step 5 (nothing left frozen from before the link).
7. Menu: Link to PO... greyed "Already covered by PO A"; Change PO... enabled, right under it; Unlink and Move enabled.
8. Red text under the picker "PO <C> has only <N> free"; nothing is sent; the popup's qty column reads "Open" with Q. "Change to" does not offer the linked PO A line (the one covering the line now); if the step 4 link split PO A, its other (free) line is still offered, which is correct.
9. Two result cards appear.
10. Card 1 "Unlinked: <SO> line <n> is no longer covered by PO A (Q)."; card 2 "Linked: <SO> line <n> now covered by PO B (Q)."
11. Covered by and Now name PO B only; Status Pinned; Ordered, Delivered, Outstanding still as in step 3.
12. Link to PO... is greyed (PO B covers the line), so the picker is read through Change PO. PO A is offered again: one line with Free Q if A held exactly Q, else two lines whose Free adds up to what PO A had free before step 4. In AutoCount no PO A qty is cut and nothing is deleted.
13. The page does not scroll sideways; the Change PO popup fits the screen, its grid scrolls inside it to reach "Change to", and Change 1 line and Cancel are both on screen.
