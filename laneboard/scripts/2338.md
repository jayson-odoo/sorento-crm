# PR 2338 hand test: the guided sample request form (SAMPLE-REQUEST-V2 slice 1)

After the owner's hand test of 12 Nov ~15:45 (F1-F4). This supersedes the earlier hand tests. It
needs no crew test records.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Run `alembic upgrade head` on the copy. It applies `srv2_0001`, `srv2_0002` and `srv2_0003`
  (the contact phone widened to 255), plus `il_0002` / `qdp_0001` if missing.
- Sign in as the owner.
- PROJECT = a registered, approved project with a long title (two lines or more at 1280 px).
- PARTY = a party linked to PROJECT that has an address (on the copy: the architect or the main
  contractor of PPF2611001, not its developer, which has no address).
- PARTY needs two contacts with phones. If it has fewer, add them in the contacts popup at step 9
  with "+ Add contact" (any made-up name, and a phone such as 011-1111 1111).

## Steps
1. From the dashboard, open the sidebar: Project Sales > Projects, then open PROJECT.
2. Click the Samples Request tab, then Create.
3. Look at the form before touching it.
4. Look at the Project field.
5. Hover over the Project text, then click the eye beside it, then close the popup.
6. Pick a debtor in Customer (or keep the one prefilled from the project).
7. Collapse Items by hand if it is open, then pick PARTY in Ship to.
8. Expand Items again and pick a product in row 1 (or keep the prefilled row).
9. Click "Choose contacts" at the right of the Contact person label. Tick TWO contacts that have
   phones and click Done.
10. In Delivery, type a date a week ahead as dd/mm/yyyy, press Enter, choose Deliver-Van.
11. Click Submit.
12. Click the new row on the tab, and look at the details. Then open the gear and Download PDF.
13. Go back to the tab, open any Draft row, then click Edit.
14. Open the same Create form at phone width (about 375 px) and repeat steps 3 to 4.
15. On the customer portal (the user form), start a new Sample Request, pick a registered project in
    "Registered project (optional)", then clear it again.

## Expected
- Step 3 (F1, as the price tag request): "Customer & project" is open; Ship to, Items and Delivery
  are collapsed, each with its one-line summary. Buttons: "Save as draft" and a blue "Submit"; no
  Preview anywhere.
- Step 4 (F2): Project is fixed text on ONE line, cut with "...", not a block of several lines.
  "Requested by" shows your name, and "Date" is its own field; no "You", no " · ".
- Step 5 (F2): the hover shows the whole "code - title"; the eye opens a popup titled "Project"
  with the whole title.
- Step 6 (F3): picking the customer opens Ship to by itself.
- Step 7 (F3): picking PARTY fills the Shipping address and opens Items by itself, unless you
  collapsed Items by hand in this step, in which case it stays collapsed.
- Step 8 (F3): picking the product opens Delivery by itself.
- Step 9 (F4): Contact person reads "Name A & Name B", and the line under it shows BOTH phones,
  in the same order ("Phone 012-345 6789 & 011-1111 1111"). If one of the two has no phone, the
  line names whose phone it is ("Phone Name B 011-1111 1111").
- Step 10: the date picker is DD/MM/YYYY. A mouse click on Deliver-Van selects it.
- Step 11: no popup. You land on PROJECT's Samples Request tab with exactly ONE toast, "Sample
  request SR-xxxxxx submitted".
- Step 12 (F4): the details' site contact shows both names and both phones; so does the PDF.
- Step 13: the Edit form opens the sections that hold values (here all four), Project is one
  clamped line, and both phones show under Contact person.
- Step 14: nothing runs past the screen edge; Items lines stack (Qty and Remark under the item),
  and the Project is one clamped line.
- Step 15 (F2): with a registered project picked, the portal shows the project once, in the
  picker ("code - title"), and no second "Project Title" field; cleared, the "Project Title" box
  is back for a project not registered yet. This is the CRM form's rule.
