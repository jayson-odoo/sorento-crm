# Hand test: PR #1860 PHOTO-FIRST-LOOKAHEAD (a photo sent with a text is answered first), PROD phone test

Owner, 25 Oct: "3 yeap test in prod". This is the owner's phone test on production AFTER the release that carries
#1860; there is no test copy for it. Prod 25 Oct 18:29: a photo and "check stock" sent together were answered text
first, because WhatsApp sends a photo only once it has uploaded, so the text overtakes it. On this release a photo that
is already queued when the text is about to reply is answered first, then the text: two messages, photo first.

## URL
Production, the owner's own WhatsApp test contact (no CRM URL; the reply arrives on the phone).

## Setup
1. The release that carries #1860 is deployed (crew confirms the deployed commit contains this PR's head).
2. The owner's own test contact on WhatsApp, with photos allowed for it (as for the 18:29 test). No other contact.
3. Pick a product photo the chatbot can read (a product or price tag photo, as in the 18:29 test).

## Steps
1. On the phone, in the chat with the business number, send the photo.
2. Within 1 second of tapping send on the photo, send the text `check stock`.
3. Wait until both replies have arrived (about 10 to 20 seconds).
4. Crew: on prod, read the two newest turns of the owner's test contact:
   `SELECT message_id, created_at, finished_at, status FROM chatbot.turns WHERE contact_respond_id = '<owner test contact id>' ORDER BY created_at DESC LIMIT 2;`
   and the order of the two sender POSTs (`integration_log`, channel of the chatbot sender, same two turn ids).

## Expected
- Step 3: two separate replies. The FIRST is the photo's reply (what was read off the photo), the SECOND is the
  "check stock" reply.
- Step 4: both rows `done`; the photo's turn has the earlier `finished_at` and its sender POST went first, even though
  the photo row may have the later `created_at` (it was queued while the text was running).
- Text first is NOT a failure in one case, and step 4 tells it apart: if the photo's `created_at` is LATER than the
  text's `finished_at`, the photo was queued only after the text had already replied (its upload took longer than the
  text's turn), and nothing may wait for it, so text first is correct. Retry, sending the text faster. If the photo's
  `created_at` is EARLIER than the text's `finished_at` and the text still went first, that is a failure.
- A photo that cannot be read: if respond.io stamped the text earlier than the photo (as at 18:29), the text is
  answered about 60 to 90 s after the photo was queued (60 s plus one photo-read poll) without waiting any longer, and
  the photo's reply follows. A text stamped after the photo waits behind it in send order, as before this PR.
