# Hand test: PR #1983 QUOTE-DRAWING-ESCALATE (escalate a missing technical drawing or photo from a quotation line)

On a quotation line whose product has no technical drawing, the Drawing cell offers Escalate; where
the product has no photo at all, the Photo cell does. Each opens ONE SLA task per product and kind
for the marketing product team of the general-enquiries route (the same team and brand rule the
WhatsApp bot uses for product attachment asks). The task shows on that team's home Dashboards, "My
pending tasks", with Respond and then Resolve. Resolving tells every salesperson who escalated it.
Email and WhatsApp go out at each hand-off on the recipient's own notification settings. A product
whose drawing is a PDF counts as having a drawing.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
None. It uses the existing general-enquiries marketing product team, members, brand tags and SLA
policy. Nothing on the copy may send a real WhatsApp or email: crew keeps the copy's Respond.io and
SMTP credentials pointed at nothing, so deliveries stay pending.

Pick a project quotation in draft whose lines include: a product with a drawing image, a product
with no drawing at all, a product with no photo at all, and (if one exists on the copy) a product
whose only Technical Specifications file is a PDF, e.g. one of the SRTWT codes. Note one member of
the marketing product team (the assignee; their role must hold the normal SLA resolve permission, as
for any SLA ticket) and one other salesperson who can open the same quotation.

## Steps
1. Open the quotation from Setup (Project Sales > the project > Quotations > the quotation) and click the `Lines` tab.
2. Look at the Drawing and Photo columns.
3. Click `Escalate` on the line whose product has no drawing, then `Escalate` in the Photo column of the line whose product has no photo.
4. Hover each tile.
5. Sign in as the other salesperson, open the same quotation, and click the line's `Escalated` tile (Drawing column).
6. Sign in as the assignee. Open the home Dashboards page and look at "My pending tasks".
7. Click `Respond` on the `Product drawing` row (and on the `Product photo` row).
8. Go back to the quotation and look at those lines' Drawing and Photo tiles.
9. Back on Dashboards, click `Resolve` on the same rows.
10. Sign in as the first salesperson and open the notification bell.
11. Open the quotation again and look at those lines' Drawing and Photo tiles.

## Expected
1. The quotation opens on its lines.
2. The product with a drawing image shows its thumbnail; the PDF-only product shows a `PDF` tile that
   opens the PDF in a new tab; the product with no drawing shows a dashed `Escalate` tile; the
   product with no photo shows the same tile in the Photo column (a product with photos but none
   chosen shows today's photo cell); a typed (off-catalogue) line shows `-`. No other new banner, badge or button anywhere on the page.
3. A toast `Escalated: you will be told when the request is resolved`; the tile turns into
   `Escalated` with today's date.
4. `Escalated <date and time> by 1 person. Due <date>.` (the due date follows the SLA policy).
5. A toast `Already escalated: added you to the request`; the tile reads `Escalated`, no second
   task opens, and the hover now says `by 2 people`.
6. Rows `Product drawing · <product code>` and `Product photo · <product code>`, `Tier 1 · Respond`, `Respond by: <date>`, with a
   `Respond` button (no Resolve yet). Clicking the row's title opens the product's page.
7. The row now reads `Tier 1 · Resolve` with a `Resolve` button.
8. The tile reads `Replied` with today's date.
9. The row leaves "My pending tasks".
10. `Product drawing request for <product code> resolved` (and the Product photo one); clicking it opens the quotation. The other
    salesperson gets the same notice.
11. Still `Escalate` (nothing was uploaded), so it can be escalated again; if a file was uploaded to the
    product in between, the thumbnail or PDF tile shows instead.
