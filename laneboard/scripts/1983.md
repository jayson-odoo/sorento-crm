# Hand test: PR #1983 QUOTE-DRAWING-ESCALATE (escalate a missing technical drawing from a quotation line)

On a quotation line whose product has no technical drawing, the Drawing cell offers Escalate. It
opens ONE SLA task per product for the team set up in Form SLA Configuration. The task shows on that
team's home Dashboards, "My pending tasks", with Respond and then Resolve. Resolving tells every
salesperson who escalated it. Email and WhatsApp go out at each hand-off on the recipient's own
notification settings. A product whose drawing is a PDF counts as having a drawing.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
Crew prepares the copy. Nothing on the copy may send a real WhatsApp or email: crew keeps the
copy's Respond.io and SMTP credentials pointed at nothing, so deliveries stay pending.

1. Sign in as the owner.
2. Sidebar: Users & Access > AI Agents. Add an agent named `Product drawings`, code
   `product_drawings` (its own code: do NOT reuse the WhatsApp bot's agent). On it, add a tier-1 team
   under team set `marketing_product` (the marketing product team) and pick an SLA policy.
3. Sidebar: SLA > Form SLA Configuration. Click Add. Form type `Product Drawing`. Check that Team set
   is pre-filled `marketing_product`. Agent code `product_drawings`, the same SLA policy, Start
   event `escalate`, Respond events `respond`, Resolve events `resolve`. Save.
4. Pick a project quotation in draft whose lines include: a product with a drawing image, a product
   with no drawing at all, and (if one exists on the copy) a product whose only Technical
   Specifications file is a PDF, e.g. one of the SRTWT codes. Note one member of the marketing
   product team (the assignee) and one other salesperson who can open the same quotation.

## Steps
1. Open the quotation from step 4 of Setup (Project Sales > the project > Quotations > the quotation).
2. Look at the Drawing column.
3. Click `Escalate` on the line whose product has no drawing.
4. Hover the tile.
5. Sign in as the other salesperson, open the same quotation, and click the line's `Escalated` tile.
6. Sign in as the assignee. Open the home Dashboards page and look at "My pending tasks".
7. Click `Respond` on the `Product drawing` row.
8. Go back to the quotation and look at that line's Drawing tile.
9. Back on Dashboards, click `Resolve` on the same row.
10. Sign in as the first salesperson and open the notification bell.
11. Open the quotation again and look at that line's Drawing tile.

## Expected
1. The quotation opens on its lines.
2. The product with a drawing image shows its thumbnail; the PDF-only product shows a `PDF` tile that
   opens the PDF in a new tab; the product with no drawing shows a dashed `Escalate` tile; a typed
   (off-catalogue) line shows `-`. No other new banner, badge or button anywhere on the page.
3. A toast `Escalated: you will be told when the request is resolved`; the tile turns into
   `Escalated` with today's date.
4. `Escalated <date and time> by 1 person. Due <date>.` (the due date follows the SLA policy).
5. A toast `Already escalated: added you to the request`; the tile reads `Escalated`, no second
   task opens, and the hover now says `by 2 people`.
6. A row `Product drawing · <product code>`, `Tier 1 · Respond`, `Respond by: <date>`, with a
   `Respond` button (no Resolve yet). Clicking the row's title opens the product's page.
7. The row now reads `Tier 1 · Resolve` with a `Resolve` button.
8. The tile reads `Replied` with today's date.
9. The row leaves "My pending tasks".
10. `Drawing request for <product code> resolved`; clicking it opens the quotation. The other
    salesperson gets the same notice.
11. Still `Escalate` (no drawing was uploaded), so it can be escalated again; if a drawing image or PDF
    was uploaded to the product in between, the thumbnail or PDF tile shows instead.
