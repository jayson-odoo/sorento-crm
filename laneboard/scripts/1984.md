# Hand test: PR #1984 QUOTE-CUSTOMER-AMEND (customers suggest qty / price changes on the quotation link)

Owner meeting 29 Oct + answers 30 Oct, mock v3 approved 30 Oct 10:28. On the quotation link, a customer
suggests a new quantity or unit price per line, or removes a line, and sends one request with an optional
note. They cannot sign while it is open. The salesperson is told (bell, email, WhatsApp), decides each
suggestion in the quotation's own Lines grid, and Apply opens the next revision as a draft. A price under
the minimum still needs a manager before it can be sent. Migration: two new tables (see Setup 1).

## Setup
1. Apply the migration SQL from the PR's `crew-migration` comment
   (`documentation/plans/project-sales/quote-customer-amend-29oct-migration.sql`). Check:
   `SELECT to_regclass('projects.quotation_amendment_requests'), to_regclass('projects.quotation_amendment_request_rows');`
   returns two names, not NULL.
2. Run the worker if you want to see the email and WhatsApp deliveries go out; the bell needs no worker.
3. Sign in as a salesperson who owns a project (or as admin), with "WhatsApp notices" on in their profile.
4. Pick a quotation of that project whose LATEST revision is issued and not accepted, with at least 3 priced
   lines. Call it Q, its revision R1, and note:
   - line A: the first priced line (its qty and unit price);
   - line B: another priced line;
   - line C: a third line;
   - the minimum price of line A, shown on its Rate cell in the Lines tab ("Minimum price RM x"). If line A
     shows none, pick another line with a minimum as line A.

## Steps
1. From the sidebar, open Project Sales, open Q's project, then open Q.
2. Take R1's customer link, the one sent to the customer when R1 was issued (the same link used in round 1).
   Do not press "Send to Customer" again: that issues a new revision and replaces R1.
3. Open the link in a private window.
4. Click "Suggest changes" (top right of the Quotation card), then open the first section.
5. On line A, type 0 into Quantity.
6. On line A, type its quantity minus 1 into Quantity.
7. On line A, type a price RM 1.00 below its minimum into Unit rate.
8. On line B, type its unit price minus 5.00 into Unit rate.
9. On line C, click the bin icon.
10. Click "Send suggestions", then "Cancel" in the dialog.
11. Click "Send suggestions" again, type "Please deliver in two batches." into "Anything else (optional)",
    and click "Send".
12. Narrow the window to 375px wide, scroll the page, then widen it back.
13. Look for "Suggest changes", the name field and the signature pad.
14. In the CRM window, open the bell.
15. Open Q, Lines tab.
16. Untick "Suggested lines only", then tick it again.
17. On line A, click Accept on Qty and Accept on Rate.
18. On line B, click Reject on Rate, type "Already at the project price." and press Enter.
19. On line C, click Accept on the removal.
20. Click "Apply decisions".
21. Reload the customer link in the private window.
22. Back in the CRM, on R2 click "Send for approval", approve it as a manager, then issue R2.
23. Reload R1's customer link.

## Expected
- Step 2: the link opens the sign page with the "Prepared by" card, the terms tick and no location prompt.
- Step 4: "Suggest changes" is the only such button and sits in the Quotation card's header (no "Not ready
  to sign this?" row under the signature). A bar with only "Discard" and "Send suggestions" appears at the
  right; no count, no totals, and no note box on the page. The table keeps its own columns (Item, Technical spec, Description, Brand, Product code, Qty, Unit
  rate, Complete set, Amount) plus one narrow icon column. Qty and Unit rate become inputs; a line priced at
  nothing (a set companion) has no Unit rate input. Description and code are not editable.
- Step 5: a message containing "at least 1" shows and Send is disabled.
- Steps 6-9: each changed cell turns amber with the quoted value under it. Line A's Amount, the section
  total in its header and TOTAL AMOUNT each show the new figure with "quoted RM x" (on one line) beneath.
  Line C is struck through, its inputs lock, the bin becomes a restore arrow, and its amount drops out of
  the section total and TOTAL AMOUNT. Lines and sections you did not touch show no "quoted" text.
- Step 10: a dialog "Send your suggestions" opens; Cancel closes it, nothing is sent, and your edits stay.
- Step 11: the dialog closes and your suggestions show in their cells. Under "Prepared by" there is only the
  banner "Your suggestions are with Sorento" with the time sent: no "Your name", no signature pad, no terms
  tick and no "Sign and accept".
- Step 12: at 375px the table scrolls sideways inside its card; the page itself never scrolls sideways and
  nothing is clipped.
- Step 13: none of them is on the page; nothing can be sent or signed until Sorento answers.
- Step 14: one notice "...customer suggested changes on <Q's reference>..." naming 3 lines. With the worker
  running, an email and a WhatsApp message to the salesperson follow.
- Step 15: no new tab, and no old "The customer asked for changes" banner with a "Revise this quotation"
  button. The Lines grid keeps its columns (#, Photo, Drawing, Product, Description, Brand, Qty,
  UOM, Rate (RM), Total (RM)). A summary row above it shows the note, "4 suggestions on 3 lines", the totals
  and "Apply decisions". Line A's Qty and Rate cells each show the quoted value struck through, the
  suggestion, and Accept / Reject; its Rate cell shows "Minimum price RM <floor>". Line C's removal sits in
  the last (row actions) cell.
- Step 16: unticked, every line shows; ticked, only A, B and C, with one row "<n> lines without suggestions".
- Step 18: the reason box opens in line B's Rate cell and the decision is saved.
- Step 20: a new revision (R2) opens as a draft. Line A carries the new qty and price and is flagged below
  the minimum, line B keeps its price, line C is gone, and the total matches. A manager's approval is asked
  for.
- Step 21: each suggestion shows its answer in its cell: line A Accepted (twice) with Amount = new qty x new
  price, line B "Not accepted" with "Already at the project price." and its old amount, line C "Removal
  accepted" with no amount. R1 can no longer be signed.
- Step 22: R2 can only be sent for approval at first ("One line is priced below its floor..."); after the
  manager approves, it issues.
- Step 23: R1's link reads "This quotation has been replaced by Quotation No. <Q>-R2." and offers nothing to do.
