three times, then the "asked 3 times with no answer" staff note. Every tap was refused: Respond.io sends
`channel.meta` as a JSON string wrapping a nested `meta`, and the CRM never found `wa_id` in it.

4. Fresh fake contact B taps the same way, but the meta string has NO `wa_id` (only `user_id`).
5. Fresh fake contact C hand-shares a card: origin `other`, same prod-shaped meta.
6. Fresh fake contact D taps with a meta string whose `wa_id` differs from the card phone.
7. Open Chat History from the sidebar, find contacts A to D, open each card turn and read its trace line.
