# PR 2357 hand test: dates read and type as dd/mm/yyyy (DATE-PICKER-UNIFY slice A)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Sign in as an admin.
- Have a purchase request that was submitted between midnight and 8:00 am Malaysia time (any day), one project with a quotation document, and some Chat History turns in the last two days.
- Set the browser language to English (United States) for step 1 if you can (it shows the old mm/dd/yyyy problem most clearly).

## Steps
1. Open Project Sales, open the project, open its quotation document, click Edit.
2. In the Date field, select the text, type 5/11/2026 and press Tab.
3. Save, then reload the page.
4. Click Edit again and click the calendar button beside the Date field.
5. Open Procurement > Purchase Requests and find the request submitted before 8:00 am.
6. Open that request.
7. Click Edit on the request document and look at the two date fields.
8. In "Expected date of delivery" type 05/11/2026 and press Tab; save; reload.
9. Open System > Chat History and click Filters.
10. Look at the Date range field.
11. Select its text, type 01/11/2026 - 12/11/2026 and press Enter.
12. Type 31/02/2026 - 01/03/2026 and press Enter.
13. Type 05/11/2026 - and press Enter.
14. Empty the field and press Enter.
15. Open a project whose Activities & notes has an entry from the last few hours (adding a note currently fails on main with a server error, not this lane) and look at its age.
16. Open SLA Management > Conversation SLA Tracking and open any row.
17. Repeat steps 9-11 on a phone-width window (375 px).

## Expected
1. The quotation page opens in edit mode.
2. The field reads 05/11/2026.
3. After reload the Date reads 05/11/2026 (not 11/05/2026, not 04/11/2026).
4. The calendar opens on November 2026 with the 5th selected, fully on screen.
5. The list's "Submitted date" for that request is its Malaysia calendar day (the day it was submitted in Malaysia, not the previous day).
6. The detail's "Submitted date" reads the same day as the list.
7. Both date fields are a text box reading dd/mm/yyyy with a calendar button beside it; no browser date control.
8. After reload "Expected date of delivery" reads 05/11/2026.
9. The Filters panel opens.
10. ONE "Date range" field (not two From/To boxes), reading yesterday - today, e.g. 11/11/2026 - 12/11/2026; its calendar button sits fully inside the panel.
11. The list shows turns from 01/11/2026 00:00 to 12/11/2026 23:59 Malaysia time; the field reads 01/11/2026 - 12/11/2026.
12. Refused: the field goes back to 01/11/2026 - 12/11/2026 and the list does not change.
13. The field reads 05/11/2026 - and the list shows turns from 05/11/2026 onwards.
14. The field goes back to yesterday - today (e.g. 11/11/2026 - 12/11/2026, the same as on load) and the list matches that range.
15. The age matches the real time since the entry (e.g. "20m ago"), not 8 hours more.
16. Every date-time reads dd/mm/yyyy, h:mm am/pm in Malaysia time (an event at 23:30 UTC reads 7:30 am the next day).
17. Same as 10-11; nothing is cut off; the calendar button is fully visible.
