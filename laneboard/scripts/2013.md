# Hand test: PR #2013 CHATBOT-SET-STOCK (a set or catalogue code answers every part)

Your salesperson asked "Pls check stock srtwc7405 x 6 sets" and the bot answered the seat cover only. Now a
catalogue code (SRTWC7405, SRTWC8826) or a set code answers every set it names, each under its own "Set <code>"
heading with its tank (X), bowl (Y) and seat cover (SC), in the same stock format as a product code. No prompt
change: the resolver finds the sets.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
1. The copy runs the PR head (no migration in this PR) with a Gemini key in the AI assistant settings.
2. Sign in as the owner (admin).
3. A staff contact that sees stock numbers, and a contact on "Availability only" (crew names them).

## Steps
1. CRM sidebar: System > Messaging > Chatbot Console. Pick the staff contact.
2. Send: `Pls check stock srtwc7405 x 6 sets`
3. Send: `stock srtwc8826`
4. Send: `ada stok srtwc8608 3 set?`
5. Send: `stock srtwc7405 and srtwc8826`
6. Send: `stock SRTWC7405-SC`
7. Pick the "Availability only" contact and send: `srtwc8826 x 6 sets`

Stock numbers vary with the copy's data; check that the parts appear. The numbers below are the crew copy's on
30 Oct.

1. The staff contact's conversation.
2. Nine headings: Set SRTWC7405-300-RL-PJ, -P, -P-PJ, -RL-P-PJ, -RL-S-PJ, -S, -S-289UF-PJ, -S-PJ, -S-UOA. Under
   each, its tank (SRTWCX7405-...), bowl (SRTWCY7405, -PJ or -UOA) and seat cover (SRTWC7405-SC or -SC-UOA): 27
   product blocks. On the crew copy: SRTWC7405-SC Total 2086, SRTWCY7405-PJ Total 1431, SRTWCX7405-RL-S-PJ Total
   1429. The seat cover repeats under each set. The reply arrives in more than one WhatsApp part.
3. Set SRTWC8826-P with SRTWC8826-SC (Total 654), SRTWCX8826-P (Total 17) and SRTWCY8826 (Total 405); then Set
   SRTWC8826-S with SRTWC8826-SC, SRTWCX8826-S (Total 330) and SRTWCY8826.
4. A Malay reply ("Kod Produk", "Jumlah") with five headings: SRTWC8608-P-RL, -RL, -RL-200, -RL-WEPLS, -S-RL-UF,
   each with its X, Y and SC part.
5. No "Which one do you mean" and no "(product set)". The sets and parts of both families, as in steps 2 and 3.
6. Only SRTWC7405-SC and SRTWC7405-SC-UOA, no set heading.
7. The got-stock line for each part of both 8826 sets, no numbers.
