# Hand test #1406 (ESCALATION-CONTROL)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Apply the latest crew-migration SQL from PR #1406 (it makes `respond_contacts.escalation_allowed` NOT NULL DEFAULT true and sets every contact to allowed; the earlier access-type column, if a copy has it, is no longer read).
2. Sign in as a user with `user_management.contacts.edit` (an admin is fine).
3. Pick two contacts on the shared dev DB: DEALER (any contact holding a dealer access type, e.g. Mocha Dealer) and Mr Loo (respond 487555417). Both read allowed after the migration.
4. Use System Management > Chatbot Console in dry run (no sends). Note one order number and one product code that do NOT exist (e.g. `SO999001`, `ZZTNOPE9`).

## Steps

1. From the sidebar open Users & Access > People > Internal Users, open DEALER, open the Chatbot tab. Read the "Can escalate to a person" switch.
2. On the Chatbot Console, as DEALER, ask for order `SO999001`.
3. On DEALER's Chatbot tab, untick "Can escalate to a person". Reload the page and read the switch.
4. On the Chatbot Console, as DEALER, ask for order `SO999001` again.
5. As DEALER, ask for product `ZZTNOPE9`.
6. As DEALER, send "can I talk to a human".
7. Tick DEALER's switch again. As DEALER, ask for product `ZZTNOPE9`, then reply "yes" to the offer.
8. Open Mr Loo's Chatbot tab and read the switch. As Mr Loo, send "can I talk to a human".

## Expected

1. Step 1: the switch is on (checked). There is no "Inherited" line and no access-type wording.
2. Step 2: `Couldn't find: "SO999001" (order). Would you like me to escalate to customer service team?` (unchanged behaviour while the switch is on). These exact texts were measured on an empty sandbox database; if the dev data answers with a did-you-mean list instead, the blocked replies in steps 4 and 5 keep that list and end with `Please refer to your salesman.` in place of the escalate question.
3. Step 3: after the reload the switch stays off.
4. Step 4: exactly `Couldn't find: "SO999001" (order). Please refer to your salesman.` No escalation question is left open (the console's open question is empty or not an escalation), no "Please choose who to route to" list.
5. Step 5: exactly `Couldn't find: "ZZTNOPE9" (product). Please refer to your salesman.` (on a code close to a real one the reply is "Here's what you want: ... But no master products matched these. Please refer to your salesman.").
6. Step 6: the reply is `Please refer to your salesman.`; the trace shows branch kind out_of_scope, lane escalation_barred, routed line "Escalation withheld: ...", no assignee and no SLA row.
7. Step 7: the miss offers "Would you like me to escalate to marketing product team?" again and the "yes" is handed over to a PIC (routed message, an assignee in the trace).
8. Step 8: Mr Loo's switch is on; his "talk to a human" is handed over to a PIC as before (his dealer access types do not block him).
