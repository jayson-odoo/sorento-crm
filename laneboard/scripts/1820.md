## Setup

1. Sign in as Teh Jayson (the E2E admin account).
2. No migration. Use an order shaped like SO426513: one SO with two lines of the same item at the same location (SO426513 itself: C-FH14 x2 at BRW-ED), both saved on the planning board but not confirmed, so the board shows Confirm (1) or more and the order reads "Saved, not confirmed". If SO426513 is already confirmed, pick any other order with two lines of one item at one location and save a decision on both lines first.
3. Do not press the final "Confirm 1 order" button (it sends the email). Cancel the dialog when done.

## Steps

1. From `/`, open the planning board from the sidebar and load the batch that holds the order.
2. Press Confirm (N) on the board. The "Confirm SO<n>" dialog opens on step 1, "Review split".
3. Without touching anything, look at the footer and the Save / Undo buttons above the sheet.
4. Look at the sheet's column headers, left to right.
5. Look at the first column of every row.
6. Press and drag on a row of the first line (anywhere left of the item code) a little, then release.
7. On the header row, point at the right edge of the QTY header until the cursor becomes a column-resize cursor, then drag it about 60px to the right and release.
8. Drag the right edge of BREAKDOWN DATE about 60px to the left and release.
9. Click the first line's BREAKDOWN QTY cell and type a smaller number (e.g. 15 instead of 20). Click somewhere outside the sheet.
10. Press Undo.
11. Narrow the window to phone width (about 375px) and repeat steps 3 to 7.
12. Press Cancel.

## Expected

- Step 3: Save and Undo are greyed out, "Next: Confirm" is enabled, and there is NO "Save or undo your split changes first" line in the footer.
- Step 4: SO DATE, S/O NO, ITEM CODE, LOCATION, QTY, NEW QTY, DELIVERY DATE, NEW DATE, BREAKDOWN QTY, BREAKDOWN DATE, REMARK, BALANCE. No "CHANGE QUANTITY" or "CHANGE DATE".
- Step 5: no grip / drag handle column; every row starts with the SO date (on a phone, with the item code).
- Step 6: nothing moves, no "Moved 20 to ..." toast, Save and Undo stay greyed out.
- Step 7: the QTY column gets wider while dragging and stays wider after release; REMARK never gets narrower than about 100px: past that the sheet grows and scrolls sideways.
- Step 8: the BREAKDOWN DATE column gets narrower and stays narrower.
- Step 9: Undo becomes active (Save stays greyed out while the line reads "5 left", as before: an unbalanced line cannot be saved), "Next: Confirm" turns disabled, and "Save or undo your split changes first" appears in the footer; BALANCE on that line reads "5 left" as plain text (not draggable).
- Step 10: the value goes back to 20, Undo greys out, "Next: Confirm" is enabled again and the footer line disappears.
- Step 11: same as steps 3 to 7; the item code column stays pinned at the left edge while the sheet scrolls sideways.
- Step 12: the dialog closes and nothing was sent.
- The board gear's "Split delivery" dialog (not part of this lane) still has its drag grips.
