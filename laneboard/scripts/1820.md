## Steps

1. From `/`, open the planning board from the sidebar and load the batch that holds the order.
2. Press Confirm (N) on the board. The "Confirm SO<n>" dialog opens on step 1, "Review split".
3. Without touching anything, look at the footer and the Save / Undo buttons above the sheet.
4. Look at the sheet's column headers, left to right.
5. Look at the first column of every row.
6. Press and drag on a row of the first line (anywhere left of the item code) a little, then release.
7. On the header row, point at the right edge of the QTY header until the cursor becomes a column-resize cursor, then drag it about 60px to the right and release.
8. Drag the right edge of BREAKDOWN DATE about 60px to the left and release.
9. Click the first line's BREAKDOWN QTY cell and type a smaller number (e.g. 15 instead of 20). Click somewhere outside the sheet.
10. Press Undo.
11. Narrow the window to phone width (about 375px) and repeat steps 3 to 7.
12. Back at full width, press Cancel, then open SO427002 (3 lines CSK14B-NL) the same way: press Confirm (N) so its "Review split" page opens for the FIRST time this session.
13. Without touching anything, look at the SO DATE column of every row (including any second table below the first).
14. Change one Breakdown qty, then press Undo. Look at the SO DATE column again.
15. Press "Next: Confirm" to reach step 2, "Confirm". Look at the footer.
16. Narrow the window to phone width (about 375px) and look at the footer again.
17. Press Back, then press Cancel.
18. Reload the page so nothing is cached. On the board, load SO426939 (3 lines SRTWC8613-RL / SRTWT907SS / WESERP10B at BRW-BB, "Saved, not confirmed"; if it is already being planned, pick any order that has never been planned), press Confirm (N) ONCE, and watch the Review split page as it opens. Do not press Back.
19. Look at the SO DATE column of every row and at the toolbar above the sheet.
20. Press "+ Split" on the first line, then Undo.
21. Narrow the window to phone width (about 375px). Look at the page header beside the SO number.
22. Repeat steps 18 to 21 for SO427002 (it opened editable before; it must still).
23. Press Cancel.
