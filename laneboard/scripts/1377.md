# Hand test: DataGrid pill cells fold to one line with a "+N" popover (PR #1377)

## URL

crew test copy

## Setup

1. Sign in as an admin (superadmin or a user with `user_management.users.view`,
   `user_management.contacts.view` and `projects.order_inquiries.view`).
2. Have on the dev DB: an administrative user with three or more roles (Mr Loo), an internal
   user (contact) with five or more access types, and an order inquiry whose Lines tab has a
   line placed on two or more SPOs (a "+1" or "+4" in the SPO column).
3. Use a 1280px wide window first, then repeat step 6 at 375px.

## Steps

1. From the sidebar open Users & Access > People > Administrative Users.
2. Find Mr Loo's row and look at the Roles column.
3. Click the "+N" chip in that Roles cell.
4. Press Escape.
5. Tab to the "+N" chip with the keyboard and press Enter, then press Escape; press Space,
   then press Escape.
6. Drag the Roles column border wider, then narrower.
7. From the sidebar open Users & Access > People > Internal Users.
8. Find the contact with many access types and look at the Access types column.
9. Click the "+N" chip in that Access types cell, then press Escape.
10. From the sidebar open Project Sales > Order Inquiries, open the order inquiry from Setup
    step 2, and open its Lines tab.
11. In the SPO column click the "+N" chip on a line that is NOT the last row of the grid.
12. Press Escape.
13. Repeat step 11 on the PO column if a line shows "+N" there.

## Expected

1. The Administrative Users list renders; every row is one line tall.
2. Mr Loo's Roles cell reads on ONE line: the first role(s) that fit (at least
   "Purchasing Manager Role"), then a "+N" chip where N = roles not shown (for three roles
   with one shown: "+2"). No role wraps under another; the row is no taller than a one-role
   row.
3. A popover opens on top of the list, listing every role of Mr Loo one per line
   ("Purchasing Manager Role", "Purchasing Executive", "Purchasing"). No neighbouring row
   or chip shows through it.
4. The popover closes. Nothing else on the page changed (no row opened).
5. Enter opens the same popover, Escape closes it; Space opens it, Escape closes it. The
   chip shows a focus ring when focused.
6. Wider: more roles show inline and N shrinks (to no chip once all fit). Narrower: roles
   fold back behind "+N" live, without a reload. The row stays one line.
7. The Internal Users list renders; every row is one line tall.
8. The Access types cell reads on ONE line: the types that fit (at least "Sorento Office"
   or whichever is first), then "+N" (seven types with one shown: "+6").
9. The popover lists every access type one per line ("Sorento Office", "Sorento Dealer",
   "Mocha Dealer", "Mocha Office", "Cabana Office", "Cabana Dealer", "End User"), on top
   of the list; Escape closes it.
10. The Lines tab renders with the SPO column as before: first SPO number as a link, then
    "+N".
11. The "+N" popover renders ON TOP of the rows beneath it: the next row's own "+N" chip and
    text are fully covered, nothing overlaps. Every SPO of the line is listed as a link.
12. The popover closes.
13. Same as 11 and 12 for the PO column.
