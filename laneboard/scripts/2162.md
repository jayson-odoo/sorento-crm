# Hand test: PR #2162 TARGET-INVOICED (sales targets can count Invoiced; Accounts in read mode)

A sales target can count Invoiced: invoices + cash sales + debit notes - credit notes, net of tax,
by document date, cancelled documents left out (the same figure as the billing documents). New
targets start on Invoiced; saved targets keep their basis. An Invoiced dealer figure is final, so
it is never marked "provisional, incl. tax". The read-only Details of a dealer target now shows
its Accounts, the same as Edit.

crew test copy

## Setup
- The copy runs on the dev database `sorento_dev_1028`, which holds the billing documents; the
  basis CHECK there already allows 'invoiced' (crew-migration on the PR).
- Signed in as a user with sales.targets.view and sales.targets.edit, window about 1280 px wide.
- Target TGT-000005 "4 SMART PLUS 2026 quarterly" (TGT-000008 works the same way).
- Before step 6, open Billing Documents in the sidebar (Finance), filter to each of the target's
  accounts, dates 1 Jan to 31 Mar 2026, and note invoices + cash sales + debit notes - credit
  notes, net of tax, cancelled documents left out. That is the expected Q1 figure.

## Steps
1. From `/`, open the sidebar Sales > Targets, then the Dealers tab.
2. Click TGT-000005.
3. On Details, look at the Target section.
4. Click the gear, then Edit.
5. Under What counts, open Counts.
6. Pick Invoiced and click Save.
7. Open the Periods tab.
8. Open the Accounts tab.
9. Click Back to targets, then open the Dealers tab.
10. Click Set target, then pick Dealer.
11. Under What counts, look at Counts.
12. Click Cancel.
