# Hand test #1718 (SR-CORE Sample Request, form v4)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Done by crew: the v4 `crew-migration:` SQL is applied to the shared dev DB, and the backend has seeded the six Sample Request statuses (Draft, Submitted, Processing, Customer confirmed, Delivered, Cancelled) and the three permissions. Nothing to run.
2. Sign in as yourself (superadmin). The admin roles hold `projects.sample_requests.view`, `.create` and `.process`. Salespeople (holders of `projects.projects.edit`) hold `.create` only and see only their own requests.
3. Pick one real registered project in Project Sales > Projects. If one exists, use a project whose customer is `300-J099`. These steps call it "the project".
4. Done by crew, after tonight's refresh of the shared dev DB to the 15 Oct prod copy: crew picks a salesperson's portal contact that is linked to a sales agent with assigned customers, switches on "Sample Request" in User Management > Contacts > that contact > Portal forms (it is off for every contact until switched on), and mints that contact's portal link. The contact must be agent-linked: the Customer Name list is the agent's own customers, and for a contact with no sales agent it is empty (`price_tag_request_service.py` `lookup_debtors_for_agent` returns no rows), so only a typed name would work.

## Steps

1. From `/`, open Project Sales > Projects in the sidebar, open the project, click the "Sample requests" tab, then click "Create".
2. Compare the form with the mock you approved.
3. In "Customer Name", type part of a debtor code or name and pick a customer from the list.
4. In row 2 of "Line items", type `tpe9101` in Item Code and pick TPE-9101. Click "Add row", type `cb2829ss-cr` in the new row, and read the message. Clear that row.
5. Tick "Mount & label sample board" in row 1 and type a board label.
6. Fill Ship To, choose a Delivery, leave "Request Delivery Date" empty, and click "Submit".
7. Open the calendar in "Request Delivery Date", pick a date next week, and click "Submit".
8. Open Project Sales > Sample Requests in the sidebar and click the new request. Then click Edit, look, and Cancel.
9. Click "Change to processing". Gear menu: "Set consignment / PO no", type CSGN-TEST / PO-TEST and save.
10. Click "Customer confirmed", pick today in the calendar, and confirm. Then "Mark delivered", pick today, and confirm.
11. Gear menu: "Print / Download PDF".
12. Create a second request, add one line, click "Save as draft", then gear menu "Delete draft". Wait for the countdown to end.
13. Portal, own link: open the contact's portal link on a phone and pick "Sample Request". Click "New". In "Customer Name", type part of one of the salesperson's own customers and pick it. Fill the rest and click "Submit". Open the submitted request.
14. Portal, impersonation: open the portal as that contact the way you did on the last hand test (the `/portal` path with no slug), pick "Sample Request", click "New", then go back and open the request from step 13.
15. On the portal request, open the gear and click "Cancel request".

## Expected

1. A full page opens with one document card titled "Consignment Requisition Form". The project is filled in, and so is the customer when the project came from a lead with a customer.
2. Header fields in this order: Sales Person, Date, Customer Name, Project Title, Registered project, For, Request Delivery Date, Ship To, Shipping Address, Contact Person, Contact Phone / Note, then the internal Consignment No and PO No. Products sit in the "Line items" table (No., Item Code, Qty, Remark). Below it: Delivery, Loan Period, Goods Taken From, Purpose. There is no Quotation field, no Alternative column and no Immediate arrangement tick anywhere.
3. The customer list filters as you type, and the picked customer shows its debtor code and name.
4. TPE-9101 is listed although you typed no dash. The second code shows exactly: "CB2829SS-CR is not in the product list. Pick the product you mean, or ask purchasing to add it as a new product before you request it." Nothing is added.
5. A board label field appears next to the tick.
6. Submit is refused and "Request Delivery Date" shows "Enter the Request Delivery Date".
7. The request saves as SR-000001 (or the next number in your company) with status "Submitted". Dates show as dd/mm/yyyy.
8. The page shows the status pill only (no step bar) and prev/next arrows. Edit shows the same fields in the same order as the detail page.
9. The pill reads "Processing". The card shows Consignment No CSGN-TEST and PO No PO-TEST.
10. The pill reads "Customer confirmed", then "Delivered".
11. A PDF opens with "SORENTO SDN BHD" and "CONSIGNMENT REQUISITION FORM" plus the number. Request Delivery Date prints as dd/mm/yyyy. The item table has the columns No, Brand, Model, Qty and Remark, with the board row first and TPE-9101 after it, and no "OR" alternative. The Delivery line ticks your mode, and nothing says Immediate.
12. The Delete draft item turns into a "Deleting in Ns" countdown with a Cancel, and no confirm dialog appears. When the countdown ends, the draft is gone from the list.
13. The portal form shows the same document-card layout, fits the phone screen with no sideways scroll, and has no Quotation, Alternative or Immediate. The Customer Name list shows only that salesperson's own customers, and the picked one submits without a "Pick one of your own customers" refusal. After Submit the request shows "Submitted", and its Created date reads dd/mm/yyyy (for example 16/10/2026, not 10/16/2026).
14. "Sample Request", "New" and the existing request all open. None shows a 404 page.
15. The portal shows "Cancelled", and the staff list shows the same request as Cancelled.
