## Setup

1. Migrate the test copy to head (`ocn_0001_order_change_notice`, after `prr_0001_si_npa_project`).
2. Users (company Sorento, any password):
   - Admin A: Order Change Notices view, create, edit, delete; in the Project Sales Admin team (the notice's admin step).
   - Sales Person A: Order Change Notices view own + create; Projects view (for the project picker); linked to a contact
     (the user's linked contact, as for the other MY REQUESTS forms).
   - Manager A: Order Change Notices view + approve only (no Edit); in the Project Sales Manager team.
   - CS Person A: Order Change Notices view + mark done (process); in the Customer Service team.
   - Project Lead A: a user who can manage project sales orders (projects manage) + Order Change Notices edit.
3. System Settings: Order Change Notice default approver = Manager A. "OCN requires a project" stays OFF.
4. SLA: the migration copies the purchase request's three steps if the copy has them. If the Form SLA Config list
   shows no Order Change Notice rows, add the three steps as on the purchase request (submit -> Project Sales Admin,
   send for approval -> Project Sales Manager, approved -> Customer Service).
5. Data: customers Customer A, Customer B Sdn Bhd; project Saffron Ridge Towers with one PUBLISHED project sales
   order that has a newer version to compare (any existing test project that has one will do); products
   ABC123-RL, ABC123-SH, XYZ456A-SS; a small PDF named customer-request.pdf.
   On the shared dev DB use the lane's ZZT fixture (crew-migration comment on the PR): project ZZT Kestrel Point
   Lofts (stands for Saffron Ridge Towers), ZZT Customer A, PO ZZT-PO-3871 versions 1 and 2, published sales order
   ZZT-PSO-0001, products ZZT-ABC123-RL / ZZT-ABC123-SH / ZZT-XYZ456A-SS.
6. Requested by (Part A, A11): a market segment with "requestor selectable" ticked and one more contact in it
   (Requestor Contact B), the same set-up the purchase request's Requested by uses.
7. Order Change Notice visibility: NOT granted to Sales Person A's contact by any tag or segment before Part A
   (Part A grants it on the contact). For Part B it must be Visible.

## Steps

### Part A: the owner's flow (round 20: the notice as a portal form controlled per contact, one "Requested by")

Setup for Part A: Sales Person A is a CRM user linked to a contact (the contact's own record shows the linked user).
Sales Person A's contact starts with Order Change Notice NOT visible (no tag, segment or contact switch grants it).

A1. Sign in as Admin A. Open Contacts and open Sales Person A's contact.
A2. Look at the contact's Portal forms (form visibility) card. Then open the contact's tag settings card (Portal forms group) and the Market Segments editor's Additional portal forms.
A3. View as Sales Person A (before granting). Look at the sidebar under MY REQUESTS. Stop viewing as.
A4. On Sales Person A's contact, Portal forms card: set Order Change Notice to Visible. Save.
A5. View as Sales Person A again. Sidebar: MY REQUESTS > Order Change Notice. Click Create.
A6. Look at the Requested by field, then fill: Customer Name Customer A, Customer PO No. XT04/3871 (R1), C.S. Incharge CS Person A, Type of change Exchange, line 1 item ABC123-RL qty before 842 qty after 0. Click Submit.
A7. Stop viewing as. As Admin A open Project Sales Admin > Order Change Notices, open the notice, click Send for approval.
A8. As Manager A (or View as Manager A): open the notice, click Approve.
A9. As CS Person A (or View as CS Person A): open the notice, click Mark done, type "Collection booked", confirm.
A10. View as Sales Person A: open MY REQUESTS > Order Change Notice and the notice; open its History tab.
A11. A Done notice is locked (approved = locked), so use a fresh one: View as Sales Person A, MY REQUESTS > Order Change Notice > Create, fill the required fields, Save as draft. On that draft: Edit, change Requested by to Requestor Contact B (pick it from the list; typed text is not accepted), Save. Open History.
A12. On Sales Person A's contact set Order Change Notice to Hidden, Save; View as Sales Person A; look under MY REQUESTS.
A13. Open the WhatsApp portal for Sales Person A's contact (portal landing) with the kind Visible.

### Part B: full regression (crew pass)

1. Sign in as Sales Person A.
2. Sidebar: under the MY REQUESTS heading click Order Change Notice (a salesperson has no Project Sales Admin group; the item shows only while the kind is Visible for their contact, Part A).
3. Click Create.
4. Customer Name: Customer A.
5. Customer PO No.: XT04/3871 (R1).
6. Registered project: Saffron Ridge Towers.
7. C.S. Incharge: CS Person A. Requested by: leave the default (Sales Person A's contact).
8. Type of change: click Exchange.
9. Line 1: item ABC123-RL, qty before 842, qty after 0.
10. Add row. Line 2: item ABC123-SH, qty before 0, qty after 842.
11. Description: Customer replaces ABC123-RL with ABC123-SH.
12. Drop customer-request.pdf on the attachment drop zone (or click Choose file and pick it).
13. Click Submit.
14. Open the SLA tab.
15. Sign in as Admin A. Sidebar: Project Sales Admin, Order Change Notices. Open the notice.
16. Click Send for approval.
17. Sign in as Manager A. Same path. Open the notice. Click Approve.
18. Sign in as CS Person A. Same path. Open the notice. Click Mark done, type "Collection booked", confirm.
19. Open the History tab.
20. Open the SLA tab.
21. Sign in as Sales Person A. Open the notice, Conversation tab, post "Noted".
22. Create a second notice: Customer B Sdn Bhd, PO 300/4800002913, type Cancellation, line XYZ456A-SS qty before 976, qty after 956, Submit.
23. Sign in as Admin A. Open the second notice. Click Send back, reason "Agree penalty charge first".
24. Sign in as Sales Person A. Open the second notice. Click Edit, change the description, Save, then Submit.
25. Create a third notice, type Delay, one line, Save as draft. On it: gear, Void, reason "Raised by mistake".
26. List: click the OCN no. header twice; Filters, Status: tick Done and Voided.
26a. As Sales Person A create a fourth notice: Customer A, PO XT04/3871 (R1), project Saffron Ridge Towers, type Advance, one line ABC123-SH qty before 842, qty after 842, date after earlier than date before; Submit. Admin A: Send for approval. Manager A: Approve. Do NOT mark it done.
27. Sign in as Project Lead A. Sidebar: Project Sales, Projects. Open Saffron Ridge Towers.
28. Open the Related tab.
29. Open the SO tab, click the published sales order.
30. Header gear "Sales order actions", then "Review a revision". Choose the newer version, click Compare.
31. Click Create the amendment. Open "Order change notice (optional)". Pick the fourth notice (it reads "..., Advance, Approved, goes back for approval"). Reason "Customer revised PO". Click Create the amendment, then Done on the "Amendment proposed" dialog.
32. On the amendment page, click Publish the amendment.
33. Sign in as Sales Person A. Open the fourth notice. Submit. Admin A: Send for approval. Manager A: Approve.
34. Sign in as Project Lead A. Sidebar: Project Sales Admin, Order Change Notices; open the fourth notice; in Linked amendment click the sales order link (it opens that amendment). Click Publish the amendment.
35. Repeat steps 2-3, 13-18 and 25 in a 375 px wide window.
36. Optional: on the project sales order create another amendment WITHOUT picking a notice (leave "Order change notice (optional)" empty), click Done, open the drafted notice from Project Sales Admin, Order Change Notices. Click Edit, choose a Type of change, C.S. Incharge and Requested by, Save, then Submit.
