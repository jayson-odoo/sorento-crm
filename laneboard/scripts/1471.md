# Hand test #1471 (STUCK-QTY-LOOP)

All turns go through the chatbot console as dry runs: nothing reaches WhatsApp. No migration is needed.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
1. Use a test contact you can message through the chatbot console (Settings > Chatbot > console, or `scripts/chatbot_console_check.py --say`).
2. Give that contact Stock visibility "Availability only" (contact override on the contact's access screen) and the low stock report access (`scm.low_stock_report` field reveal).
3. Pick two real product codes that have stock rows, e.g. SRTWC286-SH-150 and SRTWC286-SH-200.

## Steps
1. Send: check stock SRTWC286-SH-150 and SRTWC286-SH-200
2. Send: low stock report for sorento water tap
3. Send the same message again: low stock report for sorento water tap
4. Send: check stock SRTWC286-SH-150 and SRTWC286-SH-200
5. Change the contact's Stock visibility to "Compact".
6. Send: stock?
7. Change the contact back to "Availability only" and send: check stock SRTWC286-SH-150 and SRTWC286-SH-200
8. Send: 10
9. Send: check stock SRTWC286-SH-150 and SRTWC286-SH-200
10. Send: never mind the stock check
11. Send: low stock report for sorento water tap
12. Send: incoming SRTWC286
13. Send: 4
14. Send: check stock
15. Send: 7

## Expected
1. Step 1 replies "How many units for each?" with two numbered lines ending " - ".
2. Step 2 replies with the low stock report (or its own "Which product category?" question). It does NOT contain "How many units".
3. Step 3: same as step 2, no "How many units".
4. Step 4 replies "How many units for each?" again (a fresh stock ask).
5. Step 6 does NOT contain "How many units": the question asked under availability is gone after the switch; the reply is the compact stock answer or the "need at least one filter" line.
6. Step 7 replies "How many units for each?"; step 8 answers both codes at 10 each (a stock answer line per code, then "Please refer to your salesman." where that applies), no "How many units".
7. Step 10 does not contain "How many units"; step 11 does not contain "How many units".
8. Steps 12-15 (crew ruling (a), the owner reviews this nuance): step 12 lists the SRTWC286 family as a numbered pick; step 14 answers stock for the product picked in step 13 (the list is not repeated as the reply); step 15 answers incoming for the 7th product of that list.
