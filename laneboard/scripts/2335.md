# Hand test: PR #2335, quotation Attn picker + Prepared By (lane QUOTE-ATTN-PICKER)

## URL

crew test copy

## Setup

1. No migration.
2. Sign in as a user who is NOT the owner of the project you will use (e.g. yourself as admin).
3. Pick a project whose developer party has at least 2 contacts. To add one: Project page >
   Overview > Parties > the developer's contacts link > "+ Add contact".
4. For step 18 only: a proposal or quotation on any project that was already sent with the newest
   cover page layout, created by someone other than the project owner.

## Steps

1. Project Sales > Projects > open the project > Proposals tab > Add a proposal.
2. In the Header, read "Prepared By".
3. Open the Cover letter tab and read the cover preview's Prepared By.
4. Back on the Header, under Attn, click "Choose contacts".
5. Click the "Contacts" box in the dialog.
6. Click "Select all (N)".
7. Press Escape to close the list (the dialog stays open).
8. Click "+ Add contact".
9. Look at the dialog: its footer, the Contacts box, and the buttons.
10. Type a name in Name and a phone in Phone.
11. Click "Add contact".
12. Click "Done".
13. Save the proposal.
14. Open the proposal from the Proposals tab, click Edit, then "Choose contacts".
15. Click the "Contacts" box.
16. Press Escape, then click "Done".
17. Read Attn and "Prepared By".
18. Open the already-sent document from Setup 4 and download or preview its PDF; read Prepared By
    on the cover page.
19. User Management > Users > a salesperson > Impersonate user; read the confirm dialog, then
    confirm and read the yellow banner. Exit impersonation.
20. User Management > Contacts > a contact > Impersonate; read the confirm dialog, then Cancel.

## Expected

- Step 2: Prepared By = your own name, not the project owner's.
- Step 3: the cover preview's Prepared By = your own name.
- Step 5: a search box, a "Select all (N)" row, and one line per contact: name, then phone.
- Step 6: every contact is ticked.
- Step 9: the dialog's own Cancel and Done (bottom) are gone; the Contacts box is greyed out and its
  chips have no X; the only buttons are the add form's "Cancel" and the blue "Add contact".
- Step 11: the new contact appears ticked in the Contacts box, and the dialog's Cancel and Done are
  back.
- Step 12: Attn lists every ticked name joined by " & ".
- Step 15: the contacts named in Attn are already ticked.
- Step 17: Attn is unchanged (not blank); Prepared By is still your name.
- Step 18: Prepared By on the cover = the person who created that document, not the project owner.
  A row labelled "Sales" (if the design has one) still shows the project owner.
- Step 19: dialog: "Records you create are saved as <name>; the audit log shows you did it.";
  banner: the same sentence.
- Step 20: dialog: "What you submit is saved as <contact>; comments also show your name, and the
  audit log shows you did it."
