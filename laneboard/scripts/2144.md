# Hand test: PR #2144 CUSTOMER-GROUP-GATE (chatbot lists join ledgers only through customer groups)

Before this fix the chatbot never applied the office's customer groups: every ledger fell to the
trading-name rule, so all FULLSHUN ledgers in both companies, grouped or not, were one line. Now a
"Which one do you mean?" list joins ledgers ONLY through an explicit customer group. A group is one
company's (a name in both books is two lines, "NAME (SRT)" and "NAME (MCH)"). Every ungrouped
ledger is its own line under its full name. The same ungrouped full name in both company books
stays one customer, and the bot never asks "Sorento or Mocha?" for it.

## Setup
- Signed in as an admin, one browser tab, on the crew test copy (dev DB copy as is, no migration).
- In the Chatbot Console, chat as the contact **Mr Loo** (he sees both Sorento and Mocha and has
  Outstanding enabled; Jayson gets "Outstanding is not enabled", Darren sees SRT only).
- SHOP = **S TWO SANITARY WARE** (it has delivery orders). Before starting, open Customer Groups
  and note which S TWO ledgers sit in a group (and in which company) and which sit in none.

## Steps
1. From `/`, open the sidebar Chatbot Console and pick the contact Mr Loo.
2. Send: "DO for s two sanitary ware".
3. Note every line of "Which one do you mean?" and its "has DO" / "no DO" tag.
4. Reply with the number of a GROUP's line.
5. Note the DO list and its Customer header.
6. Send: "outstanding for fullshun".
7. Note every line of the list.
8. Send: "fullshun sales this month".
9. Note every line of the list.
10. Send: "DO for capriform builders".

## Expected
- Step 3: one line per S TWO customer group, named exactly as the group (with "(SRT)" or "(MCH)"
  after it when that group name exists in both companies), plus one line per UNGROUPED S TWO
  ledger under its full name, e.g. "... - [IBORN]". No ungrouped ledger is folded into a group's
  line. A line reads "has DO" only when that line's own ledgers have a delivery order.
- Step 5: the list holds only the DOs of the picked group's ledgers; an ungrouped S TWO ledger's DOs
  are not in it.
- Step 7: "FULLSHUN SANITARYWARE SDN BHD (SRT)" and "FULLSHUN SANITARYWARE SDN BHD (MCH)" are two
  separate lines, plus each ungrouped FULLSHUN ledger as its own full-name line. Never one FULLSHUN
  line covering both companies.
- Step 9: the same lines as step 7.
- Step 10: no "Sorento or Mocha?" question and no pick list for CAPRIFORM BUILDERS (the same
  ungrouped full name in both companies is one customer); the answer covers both companies' DOs.
