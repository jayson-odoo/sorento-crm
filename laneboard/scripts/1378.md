2. Have on the dev DB a sales team with three or more agents, one of them the team leader. If none exists, open Sales > Sales Teams, click Add team, name it "Hand test 1378", pick three agents and one as leader, save.

## Steps

1. From the sidebar open Sales > Sales Teams.
2. Find the team from Setup step 2 and look at the Agents column.
3. Drag the Agents column border narrower until a "+N" chip appears in that cell.
4. Click the "+N" chip.
5. Press Escape.
6. Tab to the "+N" chip with the keyboard and press Enter, then press Escape.
7. Click an agent name inside the popover after opening it again with "+N", then press Escape.
8. Drag the Agents column border wider again.

## Expected

1. The Sales Teams list renders; every row is one line tall.
2. The Agents cell shows the leader first, tagged "(Leader)", then the other agents on the same line.
3. The cell stays ONE line: the agents that fit, then a "+N" chip where N = agents not shown. No agent wraps under another; the row is no taller than a one-agent row.
4. A popover opens on top of the list, listing every agent of the team one per line, the leader first with "(Leader)". No neighbouring row or chip shows through it. This is the same popover layout as Users & Access > People > Administrative Users, Roles column.
5. The popover closes. Nothing else on the page changed (no team page opened).
6. Enter opens the same popover, Escape closes it. The chip shows a focus ring when focused.
7. Clicking a name inside the popover does NOT open the team page behind it; Escape closes the popover.
8. More agents show inline and N shrinks (to no chip once all fit), live, with no reload.
