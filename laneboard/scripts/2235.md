# Hand test: PR #2235 PRICETAG-ACC-LABELS (price tag accessories: one label, clear box picker, drop warning everywhere)

The combo's accessory list now reads "Accessories included", the same as the product's. The Accessories box's Product
picker names each choice as a list. The "does not fit its box" warning also shows in the Tag Templates designer. What
prints is unchanged: a box pinned to a slot still reads that product's own list, never the combo's (owner rule,
06/11/2026).

crew test copy

## Setup
1. Sign in as a staff user who can edit products and tag templates.
2. Product SRTKS7846 has 3 accessories on its own "Accessories included" card.
3. Product SRTWB7065 has a combo with part SRTWT7202, whose combo list is SRTW5000 and COLANDER. It has a published
   default price tag template with an Accessories box set to "This tag's list (combo or product)".
4. Note that template's name (shown in the combo's Tag preview switcher or its Price tag templates field).

## Steps
1. Open Products, then All Products, then SRTWB7065, on the Overview tab. Scroll to the Combos card.
2. Read the label above SRTW5000 and COLANDER in the combo. Search the page for "Free items".
3. Open SRTKS7846, Overview tab, and read its "Accessories included" card.
4. Open Dealer Kit, then Room Designer, then Tag Templates, then the template from Setup 4.
5. In Preview combo, pick the SRTWB7065 combo.
6. Select the Accessories box. Open the Inspector's Product select and read the options.
7. Drag the box's bottom edge up until the last card is one card short of fitting.
8. Read the bar under Preview combo.
9. Drag the bottom edge back down until every card fits.
10. Click Publish. Go back to SRTWB7065's Overview tab and read the combo's Tag preview.
11. Back in the designer, shrink the box one card short again and Publish. Re-open SRTWB7065's combo Tag preview.
12. In the designer, set the box's Product to "Main product's list" and do not Publish. Read the canvas box.
13. Set Product back to "This tag's list (combo or product)", restore the box size, and Publish.

## Expected
1. Step 2: the combo's list is headed "Accessories included", with SRTW5000 and COLANDER. "Free items" appears nowhere
   on the page.
2. Step 3: the "Accessories included" card lists the 3 accessories (unchanged).
3. Step 6: the options read "This tag's list (combo or product)" (selected), "Main product's list" and
   "Fixed part 1 product's list" (with SRTWT7202 under it). Choice groups and upgrades read "<name>, pick n product's
   list" and "Upgrade n product's list".
4. Step 8: a warning under Preview combo reads "1 accessory does not fit its box" (or "N accessories do not fit their
   box").
5. Step 9: the warning is gone.
6. Step 10: no warning above the combo's Tag preview; SRTW5000 and COLANDER both print.
7. Step 11: above the combo's Tag preview: "Tag 1a: 1 accessory does not fit its box".
8. Step 12: the box draws SRTWB7065's own accessories (empty if it has none), not SRTW5000 and COLANDER. This
   behaviour is unchanged by this PR.
9. Price badge Product select (any price badge layer) still reads "Tag total", "Main product", "Fixed part 1" with no
   "list" wording.
