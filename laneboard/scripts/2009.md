# Hand test: PR #2009 AGENTIC-P3-VISIBILITY (what the agent did, and chat formatting)

Your hand test of #1999 asked for two things: see what the agent did and changed, and see its replies formatted
like chat mode. Now, under every agent reply in the console, the steps it ran read in plain words, and every
change it made shows as `Field: before -> after` per record, also after a reload. Agent replies show bold, bullet
points and numbered lists like the Bot tab. The contact's audit history names the agent change and who made it.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
1. The copy runs the PR head (no migration in this PR; #1999's are already applied) with a Gemini key in the AI
   assistant settings.
2. Sign in as the owner (admin).
3. The same Contact S, ask A, opportunity O and dealer D as the #1999 hand test (crew names them; D is an Employee).

## Steps
1. CRM sidebar: System > Messaging > Chatbot Console. Click the `Agent` tab and pick Contact S.
2. Send: `give me a short summary of my open customer asks, with the most urgent in bold`
3. Under that reply, click `Steps`.
4. Send: `mark ask <A's product code> done, note: called the customer`, then click `Confirm change`.
5. Refresh the page and pick Contact S again if needed.
6. Send: `<D's phone> change to Business owner`, then click `Confirm change`.
7. Open Users & Access > Respond.io Contacts, open dealer D, and open its audit / history.
8. Back in the console, send: `undo that`, then click `Cancel`.

## Expected
1. The Agent tab with Contact S's conversation.
2. The reply shows real bold text and bullet or numbered lines, with no `*` or `**` characters visible.
3. Each step is a plain sentence, for example `Customer asks: 3 rows` or `Find names and codes: SRT... found`; no ids.
4. Under the reply: a `Done` badge, the step line, and `State: Open -> Done` and `Note: none -> called the customer`.
5. After the refresh the same Done block and change lines are still under that reply.
6. A `Done` block with `Role: Employee -> Business owner` for D (phone and name shown, no id).
7. An entry `Agent change for <D's phone> by <your name>` with Role Employee -> Business owner; no ids.
8. A `Cancelled` block with no change lines; D stays Business owner.
