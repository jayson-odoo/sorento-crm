# Hand test: PR #2096 PS-QUOTE-RULES (any discount and off-series lines need a routed approval; a discount request moves the project to Final Negotiation)

Owner rulings 2 Nov: an off-series line needs approval before the quotation is sent; ANY discount
(below the default price, even RM 1) needs approval; each request goes to ONE configured approver
(off-series approver, the project's discount approver, the salesperson's superior, else the
default quotation approver); a discount request puts the project in Final Negotiation.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
1. Apply the crew-migration SQL from the PR (5 nullable columns + 1 permission label).
2. Routing only picks approvers who can open the quotation (`projects.projects.view`). On the copy
   the Project Sales Manager role holds the approve grant but not view: as admin, in **Settings >
   Roles**, give Project Sales Manager **View projects** (the owner's call for production; the PR
   body lists every role with approve but no view).
3. Sign in as an admin. In **Settings > Users**, open the salesperson who owns PRJQ-2026-0017's
   project and set **Superior** to one of the two Project Sales Managers (call them Manager-1).
   Set Manager-1's own **Superior** to the other Project Sales Manager (call them CK for this test).
4. In **Settings > General**, set **Approver (project quotation lines outside the series)** to CK
   and **Default approver (project quotation discounts)** to Manager-1. Save.
5. Make sure the salesperson, a Project Customer Service user, Manager-1 and CK all have a password
   you can sign in with on the copy. The salesperson can only view a quotation; the Project
   Customer Service user (call them CS) presses Send for approval, Sign, Send and Edit.

## Steps
1. Sign in as the salesperson. Open **Project Sales > Projects**, open PRJQ-2026-0017's project and note its stage and whether the **Critical / final negotiation** card says Raised.
2. Sign in as CS, open quotation PRJQ-2026-0017 and open its **Lines** tab.
3. Look at the banner above the letter.
4. Press **Send for approval**.
5. Look at the banner again.
6. Go back to the project page.
7. Sign in as Manager-1 and open the same quotation.
8. Look at the header buttons.
9. Sign in as CK, open the same quotation and press **Approve**.
10. Sign in as CS, open the quotation, sign it if it is unsigned, and press **Send to Customer** (R1, or the next revision label).
11. Press **Edit**, change the rate of in-series line CB1508ASS to RM 1 below the price it opened at, and save.
12. Look at the banner.
13. Press **Send for approval**.
14. Go back to the project page.
15. Sign in as Manager-1, open the quotation, and look at the header buttons.
16. Sign in as CK, open the quotation, press **Reject**, type "Too low" and press **Send it back**.
17. Sign in as the salesperson and open the project page.
18. Sign in as CS, open the quotation and look at the header button.
19. Sign in as an admin, open the project, and on the **Critical / final negotiation** card set **Discount approver** to CK.
20. Clear the **Discount approver** again.
21. Sign in as the salesperson and open the project page.

## Expected
1. Note the stage (Spec In on the dev copy) and the flag (Not raised).
2. The Lines tab shows the line tagged **Non-standard**.
3. Banner: "One line is outside the project's series, so this quotation needs a manager's approval before it can be sent to the customer."
4. The press succeeds; the badge reads Pending approval.
5. Banner: "One line is outside the project's series, so this quotation is with CK for approval. It cannot be sent to the customer until they decide." (CK's real name.)
6. Stage unchanged (Spec In) and the flag still Not raised: an off-series-only request is not a discount.
7. The quotation opens; the banner names CK.
8. No **Approve** or **Reject** button: the request is CK's, and CK is above Manager-1, not below.
9. Approved; the badge reads Approved.
10. The send succeeds; the approval is spent.
11. The line saves at the lower price, still above its minimum price.
12. Banner: "One line is outside the project's series and one line is priced below the default price, so this quotation needs a manager's approval before it can be sent to the customer." (the off-series line is still there, so the request goes to CK).
13. The press succeeds; the banner says it is with CK.
14. The stage is now **Final Negotiation** and the card says **Raised** with today's Malaysia date.
15. No **Approve** or **Reject** button (the off-series line routes it to CK, who is above Manager-1).
16. The quotation moves to Rejected; the banner reads "A manager sent this back. Change the lines they asked about, then ask again." and "Their reason: Too low".
17. The project is still in **Final Negotiation** and still **Raised** (a rejected discount does not undo it).
18. The header offers **Appeal** (asking again after a rejection).
19. The card shows CK as Discount approver.
20. The card shows the placeholder "Salesperson's superior".
21. The salesperson sees the Discount approver as text ("Salesperson's superior"), not a picker.
