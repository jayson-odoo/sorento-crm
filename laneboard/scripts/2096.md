# Hand test: PR #2096 PS-QUOTE-RULES (any discount and off-series lines need a routed approval; a discount request moves the project to Final Negotiation)

Owner rulings 2 Nov: an off-series line needs approval before the quotation is sent; ANY discount
(below the default price, even RM 1) needs approval; each request goes to ONE configured approver
(off-series approver, the project's discount approver, the salesperson's superior, else the
default quotation approver); a discount request puts the project in Final Negotiation.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
1. Apply the crew-migration SQL from the PR (5 nullable columns + 1 permission label).
2. Sign in as an admin. In **Settings > Users**, open the salesperson who owns PRJQ-2026-0017's
   project (crew's masked Person-B or whoever owns it on the copy) and set **Superior** to one of
   the two Project Sales Managers (call them Manager-1). Set Manager-1's own **Superior** to the
   other Project Sales Manager (call them CK for this test).
3. In **Settings > General**, set **Approver (project quotation lines outside the series)** to CK
   and **Default approver (project quotation discounts)** to Manager-1. Save.
4. Make sure the salesperson, Manager-1 and CK all have a password you can sign in with on the copy.

## Steps
1. Sign in as the salesperson. Open **Project Sales > Projects**, open PRJQ-2026-0017's project and note its stage and whether the **Critical / final negotiation** card says Raised.
2. Open quotation PRJQ-2026-0017 (it has one off-series line, at its default price).
3. Look at the banner above the letter.
4. Press **Send for approval**.
5. Look at the banner again.
6. Go back to the project page.
7. Sign out, sign in as Manager-1, open the same quotation.
8. Look at the header buttons.
9. Sign out, sign in as CK, open the same quotation, press **Approve**.
10. Sign in as the salesperson, open the quotation, sign it if it is unsigned, press **Send** (Issue).
11. On the same quotation, start a new revision and change one in-series line's rate to RM 1 below the price it opened at. Save.
12. Look at the banner.
13. Press **Send for approval**.
14. Go back to the project page.
15. Sign in as Manager-1, open the quotation, and look at the header buttons.
16. Sign in as CK, open the quotation, press **Reject** with the reason "Too low".
17. Sign in as the salesperson and open the project page.
18. Sign in as an admin, open the project, and on the **Critical / final negotiation** card set **Discount approver** to CK.
19. Clear the **Discount approver** again.
20. Sign in as the salesperson and open the project page.

## Expected
1. Note the stage (Spec In on the dev copy) and the flag (Not raised).
2. The quotation opens on its lines; one line shows **Non-standard**.
3. Banner: "One line is outside the project's series, so this quotation needs a manager's approval before it can be sent to the customer."
4. The press succeeds; the badge reads Pending approval.
5. Banner: "One line is outside the project's series, so this quotation is with CK for approval. It cannot be sent to the customer until they decide." (CK's real name.)
6. Stage unchanged (Spec In) and the flag still Not raised: an off-series-only request is not a discount.
7. The quotation opens; the banner names CK.
8. No **Approve** or **Reject** button: the request is CK's, and CK is above Manager-1, not below.
9. Approved; the badge reads Approved.
10. Issue succeeds (R1 or the next R number); the approval is spent.
11. The line saves at the lower price.
12. Banner: "One line is outside the project's series and one line is priced below the default price, so this quotation needs a manager's approval ..." (the off-series line is still there, so the request goes to CK).
13. The press succeeds; the banner says it is with CK.
14. The stage is now **Final Negotiation** and the card says **Raised** with today's date.
15. No **Approve** or **Reject** button (the off-series line routes it to CK, who is above Manager-1).
16. The quotation moves to Rejected; the banner reads "A manager sent this back. Change the lines they asked about, then ask again." and "Their reason: Too low".
17. The project is still in **Final Negotiation** and still **Raised** (a rejected discount does not undo it).
18. The card shows CK as Discount approver.
19. The card shows the placeholder "Salesperson's superior".
20. The salesperson sees the Discount approver as text ("Salesperson's superior"), not a picker.
