# Hand test: PR #1925 RESUBMITTED-STATUS (a submission after a rejection reads Resubmitted)

Owner, 27 Oct: "change our submit after rejected to be called resubmitted across all forms", and the portal
button Submit becomes Resubmit. The test copy holds no resubmitted record yet (the backfill finds 0 on the
20 Oct restore: no complaint sits at Submitted), so the owner MAKES the resubmit here, through the portal, as
the contact would.

crew test copy

## Setup
1. The test copy runs on the 20 Oct prod restore with the `crew-migration` SQL from PR #1925 applied.
2. Sign in as an admin (Impersonate in portal needs it).
3. Optional, crew only: `python scripts/backfill_resubmitted_status.py` (dry run) from `sorento_crm_backend/`.
   Expect 0 on every form on this copy. Nothing to apply.

## Steps
1. From the sidebar, open Complaint Management > Complaints.
2. Open the Status filter, pick "Rejected", and open CMP26-0007 (or any rejected complaint).
3. Note the complaint's contact name, then open User Management > Contacts and find that contact.
4. On the contact, open the "..." menu and press "Impersonate in portal".
5. In the portal, open CMP26-0007 from the complaints list.
6. Read the send button at the bottom of the form.
7. Press it and read the dialog title.
8. Press the dialog's "Resubmit complaint" to confirm.
9. Back in the CRM tab, open Complaint Management > Complaints again.
10. Open the Status filter and pick "Resubmitted".
11. Open CMP26-0007.
12. Read the status pill and the header buttons.
13. If you are a Complaint team (Tier 1) member, open the notification bell.
14. Open Procurement > Purchase Requests, filter Status "Rejected". If a row is there, open it, impersonate its
    contact in the portal (steps 3-4), open that request in the portal and press its send button; then filter the
    CRM list by "Resubmitted" and open it. If no rejected request exists on this copy, skip this step.
15. Same for Procurement > Stock Inquiries with a rejected inquiry, if one exists; otherwise skip.
