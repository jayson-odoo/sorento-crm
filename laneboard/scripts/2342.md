# PR 2342 hand test: the Town as a map of Malaysia, project sites, trading houses, the Dealers / Projects / Other split

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- No migration: this lane changes no table.
- Sign in as an admin (the Town is admin-only).
- Build today's Town snapshot once on the test copy, between 07:00 and 22:05 Malaysia time (the scheduled tick does this every few minutes when the scheduler runs):
  `cd sorento_crm_backend && venv/bin/python -c "from app.services.town.snapshot import run_town_snapshot_tick; print(run_town_snapshot_tick())"`
- For the "no amounts" check, have a staff user who holds `dashboards.town.view` but NOT `sales.reports.view` (or grant the Town permission to a test role on the Roles screen).
- Optional, for a project site with dates: open any open project and set its Launch date and Expected delivery (from) in the sales profile, and its State; for a trading house: link a party in role Trading house that is tied to a customer which has a Project-type invoice today. Then rebuild the snapshot (command above).

## Steps
1. From `/`, click Town in the sidebar.
2. Look at the default view without touching anything.
3. Look at the sales card on the right ("How much did we sell today?").
4. Hover a lit (amber roof) shop on the Selangor island, then hover a slate-blue glass tower and a project site (a plot, frame with a crane, or building with a small flag).
5. Click a project site.
6. Close it, then click a glass tower.
7. Close it. In the row of state names under the map, click Johor.
8. Click the map icon button (Whole map) at the bottom right of the map.
9. Click Reset view (the round arrow).
10. Click the sign that reads "+N more projects" or "+N dormant" near the Selangor island (if your data has more than 30 open projects or any dormant one).
11. Click Wallboard at the top right.
12. Click Exit wallboard. Sign out, sign in as the user without `sales.reports.view`, open Town from the sidebar.
13. Shrink the browser to phone width (375 px) and scroll the Town page.

## Expected
- Step 2: the same island town as before (same houses, lamps, trees, port, board, lorries) seen from the south: Port Klang and the ships on the LEFT, Selangor's dealer shops on the street nearest you, the HQ (board, office, warehouses, depot) behind them; neighbouring islands (Perak, Pahang, Kuala Lumpur, Putrajaya) at the edges joined by bridges; a pill per state ("Selangor  N dealers, N lit · N sites · N trading houses").
- Step 3: under the invoice count a line "Dealers RM a · Projects RM b · Other RM c" whose three amounts add up to the big figure; the 3D board shows the same line.
- Step 4: shop tooltip as today; tower: "<customer name>" / "N open projects · bought today" (or "no project invoice today"); site: "<project name · code>" / "<Stage>[, final negotiation] · <groundworks / frame going up / cladding / finished / launching / no dates yet>".
- Step 5: a popup titled "<project name · code>": Stage, Final negotiation Yes/No, State, Timeline ("Launch Mon YYYY · Delivery Mon YYYY · N% of the way", or "No launch date · ..."), Estimated value; an "Open project" button that opens /project-sales/<that project>. A project with no dates is a tidy prepared plot (pad, survey pegs, blue-band hoarding, cabin), never a bare fence; a lost project never appears; a dormant one only in the count.
- Step 6: popup with the customer name, State, "Open projects it is on", "Project invoices today".
- Step 7: the camera flies to Johor at street level: Johor's dealer shops, its project sites and towers on one street, bridges to Negeri Sembilan and Melaka.
- Step 8: all of Malaysia: the peninsula states as islands, the "State not set" islet off Port Klang, Sarawak, Labuan and Sabah across the sea; only state pills show.
- Step 9: back to the Step 2 view.
- Step 10: popup "N open projects" / "N on the map · +N more · N dormant" and the list of sites with their stage and state.
- Step 11: no dealer, project or trading-house names anywhere (shops read "Shop #n"); counts, flags, lit roofs and towers stay.
- Step 12: no RM anywhere: the card line reads "Dealers n · Projects n · Other n" (invoice counts); a site popup shows "Project site" with Stage, State and Timeline only, no value and no "Open project".
- Step 13: the map, the state chips (they scroll sideways inside their row), the legend and the cards fit at phone width; the page itself never scrolls sideways.
- A dealer's state comes from its delivery orders' postcode (the last 5-digit group of the address); a dealer with no delivery address stands on the "State not set" islet. No address or postcode appears anywhere on the page.
