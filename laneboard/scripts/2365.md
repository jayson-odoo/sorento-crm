# Hand test #2365 (AFTER-SALES-P1: service jobs)

Walk the flow one step at a time. After each step, note what to improve. Step numbers match the
Expected list, so feedback can say "step 7: ...".

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Start the test copy of branch `claude/after-sales-warranty-scout-ww5dj3` at head 6a5d24672 (or later
   fix-only heads crew names). Apply the schema with the `crew-migration:` SQL on PR #2365 (or `alembic
   upgrade head` on a private copy): technicians, service jobs, Site fields on complaints, "requires service
   job" on resolutions, the service-job statuses, the SV numbering rule and the three permissions. Restart the
   backend.
2. Sign in as an admin, or as a CS user whose role can edit complaints (that role gets the new service-job
   permissions from the migration).
3. Pick one open complaint on dev (any complaint with "Required on site support" = Yes is a good case).
   Call it complaint X.
4. One active resolution exists in Complaint Resolutions (it is edited in step 16). Pick a second open
   complaint with no job: complaint Y.

## Steps

1. From `/`, open the sidebar group Complaints.
2. Click Technicians. Press Create, enter Name "Test Tech A", Phone "0123456789", Type Employee, and save.
3. Press Create again: Name "Test Tech B", Type Contractor, and save.
4. Right-click "Test Tech B" and choose Edit. Change the phone to "0198765432" and save.
5. Click Complaints and open complaint X.
6. Press Edit. In the Site section, fill address line 1, postcode "43000", city "Kajang", state "Selangor",
   contact on site and contact phone, then save.
7. On complaint X, open the gear menu at the top right and choose "Raise service job" (the Service Jobs
   section has no button while the complaint has no job).
8. Go back to complaint X, open the gear again and look for "Raise service job"; reload the complaint.
9. In complaint X's Service Jobs section, click the job line to open the job.
10. Press "Confirm date". Leave "Agreed by" empty, pick tomorrow at 10:00 am and press Confirm.
11. Type "Customer on phone" in Agreed by and press Confirm.
12. Press "Assign technician", pick "Test Tech A" and press Assign.
13. Open Complaints > Dispatch Board. Press Next (tomorrow).
14. Click the job card, then on the job page open the gear and choose "Customer rejected the visit". Type a
    reason and confirm.
15. Open the History tab.
16. Open Complaint Resolutions, edit the active resolution, tick "Requires service job" and save. Open
    complaint Y, set that resolution on it and save.
17. On complaint X's job: Confirm date again (today, an hour from now, "Customer on WhatsApp"), then gear >
    "Change technician" > "Test Tech B", then press the main button four times: On the way, Arrived,
    Complete, Verify.
18. Open Complaints > Service Jobs. Click each column header three times. Filter Status =
    Verified + Proposed, then Technician = Test Tech B. Search for complaint X's number.
19. Open the job from the filtered list and use the pager's next and previous buttons.
20. Right-click a row in the list.
21. Open Technicians and right-click "Test Tech B" (it is on the verified job): try Delete. Then choose Archive,
    and on complaint Y's job open "Assign technician" and look for "Test Tech B". Finally right-click "Test Tech
    A" (no job any more) and choose Delete; let the countdown finish.
22. Make the browser 375 px wide and repeat steps 9, 13 and 18.

## Expected

1. Service Jobs, Dispatch Board and Technicians are listed under Complaints.
2. "Test Tech A" shows in the list on one line: Name, Phone, Type "Employee", an "Active" pill.
3. "Test Tech B" shows with Type "Contractor".
4. The same dialog as Create opens, filled in. The new phone shows after save.
5. Complaint X opens with a Site card. Fields read "-" when empty, and address line 1 is pre-filled from the
   customer address in the edit form.
6. The Site card shows the saved values in the same order as the edit form.
7. The new job opens (e.g. "SV26/11-0001", status "Proposed", Scheduled "-", Technician "-"), and complaint
   X's Service Jobs section lists it on one line.
8. "Raise service job" is not offered again while the job is live, and after a reload complaint X still has
   exactly one job.
9. The job page header reads "SV26/11-0001" + a "Proposed" pill, with a subtitle naming complaint X and the
   customer. The one filled button is "Confirm date". Two tabs: Details (Visit, Site) and History.
10. Confirm is refused with a message under Agreed by. The date field shows dd/mm/yyyy, never a browser date
    box.
11. Status becomes "Confirmed". Scheduled reads "dd/mm/yyyy, 10:00 am" for tomorrow, and Agreed by reads
    "Customer on phone". The main button becomes "Assign technician".
12. Technician reads "Test Tech A". The main button becomes "On the way". "Test Tech B" was offered too.
13. The board shows tomorrow's date as dd/mm/yyyy, a "Test Tech A" column holding the job card (job no., time
    10:00 am, city Kajang, Confirmed). "Waiting for a date" does not list it (it is Confirmed).
14. Status goes back to "Proposed". Scheduled and Agreed by read "-", and Technician still reads "Test Tech A".
15. History lists, newest first, with who and when: created (Proposed), Confirmed with date and agreed by,
    Technician "-" -> "Test Tech A", Confirmed -> Proposed with the reason.
16. Complaint Y now shows one job in Proposed, raised by itself. Complaint X still has one job.
17. Each press moves the status: On the way, Arrived (Arrived time stamped), Completed (time stamped),
    Verified (time stamped, no main button left). Technician reads "Test Tech B".
18. Every header sorts asc, desc, off (and the order holds on page 2 when there is one). The filters combine. Search finds
    complaint X's job. Each row is one line, and there is no Create button and no row icon.
19. The pager follows the filtered, sorted order.
20. The menu shows Open complaint and only the moves the job's status allows.
21. "Test Tech B" offers no Delete, only Edit and Archive (it is in use). After Archive it shows "Inactive" and is
    not offered on Assign; the verified job and the board still show its name. "Test Tech A" is deleted after the
    countdown, with no confirm dialog.
22. Nothing is clipped and nothing scrolls sideways. The board's columns stack.
