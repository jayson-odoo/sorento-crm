# Hand test #2365 (AFTER-SALES-P1: service jobs, standalone After-Sales module)

Walk the flow one step at a time. After each step, note what to improve. Step numbers match the
Expected list, so feedback can say "step 7: ...".

This test follows two owner rulings from 12 Nov:
- After-sales is its own module, with its own sidebar section.
- The live Complaints module does not change at all.

Phone numbers use the same field as project sales.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Start the test copy of branch `claude/after-sales-warranty-scout-ww5dj3` at the head crew names.
2. Apply the schema with the newest `crew-migration:` SQL on PR #2365 (or `alembic upgrade head` on a private
   copy). It adds:
   - the `after_sales` module;
   - the technicians and service jobs tables;
   - the service-job statuses and the SV numbering rule;
   - three `after_sales.*` permissions, granted to the roles that can edit complaints.

   It touches no complaint table.
3. Restart the backend.
4. Sign in as an admin, or as a CS user whose role can edit complaints.
5. Note one open complaint number on dev. Call it complaint X. It is only picked as a "Related complaint".

## Steps

1. From `/`, look at the sidebar. Open the section After-Sales Service, then open the section Complaints.
2. In After-Sales Service, click Technicians. Press Create and enter:
   - Name "Test Tech A"
   - Phone "0123456789"
   - Type Employee

   Save.
3. Press Create again and enter:
   - Name "Test Tech B"
   - Phone "012345"
   - Type Contractor

   Press Save.
4. Correct the phone to "0198765432" and save.
5. Right-click "Test Tech B" and choose Edit. Change the phone to "0167654321" and save.
6. In After-Sales Service, click Service Jobs. Press Create and fill in:
   - Customer "Demo Customer One"
   - Contact on site "Demo Contact"
   - Contact phone "0123456789"
   - Address line 1 "No 8, Jalan Contoh 3"
   - Postcode "43000"
   - City "Kajang"
   - State "Selangor"
   - Problem "Basin mixer leaking"
   - Related complaint: search for and pick complaint X

   Save.
7. Open the new job from the list.
8. Go back to the list. Press Create again with:
   - Customer "Demo Customer Two"
   - City "Shah Alam"
   - Related complaint: the same complaint X

   Press Save.
9. Clear the Related complaint and press Save.
10. Open the first job (SV26/11-0001). Press "Confirm date". Leave "Agreed by" empty, pick tomorrow at
    10:00 am and press Confirm.
11. Type "Customer on phone" in Agreed by and press Confirm.
12. Press "Assign technician", pick "Test Tech A" and press "Assign technician" in the dialog.
13. Open After-Sales Service > Dispatch Board. Press Next to show tomorrow.
14. Click the job card. On the job page, open the gear and choose "Customer rejected the visit". Type a
    reason and confirm.
15. Open the History tab.
16. On the same job, press Confirm date again: today, an hour from now, agreed by "Customer on WhatsApp".
17. Open the gear, choose "Change technician" and pick "Test Tech B".
18. Press the main button four times: On the way, Arrived, Complete, Verify.
19. Open the gear and choose Edit. Change the contact phone to "0198765432" and save.
20. Open After-Sales Service > Service Jobs. Click each column header three times.
21. Filter Status = Verified + Proposed, then Technician = Test Tech B. Search "Kajang".
22. Open the job from the filtered list. Use the pager's next and previous buttons.
23. Right-click a row in the list.
24. Open Technicians and right-click "Test Tech B" (it is on the verified job). Try Delete, then choose
    Archive.
25. Open the second job. Press "Confirm date" (any date, any agreed by), then open "Assign technician" and
    look for "Test Tech B".
26. On Technicians, open Filters and set Type = Contractor and Status = Inactive.
27. Right-click "Test Tech A" (no job any more) and choose Delete. Let the countdown finish.
28. Open complaint X from Complaints. Compare it with the same complaint on the live system: header, fields,
    sections and gear menu.
29. Make the browser 375 px wide and repeat steps 7, 13 and 20.

## Expected

1. "After-Sales Service" is its own section with Service Jobs, Dispatch Board and Technicians. The Complaints
   section lists exactly what it lists on the live system, with no Service Jobs, Dispatch Board or
   Technicians.
2. "Test Tech A" shows on one line:
   - Phone in local format (012-345 6789)
   - Type "Employee"
   - an "Active" pill

   The phone field is the same as on project-sales contacts: a country picker (Malaysia) and the number.
3. Save is refused with "Enter a valid phone number, e.g. 12-345 6789." under the phone field.
4. "Test Tech B" saves, with Type "Contractor".
5. The same dialog as Create opens, filled in. The new phone shows after save.
6. The job is created as e.g. "SV26/11-0001", in status "Proposed". The phone field is the same as in step 2.
7. The job page shows:
   - Header: "SV26/11-0001" and a "Proposed" pill.
   - Subtitle: the customer name only, with no " · ".
   - One filled button: "Confirm date".
   - Details tab: a Customer card (Customer, Contact on site, Contact phone in local format, Related complaint
     = complaint X's number), a Site card and a Visit card.
   - A History tab.
8. Save is refused because complaint X already has a live service job.
9. The second job saves with no related complaint (e.g. SV26/11-0002).
10. Confirm is refused with "Enter who agreed by name." directly under Agreed by. The date field reads
    dd/mm/yyyy, with no browser date box.
11. The job updates:
    - Status: "Confirmed".
    - Scheduled: "dd/mm/yyyy, 10:00 am" for tomorrow.
    - Agreed by: "Customer on phone".
    - Main button: "Assign technician".
12. Technician reads "Test Tech A", and "Test Tech B" was offered too. The main button becomes "On the way".
13. The board shows tomorrow as dd/mm/yyyy. The "Test Tech A" column holds the job card: job no., 10:00 am,
    Kajang, Confirmed. "Waiting for a date" lists only the second job (Proposed).
14. Status goes back to "Proposed". Scheduled and Agreed by read "-". Technician still reads "Test Tech A".
15. History lists these entries, newest first, each with who and when:
    - created (Proposed)
    - Confirmed, with the date and agreed by
    - Technician "(empty)" -> "Test Tech A"
    - Confirmed -> Proposed, with the reason
16. Status is "Confirmed" again, with today's date and "Customer on WhatsApp".
17. Technician reads "Test Tech B".
18. Each press moves the status:
    - On the way.
    - Arrived, with the time stamped.
    - Completed, with the time stamped.
    - Verified, with the time stamped and no main button left.
19. The same dialog as Create opens, filled in. The new phone shows in local format, and History shows the
    change.
20. Every header sorts asc, desc and off, and the order holds on page 2 when there is one. Each row is one
    line. There is a filled "Create" and no row icon.
21. The filters combine, and search finds the Kajang job.
22. The pager follows the filtered, sorted order.
23. The menu shows only the moves the job's status allows, plus "Open related complaint" when the job has one.
24. "Test Tech B" offers no Delete, only Edit and Archive, because it is in use. After Archive it shows
    "Inactive".
25. "Test Tech B" is not offered on Assign. The verified job and the board still show its name.
26. Only "Test Tech B" shows.
27. "Test Tech A" is deleted after the countdown, with no confirm dialog.
28. Complaint X looks and works exactly as on the live system. It has no Site card, no Service Jobs section
    and no "Raise service job".
29. Nothing is clipped and nothing scrolls sideways. The board's columns stack.
