# #2391 RELATED-RECORDS-B: project tabs work after Related

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Sign in as the owner (admin). No migration, no data setup.
- Use project P = any approved project with at least one Related record (a project with linked
  sponsorship forms works).

## Steps
1. From `/`, open Project Sales in the sidebar and open project P.
2. Click the Related tab.
3. Click the PO tab.
4. Click Related again, then click SO, then Documents, then Proposals, then Samples Request, then
   Stakeholders, then Tasks, then Activity, then Overview (click Related again between each).
5. Click Related, click the Number column header once, then click any row to open its record.
6. Press the browser Back button.
7. Click the PO tab.
8. Open project P's address with `?tab=sponsorships` at the end, then click the PO tab.
9. Narrow the window to about 375 px and repeat steps 2 and 3.

## Expected
1. Project P opens on Overview.
2. The Related grid shows.
3. The PO tab opens (before the fix it stayed on Related, showing "No related records").
4. Every click opens the tab clicked; Overview opens last and the address has no `tab=`.
5. The record's own page opens.
6. You are back on P's Related tab, still sorted by Number.
7. The PO tab opens.
8. Related opens showing only sponsorship forms (address shows `tab=related`); PO then opens.
9. Same as steps 2 and 3; no sideways scroll on the page.
