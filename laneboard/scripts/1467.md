# Hand test #1467 (PICKER-ESCALATION)

Owner ruling 4 Oct 2026: "any no answer should get the escalation question, that's the gist".
A contact who may escalate gets the escalation offer on an order enquiry that finds nothing,
after a customer picker and inside an order list that was shown. A contact barred from
escalation gets "Please refer to your salesman." instead.

crew test copy

## Setup

1. Sign in as an admin who can open System Management > Chatbot Console and a contact's page.
2. Contact A: Jayden Loo (Respond 404285551). On the contact page, "Chatbot hands over to support teams" is ON.
3. Contact B: any dealer contact with "Chatbot hands over to support teams" OFF that can ask about orders (Order Enquiries access).
4. Console dry runs feed the parser's recent history for the same contact: an earlier escalated test turn can make the first message read as a request for a person. If step 2 below answers "Your request is out of the scope of my ability ...", wait for that history to age out or use another escalation-allowed contact with no recent console turns.

## Steps

1. From the sidebar open System Management > Messaging > Chatbot Console and pick Contact A.
2. Send `SRTWT5844-GM delivery for yoo living`. Read the reply.
3. Send `1`. Read the reply.
4. Press Reset. Send `DO for cheng huat sentul`. Read the reply.
5. Send `what about DOs delivered in 2019?`. Read the reply. If its header still reads `Dates: all dates` and lists the orders again, the parser missed the year (parser v3 does this on some runs): send it once more.
6. Pick Contact B. Press Reset. Send `SRTWT5844-GM delivery for yoo living`. Read the reply.
7. Send `1`. Read the reply.

## Expected

1. The console opens with Contact A selected.
2. `Which customer do you mean? Please choose:` with two YOO LIVING HOUSE lines (each with `(SRT)`).
3. The Customer / Product / Dates header for YOO LIVING HOUSE, the "Here's what you want:" lines, then `But no order matched these. Would you like me to escalate to customer service team?` (or the company's team name), with a `Yes` button, and the routing picker under it when the company has customer-service members. It is NOT the single line `No orders matched these.`.
4. A delivery order list for Cheng Huat Sentul (header, then numbered orders).
5. The header with `Dates: 01/01/2019 to 31/12/2019`, then `But no order from 01/01/2019 to 31/12/2019 matched these. Reply 'all dates' to search without the date filter, or would you like me to escalate to customer service team?`. It is NOT the single line `No orders matched these.`.
6. The same two-line YOO LIVING HOUSE picker as step 2.
7. The header, the "Here's what you want:" lines, then `But no order matched these. Please refer to your salesman.` No escalation offer, no `Yes` button, no routing picker.
