# #2408 RELATED-RECORDS-B B: Back to project from every project tab

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Sign in as the owner (admin). No migration, no data setup.
- Use project P = a project with at least one sample request, one Related record, one PO and one
  SO (the owner's report used a PPF project with SR-000003).

## Steps
1. From `/`, open Project Sales in the sidebar and open project P.
2. Click the Samples Request tab, then click a sample request row.
3. Click "Back to project".
4. Click the Related tab, then click a stock inquiry, complaint, purchase request or new product
   application row.
5. Click "Back to project".
6. Click the PO tab, open a purchase order, then click "Back to project".
7. Click the SO tab, open a sales order, then click "Back to project".
8. Open Project Sales > Sample Requests from the sidebar and click any request.
9. Narrow the window to about 375 px and repeat steps 2 and 3.

## Expected
2. The sample request opens; the top right button reads "Back to project" (not "Back to sample
   requests").
3. Project P opens on the Samples Request tab.
4. The record opens with "Back to project" at the top right.
5. Project P opens on the Related tab.
6. "Back to project" sits at the top right of the PO; it returns to P's PO tab.
7. Same for the SO; it returns to P's SO tab.
8. Opened from its own list, the request shows "Back to sample requests" as before.
9. Same as steps 2 and 3; the button fits, no sideways scroll.
