# Hand test: PR #2280 ACC-COPY-TO-PRODUCTS (copy a product's accessories list to products you pick)

The button copies the list of the product you are on to the products you tick. A product that already has a list
keeps it and gets only the entries it is missing, at the end. Each copied entry keeps the source's own name, spec,
remark and picture choice. The box style goes only to products still on "Tag template's own box". Products only,
never combos.

crew test copy

## Setup
1. Sign in with your own login (Products edit).
2. Pick three bathtubs of the same category and write their codes down:
   - SOURCE: has an Accessories included list (for example the pressure pump, soap and bath bomb set, mixer tap),
     ideally with one entry edited for this list ("Own text") and a Box style set.
   - EMPTY: has no accessories.
   - OWN: give it, before the test, one of SOURCE's accessories with its own remark (pencil, Remark "my words"),
     plus one accessory SOURCE does not have, and pick a Box style different from SOURCE's.
3. Note SOURCE's entries in order, and OWN's two entries.

## Steps
1. From the sidebar open Products > All Products, open SOURCE.
2. Scroll to Accessories included; click "Copy to other products".
3. Open Filters, Category: pick the bathtubs' category. Close the filter.
4. Search EMPTY's code; tick it. Clear the search, search OWN's code; tick it.
5. Try ticking SOURCE itself.
6. Click Next and read the sentences.
7. Click Back, then Next again, then Copy.
8. Open EMPTY's page (Products > All Products, search, open), scroll to Accessories included.
9. Open OWN's page, scroll to Accessories included.
10. On OWN's page, open the history / audit trail.
11. Optional: remove the copied entries from EMPTY and OWN (X on each), and set their Box style back.

## Expected
- Step 2: the dialog title reads "Copy N entries to other products" (N = SOURCE's entry count); the grid shows
  Product Code, Product Name, Category, Brand; every header sorts.
- Step 4: the footer reads "2 products picked" (ticks survive the search change).
- Step 5: SOURCE's box is greyed out and does not tick.
- Step 6: "2 products picked.", "1 product with no list gets the whole list.", "1 product already has a list: its
  entries stay, the missing ones are added at the end.", "<N + N-1> entries will be added, 1 entry already there
  is left as it is.", and, if SOURCE has a box style, "The box style is set on 1 product that had none.".
- Step 7: toast "Accessories copied to 2 products"; the dialog closes; SOURCE's own list is unchanged.
- Step 8: EMPTY lists SOURCE's entries in SOURCE's order, the edited one marked "Own text" with SOURCE's text;
  Box style = SOURCE's.
- Step 9: OWN lists its two own entries first (the shared one still carries "my words"), then SOURCE's other
  entries in SOURCE's order; Box style = OWN's own, unchanged.
- Step 10: the trail shows the added accessory rows under OWN, by you.
- A user without Products edit sees no "Copy to other products" button.
