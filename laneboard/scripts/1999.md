# Hand test: PR #1999 AGENTIC-P3 (agent write actions in the Chatbot Console)

The console's Agent tab can now make changes, not only read. The agent never writes on its own: it
proposes the change, the reply lists every change, and nothing happens until you click Confirm (or
answer yes in the next message). Changes run as the contact's linked user, and only when you, at the
console, also hold the permission the change needs. Three kinds of change are in this PR: customer asks
(mark done / reopen / add a note), sales opportunities (create, change stage, mark Lost with a reason)
and dealer contacts' access (Business owner <-> Employee, resign, and undo). Today's bot and WhatsApp are
unchanged.

## Setup
Crew prepares the copy. The owner runs no commands.

1. The copy runs the PR head with the two agent migrations applied (crew-migration comment on the PR) and
   a Gemini key in the AI assistant settings.
2. Sign in to the CRM as the owner (admin).
3. Crew names one internal staff contact with AI replies allowed, Agent mode on, and a linked active user
   who is a salesperson with at least one open customer ask, one open sales opportunity and one dealer
   contact (a contact linked to one of his customers) whose role is Employee. Below: "Contact S", "ask A",
   "opportunity O", "dealer D" (crew gives D's phone number).

## Steps
1. CRM sidebar: System > Messaging > Chatbot Console. Click the `Agent` tab and pick Contact S.
2. Send: `show my open customer asks`
3. Send: `mark the first one done, note: called the customer, stock confirmed`
4. Without clicking anything, open Sales > Customer Asks in another tab and find ask A.
5. Back in the console, click `Confirm change` under the reply from step 3.
6. Refresh Sales > Customer Asks.
7. Send: `move opportunity <O's title> to the next stage`
8. Click `Cancel` under that reply.
9. Send: `mark opportunity <O's title> as lost`
10. If the agent asks for a reason, answer `customer bought from a competitor`; click `Confirm change`.
11. Send: `<D's phone> change to Business owner`
12. Answer `yes go ahead` as a message (do not click Confirm).
13. Open Users & Access > Respond.io Contacts, find dealer D and look at its access types.
14. Send: `undo that`, then click `Confirm change`.
15. Send: `<D's phone> resign`, then click `Confirm change`.
16. Send: `undo the resign`, then click `Confirm change`.
17. Send: `<a phone of a contact under ANOTHER salesperson> change to Business owner`
18. Send: `mark the first ask done`, wait for the proposal, then wait 11 minutes and click `Confirm change`.

## Expected
1. The Agent tab as in #1982, with the conversation of Contact S.
2. A list of S's open asks (customer, product, age). No UUID anywhere.
3. A reply ending with `I will make these changes when you confirm:`, one numbered line naming the ask and the note in full, and `Reply yes to go ahead, or tell me what to change.` Under it: `Confirm change` and `Cancel`.
4. Ask A is still open: nothing was written by the proposal.
5. A `Done` badge and `Done. I made these changes:` with the same line; the buttons are gone.
6. Ask A is Done with the note; its history names S's linked user.
7. A proposal line `Opportunity "<O>": <old stage> -> <new stage>`.
8. A `Cancelled` badge, `Cancelled. Nothing was changed.`; O keeps its stage on Sales > Opportunities.
9. The agent asks for the Lost reason (or proposes with one if you gave it).
10. `Done`; O is Lost with that reason on Sales > Opportunities.
11. A proposal line `<phone> <name>: Employee -> Business owner (Sorento)` (the company may differ).
12. The agent confirms from your message: `Done. I made these changes:` with the same line.
13. D now holds the dealer access type(s) as well as end_user.
14. The proposal is `Business owner (Sorento) -> Employee`; after Confirm D is back to end_user only.
15. The proposal lists `AI replies on -> off` and `unlinked from <customer code>`; after Confirm D has AI replies off and is no longer linked to that customer. Contact and history remain.
16. The proposal re-links D to the same customer and turns AI replies back on; after Confirm D is as before step 15.
17. The agent says that contact is not one of yours and proposes nothing (no name of the other contact is shown).
18. `This proposal expired. Ask again to get a fresh one.`; the ask is unchanged.
