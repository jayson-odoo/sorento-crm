3. Chatbot Console contact: one that holds the field reveal keys `scm.low_stock_report` and `purchase_orders.supplier` (Contacts > the contact > Access > Field reveals). Call it BUYER.

5. Each report turn runs a reorder plan, and the bot allows 5 per contact per 10 minutes. If a turn answers the rate limit line, wait 10 minutes and repeat it.
