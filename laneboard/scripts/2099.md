# Hand test: PR #2099 PS-REGISTRATION-V3 (registration selections, party contacts, lead source, customer optional)

Register a project now picks almost everything from lists: the parties on the job (several subcontractors,
trading houses and tender parties; one consultant and one interior designer), the people at each party, the
lead source (required) and the area (the districts of the state). The customer is optional. A party's people
are kept on the party's page. The WhatsApp send picks a party of the project, and a number typed for one with
none is saved to it.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
Apply the crew-migration SQL from PR #2099 to the copy's DB (two tables, two columns, the lead-source list and the
district lists). Sign in as a project sales user who holds projects.projects.edit and projects.parties.edit.
Pick one approved project that already has a sent quotation revision (any), for steps 17-22.

## Steps
1. Open Project Sales > Parties.
2. Click the Contacts column header.
3. Click Add party, create "CONTOH Subcon A" with type Subcontractor, then "CONTOH Subcon B" (Subcontractor) and "CONTOH Arkitek" (Architect).
4. Open "CONTOH Arkitek" and, in Contacts, click Add contact, type name "Ar. Example One", leave phone empty, click Add contact.
5. Add a second contact "Example Two" with phone 0130002222.
6. Click Delete on "Example Two", then click Cancel before the countdown ends.
7. Go to Projects and click Register a project.
8. Type title "CONTOH Residensi Ujian Fasa 1", click Check, then Next.
9. Look at the Register project button without touching anything else.
10. Under Lead source, customer and salesperson, pick Lead source "Referral". Leave Customer empty.
11. Open Details, pick State "Selangor", then open Area.
12. Pick Area "Petaling", then change State to "Johor".
13. Under Parties and contacts, pick Architect "CONTOH Arkitek", then under "Contacts at CONTOH Arkitek" pick "Ar. Example One".
14. In Subcontractors pick "CONTOH Subcon A" and "CONTOH Subcon B".
15. Click Register project.
16. On the new project page, look at the Parties card, the Where this came from card and the Stakeholders tab.
17. Open the approved project from Setup, open its sent quotation, and click Send via WhatsApp on the revision.
18. Open Send to.
19. Pick a person or party that shows "No number on file".
20. Type 0145550000 in To and click Open WhatsApp.
21. Close WhatsApp, reopen Send via WhatsApp, and open Send to again.
22. Pick a party that already has a number, change To to 0199999999, and click Open WhatsApp.

## Expected
1. The Parties list loads, 25 rows per page, with a Contacts column.
2. The list re-sorts by contact count across all pages (the request carries sort=contact_count), not only the page on screen.
3. The three parties are created; the Type filter offers Subcontractor, Interior designer and Tender party.
4. "Ar. Example One" is listed with Phone "-" (no phone needed).
5. "Example Two" is listed with phone 0130002222; the Contacts column on the list shows "2 contacts" for CONTOH Arkitek.
6. The button counts down "Deleting in 10s" with Cancel; after Cancel, "Example Two" stays.
7. The Register a project page opens on step 1 Check.
8. "No clash", then step 2 Details shows four sections: Who and what, Details, Parties and contacts, Lead source, customer and salesperson.
9. Register project is disabled (Lead source is required and empty). Customer has no asterisk.
10. Register project becomes enabled with no customer picked.
11. Details has no Location field. Area was disabled until a state was picked, and now lists Selangor's districts (Gombak, Hulu Langat, Klang, Petaling, Sepang and others).
12. Area clears when the state changes; Area now lists Johor's districts (Batu Pahat, Johor Bahru, Kluang and others).
13. A "Contacts at CONTOH Arkitek" picker appears under the parties and lists "Ar. Example One" and "Example Two".
14. Both subcontractors show as picked, plus a contacts picker for each.
15. The project is registered and its page opens.
16. Parties shows Architect "CONTOH Arkitek" and Subcontractors "CONTOH Subcon A, CONTOH Subcon B"; Where this came from shows Lead source "Referral"; Stakeholders lists "Ar. Example One" (firm CONTOH Arkitek). No customer is set.
17. The Send R<n> via WhatsApp dialog opens with Send to, To and Message.
18. Send to lists each party of the project as "<name> (<role>)" and each registered person as "<person>, <party>".
19. To stays empty and the line "No number on file. The number you type is saved to <name>." shows.
20. WhatsApp opens on +60145550000 with the message; a toast says "Number saved".
21. The same pick now shows the number on file and fills To with +60145550000.
22. WhatsApp opens on 0199999999 for this send, but no "Number saved" toast appears and the party keeps its original number.
