# PR 2343 hand test: the AI panel answers "my projects" from the asker's own projects

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- The AI panel agent needs a working provider key on the test copy.
- User S: a salesperson on the agent path (holds `system.ai_assistant_chat.use` and `system.ai_assistant_chat.agent`) who owns at least 1 project. Note how many projects S owns: N.
- Note the total number of approved projects in the Projects list as an admin: T (dev data showed 16).
- On the dev DB the `salesperson` role holds BOTH `projects.projects.view` and `projects.projects.view_own`, so S sees all T projects in the app. That is a role setting, not this PR. For step 7, a user L holding `projects.projects.view_own` only (and the two AI slugs above) who owns at least 1 project: note L's count, M.
- No migration and no SQL is needed.

## Steps
1. Sign in as an admin, open Members from the sidebar, and use View as on S.
2. From the home page, open the AI assistant panel.
3. Send: which of my projects have quotations
4. Wait for the answer, then click View trace if it is offered to you (admin).
5. Send: how many projects do I own
6. Send: how many projects are there and who owns them
7. Stop viewing as S, use View as on L, open the AI assistant panel and send: how many projects are there and who owns them
8. Stop viewing as L and, as the admin, send: how many projects are there and who owns them

## Expected
- Step 3: the answer is about S's own projects only (at most N projects named), never "no projects assigned to you (out of the T total)".
- Step 4: the trace's first crm_projects_list call carries owner_user_ids (shown as [id]), with no refused call and no crm_resolve round before it.
- Step 5: the answer is N.
- Step 6: the answer is T with every owner, because S can see all T in the Projects list today (role holds `projects.projects.view`).
- Step 7: the answer is M, and only L is named as an owner.
- Step 8: the answer is T with every owner, as before this PR.
