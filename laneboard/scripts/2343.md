## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- The AI panel agent needs a working provider key on the test copy.
- User S: a salesperson on the agent path (holds `system.ai_assistant_chat.use` and `system.ai_assistant_chat.agent`), e.g. Jimmy. On dev every salesperson owns 0 projects, so N = 0. The admin owns 8 on dev.
- As the admin, note the number of approved projects in the Projects list (T, 16 on dev), and the count per owner (dev: 8 and 8).
- On dev the `salesperson` role holds BOTH `projects.projects.view` and `projects.projects.view_own`, so S sees all T projects in the app. That is a role setting, not this PR.
- Step 9 is SKIPPED on dev: it needs a role holding `projects.projects.view_own` without `projects.projects.view` (with `system.ai_assistant_chat.use` and `system.ai_assistant_chat.agent`), and the owner manages roles. Run it only where such a role exists; L is a user with it who owns M projects.
- No migration and no SQL is needed from this PR.

## Steps
1. As the admin, from the home page open Users & Access > People > Administrative Users in the sidebar.
2. In S's row menu, click "Impersonate user".
3. Open the AI assistant panel and send: which of my projects have quotations
4. Send: how many projects do I own
5. Send: how many projects are there and who owns them
6. Click "Exit impersonation".
7. As the admin (not impersonating, so View trace shows), open the AI assistant panel and send: which of my projects have quotations
8. Click View trace on that answer and open Tool calls.
9. (Skipped on dev, see Setup.) In L's row menu (Administrative Users), click "Impersonate user", open the AI assistant panel and send: how many projects are there and who owns them
10. Click "Exit impersonation" and, as the admin, send: how many projects are there and who owns them

## Expected
- Step 3: says S owns no projects, and nothing else: no unrequested totals (never "Across the CRM, there are 16 ..." or other owners), no "(not verified)", never a sentence with a gap in it.
- Step 4: "0" (or "no projects"), and the reply has no "(not verified)" and no "I left out a figure" line.
- Step 5: T (16) projects, with every owner and THEIR count as in the Projects list (dev: 8 and 8), never a split that disagrees with it. Projects are called "projects" (never "project pursuits") and each is named as in the Projects list (its short name), the same way in every answer.
- Step 8: the first row is crm_projects_list, ok, with the asker's own row count (Jimmy: 0 rows / nothing found; the admin: 8 rows, never 9), and no crm_resolve before it. A later "(extra calls) denied" row is the per-round call cap, not a refusal.
- Step 9 (skipped on dev): M projects, and L is the only owner named.
- Step 10: T with every owner and the same per-owner counts as step 5.
- Anywhere: if the assistant could not check a figure, the answer leaves that sentence out and ends with "I left out a figure I could not check against the data." It never shows "(not verified)" inside a sentence.
