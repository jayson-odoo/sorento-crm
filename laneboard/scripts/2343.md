# PR 2343 hand test: the AI panel answers "my projects" and "who owns them" from what the asker can see

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- The AI panel agent needs a working provider key on the test copy. Restart the MCP server on the test copy (its tool descriptions changed).
- User S: a salesperson on the agent path (holds `system.ai_assistant_chat.use` and `system.ai_assistant_chat.agent`), e.g. Jimmy. Note how many projects S owns, as the Projects list shows at test time: N (0 on dev today).
- As the admin, note as the Projects list shows at test time: the number of approved projects (T), each owner's count, and how many the admin owns (A).
- On dev the `salesperson` role holds BOTH `projects.projects.view` and `projects.projects.view_own`, so S sees all T projects in the app. That is a role setting, not this PR.
- Step 10 is SKIPPED on dev: it needs a role holding `projects.projects.view_own` without `projects.projects.view` (with the two AI slugs), and the owner manages roles. Run it only where such a role exists; L is a user with it who owns M projects.
- No migration and no SQL is needed from this PR.

## Steps
1. As the admin, from the home page open Users & Access > People > Administrative Users in the sidebar.
2. In S's row menu, click "Impersonate user".
3. From the Users page, open the AI assistant panel and send: which of my projects have quotations
4. Send: how many projects do I own
5. Send: how many projects are there and who owns them
6. Open Project Sales > Projects in the sidebar (the Projects list is on screen), open the AI assistant panel, start a new chat and send: which of my projects have quotations
7. Send: how many projects do I own
8. Click "Exit impersonation".
9. As the admin (not impersonating, so View trace shows), open the AI assistant panel and send: which of my projects have quotations, then click View trace on that answer and open Tool calls.
10. (Skipped on dev, see Setup.) In L's row menu (Administrative Users), click "Impersonate user", open the AI assistant panel and send: how many projects are there and who owns them
11. Click "Exit impersonation" and, as the admin, send: how many projects are there and who owns them

## Expected
- Steps 3 and 6: S's own projects only (with N = 0: S has no projects), and nothing else: no unrequested totals or breakdowns (never "Across the CRM, there are ..." or other owners' projects), no "(not verified)", never a sentence with a gap in it. Never another owner's projects presented as S's.
- Steps 4 and 7: N, exactly as the Projects list shows S owning; never another owner's count.
- Step 5: T projects, with every owner and THEIR count as the Projects list shows at test time. Projects are called "projects" and each is named by its short name as in the Projects list, the same way in every answer.
- Step 9: the first row is crm_projects_list, ok, with the admin's own row count as the Projects list shows (A rows; "nothing found" when A is 0), and no crm_resolve before it. A later "(extra calls) denied" row is the per-round call cap, not a refusal.
- Step 10 (skipped on dev): M projects, and L is the only owner named.
- Step 11: T with every owner and the same per-owner counts as step 5, and no per-project breakdown nobody asked for.
- Anywhere: if the assistant could not check a figure, the answer leaves that sentence out and ends with "I left out a figure I could not check against the data." It never shows "(not verified)" inside a sentence.
