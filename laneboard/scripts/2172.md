# Hand test: PR #2172 PRICETAG-COMBO-RENDER (price tags render every combo shape)

A combo names its own fixed parts, choice groups and upgrades. A tag template points at positions
in the combo (main product, fixed part n, pick k of a group, upgrade n), and what it needs is read
from its own layers. A template fits a combo only if it prints every fixed part; a group is "pick k
of n". A combo line prints the combo's stored price or nothing, never the parts added up. A
discontinued part is marked and blocks Approve. A line with no fitting template is escalated.

## Setup
- The shared dev DB `sorento_prod_1020` is already seeded (crew, 5 Nov). To re-seed, from the test
  copy's backend folder: `venv/bin/python scripts/seed_pricetag_combo_shapes_dev.py --confirm-db
  sorento_prod_1020` (dry run), then the same with `--apply`. It ensures the combos "Basin + tap",
  "Basin + tap colour" and "Sink + taps with top-up", the four "Combo 105 x 148: ..." templates, and
  links each template to every combo it fits.
- Data used below (dev): "Sink + taps with top-up" on SRTKS7061, selling price 849, upgrade
  SRTKT71SS top-up 150. "Basin + tap" on SRTWB1419-BL (RM 540) with fixed part SRTWT7449-BL
  (RM 480), no stored combo price. SRTBF11829's combo has four fixed parts: SRTWB2380
  (discontinued), SRTMCB8083-BL, SRTWT6203-GM, SRTW2600, and no choice group.
- A salesperson who can use the Price Tag Request form, and a marketing user for Dealer Kit > Tag
  Templates and Master Data > Products. Window about 1280 px.

## Steps
1. New Price Tag Request. Add SRTKS7061, package "Sink + taps with top-up".
2. In the Template select pick "Combo 105 x 148: 2 picks".
3. In the Kitchen tap row pick one tap and read the hint under the row. Pick a second tap, then
   try a third.
4. Pick SRTKT71SS in the Upgrade row and set the line to SP. Click the eye icon.
5. Pick SRTKT1871SS then SRTKT1862SS as the two taps (remove the others first). Save Draft, close
   the page, reopen the draft and click the eye icon on line 1.
6. Start a SECOND New Price Tag Request. Add SRTWB1419-BL, package "Basin + tap", and read the
   Template line.
7. On that line (its template is already set), set it to SP, leave the selling price box
   empty and click Approve and read the reason on the line. Type 699 and click the eye icon.
8. Clear the typed price, set the line to LP and read the List price cell and the reason.
9. Add SRTBF11829 to the same request and read its line.
10. Click Escalate to marketing, type a note and send it.
11. As marketing, open Master Data > Products > SRTBF11829, its combo, and open the "Price tag
    templates" picker. Try to tick "Combo 105 x 148: 2 fixed parts".
12. In Dealer Kit > Tag Templates open "Combo 105 x 148: 2 picks". In the Preview combo bar pick
    "Sink + taps with top-up", select the picture layer of the second tile and open its Product
    select.
13. On the request form, find the "Preview" button right of the "Lines" label. With no item picked
    it is disabled. Add SRTKS7061, then click Preview.
14. In Dealer Kit > Tag Templates open a 2-fixed template (for example "Combo 105 x 148: 2 fixed
    parts"), select a text layer, click Insert field, and in the "Combo positions" group insert
    "Fixed part 1 code" and "Fixed part 2 code" into the text. Preview it on a 2-fixed combo.
    (If the dev data has no 2-fixed combo, preview "Basin + tap", whose one fixed part prints for
    {{slot.fixed.1.code}} and whose second prints nothing.)
15. On a new request, look at the order of the cards after you pick the customer.
16. On the SRTKS7061 line delete the Upgrade row (trash icon), open "Add part" and pick Upgrade
    under "This package"; then delete the Kitchen tap row and look at the line's message block.

## Expected
- Step 2: the template reads "105 x 148 mm. Needs 2 from Kitchen tap"; the Kitchen tap row is a
  multi-select with "Pick 2" and no "Select all".
- Step 3: after one pick the hint under the row reads "Kitchen tap: pick 1 more." (a live hint; if
  Approve is clicked it also shows in the line's red row); after the second pick the hint is gone and every other tap
  is greyed and cannot be picked.
- Step 4: the tag shows SRTKS7061 and the two taps, each with its own code and its own list price,
  and the price RM 999 (the combo's 849 plus the 150 top-up of the picked upgrade), never the parts
  added up. This starter has no upgrade position, so SRTKT71SS itself is not drawn.
- Step 5: the draft reopens with ONE Kitchen tap row holding SRTKT1871SS then SRTKT1862SS, and the
  preview shows the two taps in that order.
- Step 6: only one template fits this combo, so there is no select: the line shows the label
  "Template: Combo 105 x 148: 1 fixed part". The templates that do not fit are not offered.
- Step 7: nothing is shown until Approve is clicked; the click opens the section and the line's
  red row reads "No combo selling price." and nothing is sent. Approve is never greyed, and nothing
  sits under the buttons. With a promotion picked the Selling price cell shows, up front, "Clear
  the promotion to type a price". With 699 typed, Approve is on and the tag shows
  SRTWB1419-BL with LP RM 540, SRTWT7449-BL with LP RM 480, and RM 699 as the price.
- Step 8: List price shows "-"; clicking Approve puts "No combo list price." in the line's red row; Escalate to marketing stays clickable.
- Step 9: SRTBF11829's line has no Template select or label and its eye icon is disabled; its fixed
  part SRTWB2380 shows "Discontinued". Nothing says "No tag template yet" until Approve is clicked; the click shows "No tag template yet." and, for the discontinued part, "SRTWB2380 is discontinued." in the line's red row; the package warning sits in the same block.
- Step 10: the request goes to marketing.
- Step 11: in the picker "Combo 105 x 148: 2 fixed parts" is greyed with "Prints 2 of this combo's
  4 fixed parts", "Combo 105 x 148: 1 fixed part" with "Prints 1 of this combo's 4 fixed parts",
  and the picks templates with "Needs a choice group; this combo has none". None can be ticked; a
  save that tries one is refused with the same reason.
- Step 12: the Product select lists "Main product", then "Kitchen tap, pick 1", "Kitchen tap, pick
  2" ... each with its product code. The ungrouped positions come first ("Main product", then "Upgrade 1" with SRTKT71SS on
  this combo), and the picks follow under their "Kitchen tap" group; "Kitchen tap, pick 2" is the selected one. The price text prints "RM 849" with its own RM.
- Step 13: Preview opens the whole request's tags in a popup (nothing is drawn under the Lines
  table); clicking Approve where a tag cannot be drawn shows "No tag template yet." on that line.
- Step 14: the Insert field list shows the previewed combo's own positions with codes (for example
  "Fixed part 1 code: SRTMCB8083-BL"), the preview line prints the codes of both positions, and a
  position the combo lacks prints nothing.
- Step 15: the cards run Customer, Price, Sales Order & Lines, Additional Information. Picking the
  customer opens Price; choosing List or Selling price opens Sales Order & Lines.
- Step 16: Add part lists the package's missing rows first ("Upgrade" alone after only the Upgrade
  was deleted) under "This package", then the catalogue search; picking Upgrade puts back just that
  row, after Kitchen tap, without touching Kitchen tap. With Kitchen tap deleted, the "Package
  warning" (with Restore) shows in the same block under the line as any red message.
