# Hand test: PR #1931 SALES-TOTAL-ASK (a sales total asks the basis, never defaults to Invoiced)

Owner, 27 Oct ~23:35 MYT: "total sales should ask". A chatbot sales total that names no basis is asked
"Invoiced, delivered, ordered or outstanding?" (Invoiced first), the same line a sales ranking asks, and shows no
figure until it is answered. Before this PR, "total sales Jan 2026" answered straight away on the invoiced basis.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
1. The test copy runs this branch's backend on the shared dev DB. No migration.
2. A staff test contact with the Sales report reveal (`sales_orders.sales_report`), in ONE company (Sorento), whose
   recent `chatbot.turns` row the console runner can borrow (any contact the bot has answered on this copy).
3. From `sorento_crm_backend/`, every step is one `--say` of
   `venv/bin/python scripts/chatbot_console_check.py --base-url http://127.0.0.1:<backend port> --say "<text>"`;
   repeat `--say` in ONE command so the turns are one conversation (step 1 + 2 together, step 3 + 4 together, ...).

## Steps
1. Say `total sales Jan 2026`.
2. In the same conversation, say `1`.
3. New conversation: say `total sales Jan 2026`.
4. In the same conversation, say `invoiced`.
5. New conversation: say `total invoiced sales Jan 2026`.
6. New conversation: say `total sales Jan 2026`, then `outstanding`.

## Expected
- Step 1: the reply is exactly `Invoiced, delivered, ordered or outstanding?` and nothing else; the trace line shows
  no tool call (no `crm_sales_analysis`).
- Step 2: the trace shows `crm_sales_analysis` with `basis` `invoiced`, `date_from` `2026-01-01`, `date_to`
  `2026-01-31`; the reply is the sales table for 01/01/2026 to 31/01/2026 whose basis line starts `Invoiced (invoices,
  cash sales and debit notes less credit notes, excluding tax)`, and the Excel file.
- Steps 3 and 4: the same question at step 3, the same invoiced figure at step 4 as at step 2.
- Step 5: no question; `crm_sales_analysis` runs at once with `basis` `invoiced` (a basis the message names is
  never asked).
- Step 6: after `outstanding` the trace shows `crm_report_ask` with `basis` `outstanding`, `group_by` `channel`,
  January 2026; the reply is outstanding orders by channel. Never `crm_sales_analysis`.
- Any step: never a sales figure printed before the basis is answered or named.
