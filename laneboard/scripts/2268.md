# Hand test: PR #2268 DEALER-ACCESS-PORTAL (a salesperson grants and removes dealer access through the agent)

The salesperson sends the agent the same message they used to send the owner (people, phones, Business Owner or
Employee, which dealer). The agent proposes one row per person; nothing changes until Confirm. Accounts come ONLY from
the salesperson's own customers; a dealer name never links on its own (the agent asks which accounts). Business Owner
= the company's "Business Owner" tag (dealer promotions); Employee = the base access only. Removing works the same way.
Live x3: run the whole script three times (new thread each time, fresh phones each run), as crew did for #2253.

## Setup
1. The copy runs head of PR #2268 or later, migrated (`dap_0001_dealer_access`): role salesperson holds
   `user_management.contacts.edit_own`; tags "Sorento Business Owner" and "Mocha Business Owner" exist.
2. The OWNER enables the test salesperson first (no migration does this; owner 9 Nov ~10:45): give SP1's role or
   user the AI assistant permissions (`system.ai_assistant_chat.use` and `system.ai_assistant_chat.agent`) and, for a
   WhatsApp run, tick the agent switch on SP1's own contact. Then sign in as that salesperson user, who has customers
   in Sorento (call the user SP1). Note SP1's dealer D1 (a Sorento dealer with 2 or more accounts, e.g. A/C I and A/C III) and the
   codes of those accounts.
3. Pick a dealer D2 served in Sorento by ANOTHER salesperson (not SP1), and the phone of one of D2's linked contacts
   who is a BUSINESS OWNER there (holds dealer + cabana_dealer, or the "Sorento Business Owner" tag) and whose AI
   replies are ON (P_OTHER). Note P_OTHER's AI replies, hand-over, packing list and brands before you start.
4. Three invented phones per run that are not contacts yet: P1, P2, P3 (+6011 numbers ending in the run number).
   Never use a real dealer's phone for P1-P3. Outbound stays off on the copy (no WhatsApp is sent by this test).
5. BEFORE step 5: confirm no Respond.io key resolves on the copy (the copy's `respond_workspaces` keys must not
   decrypt with its FIELD_ENCRYPTION_KEY, and RESPOND_API_KEY unset), or stop the worker's `respond_io` queue for the
   test. Otherwise the post-grant job would create P1-P3 as contacts in the LIVE Respond.io workspace.
6. The message (owner style, as the Google Form rows read):

```
Pls register for <D1 name as the salesperson writes it, e.g. without Sdn Bhd>, Sorento
1) Ali Test, P1, business owner
2) Mei Test, P2, employee
3) Raju Test, P3, ?
```

## Steps
0. Before the owner enables SP1 (optional check): as SP1 send the step 2 message. Expected: the normal "not enabled"
   behaviour (no agent proposal, no error page). Then the owner enables SP1 as in Setup 2.
1. From the sidebar, open the AI assistant panel and start a new thread.
2. Send the message above.
3. Read the reply (do not confirm).
4. Reply `all` (all of D1's listed accounts for every waiting row).
5. Read the reply and the Confirm card, click Confirm.
6. New message: `also add <D2 name> for Ali Test P1, owner, Sorento`.
7. New message: `add P_OTHER as employee for <D1 name>, Sorento`, pick `all`, confirm.
8. New message: `Mei Test P2 resigned, remove her access`, confirm.
9. New message: `Ali Test P1 is now just employee`, confirm.
10. New message: `make P_OTHER employee` (P_OTHER is a Business Owner through D2, so this is a real demote).
10b. New message: `turn off AI replies for P_OTHER`.
11. As admin, open each of P1, P2, P_OTHER on the contacts screen (Access, Tags, Customers, History).

## Expected
- Step 3: one row per person. Rows 1 and 2 WAIT with a pick listing D1's Sorento accounts that belong to SP1 only
  (never another salesperson's accounts, never a Mocha account). Row 3 is not changed: "Unknown access: ?." (or "not given")
  (never guessed as employee). No name is linked without the pick.
- Step 4: the reply after `all` keeps row 3 as "Unknown access: ?." (never "is not in your message"), and the
  Confirm card repeats, for each picked row, `you wrote "<D1 name as typed>"` beside the D1 codes it links.
- Step 5: after Confirm, P1 and P2 are created contacts, linked to every picked D1 account. P1 carries the tag
  "Sorento Business Owner" and holds access types dealer, cabana_dealer, end_user; P2 holds end_user only and no
  owner tag. Both: company Sorento, AI replies on, stock replies on, the three enquiry agents, stock locations BRW +
  MWH. WhatsApp state: the Respond step is queued (on a copy without a Respond key it is a logged no-op).
- Step 6: D2's accounts are never linked or listed. The reply either says there is no such customer in your accounts,
  or (when D2's name shares its first word with one of SP1's own accounts) offers SP1's OWN accounts as a pick; never
  any of D2's accounts.
- Any row naming no account of SP1 (e.g. `add P9 as owner, Sorento` with no dealer): refused "Name one of your
  accounts for this person (code or name)." 
- Step 7: the row applies with the lines "also has access through another account" and "kept as they are: AI
  replies, stock, brands and media (another account also has this person)"; the other dealer's account is
  never named in the reply, the card, or the contact's history as seen by SP1. P_OTHER keeps its D2 link, and its
  AI replies, hand-over, packing list, stock and brand settings are unchanged (only the link, company, end_user,
  agents and segment are added). Asking for P_OTHER as OWNER instead is refused with the step 10 text.
- Step 8: P2 is unlinked from D1's accounts; since that was P2's last link, the card shows "AI replies on -> off".
- Step 9: P1 loses the "Sorento Business Owner" tag and the dealer, cabana_dealer types; keeps end_user and the links.
- Step 10: refused: "This person also has access through another account; ask the office to change their level."
  P_OTHER stays a Business Owner (types and tag unchanged).
- Step 10b: refused: "This person also has access through another account; ask the office to change their settings."
  P_OTHER's AI replies stay ON. No step anywhere proposes a change to P_OTHER's AI replies or other settings.
- Every refusal and every "Not changed" reason is the tool's own wording (the reply never paraphrases one
  without a tool call), and a pick table's "You wrote" column shows exactly what SP1 typed (never a re-searched
  or corrected name).
- Every dealer access reply (steps 3 to 10b) is a table, a refusal in the tool's words, or the Confirm card; never
  free text from the model. A reply of exactly "I need to check that first." means the model made no tool call
  (count it as a slip in the run notes).
- A row whose company SP1 did not write is refused: "Say which company this person is for: Sorento or Mocha." (try
  once: a Mocha dealer's name with "Sorento" left out and the model sending SRT).
- A pick answers only the table just shown: send an unrelated message between a table and `all`, and the `all`
  links nothing from the older table (an old code is refused: "is from an earlier list that is closed now").
- Step 11: History shows one agent entry per change with SP1 as the actor. A staff member's phone (try SP1's own
  phone once in a later run) is refused: "This number belongs to a staff member."
- All three runs give the same outcomes (same rows waiting, same refusals, same wording).
