# Hand test #1447 (REPORT-ENGINE)

## URL
crew test copy (in-app Chatbot Console: System > Messaging > Chatbot Console)

## Setup

1. Apply `documentation/plans/chatbot/report-engine-crew-publish-prompt.sql` to the copy's DB. It is
   idempotent and inserts the parser prompt version whose body md5 is
   `ff6bb49471f85e38ee395ec43b6f7465`. Check it with
   `SELECT version FROM ai_prompt_versions WHERE name='chatbot_semantic_parser' AND md5(template)='ff6bb49471f85e38ee395ec43b6f7465';`.
2. Move the parser `production` label onto that version, either on the admin Prompts screen or with
   the label SQL posted on PR #1447. Without the move, the ranking still works through the
   deterministic reroute, but brand / category words may read differently.
3. The parser model must be `gpt-5.4-mini` (`ai_assistant_configs.model`). On `gpt-4o-mini` the
   ranking vocabulary is missed.
4. Pick a test contact that holds the `sales_orders.sales_report` field reveal and is NOT linked to
   any customer (staff). Pick a second contact with the same reveal that IS linked to a customer
   (dealer).
5. Cross-check numbers for step 1, quantities only (delivered DO lines, Sorento + Water Closet,
   2026). Add `AND o.company_id IN (<the contact's companies>)` on a multi-company DB:
   ```sql
   SELECT coalesce(sa.sales_agent, '(no agent)') AS agent, sum(ol.quantity) AS pcs
   FROM order_lines ol JOIN orders o ON o.id = ol.order_id
   JOIN products p ON p.id = ol.product_id JOIN brands b ON b.id = p.brand_id
   JOIN product_categories pc ON pc.id = p.category_id
   LEFT JOIN sales_orders so ON so.id = o.sales_order_id
   LEFT JOIN sales_agents sa ON sa.id = so.sales_agent_id
   WHERE o.is_cancelled IS FALSE AND o.deleted_at IS NULL AND o.order_number NOT LIKE 'REP%'
     AND o.order_date >= DATE '2026-01-01' AND o.order_date < DATE '2027-01-01'
     AND lower(b.brand_name) = 'sorento' AND lower(pc.category_name) = 'water closet'
   GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC;
   ```

6. The ranking route allows 10 rankings per contact per 10 minutes (security review). The staff
   steps below make 7. Rerunning the script straight away can show
   `Too many reports in the last 10 minutes.`, so wait 10 minutes or use another contact.

## Steps

Staff contact, one conversation, in order:

1. "who's the top 3 salesman for sorento water closet this year"
2. "top 3 salesman for sorento water closet this year by quantity"
3. "5"
4. "last month"
5. "top salesman for sorento last month"
6. Answer the bot's question with "3".
7. "top 3 salesman for cabana"
8. Answer the bot's question with "this year".
9. "top 10 sales items for sorento"
10. "top 1000 customers for sorento this year"

Dealer contact:

11. "top 3 salesman for sorento this year"
12. "top 3 products for sorento this year"

## Expected

1. Step 1 shows three rows ranked by amount (RM), with this header:
   `Top 3 sales agents by delivered sales, Brand <Sorento's name>; Category <Water Closet's name>, 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31`.
   The names are printed as stored, e.g. `Brand Sorento; Category WATER CLOSET`.
   - Each row reads `n. <AGENT> RM x, y pcs`. Then `and N more` (when there are more) and `Total RM ..., ... pcs` for the WHOLE set.
   - There is NO sales-agent filter in the header.
   - No "Top 3 selling items" reply and no WT agents used as a filter.
   - The pcs agree with the setup SQL for the same agents.
2. Step 2: the same header, with rows ordered by pcs (the SQL order).
3. Step 3: the same ranking (same brand, category and period) as top 5.
4. Step 4: the same ranking re-run for last month; the header shows last month's dates.
5. Step 5 has no number, so the bot asks `How many? For example top 5.` and nothing runs.
6. Step 6 runs top 3 for Sorento, last month.
7. Step 7 has no period, so the bot asks
   `Which period? For example this month, September, 2026, or 1 to 15 Sep.`. The bot never borrows the earlier period.
8. Step 8 runs top 3 sales agents for Cabana, 2026.
9. Step 9 is the top selling items report (`Top 10 selling items`), not a sales agent ranking.
10. Step 10 is a customer ranking (`Top ... customers by delivered sales, Brand <Sorento's name>, ...`). There is no "1 to 100" refusal; the ceiling is 1,000.
11. Step 11 (dealer): `That breakdown is not available for your account.`
12. Step 12 (dealer): only the dealer's own purchases, by product.
