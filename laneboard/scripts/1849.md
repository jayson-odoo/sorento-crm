## Setup

1. Sign in with your own admin account (admin, Project Sales Manager and Project Sales Coordinator hold the new "Approve Project Registrations" permission).
2. Pick a portal contact who is one of your salespeople and is linked to an active sales agent (Sales > Sales Agents shows the linked contact). Call it the salesperson contact.
3. Open that contact from the sidebar (User Management > Internal Users), and under Portal forms set "Project Registration" to "Always show".
4. In Project Sales > Pipeline, open project `PRJ-000002` (on this copy only PRJ-000002 and PRJ-000003 have a developer; use PRJ-000003 if 000002 is not open). Note its full title and its developer exactly as shown. Call it P. Crew has seeded the project embeddings on this copy, so no worker is needed.

## Steps

1. On the salesperson contact's row, open the actions menu and choose "Impersonate in portal".
2. In the portal, open the form selector and choose "Project Registration", then press New.
3. Read the Sales agent and Date fields without typing anything.
4. Press AI Extract, paste P's full title into the dialog and run it. Confirm and prefill.
5. Clear the State field and press Submit.
6. Fill what AI Extract left empty: type P's developer name in Developer (required) and pick P's state, or any state if P names none. Open Customer name, type a few letters of a customer that is NOT one of this salesperson's customers, then type one that is and pick it. Type Distributor name `Distributor D`. Press Submit.
7. Read the form after submit, then go back to the Project Registration list.
8. Stop impersonating. In the CRM, from `/`, open Project Sales > Pipeline from the sidebar, in board view. Read the "Awaiting approval" lane.
9. Switch to the grid view and read the new row. Then open Filters and pick "Awaiting approval".
10. Open the new project. Read the header and the lines under it.
11. Press the Possible duplicate link, then come back.
12. Press Approve.
13. Press Edit. Under Developer read the hint, then pick P's developer, keep the title exactly as P's title, and Save.
14. Press Approve.
15. Press the link in the refusal message, then come back.
16. Press Edit, add ` PHASE 2` at the end of the title, Save, then press Approve.
17. Read the header, then go back to the Pipeline board.
18. Impersonate the salesperson contact again and open the Project Registration list.
19. Register another one with any short title, a state and a developer, and submit it. In the CRM open it from the "Awaiting approval" lane, press Reject and give the reason `Already registered`. Look at the board's "Rejected" lane. Back in the portal, open it, change its title and submit again.
20. Shrink the window to phone width (375px) and repeat steps 2, 8 and 10.
