## Setup

1. Sign in with your own admin account (admin roles hold the new "Approve Project Registrations" permission).
2. Pick a portal contact who is one of your salespeople and is linked to an active sales agent (Sales > Sales Agents shows the linked contact). Call it the salesperson contact.
3. Open that contact from the sidebar (User Management > Internal Users), and under Portal forms set "Project Registration" to "Always show".
4. In Project Sales > Pipeline, open project `PRJ-000002` (on this copy only PRJ-000002 and PRJ-000003 have a developer; use PRJ-000003 if 000002 is not open). Note its full title and its developer exactly as shown. Call it P. Crew has seeded the project embeddings on this copy, so no worker is needed.

## Steps

1. On the salesperson contact's row, open the actions menu and choose "Impersonate in portal".
2. In the portal, open the form selector and choose "Project Registration", then press New.
3. Read the Sales agent and Date fields without typing anything.
4. Press AI Extract, paste P's full title into the dialog and run it. Confirm and prefill.
5. Clear the State field and press Submit.
6. Fill what AI Extract left empty: type P's developer name in Developer (Developer is required on submit) and pick P's state, or any state if P names none. Open Customer name, type a few letters of a customer that is NOT one of this salesperson's customers, then type one that is and pick it. Type Distributor name `Distributor D`. Press Submit.
7. Read the form after submit, then go back to the Project Registration list.
8. Stop impersonating. In the CRM, from `/`, open Project Sales > Registrations from the sidebar and read the new row.
9. Click the link in the row's Possible duplicate cell, then come back.
10. Open the registration. Read what the form shows.
11. In Developer pick P's developer, keep the title exactly as P's title, and press Approve.
12. Press the link in the refusal message, then come back.
13. Change the title by adding ` PHASE 2` at the end and press Approve.
14. Open the newly created project from Project Sales > Pipeline and read its overview.
15. Impersonate the salesperson contact again and open the Project Registration list.
16. Register another one with any short title, a state and a developer, submit it, then in the CRM open it, press Reject and give the reason `Already registered`. Back in the portal, open it.
17. Shrink the window to phone width (375px) and repeat steps 2, 8 and 10.

## Expected

- Step 3: Sales agent shows the salesperson's NAME, not an agent code, and cannot be edited. Date is empty until submit.
- Step 4: the dialog proposes at least the title (P's text). It proposes Developer, State, Mukim and Type of building only when the title names them; for a title that names none of them those fields stay empty, which is correct (nothing is guessed). After Confirm every proposed value is filled and still editable. (AI wording can vary slightly.)
- Step 5: refused, State (and Developer, if still empty) named as required; nothing is submitted.
- Step 6: the customer that is not this salesperson's never appears in the list and typed text alone is not accepted; the salesperson's own customer can be picked. Status becomes `Pending`.
- Step 7: the form is read-only; Date shows today (DD/MM/YYYY); PPF No. is empty; "Possible duplicate:" names P (code and title). P's exact title and developer score about 0.99 against P (crew measured 0.986 for PRJ-000002; the bar is 0.80), so P is always named. A loose rewording of P scores lower (crew measured 0.885 for a reworded PRJ-000003, still named; abbreviations spelled out 0.706, not named: a known limit of the 0.80 bar).
- Step 8: Date today, the long title truncated (full text on hover), Developer, State, Sales agent = the NAME, Possible duplicate = P's code and title as a link, Status `Pending`.
- Step 9: the link opens P's project page.
- Step 10: only the legacy form's fields: Project title, Developer, Type of building, State, Mukim, Main contractor, Architect, Salesperson; Date, Sales agent, Distributor, Customer and Your ref sit read-only under the header. No value, brands, dates or template fields.
- Step 11: refused with "Already registered as <P's code> <P's title>"; nothing is created and the registration stays Pending.
- Step 12: the link opens P's project page.
- Step 13: approved; the page returns to the list.
- Step 14: the project code is `PPF` + 2-digit year + 2-digit month + 3 digits (for example `PPF2610003`); State and Mukim show in the overview's "The development" card; the owner is the salesperson linked to the contact.
- Step 15: the approved one shows `Approved` and its PPF number.
- Step 16: `Rejected` with the reason `Already registered`; the form is editable again and can be resubmitted.
- Step 17: nothing is clipped; the form, list and review screen are usable.
