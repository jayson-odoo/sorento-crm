## Steps

1. From the sidebar open Project Sales Admin > Consignment.
2. Look at the toolbar, then open the Actions menu. Close it.
3. Tick CG-202608-072 and click `Plan selected (1)`.
4. Go back to the Consignment list (sidebar), untick CG-202608-072, tick CG-202606-062 and click `Plan selected (1)`.
5. Read the board's rows and the four summary buttons above the grid (Use own location / Borrow / Use BRW / Buy).
6. Tick line 16 (SRTWT165), click `Decide`, pick `Borrow`.
7. Open the Location dropdown in the dialog.
8. Pick `CON`, type a reason (`goods at dealer`), click `Save 1 line`.
9. Click `Confirm (1)`.
10. From the sidebar open Project Sales Admin > Consignment and open CG-202606-062.
11. Back on the Consignment list, tick CG-202606-062, open Actions, click `Reset planning (1)`, read the dialog, then click `Reset planning`.
12. From the sidebar open Project Sales Admin > Sales Orders, tick one open project sales order, click `Plan selected (1)`, tick one of its lines, `Decide` > `Borrow`, open the Location dropdown. Close the dialog without saving.
