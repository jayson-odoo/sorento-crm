# Hand test: PR #2339 NON-CANCELLABLE-ITEMS (bathtubs and other bulky items cannot be cancelled after the SO is confirmed)

A series (or the company default list) marks categories or models Non-cancellable. A quotation line of such an item
shows a "Non-cancellable" badge under its description on screen, and "Non-cancellable" in a REMARK column in the PDF,
the sign page and the Excel export. Once a published SO consumes the line, cutting it in the CRM needs a sales
manager's override with a reason; the override shows in the proposal's History and the SO's History tab. Old
quotation lines are not re-marked: only lines saved (or recomputed) after the setting changes carry the mark.

crew test copy

## Setup
1. Sign in with your own login (admin; admin always holds "Override a non-cancellable item").
2. Pick a project series that lists a bathtub category (Project Sales > Configuration > Series). Write down one
   bathtub model code under it (TUB-A) and one tap or basin code in another category of the same series (TAP-B).
3. Pick a project whose proposal uses that series and is still a Draft (not sent to the customer).
4. Steps 12 to 15 (the block) use the crew fixture, already on the test copy (crew applies
   `laneboard/fixtures/2339-non-cancellable.sql`; nothing for you to run). It is project **ZZT-NC-PRJ** "ZZT
   Non-cancellable fixture project" with Draft proposal **ZZT-NC-Q1** (one line ZZT-NC-TUB-A, qty 5, marked
   Non-cancellable) and published sales order **ZZT-NC-SO-1** whose line uses that proposal line. Why a fixture: the
   block fires only on a proposal line a published project SO uses, and the CRM makes that link only when it drafts
   the SO itself from a customer PO matched to the proposal. SOs adopted from AutoCount never carry it (0 of 29,544
   project SO lines on the 12 Nov copy), so the block cannot fire on an existing SO, only on SOs the CRM drafts
   after this ships.

## Steps
1. From the sidebar open Project Sales > Configuration > Series, open the series from Setup step 2.
2. In "Non-cancellable categories" pick the bathtub category. Click Save changes.
3. Scroll to Products, scroll the table right, find TUB-A and TAP-B.
4. On TUB-A set Non-cancellable to "No", then click the row's tick to save it. Then set it back to "Follows
   category" and click the tick again.
5. From the sidebar open Project Sales > Configuration > Non-cancellable Items. Click Create.
6. Type: Category; Category: a basin category; Non-cancellable on; Notes "test"; click Create.
7. Right-click that row and choose Delete; let the countdown run out.
8. Open the project from Setup step 3, Proposals tab, open the Draft proposal, then Proposal actions (the ... menu)
   > Edit proposal.
9. Add a line TUB-A qty 10 and a line TAP-B qty 10. Save proposal.
10. Look at the lines grid.
11. Sign the proposal and send it to the customer (Download PDF stays disabled until then), then Download PDF.
    Also open the customer sign link and Download Excel.
12. From the sidebar open Project Sales > Projects, open ZZT-NC-PRJ, Proposals tab, open ZZT-NC-Q1, Proposal actions >
    Edit proposal. Lower ZZT-NC-TUB-A from 5 to 4, click Save proposal.
13. In the "Non-cancellable lines" dialog, type the reason "owner test" and click Override.
14. Still in Edit proposal, click the History (clock) button next to Save proposal.
15. Back on ZZT-NC-PRJ, SO tab, open ZZT-NC-SO-1, open its History tab.

## Expected
- Step 2: saves; reopening the series shows the bathtub category under "Non-cancellable categories".
- Step 3: TUB-A shows "Follows category" on one line and, under Applies, a "Non-cancellable" badge; TAP-B shows
  "Follows category" and no badge.
- Step 4: after the tick with "No" the Applies badge disappears; after the tick with "Follows category" it returns.
- Step 5: the toolbar button reads "Create", and so do the dialog's title and button. Step 6: a row "basin category / Category / Yes / test" appears, with
  no icon buttons on the row. Step 7: right-click shows Edit and Delete; the row is gone after the countdown.
- Step 10: a yellow "Non-cancellable" badge sits under the TUB-A line's description only; Total fully visible at
  laptop width; TAP-B has none.
- Step 11: the PDF has a REMARK column just before GRAND TOTAL with "Non-cancellable" on TUB-A only; nothing added
  to the terms; the sign page and the Excel export show the same.
- Step 12: the save is refused and the "Non-cancellable lines" dialog lists ZZT-NC-TUB-A "5 to 4"; Override stays
  disabled until a reason is typed.
- Step 13: the save goes through.
- Step 14: History lists "Non-cancellable override: ZZT-NC-TUB-A 5 to 4. Reason: owner test" with your name and time.
- Step 15: the SO page opens with a History tab (empty for the fixture: it was written without a user). An override
  made from the SO side (amendment publish, or Transfer to Cancel SO on the Fulfilment Planning board) lands here;
  neither can be walked on the fixture, which has no AutoCount lines (backend tests cover both).
- After the test crew can remove the fixture with `laneboard/fixtures/2339-non-cancellable-revert.sql`.
- A user without the permission (for example a salesperson) gets a plain message "... A sales manager can override
  it with a reason." and no override dialog.
- The AutoCount alert (bell + email to override holders when AutoCount cuts or cancels such a line) cannot be
  triggered on the test copy: it needs an AutoCount ingest.
