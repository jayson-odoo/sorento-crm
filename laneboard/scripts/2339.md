# Hand test: PR #2339 NON-CANCELLABLE-ITEMS (bathtubs and other bulky items cannot be cancelled after the SO is confirmed)

A series (or the company default list) marks categories or models Non-cancellable. A quotation line of such an item
shows a "Non-cancellable" badge under its description on screen, and "Non-cancellable" in a REMARK column in the PDF,
the sign page and the Excel export. Once a published SO consumes the line, cutting it in the CRM needs a sales
manager's override with a reason; the override shows in the proposal's History and the SO's History tab. Old
quotation lines are not re-marked: only lines saved (or recomputed) after the setting changes carry the mark.

## Setup
1. Sign in with your own login (admin; admin always holds "Override a non-cancellable item").
2. Everything below runs on the crew fixture, already on the test copy (crew applies
   `laneboard/fixtures/2339-non-cancellable.sql`; nothing for you to run). All of it is fake and starts with ZZT:
   - series **ZZT NC Fixture Series**: category ZZT NC Bathtubs (marked Non-cancellable) with model
     **ZZT-NC-TUB-A**, and category ZZT NC Taps (not marked) with model **ZZT-NC-TAP-B**;
   - project **ZZT-NC-PRJ** "ZZT Non-cancellable fixture project" with two proposals:
     **ZZT-NC-Q1** (Draft; its ZZT-NC-TUB-A qty 5 line is used by the sales order below) and
     **ZZT-NC-Q2** (signed and sent: ZZT-NC-TUB-A x10, ZZT-NC-TAP-B x10);
   - published sales order **ZZT-NC-SO-1**, linked to AutoCount order **ZZT-NC-CORE-1** (line 1, ZZT-NC-TUB-A qty 5).
3. Why a fixture: the block fires only on a proposal line a published project SO uses, and the CRM makes that link
   only when it drafts the SO itself from a customer PO matched to the proposal. SOs adopted from AutoCount never
   carry it (0 of 29,544 project SO lines on the 12 Nov copy), so the block cannot fire on an existing SO, only on
   SOs the CRM drafts after this ships.
4. Step 16 cancels the fixture's AutoCount line, so it runs once per fixture; crew resets it with the revert file
   then the fixture file.

## Steps
1. From the sidebar open Project Sales > Configuration > Series, open ZZT NC Fixture Series.
2. In "Non-cancellable categories" remove ZZT NC Bathtubs, click Save changes; add it back, click Save changes.
3. Scroll to Products, scroll the table right, find ZZT-NC-TUB-A and ZZT-NC-TAP-B.
4. On ZZT-NC-TUB-A set Non-cancellable to "No", then click the row's tick to save it. Then set it back to
   "Follows category" and click the tick again.
5. From the sidebar open Project Sales > Configuration > Non-cancellable Items. Click Create.
6. Type: Category; Category: ZZT NC Taps; Non-cancellable on; Notes "test"; click Create.
7. Right-click that row and choose Delete; let the countdown run out.
8. From the sidebar open Project Sales > Projects, open ZZT-NC-PRJ, Proposals tab, open ZZT-NC-Q1, then
   Proposal actions (the ... menu) > Edit proposal.
9. Add a line ZZT-NC-TAP-B qty 10. Save proposal.
10. Look at the lines grid.
11. Back on ZZT-NC-PRJ, Proposals tab, open ZZT-NC-Q2 (sent). Proposal actions > Download PDF, then Download Excel
    (both arrive in My Downloads, top bar). Proposal actions > Copy counter-sign link, open the link.
12. Open ZZT-NC-Q1 again, Proposal actions > Edit proposal. Lower the ZZT-NC-TUB-A qty 5 line to 4, click Save
    proposal.
13. In the "Non-cancellable lines" dialog, type the reason "owner test" and click Override.
14. Open Edit proposal again and click the History (clock) button next to Save proposal.
15. From the sidebar open Fulfilment Planning. Tick ZZT-NC-CORE-1, click Plan ZZT-NC-CORE-1.
16. Tick line 1 (ZZT-NC-TUB-A), Decide > Transfer to Cancel SO, reason "customer cancelled", Save 1 line.
    Click Confirm (1), Next: Confirm, Confirm 1 order. In the "Non-cancellable lines" dialog type the reason
    "owner test board" and click Override.
17. Open ZZT-NC-PRJ, SO tab, open ZZT-NC-SO-1, History tab.

## Expected
- Step 2: saves; reopening the series shows ZZT NC Bathtubs under "Non-cancellable categories".
- Step 3: ZZT-NC-TUB-A shows "Follows category" on one line and, under Applies, a "Non-cancellable" badge;
  ZZT-NC-TAP-B shows "Follows category" and no badge.
- Step 4: after the tick with "No" the Applies badge disappears; after the tick with "Follows category" it returns.
- Step 5: the toolbar button reads "Create", and so do the dialog's title and button. Step 6: a row
  "ZZT NC Taps / Category / Yes / test" appears, with no icon buttons on the row. Step 7: right-click shows Edit and
  Delete; the row is gone after the countdown.
- Step 9: saves (adding a line or saving without cutting the used line is never blocked).
- Step 10: a yellow "Non-cancellable" badge sits under each ZZT-NC-TUB-A line's description only; Total fully
  visible at laptop width; ZZT-NC-TAP-B has none.
- Step 11: the PDF has a REMARK column just before GRAND TOTAL with "Non-cancellable" on ZZT-NC-TUB-A only; nothing
  added to the terms; the Excel export and the sign page show the same.
- Step 12: the save is refused and the "Non-cancellable lines" dialog lists ZZT-NC-TUB-A "5 to 4"; Override stays
  disabled until a reason is typed.
- Step 13: the save goes through.
- Step 14: History lists "Non-cancellable override: ZZT-NC-TUB-A 5 to 4. Reason: owner test" with your name and time.
- Step 16: Confirm is refused and the "Non-cancellable lines" dialog lists "ZZT-NC-CORE-1 line 1, 5 to 0"; after
  Override it reads "1 of 1 orders confirmed ... Transferred to Cancel SO: line 1 ZZT-NC-TUB-A".
- Step 17: History lists "Non-cancellable override: Transferred to Cancel SO" with line "ZZT-NC-CORE-1 line 1",
  "5 to 0. Reason: owner test board", your name and time.
- A user without the permission (for example a salesperson) gets a plain message "... A sales manager can override
  it with a reason." and no override dialog.
- The AutoCount alert (bell + email to override holders when AutoCount cuts or cancels such a line) cannot be
  triggered on the test copy: it needs an AutoCount ingest.
- After the test crew can remove the fixture with `laneboard/fixtures/2339-non-cancellable-revert.sql`.
