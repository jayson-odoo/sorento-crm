# Hand test: PR #2159 QUOTE-SCOPE-COMPACT (no duplicate scope header on the Lines tab)

The scope card under the tabs (repeated scope name, pen, red Remove scope) is gone. The tabs are
the scope header: right-click a tab for Rename / Remove scope; on a phone the open tab shows a ...
for the same menu. Lines, totals, sections and columns are unchanged.

crew test copy

## Setup
- Signed in as an admin, one browser tab, window about 1280 px wide.
- Any project you can edit (call it PROJ).

## Steps
1. From `/`, open the sidebar Project Sales > Projects and open PROJ.
2. Open the Proposals tab and click Add a proposal.
3. Click the Lines tab.
4. Click + at the end of the scope tabs, name it "b", click Add.
5. Click the "Scope 1" tab.
6. Right-click the "Scope 1" tab.
7. Choose Rename, change the name to "Townhouse", click Save changes.
8. Click Add a line and pick any product, quantity 2.
9. Right-click the "b" tab and choose Remove scope.
10. Narrow the window to about 375 px (or open the copy on a phone).
11. Tap the ... on the open "Townhouse" tab.
12. Press Escape, then click Cancel on the proposal.

## Expected

  no box around the table's box.
- Step 6: a small menu with Rename and Remove scope (red).
- Step 7: the Edit scope dialog (Scope name, Series, Notes) opens with "Scope 1"; afterwards the
  tab reads "Townhouse".
- Step 8: the line and its total behave exactly as before.
- Step 9: "b" disappears at once, no confirm dialog; "Townhouse" stays with its line.
- Step 10: no sideways page scroll; the open tab now shows a ... beside its $ (not shown at
  1280 px with a mouse).
- Step 11: the same menu, Rename and Remove scope.
- Step 12: nothing was saved (the proposal list has no new proposal).
