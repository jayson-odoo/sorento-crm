# PR 2381 hand test: Order Requests (raised by Customer Service, approved by the CS manager, routed to purchasing)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Apply the crew-migration SQL on the PR (the base comment and the `.view` delta).
- Fake users only:
  - Requester A (Customer Service, not a salesperson): role with `procurement.spare_part_requests.create`
    and `procurement.spare_part_requests.view` ("Create Order Requests", "View Order Requests"), no
    `products.cost.view`.
  - Approver B (the CS manager user): role with `procurement.spare_part_requests.view` only.
  - Salesperson C: salesperson profile, role with `procurement.spare_part_requests.create` only.
  - Viewer D: role with `projects.projects.view` only.
  - Buyer E: purchasing role (order inquiries view + acknowledge, `products.cost.view`).
- Approver team: under the Customer Service access agent, a team set whose tier 1 team holds ONE
  member, Approver B (tiers 2 and 3 empty). SLA Management > Form SLA Configuration: Add stage, form
  type "Order Request", that agent and team set, no next stage.
- Two catalogue products (e.g. ZZX-100, ZZX-220) and a location (any, e.g. MWH).

## Steps
1. Sign in as Viewer D. Open Procurement > Supply Chain.
2. Sign in as Salesperson C. Look at the sidebar under Procurement and under MY REQUESTS.
3. Sign in as Requester A. From `/` click Procurement > Supply Chain > Order Requests.
4. Click Create.
5. Choose currency MYR and the default location.
6. Add ZZX-100: change Description to "Hinge set, soft close, left", Qty 10, Unit price 12.00.
7. Add ZZX-220: Qty 4, Unit price 35.00.
8. On ZZX-100 click the paperclip and add one photo.
9. Click Submit.
10. Open the SLA Tracking tab.
11. Sign in as Approver B. From `/` click Procurement > Supply Chain > Order Requests and open the request.
12. On the Lines tab change ZZX-100 Qty to 6 and ZZX-220 Unit price to 32.00.
13. Click Approve, then Approve in the popup.
14. Open the History tab.
15. Go back to the Order Requests list.
16. Sign in as Buyer E. Open Procurement > Supply Chain > Order Inquiries; switch to the Lines view.
17. Open the request's order inquiry.
18. Resize the window to 375 px wide and repeat steps 11 to 14 on a second request.

## Expected
1. No "Order Requests" entry anywhere in the sidebar.
2. No "Order Requests" under Procurement; one "Order Requests" under MY REQUESTS.
3. The Order Requests list (Search, Filters, Columns, Create on one row). No "spare part" wording anywhere.
4. "New Order Request" form.
5. Currency MYR; the location on new lines.
6. Description "Hinge set, soft close, left"; line Total 120.00.
7. Line Total 140.00; request total MYR 260.00.
8. The paperclip shows 1.
9. (F1) The request page opens with its number in the form OR-YYMM-NNNN (e.g. OR-2611-0001), never OI-, status "Pending approval", previous / next arrows.
10. (F2) The SLA Tracking tab shows who must act next (Approver B's team / Approver B), the stage and the due date-time (dd/mm/yyyy, h:mm am/pm), as on a Complaint or Purchase Request.
11. The request (OR- number) is in the list and opens with its tabs; prices are shown.
12. (F3) Lines: "Product code" and "Description" are separate columns, every row on one line (long text cut with "..." and shown in full on hover); ZZX-100 Qty 6 with "was 10" under it; ZZX-220 Unit price 32.00 with "was RM 35.00" under it, in full; no sideways scroll in the Lines card at 1280; "Location" header in full.
13. The popup lists the two changes; after Approve the page stays on the request (no "Could not load"), status "Approved".
14. History: date-time and "by Approver B" apart, no " · "; "Unit price 35.00 -> 32.00" with values; the request named by its OR- number.
15. The approved request is still in Approver B's list.
16. (F4) The row reads type "Order request", S/O no "-", number OR-...; the ZZX-100 line shows "Hinge set, soft close, left". The purchasing notification names the OR- number and says "order request".
17. Unit price and Total shown; Acknowledge available.
18. Same results, no page sideways scroll at 375.
