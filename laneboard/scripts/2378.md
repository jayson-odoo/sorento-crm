# Hand test: PR #2378 QUOTE-LINE-NOTE-REMARK (the line note shows to the customer in REMARK)

A note written on a quotation line (note icon in the Lines editor) now prints in the REMARK column the customer
reads: the PDF (also what the email attaches), the sign link page and the Excel export. "Non-cancellable" comes
first, the note sits under it on its own line. A line with only a note shows just the note. An empty note adds
nothing. Long notes wrap and are never cut. The note popup says "Shown to the customer".
Stacked on #2339 (NON-CANCELLABLE-ITEMS): uses its fixture.

## Setup
1. Sign in with your own login (admin).
2. Crew applies `laneboard/fixtures/2339-non-cancellable.sql`, then `laneboard/fixtures/2378-line-note.sql`
   (nothing for you to run). It gives proposal ZZT-NC-Q2 (signed and sent) two line notes:
   - ZZT-NC-TUB-A (Non-cancellable): "Lead time 6 weeks" / "Deliver to site store" (two lines);
   - ZZT-NC-TAP-B (not Non-cancellable): a long note starting "Colour matt black." and ending "END OF NOTE".
3. After the test crew removes it with `laneboard/fixtures/2378-line-note-revert.sql`.

## Steps
1. From the sidebar open Project Sales > Projects, open ZZT-NC-PRJ, Proposals tab, open ZZT-NC-Q2.
2. Proposal actions (the ... menu) > Download PDF. Open it from My Downloads (top bar).
3. Proposal actions > Download Excel. Open it from My Downloads.
4. Proposal actions > Copy counter-sign link. Open the link in a new tab at laptop width.
5. Make the window phone width (about 375 px) and look at the same link.
6. Back on ZZT-NC-PRJ, Proposals tab, open ZZT-NC-Q1, then Proposal actions > Edit proposal.
7. Click the note icon on the ZZT-NC-TUB-A line.
8. Close the popup without typing, then click Cancel.

## Expected
- Step 2: the PDF has a REMARK column just before GRAND TOTAL. ZZT-NC-TUB-A reads "Non-cancellable", then
  "Lead time 6 weeks", then "Deliver to site store", each on its own line, no " · ". ZZT-NC-TAP-B reads the long
  note in full, wrapped over several lines, ending "END OF NOTE".
- Step 3: the Excel REMARK cells hold the same text, same line breaks, cells wrap (no cut text).
- Step 4: the sign page's REMARK column shows the same as the PDF, in the same column position; the long note wraps
  inside its cell and ends "END OF NOTE"; no "...".
- Step 5: the table scrolls sideways, nothing else on the page scrolls sideways; REMARK cells still show the full text.
- Step 7: the popup shows the note box and, under it, the small grey line "Shown to the customer".
- Step 8: nothing changed on ZZT-NC-Q1.
- A proposal with no Non-cancellable line and no note shows no REMARK column at all (as before).
