# PR 2379 hand test: no "Below default price" flag on quotation lines

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Sign in as a user who can edit project quotations (sales or admin).
- Q = QT-2026-0002 on test project "ZZT outcome 2197 A" (the PR 2330 test quotation): its lines
  are priced below their default price, and its banner reads "... priced below the default price".
- P = test proposal PRJQ-2026-0021 on test project "ZZT outcome 2197 C": it has a saved line below
  its minimum price.
- No migration and no SQL is needed.

## Steps
1. From the home page, open Project Sales in the sidebar, open the project, then open Q.
2. Look at the Product cell of every line on each area tab.
3. Open the Flags filter beside "Search lines".
4. Close it and read the approval banner.
5. Open the gear and choose Edit quotation (Revise first if asked).
6. On any line, type a rate a little below its current rate and wait one second.
7. Cancel the edit. Open P from its project.
8. Look at the Rate cell of the line below its minimum price.
9. Open the Flags filter and pick "Below minimum price".
10. Repeat steps 1 to 4 with the window 375 px wide.

## Expected
- Step 2: no line shows "Below default price". A line outside the series still shows
  "Non-standard" under its product code.
- Step 3: two options only, "Non-standard" and "Below minimum price", each with its count.
- Step 4: the banner reads exactly as before ("N lines are priced below the default price ...").
  The count in front of "priced below the default price" is plain text, not a link; the
  "outside the project's series" and "priced below the minimum price" counts are still links.
- Step 6: no "Below default price" appears under the product code.
- Step 8: the red "Below minimum price" pill sits under the rate, exactly as before.
- Step 9: only that line shows; its item number is unchanged.
- Step 10: same as steps 2 to 4; no row wraps onto a second line because of a flag.
