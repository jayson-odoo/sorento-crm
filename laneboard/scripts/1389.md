# Hand test #1389 (PO-NO-AUTO-ASSIGN)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Sign in as a user who can open Supply Chain > Project Demand > Stock Debt (projects.stock_debt.view).
2. The case is B2155-NL-BLUE with PO 202609-S0109 line 8 (15,000 to a BB bin, no S/O, nothing placed) on the dev DB. Before this change its drawer spread that 15,000 over SO373923 line 224 (36), SO382618 lines 2776 / 2864 / 3072 / 3168 (100 each) and more. If a link has been made on that PO line in AutoCount since, the linked lines read Pinned and the figures below shrink by the linked quantity; the relationships hold.
3. Note the PO line's Delivery date on Procurement > Purchase Orders > 202609-S0109, line 8 (the month its Supply row files under).

## Steps

1. From the sidebar open Supply Chain > Project Demand > Stock Debt, set Only products in debt off and search B2155.
2. Read the row for B2155-NL-BLUE (there are two products with that code; use the one whose PO 202609-S0109 line 8 lands on, the row with the larger figures) and note the balance of the PO line's delivery month and of the months holding SO373923 and SO382618.
3. Click the cell of the month SO382618 lines 2776 / 2864 / 3072 / 3168 are due in. Demand tab: read Status, Assigned and Short on those four rows and on any other row of that month.
4. On the same rows open Covered by (the From column).
5. Switch to the Supply tab of that same cell.
6. Close it and click the cell of the PO line's delivery month (Setup step 3). Supply tab: find the row for PO 202609-S0109 line 8 and read Qty, Received, Outstanding, Free and Assigned to.
7. Press the Total footer row's Free figure against the cell you pressed: add every Free on the Supply tab, subtract every Short on the Demand tab.
8. Find a product on the board whose drawer shows a line Covered by an SPO or PO through an Order Inquiry placement (any product with a placed line on Supply Chain > Order Inquiries) and open its cell.
9. Find a product whose PO line carries an S/O (Purchase Orders > a PO > lines tab, S/O column not "-") and open the month its named SO line is due in.
10. Open Fulfilment Planning for SO382618 (Plan on one of the rows from step 3) and read the proposal for one of those lines.

## Expected

1. The board opens with the B2155 rows listed.
2. The delivery month's balance INCLUDES the PO line's outstanding 15,000 as free supply (it went up by what the walk used to spend on other lines); the months holding SO373923 and SO382618 went DOWN by the quantity those lines used to draw from the PO on their own date. A line the PO used to clear LATE already booked its shortfall, so for those lines only the delivery month moves (up) and the row total rises by the same amount.
3. SO382618 lines 2776 / 2864 / 3072 / 3168 read Short 100 each, Assigned 0 (or only what on hand, a placement or a linked document gives them); no row of that month is Assigned from PO 202609-S0109 line 8. SO373923 line 224 reads Short 36 in its own month for the same reason.
4. Covered by lists no PO 202609-S0109 entry on any of those rows; an On hand entry, a placement or an S/O-linked document may still appear.
5. The Supply tab of that month lists no PO 202609-S0109 line 8 row (it files under its own delivery month) unless a row of this month is pinned to it, in which case it is listed with Free 0.
6. PO 202609-S0109 line 8 reads Qty 15,000, Received 0, Outstanding 15,000, Free 15,000, Assigned to empty. Late is stated as days late if the delivery date has passed; the row is never "overdue, not counted".
7. Free less Short equals the balance printed on the cell that opened the drawer, for both cells opened in steps 3 and 6.
8. The placed line still reads Pinned, Covered by names the document with its Order Inquiry number, and the Supply row's Assigned to names that SO line. The document's unplaced remainder shows as Free.
9. The named SO line reads Pinned from that PO line (Covered by names it), any earlier line of the same group that is not named reads Short, and the PO line's Assigned to names only the S/O line.
10. The board's proposal is unchanged from before this change: the ladder still nets the PO as supply at issue + lead (only the Stock Debt page stopped assigning documents by its own walk).
