# Hand test: PR #2204 PRICETAG-DESIGNER-GAPS (image flip, template settings, feature icons)

Items 1 and 4 are ready to test now. Item 3 (feature icons) is added below once it lands.

crew test copy

## Setup
- Signed in as an admin, browser about 1280 px wide.
- At least one tag template with an image layer showing a tap picture (any template from Dealer
  Kit > Room Designer > Tag Templates; add an image layer and choose a picture from the asset library if none has
  one).
- Note the template's name, family and size shown under the canvas (for example "85 x 130 mm").

## Steps
1. From `/`, open the sidebar Dealer Kit > Room Designer > Tag Templates and open the template (pencil).
2. Click the tap image on the canvas.
3. In the right panel under Image, click "Flip horizontal".
4. Click "Flip horizontal" again, then click "Flip vertical", then click "Flip vertical" again.
5. Click "Flip horizontal" once more (leave it on), wait for "Saved" in the header, then click Publish > Publish.
6. Open a price tag request that uses this template (Dealer Kit > Price tag requests > a request > Design) and look at the tag preview.
7. Back in the template, click "Settings" in the canvas toolbar (right-hand group).
8. Change Name to "Kitchen Sink with 1 Tap", change Family to another family, click Save.
9. Click "Settings" again. Change Width to a value 20 mm smaller than the current width. Read the yellow box. Click Cancel.
10. Click "Settings" again. Change Width back to the original and the Height to 10 mm larger. Click Save.
11. Open the Versions button in the toolbar.
12. In the left rail Tag Size control, type a width 30 mm smaller than the current one and press Enter.
13. Go back to the templates list.

## Expected
- Step 3: the tap picture mirrors left-right in place (same box, same position); the button shows pressed.
- Step 4: each click toggles; after the last click the picture is back to normal; vertical mirrors top-bottom.
- Step 5: header badge reads "Live vN" (N one higher than before).
- Step 6: the tag preview shows the tap mirrored, same as the canvas.
- Step 8: toast "Template settings saved"; the page title reads "Kitchen Sink with 1 Tap"; the badge still reads the same "Live vN" (no new version).
- Step 9: the dialog shows "Layers keep their place and may need moving.", and when a layer reaches past the new width also "1 layer sits outside <W> x <H> mm." (or "N layers sit"), and "The live version keeps its size until you publish."
- Step 10: the canvas grows 10 mm taller; the size under the canvas updates; "Saved" appears in the header; the badge is unchanged.
- Step 11: the Versions list has the same number of versions as after step 5.
- Step 12: if a layer now reaches past the new edge, a yellow toast says "N layer(s) sit outside <W> x <H> mm and may need moving".
- Step 13: the list shows the new name and family on that row.
