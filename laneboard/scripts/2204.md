# Hand test: PR #2204 PRICETAG-DESIGNER-GAPS item 3 (feature icons)

Items 1 (image flip) and 4 (template settings) merged as #2207. This script covers item 3 only:
a template's "Feature icons" layer shows every Dealer Kit icon whose "Show when" rule matches the
product the layer reads from (the main item or a combo position).

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Migration `dkfi_0001_asset_show_when` applied on the copy's DB (crew-migration block on #2204).
- Signed in as an admin, browser about 1280 px wide.
- Three small picture files ready to upload: a hot (red) icon, a cold (blue) icon, a "SUS 304" icon.
- Product codes noted in advance (Master Data > Products > a product > Specifications):
  - MIXER: a tap whose Water supply reads Mixer.
  - COLD: a tap whose Water supply reads Cold only.
  - NOSPEC: any product with no Water supply value.
- A kitchen sink combo that includes one of those taps as a part, for step 14.

## Steps
1. From `/`, open the sidebar Dealer Kit > Room Designer > Tag Templates and open a price tag template (pencil).
2. In the canvas toolbar, click "Add Feature icons".
3. With the new layer selected, click "Manage icons" in the right panel.
4. Click "Upload" and upload the hot icon; repeat for the cold icon and the SUS 304 icon.
5. On the hot icon's tile click "Show when: <its name>"; pick Spec "Water supply", Values "Mixer"; click Save.
6. On the cold icon: "Show when", Spec "Water supply", Values "Cold only" and "Mixer"; Save.
7. On the SUS 304 icon: "Show when", Spec "Steel grade", Values "304"; Save. Close the dialog.
8. Preview the template with product MIXER (the canvas's own product preview, as for any bound layer).
9. Preview with COLD.
10. Preview with NOSPEC.
11. In the right panel set Icon size (mm) to 12, Spacing (mm) to 3, choose Centre; then click "Move <hot icon name> down".
12. Publish the template (Publish > Publish).
13. Open a price tag request with a line for MIXER using this template; open Design, then the print preview / Download PDF.
14. On the kitchen sink combo's template, add a Feature icons layer, point its part / position picker in the right panel at the tap, and preview with the combo ("Preview combo").
15. Open "Show when" on the hot icon again, clear the Spec, Save, and preview MIXER again.

## Expected
- Step 2: a dashed box labelled "Feature icons" appears on the canvas.
- Step 4: each upload appears in the dialog's list; nothing is placed on the canvas.
- Steps 5-7: a toast confirms each save; reopening "Show when" shows the saved spec and values.
- Step 8: the hot and the cold icon side by side, packed from the left, no gap.
- Step 9: only the cold icon, at the left edge (no gap where the hot icon would be).
- Step 10: no icons; the dashed "Feature icons" box still shows in the editor.
- Step 11: icons grow, 3 mm apart, centred in the box; the cold icon now comes before the hot one.
- Step 13: the request preview and the PDF show the same icons, order and placement as the canvas in step 11.
- Step 14: the icons follow the TAP's water supply, not the sink's.
- Step 15: the hot icon no longer shows for MIXER; the cold icon still does.
