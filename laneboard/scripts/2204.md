Items 1 (image flip) and 4 (template settings) merged as #2207. Item 3, after the owner's 5 Nov
rulings: each spec VALUE carries its own icons (Master Data > Product Specifications > a spec >
Values and words), each spec has an "Icon order", and a template's one "Feature icons" layer shows
the product's icons in that one sequence, packed with no gaps, following the combo position.

## Setup
- Migration `dkfi_0001_spec_value_icons` applied on the copy's DB (crew-migration block on #2204).
- Signed in as an admin (has Product Specifications edit and Dealer Kit library rights), ~1280 px.
- Four small pictures ready to upload: hot (red), cold (blue), combined (red-blue), pull-out.
- Product codes noted in advance (Master Data > Products > a product > Specifications):
  - MIXER: Water supply = Mixer. COLD: Water supply = Cold Only. NOSPEC: no Water supply.
  - PULLMIX: a mixer tap whose Spout is Pull out (or Pull-out shower = Yes).
- A kitchen sink combo that includes one of those taps as a part, for step 14.

## Steps
1. From `/`, open the sidebar Dealer Kit > Room Designer > Tag Templates, open a price tag template, click "Add Feature icons", then "Manage icons" in the right panel, and upload the four pictures. Close the dialog.
2. From the sidebar open Master Data > Product Specifications, open "Water supply", go to the Values and words tab and switch to edit.
3. On the Mixer row click "Choose icons for Mixer" and pick hot, then cold; on the Cold Only row pick cold. Save.
4. On the Details tab of "Water supply" set Icon order to 2. Save.
5. Open "Spout" (or "Pull-out shower"): on the Pull out (or Yes) row pick the pull-out icon; set its Icon order to 1. Save.
6. Back in the template, click "Preview the whole tag" and pick MIXER.
7. Preview with COLD, then NOSPEC, then PULLMIX.
8. Set Icon size (mm) 12, Spacing (mm) 3, Centre. Publish.
9. In Water supply > Values and words (edit), on Mixer use "Move cold earlier". Save. Preview MIXER again in the template.
10. On Mixer remove hot and cold, pick the combined icon instead. Save. Preview MIXER and PULLMIX again.
11. Open Dealer Kit > Room Designer > Price Tag Requests, open an existing request with a tap line, Design, Use template > this template on that line; open the request preview. Put the line back to its old template afterwards.
12. On the kitchen sink combo's template add a Feature icons layer, point its part / position picker at the tap, and use "Preview combo".
13. Clean up: remove the icons from Mixer, Cold Only and Pull out, clear both Icon order numbers, Save.

## Expected
- Step 1: a dashed box labelled "Feature icons"; uploads appear in the list, nothing lands on the canvas; no "Show when" anywhere on the icon screen.
- Step 3: each row shows its icons as small thumbnails in pick order; after Save they stay; no IDs shown.
- Step 6: hot then cold, packed from the left, no gap.
- Step 7: COLD = cold only at the left edge; NOSPEC = no icons (dashed box stays in the editor); PULLMIX = pull-out, hot, cold.
- Step 8: icons 12 mm, 3 mm apart, centred in the box; the layer offers no reorder buttons.
- Step 9: MIXER now shows cold then hot (the order set on the value, for every template).
- Step 10: MIXER shows the one combined icon; PULLMIX shows pull-out, combined.
- Step 11: the request preview shows the same icons for that tap's water supply as the canvas, in the same order, size and placement.
- Step 12: the icons follow the TAP, not the sink.
- Step 13: tags preview with no feature icons again.
