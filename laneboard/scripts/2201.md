# Hand test: PR #2201 AGENT-REPORTS (sales report asks and follow-ups go to the agent, switch off by default)

With the new switch on, a sales or outstanding report ask in the AI assistant panel and every
follow-up after it ("clear all", "all brands", "by dealer and project") are answered by the agent,
which keeps the report you see and changes it. With the switch off, nothing changes.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Signed in as an admin about 1280 px wide, on the dev copy of prod data (September 2026 sales present).
- The AI assistant's Gemini key and the router's Jev key are set (System Management > AI assistant), as on dev today.
- Run `crew-migration` SQL from the PR first (adds `system_settings.chatbot_agent_reports_enabled`, default false).
- Start a NEW panel conversation for each part below (the bubble's new-conversation button), so no older report is held.

## Steps
1. From `/`, open the sidebar Users & Access > Settings > Chatbot Media.
2. In the Agent budgets card, check the switch "Report asks go to the agent".
3. Leave it off. Open the AI assistant bubble, start a new conversation, send: Total quantity sale for Sorento brand kitchen sink on September 2026
4. Back on Chatbot Media, turn the switch "Report asks go to the agent" ON and click Save settings.
5. Open the AI assistant bubble, start a new conversation, send: Total quantity sale for Sorento brand kitchen sink on September 2026
6. If it asks which sales basis, reply: invoiced
7. Send: sorento total slaes delaer and proejct
8. Send: clear all
9. Send: sorento total slaes by delaer and proejct
10. Send: all
11. Send: all brand all category
12. Send: for all brands and categories
13. Open the bubble's steps / trace for the last message (the agent steps view).
14. Back on Chatbot Media, turn the switch OFF and Save settings.

## Expected
- Step 2: the switch is OFF (default).
- Step 3: today's behaviour: the bot (parser path) answers, exactly as before this PR.
- Step 5: the agent answers. Either it asks once which sales basis (invoiced offered first), or, after step 6, the card:
  "Sales by invoiced sales", lines for Brand SORENTO and Category Kitchen Sink, Period 01/09/2026 - 30/09/2026,
  Total 2,270 pcs and RM 1,066,977.87.
- Step 7: the same filters (Brand SORENTO, Kitchen Sink, 01/09/2026 - 30/09/2026) broken down by channel: a Dealer row
  and a Project team row whose total is 2,270 pcs.
- Step 8: an answer at once (no question): every filter gone (no Company, Brand or Category line), still invoiced,
  still 01/09/2026 - 30/09/2026, one company-wide total.
- Step 9: NO "brand, account type or company?" question. A channel breakdown (Dealer / Project team) with Sorento
  as the company or the brand, period 01/09/2026 - 30/09/2026.
- Step 10: a channel breakdown, no extra question.
- Steps 11 and 12: no Brand line and no Category line; channel breakdown kept; same period and basis.
- Every reply: no "sorry", no "busy", dates written dd/mm/yyyy, the report card layout (title, filter lines, rows, total).
- Step 13: each of steps 7 to 12 shows the route "Routed to the agent: report_held".
- Step 14: after saving OFF, a new conversation answers report asks through the bot again (as in step 3).
