# Hand test: PR #2102 PS-QUOTE-VIEWS (view count on the quotation sign link)

Owner 2 Nov: "View count, yes, we need to do." The customer's opens of the sign link are counted
(one per browser per 30 minutes; link previews and signed-in staff do not count) and the quotation
page shows "Viewed N times, last ..." with a view log.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
Apply the `crew-migration:` SQL from the PR (one new table). Sign in as a salesperson on
http://tehs-mac-mini.tail6fc4c7.ts.net:3109, the host the copied sign link points at (sign-ins are
per host). Pick a
project with a quotation that has been issued at least once (R1 or later); note its Quotation No.
You need a SECOND browser that is NOT signed in to the CRM (a private window works).

## Steps
1. Open Project Sales, open the project, open the Quotation tab, open the issued quotation.
2. Read the line under the customer's name at the top.
3. From the gear menu choose `Send via WhatsApp` and click `Copy link`.
4. Paste the link into the signed-in browser's address bar and open it. The copied link uses the test copy's tailnet host, so this browser must be signed in on that same host (http://tehs-mac-mini.tail6fc4c7.ts.net:3109); a sign-in on another host or port does not carry over, the open counts, and step 5 fails.
5. Go back to the quotation page and refresh it.
6. Paste the link into the private window and open it.
7. Refresh the private window twice.
8. Go back to the quotation page and refresh it.
9. Click the `Viewed 1 time, ...` line.
10. Revise the quotation, then issue the next revision with `Send to Customer R2` > `WhatsApp`, then refresh the page.
11. Open the OLD (R1) link in the private window again after 30 minutes, or in a second private window now.
12. Refresh the quotation page and click the line.
13. Shrink the window to phone width (375px) and click the line again.

## Expected
1. The quotation page opens.
2. `Not viewed yet`.
3. The link is copied.
4. The customer sign page opens.
5. Still `Not viewed yet` (a signed-in CRM browser does not count).
6. The sign page opens in the private window.
7. The page reloads twice.
8. `Viewed 1 time, last <today's date>, <time of step 6>`.
9. A `View log` opens with one row: the time of step 6, `<Quotation No.>-R1 · Computer`.
10. The line reads `Not viewed yet` (it follows the newest revision).
11. The R1 page opens (it may say it has been replaced by R2).
12. The line still reads `Not viewed yet`; the log lists two rows, both `...-R1`, newest first.
13. The line wraps under the customer's name and the log fits the screen with no sideways scroll.
