# Hand test: PR #2199 PORTAL-IMPERSONATION-ISOLATE (view-as stays in the tab that started it)

Impersonating a portal user (a salesperson contact, or "Impersonate user" on a staff user) now
applies only to the tab where you started it. Every other tab, and any other browser you are
signed in on, stays you with full access. Exit in that tab returns it to you with no sign-in.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Signed in as an admin (superadmin or admin role) in one browser window, about 1280 px wide.
- A contact linked to an active Salesperson user (any salesperson whose contact you can find in
  Users & Access > Contacts). Note the contact's name.
- No view-as open: if a yellow "You are currently impersonating" bar shows anywhere, click Exit
  Impersonation first.

## Steps
1. Tab A: from `/`, open the sidebar Users & Access > Contacts and find the salesperson's contact.
2. Open the row's "..." menu, choose Impersonate in portal, then click Impersonate.
3. Tab A: note the page and the yellow bar.
4. Open a new tab B (Ctrl/Cmd+T), go to the CRM root `/`.
5. Tab B: from the sidebar open Master Data > Products.
6. Tab A: press reload.
7. Tab A: click Exit Impersonation in the yellow bar.
8. Tab B: press reload, then open Products again from the sidebar.
9. Optional, second browser or private window signed in as the same admin: start a view-as there
   (Users > a staff user > gear > Impersonate user), then go back to tab B and reload.

## Expected
- Step 3: tab A shows My Requests with the bar "You are currently impersonating <salesperson
  name>".
- Step 4: tab B shows your own Dashboards, NO yellow bar, your full sidebar.
- Step 5: the Products list loads with rows (no "no access" page, no sign-in).
- Step 6: tab A still shows the yellow bar for the same salesperson.
- Step 7: tab A reloads as you, no yellow bar, no sign-in page.
- Step 8: tab B is unchanged: still you, Products loads, never asked to sign in.
- Step 9: tab B stays you with no yellow bar; only the tab that started the view-as shows it.
