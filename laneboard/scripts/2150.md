# Hand test: PR #2150 MYTEAM-BY-SEGMENT (My Team shows only tasks whose contact segment/brand I serve)

Before this fix the dashboard My Team tab showed every DO Customer Service teammate's open tasks,
so project CS saw retail tasks and vice versa. Now a member whose team membership is tagged with
market segments (or brands) sees a teammate's task only when the task's contact carries one of
those segments (and its brand is one they serve). Untagged members still see everything, a
contact with no segment shows to everyone, and a colleague you actively cover shows in full.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Run the read-only check on the test copy's DB and keep the output:
  `psql "$DATABASE_URL" -v ON_ERROR_STOP=1 -c 'BEGIN READ ONLY' -f sorento_crm_backend/scripts/check_do_cs_member_segments.sql`
  Section 1 lists each DO Customer Service member's segments/brands per team, section 2 counts
  their open tasks by contact segment (call the retail count R, project P, none N).
- Signed in as the owner (admin), one browser tab.
- If the owner is not a member of DO Customer Service: User Management > Teams > DO Customer
  Service > Add member > add yourself, then untick "Auto-assign (round robin)" on your row so no
  live task is routed to you. Remove yourself again after step 10.
- No active coverage of anyone (dashboard Coverage tab shows nothing you cover).

## Steps
1. From `/`, look at the dashboard task widget and click My Team. Note the badge count (ALL) and
   that rows with retail contacts and rows with project contacts both appear.
2. Open the sidebar User Management > AI Agents, open the agent that links DO Customer Service,
   and on your own DO Customer Service member row set market segments to Project only (other
   memberships of yours: leave untagged).
3. Go back to `/` and click My Team. Note the badge count (PROJ).
4. Open three rows and check each contact's segment (contact panel or Respond contact).
5. Back on the member row, change your segments to Retail only.
6. Go back to `/`, click My Team, note the badge count (RET).
7. Open the dashboard Coverage tab and start covering one retail CS colleague (no end date).
8. Click My Team again.
9. Stop that coverage, then open the sidebar My Team Tasks page (SLA management) with your
   segments still set to Retail only.
10. Clear your segment tags back to "All contacts", reload `/` and click My Team.

## Expected
- Step 1: ALL counts every open task of your DO Customer Service teammates (you excluded).
- Step 3: PROJ is smaller than ALL by about R (retail-only contacts disappear). No row's contact
  is tagged Retail only; contacts tagged Project, both, or nothing remain.
- Step 4: every opened row's contact is Project, Project + Retail, or has no segment.
- Step 6: RET is smaller than ALL by about P; no row's contact is tagged Project only.
- Step 8: every open task of the colleague you cover is listed, including any project-only ones;
  other teammates' project-only tasks stay hidden.
- Step 9: the page shows the same filtered set as the dashboard tab (no project-only contacts),
  and its total matches RET.
- Step 10: the count is back to ALL.
- Throughout: the Coverage picker and the Reassign dialog list the same colleagues as before
  (segment tags do not narrow them, by design).
