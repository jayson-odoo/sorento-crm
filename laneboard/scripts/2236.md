# Hand test: PR #2236 PRICETAG-DISCONTINUED-PICK (discontinued combo options stay selectable)

On the price tag request form, a discontinued option in a combo's choice group ("Not sure, any of N") can now be
picked. It still reads "Discontinued" so the requester knows. Approve no longer stops on a discontinued product at all,
picked or fixed: a discontinued fixed part shows a "Discontinued" badge as a warning only (owner ruling 7 Nov, 1a).

Nothing in this script approves or submits a request. Approve sends WhatsApp messages through respond.io
(`price_tag_request_service.py:1427`, `price_tag_notify.py`), and an approved request can only be voided. So Approve
is clicked only while the Customer field is EMPTY. The form then stops in the browser, before anything is sent: it
marks Customer as missing and writes each line's problems (`PriceTagRequestForm.tsx:1438-1450`). That list is what
this test reads.

## Setup
1. Sign in as a NON-admin salesperson. Admin and superadmin cannot create price tag requests in the CRM: they get no
   Add button, and the create route refuses them with "Staff create requests from the staff pages."
   (`my_requests.py:214-215`). The staff request page (`PriceTagRequestDetail`) has no line picker. The user must:
   - hold `dealer_kit.price_tag_requests.view_own` and `dealer_kit.price_tag_requests.create`, but NOT
     `dealer_kit.price_tag_requests.view` (the Salesperson role), and
   - be linked to a contact.
   Alternative: sign in to the dealer portal as a contact with Price Tag Request access, and use Price Tag Request >
   New Price Tag Request instead of steps 1-2 below. The form and all later steps are the same.
2. On Master Data > Products, confirm:
   - SRTWT5831-BL is Discontinued;
   - SRTKS7061-BL has a combo whose tap group lists SRTKT1633SS-BL, SRTKT1634SS-BL, SRTWT5830-BL, SRTWT5831-BL and
     SRTKT71SS-BL.
   No template, sell price or template link is needed: the picker shows without them, and steps 1-9 never approve.
3. Discontinued fixed-part example for steps 10-12: host SRTBF11712, combo "SRTBF11712 + SRTMCB21701" (sell RM 1799).
   SRTMCB21701 is its discontinued fixed part.

## Steps
1. From the sidebar, open Dealer Kit > Room Designer > Price Tag Requests.
2. Click Create (top right of the list; it opens New Price Tag Request).
3. Leave Customer EMPTY. On line 1, pick item SRTKS7061-BL.
4. In the line's Package select, pick the combo that carries the tap group.
5. Open the "Not sure, any of 5" select under the line, and look at the SRTWT5831-BL option.
6. Click SRTWT5831-BL.
7. With Customer still empty, click Approve.
8. Pick any Customer, then click Save Draft. Do NOT click Approve from here on.
9. Reload the page.
10. Clear Customer again. Click the Add line button, and on the new line pick item SRTBF11712.
11. In that line's Package select, pick "SRTBF11712 + SRTMCB21701".
12. With Customer empty, click Approve.

## Expected
- Step 5: SRTWT5831-BL RM 285 is listed with "Discontinued" under it, in normal (not greyed) text, and can be hovered
  like the other four.
- Step 6: the select now shows SRTWT5831-BL RM 285; no error on the line.
- Step 7: nothing is sent: no success toast, the request does not leave the page, and Customer shows "Select the dealer these tags are for."
  Line 1 does NOT show "SRTWT5831-BL is discontinued." Other messages on the line, for example "No combo selling price."
  or "No tag template yet.", come from this combo's dev data and are unrelated; note them if they appear.
- Step 8: the draft saves (success toast, no error).
- Step 9: line 1 still shows SRTWT5831-BL in the tap select.
- Step 11: SRTMCB21701's row under line 2 shows a yellow "Discontinued" badge.
- Step 12: nothing is sent (Customer shows "Select the dealer these tags are for."). Line 2 does NOT show "SRTMCB21701 is discontinued." and
  line 1 does NOT show "SRTWT5831-BL is discontinued."
- Afterwards: the request is still a Draft in the list, never approved.
