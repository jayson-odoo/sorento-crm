6Open questions (also in the crew ask)

  1. Prepared by. No screen writes the quotation's own signatory name today. (a) the project's salesperson (recommended: already on every project), (b) whoever issues the quotation, (c) a new typed field.
  2. Where Payment Terms and Validity defaults come from. (a) two fields beside the company T&C template on the Quotation templates page (recommended: one place for all company terms), (b) fixed system-wide text in System settings.
  3. Letterhead on page 1. The workbook has none. (a) keep the company letterhead on top (shown), (b) rows only.
