4. SCM purchase order detail, Lines tab (PO 202607-S0100 and 202607-S0083)

Notes (not part of the UI)

  • "Qty received" becomes "Transferred": the figure is AutoCount's TransferedQty onto a shipping order, not a goods receipt. Values and footer total unchanged (all 10 lines of the first PO read fully transferred).
  • The actual receipt lives on the SPO chip (Received qty at bin, from the Goods Received document), which is why every transferred line now says whether it landed.
  • Outstanding qty is the page's own formula (ordered minus received minus cancelled, floored at 0); the cancelled line of PO 202607-S0083 therefore reads 728 because its Qty cancelled is 0 on dev. Existing behaviour, not changed here.
  • Neighbouring columns not touched (Placed, Qty cancelled flags, row actions) are left out of the mock.
