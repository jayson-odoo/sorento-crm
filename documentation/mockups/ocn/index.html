Order Change Notice (OCN), mock

Draws the RECOMMENDED answers of the behaviour card (documentation/plans/procurement/CARD-ocn-order-change-notice-12nov.md). Each spot that depends on a question carries a dashed purple tag Q1 to Q5. Q1 four types as an editable lookup. Q2 risk shown, no routing. Q3 structured lines plus description. Flow (owner d1): salesperson, sales admin, management, Customer Service, exactly like the purchase request and sponsorship form; no Purchasing step. Q5 project optional. All names, numbers and codes are invented. FE = sorento_crm_frontend/.
