0c. Questions for the owner PLEASE ANSWER

Q-A. Your note ended "...repeated over and over every page that ca..." What was the rest?

Our guess: (a) "...that can show the project details" (the right band above: title, ref, area, prepared by, date). (b) "...that can be changed per page" (a band with per-page text). (c) something else: please type it.
Recommend (a): it is what the sample prints, and per-page AREA and COMMENT are already typed on the tab.

Q-B. Price on the spec sheet?

Your sample prints no price; you mentioned "price in one of the area". (a) No price (as the sample). (b) An optional Price area showing the quoted unit price of the issued revision; a template without it prints none. (c) The list price.
Recommend (b): the quotation's own price is what the customer agreed to; the area is optional so the no-price catalogue stays possible.

Q-C. One header band or also a top/bottom band?

(a) Any fixed area anywhere on the page repeats (the right band is just the sample's choice; a top or bottom band works the same way). (b) Only a right band.
Recommend (a): it is how the template already works; nothing extra to build.
