Under option B the 88 is lendable: the line is due at or beyond today + lead time + 14 days, the same window the fulfilment board uses to name a borrow donor. 4. Rebalance · Preview (read-only)

Pressing Rebalance runs the board's own machinery for each receiving sales order and shows what it would write, nothing written yet: one Borrow per receiving line (the same order_borrow component the fulfilment board's Confirm posts) and the order-back Buy the donor gets at its own required date. The planner then presses Confirm N orders, or Cancel. Same permission as the board's Confirm.
