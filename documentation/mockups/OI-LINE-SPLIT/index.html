  • Split rows never net into a CANCEL: a split left on a new line (dropped on no line) is a delivery of that qty at its own date, with its own row on the sheet, in the email and in the Excel. CANCEL appears only for a split the user explicitly made a cancel.
  • Typed dates will commit on leaving the cell or on Enter, not mid-typing (approved fix, components/ui/date-picker.tsx:84-92).
  • 752 extra: 182 if the 37 moved to 608 (649 = 467 + 182); the email said ORDER 145 = 649 - 504; owner to confirm.
