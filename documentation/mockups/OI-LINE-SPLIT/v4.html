Split delivery: one sheet, drag the rows to allocate (OI-LINE-SPLIT, mockup v4)

v4 after the owner's note on v3 (Lavish, 17 Oct 13:50): "After i split ady can i like drag and drop the rows to allocate, like make it more intuitive, you take the scenario i gave you where there is some qty to 14 then remaining qty go to other date, somewhere like 20/11". Same one-page sheet as v3 (every line, splits stacked downward under their line, REMAIN for untouched lines). New: every split row has a grip ⠿, and what is left on a line shows as an orange "403 left" chip. Drag either onto another line of the same item: the qty goes to that line's date and AutoCount line. While dragging, "New date" and "Cancel" rows open under the item. Press Play the owner's case to watch 417 become 14 @ 20/9 + the rest on the 20/11 line, then try it yourself.

4Keyboard

Excel habits, plus a keyboard drag. Listed in the Outline guide, not on screen.

WhereKeys
Any cellTab / Shift+Tab next / previous cell · Enter or ↓ the same column one row down · Shift+Enter or ↑ one row up
REMAIN celltype a number or a date: the line splits and the row takes it
Change quantity / change dateCtrl/Cmd+Enter add a split row under this line (filled with what is left) · Ctrl/Cmd+Backspace remove this split row · dates type as d/m/yyyy (20/9/2026), or pick from the calendar
Grip ⠿ or "left" chipSpace pick up · ↓ / ↑ next / previous line of the item (then New date, Cancel) · Space or Enter drop · Esc put back
AnywhereCtrl/Cmd+Z undo the last change (a drop also offers Undo in its toast) · Esc closes a calendar or list
