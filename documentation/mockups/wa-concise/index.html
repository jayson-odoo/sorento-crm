Cost reality first

Meta bills per message, and almost every reply is already one message. The v1 rules alone took the sample from 7.84M to 7.26M characters (7.5%); the v2 line format cuts more text, but billed messages move only from 12,938 to 12,931 once WA-MSG-TRIM's 3,900-character split lands (14,083 to 13,932 at today's 1,800). These rules make replies easier to read; they do not cut the bill. The bill is driven by one reply per incoming message, which stays as ruled.

Not changed

  • Availability replies for dealers (one sentence per product, ETA date or "no stock and no incoming") are not touched by any rule.
  • "Would you like me to escalate to ... team?" stays word for word (the yes/no reader depends on it).
  • One reply per incoming message, complaint/SLA notices, Respond.io auto messages, the escalation merge, compact view and 3,900 split (WA-MSG-TRIM).
  • Values are never translated. Numbering stays, because number picks depend on it.

Edge cases

  • No stock: "*Incoming:* none", "*Stock:* 0", "*PO:* none" on three lines in the product block. "PO: none" stands for today's "nothing on order" (PO lines and unshipped SPO allocations, answer.py:1406).
  • PO placed: "*PO:* placed" followed by the PO lines as today.
  • No ETA: "*Incoming:* none" appears once, in the product block. An incoming row without an ETA keeps today's wording for it. Dealer availability lines unchanged.
  • Sorento + Mocha: one block per company and product, "*Company:*" first as today (example 5). A company that returned nothing keeps its "*Mocha:* no stock records for X." line.
  • Many locations: one line per location (SRT382-6-DIY had 13), no blank lines between them.
  • Files: "I have attached the file(s) below." stays whenever a file really goes out. In example 3 no file is recorded with the reply (UNVERIFIED whether one was sent), so the after drops it.
  • Picks: lists of several blocks keep their numbers ("1. *Product Code:* X") because number picks depend on them; a single product has no number.
  • en / ms / zh: reply templates are English (10 of 14,640 outgoing contain Chinese, all are "Here is what I heard" echoes). Rules are language-independent.
  • Staff vs dealer: the escalation offer is still gated exactly as today.
