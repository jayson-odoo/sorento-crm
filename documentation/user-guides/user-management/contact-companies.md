# Contact companies

A contact's **Companies** are the companies whose records the WhatsApp chatbot may tell that contact about (Sorento, Mocha, or both). A contact with no company is told nothing at all, so when the bot answers "nothing found" for something that plainly exists, check this first.

Only a superadmin or admin can see or change a contact's companies. Other users do not see the field, the column or the filter.

## Where to find it

* **Contact page:** open a contact under [**Contacts**](/user-management/contacts). **Companies** sits in **Contact Information** on the **Profile** tab.
* **Contacts list:** the **Companies** column shows the same companies as the contact page, in the same order. Click its header to sort by it. Use **Columns** to show or hide it.
* **Filter:** **Filters**, then **Company**. Pick one or more companies to list the contacts that have any of them.

## Changing a contact's companies

Click the pencil next to **Contact Information** and edit **Companies**, or apply a contact tag that sets companies.

## Companies are not the same as linked customers

The companies of a contact's **linked customers** (the **Customers** column, or the Customers section on the contact page) do not decide what the contact can be told. For example, a dealer whose three linked customer accounts are all in Mocha can still have both Mocha and Sorento as companies. In that case the list and the contact page both show **Mocha, Sorento**.
