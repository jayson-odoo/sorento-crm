# Project Sales Rep - Review a price tag design, ask for changes, approve and collect

What happens to a price tag request after you submit it: how you print your tags, how you read the
design, how you ask for changes by pointing at the tag itself, and how the request closes.

Filling in and submitting the request is a different guide - see
[Submit via portal](submit-via-portal.md#price-tag-request).

## Printing your tags

There's nothing to choose when you submit any more - every request you raise prints itself.
Once you approve the design, the PDF is prepared for you to print, and **Download PDF** is how you
get it (see [Downloading the PDF](#downloading-the-pdf) below).

For the rare request the office prints on your behalf, that's a setting only they can change, on
their side, while the request is still open - not something you pick on the form.

You can see which way a request is set under **Additional Information** on the submitted request,
as **Printing: I print myself** or **Printing: Office prints**. It reads **Not set** on old
requests raised before this existed.

* **I print myself** - after you approve, the request is finished; the PDF appears under
  **Download PDF**.
* **Office prints** - after you approve, the office prints the tags and holds them for you. The
  request carries on to **Ready for collection** and closes when the tags are picked up.

## Reading the design

Once marketing marks the design ready, the request shows a **Design** card as the first thing on the
page, above Customer, Sales Order & Lines and the rest. It stays there through changes requested,
approval and collection, so you can always go back and look at what you approved.

1. Open the request from your portal dashboard (the WhatsApp message you got has a link straight to
   it).
2. The **Design** card draws the whole sheet at page width. If the design runs to more than one
   sheet, a **Sheet 1 / 2** pager sits in the card header.
3. Click **Open** for the full-screen view.

In the full-screen view:

* The header shows the request number, the sheet counter, a zoom control and **Download PDF**.
* **Ctrl + wheel** (**Cmd + wheel** on a Mac) zooms in and out around the pointer. A plain wheel
  scrolls the page as usual.
* Drag with the mouse or finger to move around once the sheet is bigger than the window.
* The keys **+** and **-** zoom, and **0** goes back to Fit. Left and right arrows change sheet.
* The percentage in the middle opens a menu with **Fit** and fixed steps.
* On a phone or tablet, pinch to zoom.

### Downloading the PDF

Once your request reaches **Approved** (or later), **Download PDF** works even before a PDF
exists yet - you don't have to wait for anyone else to trigger it:

* No PDF ready yet: click it. It reads **Preparing your PDF** while one is being made, then the
  file downloads on its own the moment it's ready.
* An export failed: it reads **PDF failed, try again** - click it again to have another one made.
* Before approval - at **Design Ready** or **Changes Requested** - it reads
  **Available after approval** and is disabled, since a proof isn't for printing.

## Asking for changes

While the request is at **Design Ready**, the design is waiting on you. There is no "request
changes" button to press first - you point at the thing you want changed.

1. **Click on the tag** at the spot you mean. A numbered pin drops there and a comment box opens.
   To mark a whole area instead, **drag a box** over it; the pin sits on its corner.
2. Type what needs to change and click **Add**. **Cancel**, the **Escape** key, or an empty comment
   throws the pin away.
3. Repeat for everything you want changed. Pins work the same in the card and in the full-screen
   view, so you can zoom in first and still place them.
4. The pins you have placed are listed under the design, each with the tag it sits on and a delete
   control.
5. If something is about the whole design rather than one spot, type it into
   **Anything else (optional)**.
6. Click **Send 1 change request** (the count follows the number of pins). The request moves to
   **Changes Requested**, you get a confirmation message, and the portal returns to your list.

Nothing is sent until you press Send, so you can place, reword and delete pins freely first.

When marketing sends the next design, your earlier pins are still on it, drawn grey, so you can see
what you asked for last time. Grey pins are read-only. New pins on the new design start a fresh
round.

## Approving

While the request is at **Design Ready**, an **Approve** button sits in the **Design** card header.
Approving is final: the pins close with the round and the request moves on.

* **I print myself** - the request stays at **Approved** and that is the end of it. The PDF starts
  preparing straight away; open the design and click **Download PDF** any time - it reads
  **Preparing your PDF** until it's ready and downloads on its own from there, or wait for the
  WhatsApp message that says it's ready.
* **Office prints** - the office prints the tags. When they are printed, the status becomes
  **Ready for collection** and you get a WhatsApp.

## Collecting the tags (office print only)

At **Ready for collection** the request shows a line reading *"Ready to collect since <date>"* with
a **Mark collected** button next to the status. Press it when you have the tags in hand. The office
can also mark it collected for you at the counter.

If nobody presses it, the request marks itself collected after the number of days your company has
configured (7 by default). **Collected** is the end of the request.

## How you'll be notified

Every change of status reaches you on **WhatsApp** through Respond.io, with a link back to the
request. Nobody has to remember to tell you.

| What happened | What the message tells you |
|----|----|
| You submitted the request | It was received and designing will start shortly. |
| Marketing started designing | Who is designing it. |
| The design is ready | It is ready for your review. |
| You sent change requests | How many were sent, as a confirmation. |
| You approved (I print myself) | The PDF is being prepared. |
| You approved (Office prints) | The office will print and tell you when it is ready. |
| The PDF finished (I print myself) | It is ready to download. |
| The office printed the tags | They are ready for collection at the office. |
| The request was marked collected | It is closed. If the system closed it on the timer, the message says so and after how many days. |
| The request was rejected or voided | That it was rejected, with the reason the office typed, when they typed one. |

There is still no inbox inside the portal - WhatsApp is where these arrive.
