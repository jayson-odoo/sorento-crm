# Sales targets: what counts

Use this when you set up a sales target and choose which sales count towards it.

## Steps

1. Open **Sales**, **Targets**, then a target (or **Add** for a new one).
2. In the **Details** tab, the **What counts** section has three choices:
   - **Measure**: **Amount (RM)** or **Quantity (units)**.
   - **Counts**: **Invoiced**, **Ordered** or **Delivered**. A new target starts on **Invoiced**.
   - **Applies to**: **All products**, or chosen categories, products or brands.
3. **Save**. The **Periods** tab shows each period's achieved figure on the basis you chose.

## What each basis counts

- **Invoiced**: the sales in AutoCount's billing documents: invoices, cash sales and debit notes,
  less credit notes. Amounts are net of tax. A document counts in the period its own date falls in.
  Cancelled documents never count. This is the same figure as the sales reports, to the cent.
  - **Quantity**: a credit note's quantity is taken off, in the credit note's own period.
  - A dealer target counts the documents of its accounts; an agent target the documents of the
    agent (and their linked agents); a team target the documents of each member while they were
    in the team.
- **Ordered**: sales order lines, on the sales order date.
- **Delivered**: delivered quantities, on the delivery order date.

A saved target keeps its basis. Change it with **Edit** in the gear menu.

## What the chatbot says

When the chatbot answers "how am I doing on my target", an Invoiced dealer target is the final
figure; an Ordered dealer target is still marked "provisional, incl. tax".
