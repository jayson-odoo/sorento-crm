# Dealer access: who can use your customers' accounts

Use this to see which dealer contacts can use your customers' accounts on WhatsApp, to give new
people access, and to change or remove access.

## The list

1. Open **Sales**, then **Dealer access**.
2. Each row is one person in one company: their name, phone, company, the customers they are
   linked to, and their access (**Business Owner** or **Employee**).
3. The customer chips show each customer's name. Hover a chip to see its code. When two linked
   customers share a name, both chips add their code, for example "Kedai Contoh (300-X001)".
4. **Search** finds a person by name, phone, or any of their customers' names or codes.

### Filters

Open **Filter** to narrow the list. Filters and the search text stay in the page address, so a
copied link opens the same view.

- **My customers** is on by default: only people linked to your own customers. Turn it off to see
  every dealer contact of an active customer in your companies, yours or not.
- **Customer**: type a customer's name or code and pick one or more. A person linked to any of
  them shows.
- **Company** and **Access** narrow the list further. All filters apply together.
- **Clear all** removes every filter and turns **My customers** back on.

### People you cannot change

A person linked only to another salesperson's customers shows without **Edit** or **Unlink**.
Click the row to see their details; nothing there can be saved.

## Add people

1. Click **Add people**.
2. Pick the **Company** (one or both of your companies).
3. Pick **My accounts**: type a customer's name or code. Pick at least one account in every
   company you picked.
4. For each person, type the **Name** and **Phone** and choose **Access**. **Add person** adds
   another row. Each person gets the same access in every company you picked.
5. **Save**.

If a row cannot be added, the reason shows under that row and nothing is saved for anyone. Fix
or remove the row and save again.

- "Ali Contoh already has access.": this number already belongs to a person on the list. Click
  **Edit Ali Contoh** to change their access, or **Show Ali Contoh** to see it when they are not
  yours to change.
- "This number already has access.": the number is linked to a customer in your company that the
  list does not show (for example a closed account). Ask the office.
- "This number already has access in another company.": the number belongs only to a company you
  do not work in. Ask the office.
- A staff member's number, or the same number twice in one form, is also refused.

### Respond.io

After saving, each new person is found in Respond.io, or added there, straight away so the bot
can answer their first message. If Respond.io cannot be reached, the access is still saved and
the form says "Access given. Not yet in Respond.io:" with the reason. Click **Try again**.

If Respond.io does not answer at all, the form does not keep asking it for the other people: they
all get the same reason, and **Try again** asks again for each.

A person Respond.io does not have yet shows **Not in Respond.io yet** in the **Respond.io** column
(on a phone, under their access). Open the row's
**...** menu and click **Try again in Respond.io**.

## Change or remove access

- **Edit** (or click the row): change which of your accounts the person holds and their access.
  Customers of other salespeople show under **Other customers** and are not changed.
- **Unlink from** a customer in the row's **...** menu removes the person from one of your own
  customers. The button counts down with a **Cancel**; when it ends the link is removed.
