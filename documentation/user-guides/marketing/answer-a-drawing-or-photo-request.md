# Marketing - Answer a drawing or photo request from a quotation

Use this when you are on the marketing product team and a salesperson asks for a product's technical drawing or photo from a quotation line (their **Escalate** button). Each request is a task with an SLA clock, on your dashboard like any other pending task.

## Where you find it


1. Open the **Dashboard** and the **My pending tasks** widget.
2. The task opens a side panel titled **Product drawing · {code}** or **Product photo · {code}**, with *From {salesperson} · With {assignee}* underneath.
3. **Waiting on this** lists every quotation waiting for this file. If it is empty: *No quotation is waiting*.

There is one open request per product per kind (drawing or photo). When several salespeople ask for the same product, they all join the one request.

## Steps


1. Click **Upload in Resources** and upload the file on the product (a drawing, or a PDF filed as **Technical Specifications**, counts as a drawing).
2. Adding the file tells every salesperson waiting: *Drawing added for {code}* (or *Photo added ...*). It does **not** close the task.
3. If there is no WhatsApp conversation with the salesperson, click **Respond** to mark that you answered (*Marked as responded*). With a conversation, your first reply counts as the response.
4. When you are done, click **Resolve ticket**. The confirm **Mark as resolved** says it stops the SLA clock and tells the salesperson; click **Confirm**. You see *Request resolved*, and every requester gets *Product drawing request for {code} resolved*.

Other buttons: **Reassign** (give it to a colleague) and, under the overflow menu, **Extend** (more time). They need the conversation SLA **reassign** / **extend** permissions.

## Things that can stop you

* **Resolve ticket** is greyed out with *Your role cannot resolve SLA tasks* or *Assigned to {name}* when it is not yours to close.
* *This task is assigned to someone else, so you cannot act on it.*
* *This request is already resolved.*

Product drawing requests are no longer configured under **SLA > Form SLA Configuration**; they follow this flow.
