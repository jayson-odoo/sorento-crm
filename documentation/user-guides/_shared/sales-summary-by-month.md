# Sales - Summary by month

The **Summary** of **Sales > Sales & outstanding** can put the months in its columns (or rows): open **Configure summary** and choose **Month of the year**.

## Which months show

You see the months your **Period** covers, January to December order:

- **01/11/2026 - 14/11/2026** shows one column, November.
- **15/11/2026 - 10/02/2027** shows January, February, November and December. Each month adds up every year in the period, so January holds January 2027 only here.
- A period of a whole year or longer shows all twelve months.

A month in the period with no sales stays in the summary as a blank column. The totals are the same as the tab's own total.

## "This summary is more than 5,000 cells"

The summary shows at most 5,000 cells (rows times columns, blank cells included). A shorter period also means fewer month columns, so a summary by customer over two weeks fits. If you still hit it, group by something coarser (for example Account type instead of Customer), choose a shorter period, or export to Excel, which has no limit.
