# AI assistant side panel - ask questions and make changes from any page

Use the AI assistant panel to ask how something works, look up stock or sales, ask about the page you are on, and make a small set of changes after you confirm them.

## Who gets what

| Permission | What it gives you |
| --- | --- |
| **Use AI Assistant Chat** | The panel itself |
| **Use the AI Assistant on the chatbot's brain** | Your messages are answered by the same thinking as the WhatsApp chatbot, as described on this page. No role has it by default. Without it, the panel answers the way it did before |
| **View AI Assistant Settings** | You also see how each reply was reached: the **Agent** or **Chat** pill, **Steps** and **View trace** |

Records and figures follow your own permissions and your active company: if you cannot open a record on its screen, the assistant will not show it to you either. Reference names, such as products, brands and warehouses, help it read your question and may appear in a reply, whatever your permissions. Complaints and SLA tracking are not split by company, so you see them across companies, as on their own screens.

## Steps

1. Open the **AI assistant** tab on the right edge of any page.
2. Type your question and send it. The assistant knows which page you are on and which record you have open, so "what happened to this request" works on a record's page.
3. To start fresh, press **New conversation**. A new conversation does not remember the last one.
4. To go back to an earlier conversation, press **History**, then pick it. You can search the list.
5. To put the panel away, press **Collapse**.

## What you can ask

* **Quick lookups**, such as the stock of a product code. These are answered for your active company.
* **Reports**, such as your top customers by invoiced sales for a month. If that month has no sales, it says so.
* **How do I...** questions. The answer comes from these user guides, as numbered steps.
* **The page you are on**, such as what a record's status is.
* **Complaints**, such as open complaints, how many a product has, or one complaint by its number.
* **SLA tracking**, such as the dashboard's counts or recent tracking rows.
* **Projects**, such as which projects are at a stage, one project by its code, its quotations, or the forecast.
* **Order totals**, such as delivered value by customer or average delivery time.

If the question is vague, it may ask you back.

To capture a product idea, use **Ideas > All ideas**: the assistant points you there and does not record the idea itself.

## Making a change

The assistant does not change a record until you confirm it. The one thing that happens without a card is a request for help: see the next section.

1. Ask for the change, such as logging an opportunity, approving a purchase request, closing a complaint, cancelling an order or raising an IT support ticket.
2. A card shows what will change, with the value before and after where there is one.
3. Press **Confirm change**, or **Cancel** to leave everything as it is. The card then reads **Done** or **Cancelled**. Answering yes in a message also confirms the card, the same as pressing **Confirm change**.
4. Some actions, such as approving a purchase request or closing a complaint, can wait for a few seconds before they apply. While the countdown runs, press **Undo** to take it back. After Undo, nothing has changed and nobody has been told.

A confirmed change does what the same action does on its screen, including telling the people that action normally tells. On a purchase request or a complaint, the card says who will be told.

* **Closing a complaint** tells the contact on WhatsApp once it applies, if the complaint has a contact to reach; the card says so. The complaint must be approved first.
* **Cancelling an order** applies at once, with no countdown and no **Undo**. A yes typed in a message cancels it just as the button does, so read the card before you answer. One confirm can cancel several orders; the card lists each one. An order that comes from AutoCount cannot be cancelled here.
* **Raising an IT support ticket** creates a draft. The reply links to it, and you review and submit it on that page. You need permission to open and edit tickets.

If you reload the page, the panel opens empty. Reopen the conversation from **History**: it looks the same as before, and a card you already answered does not ask again. A countdown keeps running during the reload, and it applies when it ends, so reopen the conversation quickly if you want to press **Undo**.

## Asking to speak to someone

Ask for a person during a stock or sales question. The reply names who was assigned and says they will follow up directly. A message the assistant reads as a request for help can do the same even if you did not ask for a person by name, and it creates their task straight away, with no card to confirm.

## What gets created

* Your conversation, saved in **History**.
* Any record you confirmed, under your name.
* If a person was asked for, a task in their **My Pending** with an SLA clock. It cannot be escalated by hand from there.
* Report files go to your **My Downloads**. Other files, such as a drawing, a certificate or a picture, come back on the reply.

## How you'll be notified

Replies appear in the panel. A conversation in the panel sends nothing to WhatsApp unless you confirm a change that does, as described above. A person you asked for gets an in-app notification, not a WhatsApp message.

There is a daily limit per person on the longer answers, such as reports and changes, across all your conversations. Past it, the assistant still answers quick lookups. If you send many messages in a short time, it asks you to try again shortly.
