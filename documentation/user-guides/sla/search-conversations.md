# Conversations inbox - find a chat by name, phone or message text

Use this to find a WhatsApp conversation when you remember a word from it, not just the
customer's name or number.

## Where to find it

**SLA > Conversations** (salespeople: **MY WORK > Conversations**). Page header:
**Conversations**. Tabs: **Mine**, **Mentioned**, **Unassigned**, **All**.

## Steps

1. Type in the search box (*Search name, phone or message*).
2. Conversations that match on the contact's name or phone show as usual.
3. Conversations that match on **message text** show the newest matching message as a snippet,
   with your word highlighted, its time, and an arrow for incoming or outgoing. They are sorted
   newest match first.
4. Click a conversation. The thread opens on that message, marked **Matches your search**.
5. To get back to the latest messages, click the jump-to-latest arrow (*Jump to the latest
   messages*), or just send a reply.

## Good to know

* Search is a plain match, upper or lower case, in any language (Malay, Chinese, English).
* Only WhatsApp messages on that conversation's own channel are searched.
* Sign-in codes (OTPs) are never found by their digits.
* You see only the conversations you may see: **View Conversations** for all, or **View Own
  Conversations** for your own customers.
