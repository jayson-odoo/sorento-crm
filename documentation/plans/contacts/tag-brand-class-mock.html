"Hidden" means: the chatbot and MCP do not list, price or quote stock for it, and order and PO status replies leave its lines out (the brand rule's surfaces). The portal, staff screens and quotations are unchanged (Q5 a).

D. Proposals for the two side effects of an allow list

No-class products: "No class" is an option in the list, and it is ticked for you the first time you pick any class (you can untick it). Why: most AutoCount items carry no class, so an allow list without it would silently hide almost the whole catalogue.

New AutoCount classes: hidden until ticked on the tag or contact. Why: that is what "allowed" means, and a new premium series should stay out of dealer chats until someone decides; the class appears in this picker as soon as the sync brings it in.

One option per class code, across companies: ticking DIY allows DIY in Sorento and Mocha alike. Why: AutoCount ItemClass has no brand link and the same code lives in both books; the owner thinks in codes, not company rows.
