Rules this mock shows

Columns (owner, 17 Oct)
On hand, SO, PO, Incoming PL, SPO, Forecast. No status or health level anywhere; negative numbers print red, nothing more.
Forecast: AutoCount's formula, confirmed
On hand - SO + PO + SPO. The 459 was SPO (BRW-BB 50, BRW-IR 385, BRW-IB 24). SRTWB1515: 499 - 10,390 + 2,438 + 459 = -6,994, AutoCount's figure, once the four locations inactive in the CRM are counted (Q6 b). BRW-BB: 0 - 7,312 + 1,734 + 50 = -5,528, equal to AutoCount.
SO counts like AutoCount (owner, Q3 b)
Ordered less delivered over open lines, covered lines included, Transferable = F orders left out. Counting them, the CRM reads 10,583; leaving out the 193 on 27 lines of 5 F orders at BRW-BB gives 10,390, AutoCount's figure to the unit. The fulfilment board counts F orders and drops covered lines, so its own figures can differ from this panel.
Outstanding, not cancelled, approved (owner, 17 Oct)
Every document row and every SO / PO / SPO total counts only what is still outstanding on lines that are open: SO ordered less delivered (AutoCount's delivered already includes cancelled qty), PO ordered less received, SPO allocated less received; a cancelled line or document counts nothing. The approval AutoCount holds and the CRM stores is the SO's Transferable flag: F = not approved for the queue, left out exactly as AutoCount's report leaves it out. PO and SPO carry no approval field in the CRM.
Incoming PL (Q2 a), never counted twice
Per item, the packing-list qty still to land that is on no SPO (by link or by container number). SRTWB1515: 686 on 5 packing lists, of which 459 is already on SPOs (and in the SPO column), so 227. Forecast incl. incoming PL = -6,767.
SO due by
Cuts SO only, by delivery date; supply is never cut. A line with no date or AutoCount's placeholder 1 Jan 2030 ("3001") is "No date" and counts in every view: 4,411 of SRTWB1515's 10,390.
Every location AutoCount shows (Q6 b)
Locations inactive in the CRM (CON, JB SHOWR, PJ-SR, REJ100%) are listed with a small "Inactive in CRM" tag and counted: the CRM's active 495 + 4 = AutoCount's 499. CON is 1 in AutoCount's screenshot and 32 in the CRM's 15 Oct copy: data timing, the panel prints the last push.
SO totals, Group by (owner, 17 Oct)
One totals view with a Group by switch: Project, Customer, Salesperson, Delivery month. Same table whatever the angle; no tab per angle.
Search the locations (owner, 17 Oct)
One search box over both tabs: it filters location rows (By location) or location columns (SO totals). A "Shown" subtotal sums what is visible; the item's full totals stay on screen.
Read-only throughout: nothing is written to AutoCount.
