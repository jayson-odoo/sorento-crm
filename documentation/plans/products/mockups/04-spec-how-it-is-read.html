Mockup 4 of 6: the rule form, Add a rule (final)

Issue #1286, slice S1, round 4, final. Opened from the rules grid (mockup 6). The form asks for the same parts as the grid's columns, in the same order, and every selector is the system's own dropdown (SearchableSelect) or multi-select (SearchableMultiSelect). No sentence, no pattern box, no Advanced. The added rule, product codes and counts are illustrative; the rules in the cards below are real built-in rules.
