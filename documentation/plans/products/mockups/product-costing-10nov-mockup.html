Product costing: Purchasing, Pricing, partners

Lane PRODUCT-COSTING, mock v3 on the owner's answers of 10 Nov (23:3x and 23:4x).

  • Exchange rates are dated, with history.
  • SST is one company setting.
  • Transport is set per category, with a per-product override.
  • Mark-up is the achieved mark-up, read-only.
  • The selling price is AutoCount's list price and is never written back.
  • The cost basis is a method: company default, with a per-product override.
  • Partner costing is generic: business partners, each with its own products, mark-up and optional cost method. Bath Plus (AutoCount AED_BATHPLUS) is the first partner, at 8%.

Case: WB 245 basin (CGB9032B) from the owner's workbook. The RM 79.00 selling price, suppliers B, L and D, and the September rate are illustrative inputs. Items tagged v2 changed on 23:3x and v3 on 23:4x. "Example partner" is illustrative, to show more than one partner.

Read-only here. A product joins or leaves a partner from that partner's page. Partner margin = (selling - partner price) / partner price Q7. Method precedence: product override, then partner override, then company default Q6.

Empty states: "No cost (method: Primary supplier)", "Set a primary supplier", "No rate for EUR on 10 Nov", "Selected supplier no longer linked", and "No transport rule" (adds + 0 and stays flagged). An unknown cost is never shown as 0. A method never silently falls back to another.

3. Pricing tab, edit: choosing the cost method v2

The layout is the same as in view. The method becomes a clearable select: clearing it returns to the company default. Each option previews the cost basis it would give on this product today, so the effect is visible before saving. "Selected supplier" adds a supplier select. The transport override sits below it.

7. Exchange rates, dated with history v2 (the existing SCM Currency rates panel, extended)

One row per currency per effective date. The rate used for a date is the latest row on or before it. Future-dated rows take effect on their day. Every add, edit and delete is audited. MYR is 1 by definition and has no row. Existing rates are carried over as their first row.
