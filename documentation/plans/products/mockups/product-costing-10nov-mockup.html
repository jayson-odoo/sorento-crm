Product costing: Purchasing tab, Pricing tab, B Plus costing

Lane PRODUCT-COSTING, mock for owner review. Built on recommendation (a) for Q1 to Q5 of the behaviour card (PR #2321). Case: WB 245 basin (CGB9032B) from the owner's workbook. The RM 79.00 selling price and suppliers B and C are illustrative inputs. Every figure below follows the formula: RMB 34.50 / 1.6 x 1.1 + RM 8 = RM 31.72. Tags marked Qn depend on that card question.

1. Product detail, Purchasing tab (today's Suppliers tab, renamed and extended)

Who sees it: users with procurement.product_suppliers.view and products.cost.view (#2319). Each supplier's cost is turned into MYR landing cost, then marked up against the product's single selling price. The selling price is the same on every row.

Landing cost = unit cost / rate x SST 1.10, then Ceramics transport + RM 8.00 Q1. MYR row: 42.00 x 1.10 + 8 = 54.20 (SST on local purchases Q1). Mark-up = (selling - landing) / landing. A row opens to its dated cost list, as it does today. Hover a landing cost to see its steps.

2. Product detail, Pricing tab (new; same layout in view and edit)

Who sees it: products.cost.view only. The selling price is AutoCount's list price and is read-only here Q3. The mark-up is computed from it, not typed in Q2. The cost basis is the primary supplier's cost in force today Q4.

4. Costing settings (System Settings, Costing section, only if Q1 = a)

The rate is not entered here: it is the existing currency rate (SCM currency rates screen). This page holds SST, the B Plus mark-up and one transport rule per category. A product can override its category's rule from its Pricing tab. Every change is audited.
