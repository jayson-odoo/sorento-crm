Lane PRODUCT-COSTING, mock v5 on the owner's note of 11 Nov (00:2x): one costing page that is the owner's Internal_Costing_Formula workbook (Sheet1) inside the system, delivered the way Sales > Targets was. One grid, the same toolbar, Edit then one Save (as Sales > Targets), Excel export, history per row. Amended 11 Nov 00:3x: mark-up per row per party (QA), no Excel import (QC), Edit / Save (QD), "Has cost" on by default (QE, working answer).

WB 245 (CGB9032B, RMB 34.50, +RM 8, 8%) is the workbook's own row. The other rows use the workbook's category rules with masked codes and illustrative prices. Party = the customer (debtor) record. Bath Plus = 304-R017 "RELATED - BATHPLUS SDN BHD". Management only (products.cost.view). Nothing is written back to AutoCount.

1. Products > Costing (the page; Bath Plus picked)

The party picker defaults to Company standard. In that view Mark-up is the achieved mark-up and Final is the AutoCount selling price, both read-only. Picking a party (here Bath Plus) shows its products at its mark-up. A row opens to its suppliers ("margin if bought from this supplier"). Columns follow Sheet1 left to right.

Workbook formula, one row: 34.50 / 1.6 = 21.5625, x 1.10 = 23.71875, + 8 = 31.71875 landing, x 1.08 = 34.25625 final. Rate is units per MYR 1, as the workbook writes it, taken from the dated rate in force on the "as of" day. Margin = (selling - final) / final. A dot marks a value set on that row rather than inherited. Filters: Category, Brand, Cost method, Has cost. "Has cost" is on when the page opens, so it shows the costed products the way the workbook does; turn it off to see the gaps. Each row's menu has History, showing who changed its mark-up, transport or method, when, and from what.

Editable: Mark-up (this party's row, cleared = the party's 8%), Transport (the product's override, cleared = the category rule), Cost method (the product's override, cleared = the party's or the company default). Transport and method belong to the product, so a change shows "applies to every party". Landing, Final and Margin recompute as you type. Save writes one audited change per changed cell. Cost, rate and SST are not edited here: supplier costs come in through the existing Upload cost price, and rates and SST through Settings. In Company standard view only Transport and Cost method are editable.
