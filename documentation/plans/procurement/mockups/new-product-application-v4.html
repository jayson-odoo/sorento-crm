New Product Application, mock v4 (paper form layout)

v4 = v3's flow, laid out like the company's paper NPA form. The flow is unchanged (owner, 18 Oct: "the flow is ok already"): create from a responded stock inquiry or from the list, submit, approve, purchasing links the code AutoCount sends, Revise, cancel, the portal equivalent. Only the screen changes: a bordered two-column sheet, top to bottom in the paper's order, with a PURCHASING FEEDBACK section at the end. Case data is the invented sandbox case (SI26-0412, XY-1234); nothing from the uploaded form is used (public repo).

Owner decisions drawn with crew's recommended answer as the default. Each one is tagged D1 to D4 on the frames. Correct any of them in one pass; the build follows your answer.
Default drawn hereAlternative
D1Keep several products per application, each drawn as one paper-style product block. A one-product application looks exactly like the paper.One product per application (as on paper). Needs a new application per product.
D2Cost and Lead time move to PURCHASING FEEDBACK, filled by purchasing on an Approved application, next to Link product. The applicant no longer types cost. Cost stays hidden on the portal; Lead time shows there.Applicant keeps typing cost (today), no Lead time.
D3The applicant fills the paper fields only. Product type is required; Qty, Dimension, Material, Thickness, Packing, Brand and Image are optional. Item group, Base UOM, Selling price and Supplier leave the applicant form: purchasing sets them in AutoCount when opening the code, and they show here read-only from the linked product.Purchasing types Item group / Base UOM / Selling / Supplier into PURCHASING FEEDBACK as well (double entry with AutoCount). Or the applicant keeps choosing Item group + Base UOM (today).
D4"Download Excel" on the record, in the paper template's layout (A4 portrait, fit to one page), file named NPA_<YYYYMMDD>_<initials>_<Customer> (<codes>).xlsx.Also a PDF of the same sheet, if purchasing still prints. Or no download.

Crew decided without the owner: title "NEW PRODUCT APPLICATION"; REFF shows the NPA number; both TARGET DELIVERY and REMARK kept; labels renamed to the paper's; image upload reuses the stock inquiry attachment section.
