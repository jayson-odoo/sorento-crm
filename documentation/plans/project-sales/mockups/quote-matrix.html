Quotation grid scopes, mock v2

Owner answers (2 Nov ~22:00) drawn here: no "No. of units" (the real quantity is punched into each cell); no style switch, one generic engine that always totals per column header and per column; same product on several rows allowed; customer suggestions per row AND per cell; no Excel import. Backward compatible: a scope without columns is today's quotation, unchanged.

Real case: the owner's project workbook, anonymised. UNIT (3 unit types x 4 areas), LEVEL 9 (4 locations, 19 rooms + break tank), GUARD HOUSE and RECYCLE & BIN (no columns). Rates and counts are made up; every total on this page is computed from the cells shown. Rules R1-R22: documentation/plans/project-sales/CARD-ps-quote-matrix-02nov.md.
