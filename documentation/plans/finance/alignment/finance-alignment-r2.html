3In plain words

What a billing document is

Any AutoCount document that bills or un-bills a customer:

  • Invoice IV
  • Cash sale CS: paid on the spot, often with no sales order
  • Credit note CN: money given back
  • Debit note DN: an extra charge after the invoice

All four have the same shape. The header holds number, date, customer, agent, currency, subtotal, tax and total. Each line holds item, quantity, price, discount, tax code and line total.

Why one table with a type

Every question you will ask crosses the types. "Sales this month" is IV + CS + DN - CN, and "which credit notes hit this invoice" joins two of them. With one table, each of those is one query with a filter. With four tables, every report, list and permission is written four times, and the four drift apart. ERPs such as Odoo keep them together for the same reason.

What AutoCount will send

After the NDA, the shared service pushes each document whole, to the same CRM door that already takes your sales orders every day, for Sorento or Mocha.

  • Pushing the same document twice changes nothing.
  • An edited document replaces the old one.
  • A cancelled document stays visible but stops counting.
  • History comes in through the same door, from the date you set on the shared service (1 Jan 2023 planned).

What the CRM will show

  • Invoiced as a third basis on the Yearly comparison and the Sales report, grouped by each document's sales agent and that agent's team on the document date.
  • Finance has its own View and Export permissions. Admin and superadmin have them; you grant anyone else on the Roles screen.
  • FINANCE > Billing Documents: a list and a record page, both read only.
  • On a sales order and on a customer, the billing documents that belong to them.

What is stored (schema finance, linked to what the CRM already holds)

How a document arrives

The invoiced basis

Invoiced = invoices + cash sales + debit notes - credit notes, excluding tax, in ringgit, filed by each document's own date. A cancelled document is not counted. A credit note reduces the month it is dated in, never the invoice's month.

Grouping (your ruling on Q8). Each document counts for its own sales agent, in the team that agent was in on the document date. An agent who moved team on the 15th counts for the old team up to the 14th and the new team from the 15th. A document with no agent, or whose agent was in no team that day, counts under No team, shown last and only when it has something in it, so nothing drops out of the total. The fix for a No team figure is on the Sales Teams screen.

Why this basis: the scout on #1269 found the gap comes from order month vs invoice month, tax, credit notes, cash sales with no order, and project billing lag. Only an invoiced basis removes all five.

6Open questions (answer on issue #1309)

Questions 1 to 13 are closed by your rulings, except 5, which is being confirmed. Questions 14 to 16 are new: your rulings raised them. The recommendation is in bold.

Q5. Do dealers see amounts (their invoices) on the portal? Confirming

Your note "yes" sat on the line "No. Staff only for now", read as agreement. The orchestrator is confirming with you in chat.

No: staff only. The plan is built that way; a yes would add a portal slice and changes nothing in S0.

Q14. How does a sales team become a block on the invoiced basis?

(a) Each team is its own block, headed by the team's name, so teams named "Dealer" and "Project team" give the PDF's DEALER and PROJECT TEAM rows. (b) Every team is mapped to one of two fixed blocks, DEALER or PROJECT TEAM, by a new setting on the team.

(a). Nothing new to build or keep in step, and the report shows exactly the teams you define. (b) is worth it only if you keep more teams than blocks.

Q15. When an invoice's own agent differs from the agent on the sales order its lines came from, whose team does it count for?

(a) The invoice's own agent. (b) The sales order's agent, line by line.

(a). A cash sale has no sales order, one document stays in one block, and every document carries its own agent. The shared service will report how often they differ; if often, we revisit.

Q16. Who grants the billing feed's write permission to the AutoCount feed's role?

(a) The migration, to every role that already receives sales orders from the feed, as the ingest v2 permissions were granted. (b) An admin, on the Roles screen, before the first push.

(a). The feed works on the day the module ships and no one has to remember a grant. People's access to Finance stays on the Roles screen either way.

8Not built, and what would start each

  • Receipts, payments, paid or outstanding per invoice: you ask to see collections or a customer's balance in the CRM.
  • Accounting ledgers: never without a ruling from you, because AutoCount is the ledger.
  • Ageing and statements: these follow receipts.
  • AR-module documents: the tally shows they are part of the PDF.
  • A tax master: a screen needs a tax code's name beyond what the line carries.
  • A delivery order link: delivery orders arrive through the shared service with trustworthy amounts.
  • Credit notes split across several invoices: AutoCount allocates that way, and you need to see the split.
  • Dealer invoices on the portal: you ask for them (staff only for now, being confirmed).
  • A start-date setting in the CRM: not while the shared service owns the start date.
  • A team-to-block mapping: you answer (b) to question 14.
