2In plain words

What a billing document is

Any AutoCount document that bills or un-bills a customer:

  • Invoice IV
  • Cash sale CS: paid on the spot, often with no sales order
  • Credit note CN: money given back
  • Debit note DN: an extra charge after the invoice

All four have the same shape. The header holds number, date, customer, agent, currency, subtotal, tax and total. Each line holds item, quantity, price, discount, tax code and line total.

Why one table with a type

Every question you will ask crosses the types. "Sales this month" is IV + CS + DN - CN, and "which credit notes hit this invoice" joins two of them. With one table, each of those is one query with a filter. With four tables, every report, list and permission is written four times, and the four drift apart. ERPs such as Odoo keep them together for the same reason.

What AutoCount will send

After the NDA, the shared service pushes each document whole, to the same CRM door that already takes your sales orders every day, for Sorento or Mocha.

  • Pushing the same document twice changes nothing.
  • An edited document replaces the old one.
  • A cancelled document stays visible but stops counting.
  • History comes in through the same door.

What the CRM will show

  • Invoiced as a third basis on the Yearly comparison and the Sales report.
  • FINANCE > Billing Documents: a list and a record page, both read only.
  • On a sales order and on a customer, the billing documents that belong to them.

The invoiced basis

Invoiced = invoices + cash sales + debit notes - credit notes, excluding tax, in ringgit, filed by each document's own date. A cancelled document is not counted. A credit note reduces the month it is dated in, never the invoice's month. Debit notes are in the sum only if you answer yes to Q7.

Why this basis: the scout on #1269 found the gap comes from order month vs invoice month, tax, credit notes, cash sales with no order, and project billing lag. Only an invoiced basis removes all five.

5Open questions (answer on issue #1309)

Each can be answered yes, no, or with one pick. The recommendation is in bold.

Which document types on day one?

(a) IV, CS, CN and DN. (b) IV, CS and CN only. (c) IV only.

(a) All four. They are one shape, and leaving debit notes out would make the invoiced figure short by every extra charge.

Is history backfilled, and from when?

(a) From 1 Jan 2024. (b) From 1 Jan 2025. (c) No, new documents only.

(a) 1 Jan 2024, because the Yearly comparison shows 2024, 2025 and 2026.

How is tax stored?

(a) Net (excluding tax), tax and total, all three as AutoCount sends them. (b) Tax-inclusive total only. (c) Net only.

(a). The report sums the net, the record page shows all three, and nothing is recomputed.

How does a credit note reference its invoice?

(a) One invoice on the credit note's header, keeping the invoice number even when that invoice is not in the CRM. (b) Per line. (c) Not at all.

(a), unless the shared service reports that AutoCount only allocates by knock-off.

Do dealers see amounts (their invoices) on the portal?

Yes or no.

No. Staff only for now. The portal shows no orders today.

What does the NDA allow the shared service to send?

(a) Document headers and lines, with customer, agent, product and tax fields. (b) Headers only. (c) Something narrower, which you name.

(a). It is everything this plan uses, and it includes no bank, general ledger or payment details.

Does the invoiced basis add debit notes?

Yes or no.

Yes, giving IV + CS + DN - CN. Please confirm this against the AutoCount report's options.

Channel grouping on the invoiced basis (left open)

(a) The agent's current dealer or project class. (b) The PDF's people grouping: the agent's sales team as of the document date, from the dated teams of #1260. (c) Debtor type.

Provisionally (b), because the PDF's "16pax" footnote says it groups people. Please confirm from the AutoCount report's options before S1 builds it.

Who sees Finance > Billing Documents?

(a) Admin and superadmin now, with others granted on the Roles screen. (b) Also every sales role.

(a).

Include AutoCount's AR-module documents now (AR Invoice, AR CN, AR DN, which have no item lines)?

Yes or no.

No. Add them only if the tally shows they are part of the PDF.

Plan receipts and payments now?

Yes or no.

No. The plan names what would start them: you asking for collections or a customer's balance in the CRM.

Report foreign-currency documents in ringgit, using AutoCount's own converted amount?

Yes or no.

Yes.

Keep a cancelled document visible with a Cancelled badge, and leave it out of every total?

Yes or no.

Yes.

7Not built, and what would start each

  • Receipts, payments, paid or outstanding per invoice: you ask to see collections or a customer's balance in the CRM.
  • Accounting ledgers: never without a ruling from you, because AutoCount is the ledger.
  • Ageing and statements: these follow receipts.
  • AR-module documents: the tally shows they are part of the PDF.
  • A tax master: a screen needs a tax code's name beyond what the line carries.
  • A delivery order link: delivery orders arrive through the shared service with trustworthy amounts.
  • Credit notes split across several invoices: AutoCount allocates that way, and you need to see the split.
  • Dealer invoices on the portal: you answer yes to Q5.
