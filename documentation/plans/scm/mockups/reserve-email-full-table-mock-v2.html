3. Open point L: OIs from the 20 Sep legacy import

The 20 Sep import stamped the owner's own account as the raiser of every OI it created, and nothing on an OI marks it as imported.

  • L1 (recommended), shown above: no special case. Those OIs form their own email with the owner's account on Cc, and no CS sees another CS's orders. No new rule is needed, and this group disappears once the legacy OIs complete.
  • L2: those OIs go in one email to the requester only, with no raiser on Cc. This needs a new way to recognise "legacy" (a setting naming the importer account, or a raised-before-21-Sep cutoff).

Gmail clips a body above about 102 KB, which in this layout is about 120 lines in one email. Gmail's "View entire message" still opens every row. Real volume on the dev copy: 71 request lines in 30 days, and the per-CS split makes each email smaller again. A test pins the 110-line size.

On a phone (375px) the 9 columns are wider than the screen, and the mail app scrolls the table sideways.

A one-OI email (User-D above) uses the same table. Its button goes straight to that OI's page, not a filtered list.
