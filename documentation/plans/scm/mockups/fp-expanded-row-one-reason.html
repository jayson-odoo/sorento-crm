  1. 1
    One text box, Reason, in the right column. It replaces "Why this differs" and serves every reason the row needs: the amend reason, the borrow reason and, for a discontinued product, why it is still bought. The Discontinued badge sits beside the label when the product is discontinued, with no sentence. The asterisk shows only when a reason is actually needed (the line differs from its suggestion, a borrow the engine did not suggest, or Reject); discontinued alone does not make it required.Issue #1217: "our expanded row section should only have one text input". R9 (Q12 accepted: optional for discontinued). Id line-reason-{key}.

The one Reason box is saved onto every borrow row; a borrow the engine suggested keeps its own reason while the box is blank.
