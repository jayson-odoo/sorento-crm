9. Risks

  • Older plans move under a newer upload (R2): the stock snapshot is per supplier. Stated on the record's subtitle (which file it started from); the honest reading, but a surprise the first time.
  • Chat depends on Respond.io state: a contact must exist and a template must be approved outside the 24h window. Email ships regardless.
  • Open tracking is a write on GET: best-effort, own transaction; a stamp failure never blocks the supplier.
  • XLSX-is-their-file inherits their file: a malformed retained workbook (no item_code column) falls back to the no-file model and says so in the notice.
  • "Add to existing draft" is gone (list and detail, Q6): a PI always opens a new draft; merging drafts is not asked.
