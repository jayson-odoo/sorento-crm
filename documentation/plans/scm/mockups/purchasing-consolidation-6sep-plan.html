4. Loading plan: formula and columns

Todaysuggested = max(need - on hand - incoming SPO, 0). Incoming PL and PO are shown beside it, never subtracted (the tooltip says so). Fourteen hardcoded columns, no visibility config. Incoming SPO sums spo_allocations on unreceived shipments; Incoming PL sums inbound_shipment_lines on unreceived shipments, so a packing list that already has its SPO is counted in BOTH.
R6. Incoming PL enters the formula as the part not yet turned into an SPO incoming_pl_unallocated = Σ GREATEST(quantity_shipped - spo_allocated_quantity - quantity_received, 0) over unreceived shipments, then
suggested = max(need - on hand - incoming SPO - incoming_pl_unallocated, 0).
Subtracting the full Incoming PL would take every planned container off twice. Example: need 100, on hand 10, SPO 20 from shipment X, X's line ships 50 of which 20 are on that SPO: suggested 40, not 20.

13. Rejected alternatives

  • URL renames for the moved pages: breaks bookmarks, OLD_PATHS, every hardcoded href="/scm", for no visible gain.
  • Column-visibility config on the loading plan grid for three deletions.
  • Netting the FULL incoming PL: double-subtracts every container that already has its SPO.
  • A new spo_so_coverage table for retail takes when order_link_claim already pairs the two.
  • Remarks editable by the supplier on the public page: read-only by design, the remark is our instruction.
  • LLM translation without a memory: non-deterministic on every export.
  • Keeping Incoming Containers as a second listing of the same table with its own upload.
