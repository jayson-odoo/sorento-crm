"Free by my date" is NOT the plain Available. Plain Available for SRTWB242 in BB = 199 on hand − 1,355 open (every dated line incl. 2027) = −1,156, so nothing could ever use own stock. Date-aware: at JEREMY's 15 Sep only the lines due BEFORE him count (CINDY 144), so free = 199 − 144 = 55 and he uses own stock. TERA's 501 due Jun 2027 and JOHNSON's 2030 line never drag an earlier line negative (R24). Borrow starts only when free by my date cannot cover the whole unit.
Under FIFO by date, an earlier line never borrows from an UNPINNED later line of its own group: it simply takes the pile first and the later line goes short (its debt appears in its month, no ORDER_BACK). Borrow is what happens when the supply is PINNED (confirmed decision or placement link) or sits in ANOTHER group. The trail of the line that went short names who took the pile (the existing "who is ahead of me" dialog).
