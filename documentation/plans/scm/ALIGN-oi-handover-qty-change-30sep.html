Questions (as asked)

Q1. Row model: how does a quantity change reach the email?

(a) recommended One line per sales order line whose total changed in this write. The settled row prints the line's total: QTY = what purchasing already held for the line (all its live buy rows), QTY CHANGE TO = the new Buy, REMARK = the date verb plus ORDER n or CANCEL BALANCE n NOS for the difference. The separate ORDER remainder row is not printed as its own line (it still exists in the worklist as the row purchasing acknowledges, and the remark's ORDER n is that row's quantity). This is exactly how a settle-in-place already prints today (_settle_row_in_place), so the two paths read the same.

(b) Keep the separate ORDER row, and add LINE TOTAL 172 to 436 to the remark of the ADVANCE row.

Why (a): purchasing reads the manual mail in line totals, not in inquiry rows; the old value in QTY and the new value in QTY CHANGE TO is the column the owner asked for; (b) leaves two rows for one line and hides the change in prose.

Q2. Width: how does the table stop being cramped?

(a) recommended A wide variant of the branded shell for table emails: 900px card, 24px side padding, per-column widths, dates and quantities never wrap, REMARK gets the slack and is the only cell that wraps. The choice is one setting on the template (Width: Standard 600 / Wide 900) in System Management, Email Templates, so any other table email can opt in; the handover template ships wide. Below 940px the card goes edge to edge, as today's does below 620px.

(b) Keep the 600px card and print each line as a stacked card (label: value pairs) instead of a table.

Why (a): the manual mail purchasing compares against is a table; stacked cards make a six-line handover a long scroll and lose the column-by-column read.

Q3. DELIVERY DATE CHANGE TO: column or remark?

(a) recommended Keep it as its own column, printed only when a line in the email moved a date (as today).

(b) Fold it into REMARK ("ADVANCE to 25/09/2026").

Why (a): the old and new date side by side is what makes an ADVANCE actionable, and with the wide shell it no longer costs the other columns.

What does not change

  • The worklist rows: the placed row keeps its own quantity and links, the remainder is still raised as its own ORDER row, and purchasing still acknowledges rows. Only the email's reading changes.
  • Subject line, headline verbs, the S/O table, the plain-text part's pipe table, attachments, recipients.
  • Every other email keeps the 600px card.
