# UAC: OI worklist Customer and Project columns

- AC-1 Worklist shows a Customer column and a Project column where Project / customer was, in that order.
- AC-2 Each is sortable on its own; sort survives in the remembered view preferences like any other column.
- AC-3 A pre-order row still shows its pre-order note (in the Project cell).
- AC-4 Excel export and search are unchanged.
- AC-5 Usable at 1280px and 375px, long names truncate with a title tooltip.
- AC-6 A row raised again by a re-confirm shows an info icon beside Raised at whose tooltip lists every earlier raise of that SO line (date, time, raiser), newest first; a first-time row shows no icon.
- AC-7 Excel export is unchanged by the history field.
- AC-8 Actions menu offers Unconfirm (N) for ticked Confirmed or Changed rows; pressing it puts them back in To confirm with the confirmer cleared, no dialog, no email; awaiting and rejected rows are skipped and counted.
- AC-9 Unconfirm is hidden without the acknowledge permission and cannot touch another company's rows.
