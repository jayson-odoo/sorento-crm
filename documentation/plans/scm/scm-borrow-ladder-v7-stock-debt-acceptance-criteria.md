# UAC - Borrow ladder v7 and the Stock Debt view

Plan: `PLAN-scm-borrow-ladder-v7-stock-debt.md`. Status: APPROVED 29 Aug 2026 (R1-R36; scenario table signed off).

Tags: `[BE]` backend, `[FE]` frontend, `[E2E]` real clicks FE->BE->DB (agent-browser evidence run), `[T]` test only.

## Journey

**Planner (CS)** uploads the SO book, opens Project Demand -> Fulfilment Planning, ticks orders, Plan. Every planning unit (sales order + item + location + required date) receives ONE whole proposal, walked in this order and stopping at the first rung that covers the whole unit:

1. **Use** the own ownership group (`*-BB` across sites, flagged bins only): the group's supply not yet assigned to an earlier line. No debt.
2. **Borrow on hand** from a late order that has on-hand supply assigned to it: same agent first, then latest required date, then same group, then fewest transfers. A donor is late when its required date is at or after today + lead time + 14 days; never past-due, never TBA. Decided (confirmed) lines donate too. The donor receives an ORDER_BACK inquiry row at its own required date, and a stock transfer when the stock sits at another warehouse.
3. **Borrow incoming** (the nearest SPO or PO line arriving by the asker's date) from a late order the same way; the placement link moves to the asker, the donor receives an ORDER_BACK.
4. **Pool** (site pools, net above zero).
5. **Buy** the whole unit.

The trail names the donor SO, its agent, its required date and the month its debt lands in. Confirm is unchanged.

**Purchasing or planner** opens Project Demand -> Stock Debt: one row per product, one column per month, cell = cumulative balance (on hand today + SPO/PO by arrival - demand by required date; confirmed holds pinned, the rest first-come by date). Red when the month is earlier than today + lead time (cannot be bought in time), amber later, green when in surplus. "2030-01" and "No date" columns sit at the far right and take no supply. A cell drills to its lines; each line has Plan, which opens the board with that order selected. The reorder run keeps buying against the same committed demand and ORDER_BACK rows; nothing new for purchasing to do.

**Admin** flags bins on Inventory -> Warehouses (`Fulfilment planning` on/off; seeded on for `*-BB *-IB *-IR *-NTC *-AM`) and sets the TBA date on Supply Chain -> Policies -> Fulfilment Priority.

## S1 - The flag and the policy field

- **AC-S1-1 `[BE]`** Given migration 443 runs on a database holding the warehouses of the prod copy (83 rows, 61 active on the 29 Aug dev copy), when it completes, then `warehouses.fulfilment_planning` exists (boolean, not null, default false) and is true for exactly the active bins whose code ends in `-BB`, `-IB`, `-IR`, `-NTC` or `-AM` (24 on the 29 Aug dev copy; the predicate is the criterion, the count is the measurement) and false for every other row, pools included. The seed is a one-off STARTING POSITION: it runs only where the column is created (and, in `bootstrap_env`, only on a database that has never been stamped), so a re-run leaves a bin an admin turned off exactly as they left it.
- **AC-S1-2 `[BE]`** Given the policy row, when migration 443 completes, then `scm.priority_policy.tba_date_from` exists (date, not null, default `2029-01-01`) and `cross_group_borrow_max_qty` / `cross_group_borrow_max_pct` are gone; `GET /scm/policies/fulfilment-priority` returns `tba_date_from` and no longer returns the two cap fields; `PUT` accepts `tba_date_from` and rejects a CHANGE to a value earlier than today (422).
  - *Corrected 30 Aug 2026, review of S2.* "Rejects a value earlier than today" was read as a rule about the VALUE, so once a configured date had quietly passed, every save of any other setting was refused - the panel sends the whole record back, so no weight, no coverage date and no class weight could be saved again until somebody also picked a new TBA date. The rule is about MOVING the TBA line back: 422 only when the submitted date differs from the active policy's and is before today. The check therefore lives in the route (it needs the active revision) rather than in the write schema, and the panel mirrors it only while the input is dirty.
- **AC-S1-3 `[FE]`** Given Inventory -> Warehouses, when the list renders, then a `Fulfilment planning` column shows On/Off per row, and the edit page (Planning tab, under `Available for planning`; the read view mirrors it in the same position) carries a `Fulfilment planning` switch that saves through the existing `PUT /warehouses/{id}`; the field reaches the list after save without a reload.
- **AC-S1-4 `[FE]`** Given Supply Chain -> Policies -> Fulfilment Priority, when the panel renders, then it shows `TBA date from` (date input, not null, with a `Reset` back to the default `2029-01-01`, no explanation text) beside `Reorder coverage until`, and the two cross-group cap inputs are gone; Save persists it.
- **AC-S1-5 `[BE]`** Given a warehouse with `fulfilment_planning = false`, when `_facts_for`, the ladder's `group_net`, the board's cell stock context, `_group_take_candidates`, the donor lists and the supply timeline read stock or demand IN ORDER TO PROPOSE, then that warehouse's on hand, incoming and sales-order lines are absent from all of them (pinned by one shared predicate, the way `pool_predicate.py` is shared).
- **AC-S1-5b `[BE]`** (boundary, ruled in review 29 Aug) The flag narrows what a PROPOSAL may draw, never what a screen may state, and never a non-planning consumer of a shared seam. So `free_stock_by_location` / `stock_levels_by_location` / `held_stock_by_location` keep answering for every ACTIVE bin (on hand printed beside a free of 0 reads as a defect, not as a policy); the batched `netting_for_products` door keeps its full ownership-group index unless a caller asks `planning_only=True`, so the order-inquiry deficit gate still sees a group's real negative net; and a COVERED row's warehouse stays in the board's counted read set, so a frozen decision is printed beside figures rather than dashes.
- **AC-S1-6 `[T]`** Given a sales order whose only line sits at a flagged-off bin, when the board is built for it, then the line is listed with the verdict `Outside fulfilment planning` and no proposal, and Confirm skips it. The verdict belongs to an ACTIVE bin with the flag off only: a DEACTIVATED bin keeps the verdict it carried before the flag existed, because it was already outside every read and the sentence would point at a switch that is already the right way up.

## S2 - The supply assignment and the Stock Debt view

Pure maths module `app/services/scm/supply_assignment.py`, beside `coverage_timeline.py`.

- **AC-S2-1 `[T]`** Given one product with on hand 100 at BRW-BB today (1 Sep), an SPO of 50 arriving 15 Sep, a PO line of 200 arriving 20 Oct (issue + lead), and lines A (BRW-BB, 80, due 5 Sep), B (BRW-BB, 60, due 10 Sep), C (MWH-BB, 40, due 1 Oct), D (BRW-BB, 300, due 1 Nov), when `assign(product)` runs, then A takes 80 on hand; B takes 20 on hand, is short 40 on 10 Sep and is cleared by the SPO on 15 Sep (late); C takes the SPO's remaining 10 at its own date, is short 30, and is cleared by the PO on 20 Oct (late); D takes what is left of the PO (170) and is short 130; month balances Sep +10, Oct +170, Nov -130 (cumulative), Nov red because 1 Nov is inside `today + lead`; and the per-line result marks A covered, B late, C late, D short.
  - *Corrected 30 Aug 2026 while S2 was implemented; the four STATUSES are unchanged.* The original wording ("C takes 10 of the SPO and is short 30, D takes 200 PO and is short 100; Sep -80, Oct -210, Nov -210") contradicted itself twice: it called C `late` while giving it no late supply, and its month figures do not foot - the stated events sum to -130 by the end, never -210. The rule the plan states (3.1: "a line that cannot draw at its date is `short`; if supply later covers it, `late`") produces exactly the four statuses that were signed off, and the quantities and balances above are what that rule gives. The golden fixture `tests/scm/fixtures/supply_assignment/ac_s2_1_first_come_by_date.json` carries them.
- **AC-S2-1b `[T]`** (ruled 30 Aug, review of S2) Given a line whose own ownership group has no free supply at its date but another PROJECT group (flagged bins, not a site pool) holds free supply then, when `assign` runs, then the line draws that group's free pile (own group first, then the other project groups oldest-first, pools never) and is `covered` with no debt, exactly as ladder step 1 reads it (PLAN 3.2); the product-wide month balance and the per-line status therefore agree, and a product whose only shortfall was cross-group is not in debt. A confirmed hold (`so_line_allocations` or an `order_inquiry_links` placement) is pinned to its line even when its bin sits outside the read span (a site pool, an unflagged bin, another group under `group=`): pinned means pinned (R21), never `short`.
- **AC-S2-2 `[T]`** Given a line whose confirmed decision holds 30 at BRW-BB, when `assign` runs, then that 30 is pinned to the line before any first-come assignment, whatever its required date.

- **AC-S2-4 `[T]`** Given a PO line of 100 with an SPO of 40 already placed on it (`spo_allocations.po_line_id`), when the supply timeline is built, then the PO contributes 60 at its arrival and the SPO 40 at its own arrival; every PO line arrives at `issue_date + supplier lead time` (R29); its `expected_date` is carried as `bought_for`.
