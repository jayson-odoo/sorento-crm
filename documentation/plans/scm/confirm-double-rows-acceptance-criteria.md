# UAC: CONFIRM-DOUBLE-ROWS

Plan: `PLAN-confirm-double-rows-28oct.md`.

- AC-1: Confirming a line's unchanged decision again leaves the active revision holding exactly what the previous
  press held: the same allocation rows, the same quantities, the same recorded shortfall.
- AC-2: A Reserve recorded short (fully or partly) on one press is never written in full on a later identical press.
- AC-3: A changed decision replaces the previous allocation: the active revision holds the new decision alone.
- AC-4: A first confirm is judged exactly as before (no change for an order with no active revision).
