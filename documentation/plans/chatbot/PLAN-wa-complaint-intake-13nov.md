Status: Plan (discovery + behaviour card pending owner answers). Track: L (lead pattern).
Lane: WA-COMPLAINT-INTAKE. Owner ask 13 Nov 2026, demo Mon 16 Nov 09:30.

## Goal

Generalise the WhatsApp ideation path into typed submissions (type = idea | complaint | later
types, one shared path). A dealer with AI access reports a complaint on WhatsApp; the bot gathers
what a complaint needs, confirms back, and creates the complaint in the CRM complaints module
through the existing create function, with a link back to the conversation; CS is notified as usual.
