1. What went wrong in your console case

"Dealer <customer F> oct total sales for water closet". The parser got the words right; each wrong reply is a code step.

Parser

But it labels the ask "sales_analysis" (company totals), 6 of 6.

Reply 1

10-way customer pick on "water"
The category word is also searched as a customer name (order asks widen a category to customers), and the customer picker counts every word.
Slice 1: a product-kind word never feeds the customer picker.

Reply 2

"Which one do you mean? 1. <F>"
Two rows of one company merge into one option after the "2 or more?" check.
Slice 1: one company left = no question, answer it.

Reply 3

"Couldn't pin down 'water closet' (promotion). Water Closet.pdf x2"
After the pick the category falls back to a promotion search; promotion names are the flyer file names. And the sales total has no category filter to use.
Slice 1: categories resolve to categories only; the sales total takes a category filter.

2. Target shape

One rule for every list: the parser marks the words and what kind of thing they are; code decides which list value they mean, and asks when it is not sure.

Parser (slim v63)

  • ask kind, period, quantity, follow-ups, picks: v63 rules kept word for word
  • marks words as: product code, customer, product kind, property, file type, brand
  • copies words exactly as typed
→

Span index (new, code)

  • categories, classes, product types, spec values, file types, certificate kinds
  • exact → alias rows (tandas, 马桶, gambar) → near spelling → meaning (multilingual)
  • reads only the marked words, never the whole message
→

Decide (code)

  • one clear value: filter
  • two close, or meaning-only: one question, at most 3 options
  • none: say the word back
  • never falls back to promotion, customer or a file
Product codes stay exact. Codes never go into the index or into the parser's input as candidates (that is what made v103 swap SRTWC286 for SRTWC286-SH on 11 test items). The parser copies the typed code; the resolver does exact, then "did you mean" with the parser's pick first (your 23 Oct rule). Every prompt test carries a zero-code-swap bar.
Small lists stay full lines in the prompt (your 22 Oct ruling): brands (12), sales channels (2), promotion kinds (5).
The vocabulary the prompt carries today becomes data: the Malay / Chinese words (tandas, 马桶, sinki, gambar, lukisan teknikal, sijil) move into the existing category synonyms column and alias rows, so nothing is lost when they leave the prompt.
