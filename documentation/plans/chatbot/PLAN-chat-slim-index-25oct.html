Chatbot: retrieval before the parser (plan v2: phase 1 approved)

Lane CHAT-SLIM-INDEX-PLAN, 25 Oct 2026, revised after your 17:26 note. Plan only, no code. Detail and file:line evidence: documentation/plans/chatbot/PLAN-chat-slim-index-25oct.md. Customer names masked.

Yes to your idea. A retrieval step runs before the parser. It looks up every list (categories, classes, brands, specs, sales channels, promotion kinds, file types, locations, sales agents, customer names) by meaning AND spelling, in any language. It hands the parser a short candidates list, and the parser decides. Product codes stay exact and never go into it. We prove the retrieval first, for cents, with no parser money: if it cannot find the right value 95% of the time for a list, that list is not moved.

How: every 1-4 word piece of a message is looked up in every list by spelling (what the index does today) and by meaning (text-embedding-3-large, works across English, Malay and Chinese), the two rankings merged, top 5 and top 10 kept per list. Four arms: spelling only (today), meaning only, both, both + the Malay / Chinese words v63 carries today (tandas, 马桶, gambar, sijil) added as data.
Messages: real prod chat messages, read-only and masked, pulled by crew the same way as INDEX-HYBRID round 2 (you allowed that read on 25 Oct ~12:10); every message naming any list value, plus 50 naming none; your console cases; round 2's 90 real rows. Test-set texts are dropped. Each message is labelled with the values it names; crew checks 10%, you check 20.
Report, per list and per language: recall at top 5 and top 10, lines shown per message, wrong candidates on the 50 "names nothing" messages, every miss listed. A list with fewer than 30 real mentions is reported "not enough real data" and is not moved (Malay is thin: 5 real list asks in 33k messages).
Cost: embeddings only, about USD 0.02. Nothing runs the parser.
