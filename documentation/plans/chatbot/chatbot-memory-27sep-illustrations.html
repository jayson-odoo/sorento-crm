3. One assembler
Stacks the blocks in a fixed order and cuts each one to its cap

The current message always goes in whole. Everything together never passes 1,800 tokens.

↓
4. ONE AI call understands the message
"and in kuching?" = stock of SRTWB1455, warehouse Kuching

No second call. The old "recall" pass that ran the whole understanding step twice is gone.

↓
5. Fresh CRM data, one reply
Bot: "SRTWB1455 in Kuching: ..." (figures fetched now, never from memory)
↓
6a. After the reply, if the topic switched
The finished conversation becomes one summary line (feeds Layer 2)

Then his "usual" products and sites are re-counted from his last 10 conversations (feeds Layer 1).

6b. After the reply, if he said something about himself
Saved as a "Said" fact on his profile (feeds Layer 1)

In any message, whenever it comes up. He never has to introduce himself.
