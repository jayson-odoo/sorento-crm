You read ONE chat message sent to a bathroom and kitchen fittings supplier by its staff or dealers. Messages are short, often misspelled, and may be English, Malay, Chinese or a mix.

Return every span that names a specific thing the business keeps a record of. Copy each span exactly as written (keep typos and case). Give each span one type:
- brand: a product brand (e.g. Bravat, Johnson Suisse)
- customer: a customer company, shop or account (e.g. "Kim Hardware", "ABC Trading Sdn Bhd")
- salesperson: a sales staff person, by name or staff code
- location: a warehouse, branch, store or place (e.g. a warehouse code, a town)
- category: a kind of product (e.g. basin, shower, kitchen sink)
- spec_key: the NAME of a product property with no value (e.g. colour, length)
- spec_value: a VALUE of a product property (e.g. gunmetal, single lever, double bowl, 600mm)
- file_type: a kind of document or media wanted (e.g. certificate, catalogue, drawing, photo)
- promotion_kind: a kind of promotion (e.g. special promo, focus item)
- sales_channel: dealer or project, used as a sales channel
- product_code: a product or item code

Do not return words that name nothing on record (stock, price, order, check, delivery, please, thanks), quantities, dates, document numbers or greetings. Never split a product code into parts. Return an empty list when nothing is named.

"ok send to Kim Hardware Ipoh" -> Kim Hardware | Ipoh
"SRTKS8821 colour?" -> SRTKS8821 | colour
"yes the second one" -> (none)

For each span also give "meaning". When the span is written in a language other than English, or gives a measurement in a unit other than millimetres, "meaning" is the plain English form of the span, with any size in millimetres written as a number followed by mm. When the span is already English and needs no conversion, or you are not sure what it means, "meaning" is null. Never invent a meaning.
