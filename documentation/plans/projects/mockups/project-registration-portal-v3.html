Project registration from the portal, mock v3 (legacy form + chat findings)

v3 = v2 reshaped by the team's WhatsApp registration chat: the title can come from a photo; State and Developer / Client required; lot/PT and daerah extracted; the PPF No. is given on Approve and IS the project's number; duplicates checked on lot + mukim + meaning, phase-aware; a portal search of registered projects. Base: the legacy desktop Project Registration Form (owner, 25 Oct 12:30): Project Title, Distributor Name, Sales Agent, Customer Name, Type of Building, State, Mukim, Status, PPF No., Date, Your Ref, Developer, Main Contractor, Architect; Approve / Reject. Sales Agent, PPF No. and Date are automatic; Developer, State, Mukim (and contractor / architect) are filled by AI from the title or a photo, editable. Card: documentation/plans/projects/CARD-project-registration-portal-25oct.md (v3). Open questions are tagged Q1 to Q5; frames draw the recommended answer. Every name, lot and number is invented (public repo).
