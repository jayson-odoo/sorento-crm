PO and delivery schedule, simplified (mock v2)

Owner, 15 Oct: "the PO part and the delivery schedule part are badly designed, too complicated." Proposal: one Orders tab on the project, one page per PO, and every line showing ordered, scheduled, delivered and still to deliver. Dates change where they are shown. One sign-off instead of three. Existing components only (map at the bottom). Data: the real project case PRJ-000001, masked by crew (names, PO numbers, prices scaled by one constant): 50 PO lines, 15 deliveries, 3 sales orders, 0 published.

Fixed on the way (defects found in discovery, independent of the redesign)

  • D1 Two places edit a scanned PO's lines and only one reaches the sales orders; confirming a new version wipes the other. Proposed: the PO's lines are edited in one place.
  • D2 Approve and Countersign gate nothing (question 1).
  • D3 Sales orders can be built from a schedule nobody confirmed. Proposed: Build waits for a confirmed schedule.
  • D4 The next-step card's links land on a list and the PO is picked again.
  • D5 The PO is re-picked in the schedule upload, the build dialog and the amendment picker.

Questions for the owner (Q1 to Q3 open; Q4 and Q5 answered)

1. Approve and Countersign: drop them, or make them mean something?Today a PO needs Confirm, then Approve, then Countersign by a second person, but the build and publish never check the last two. (a) Drop them: Confirm is the only sign-off. (b) Keep them and make Build sales orders wait for Countersign.
Recommendation: (a). They stop nothing today, so nobody relies on them; if a second pair of eyes is wanted, (b) is a small follow-up.
2. Changing a delivery date: edit in place, or re-upload only?(a) Click the date on the Deliveries tab and change it; draft sales orders follow, a published one gets an amendment started for you. (b) Dates change only by uploading a revised schedule.
Recommendation: (a). A date usually moves by phone or WhatsApp; making staff wait for a revised document to move one date is the heavy part today. The next uploaded revision still wins.
3. One page per PO?Merge the PO record, the PO document review and the schedule review into one PO page with Lines, Deliveries, Sales orders and Documents tabs. Sales order detail pages stay as they are.
Recommendation: yes. One job, one place; today one PO visits 3 project tabs and 4 pages.
4. Deliveries: read from AutoCount only?(a) Delivered quantities come only from AutoCount (as today, shown on the PO). (b) Staff also record deliveries in the CRM.
Answered (a): AutoCount is the source of truth.
5. Build sales orders: keep the 'split by' choice?(a) Keep the choice (area, delivery date, delivery month) in one dialog. (b) Always split by delivery date, no dialog: one sales order per delivery.
Answered (a): keep the choice, in one dialog instead of two.
