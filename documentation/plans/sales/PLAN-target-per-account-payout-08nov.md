## Owner rulings (8 Nov ~22:20)

1. Separate targets stack. A combined multi-account target pays on top of an account's own ladder
   target. The "Replaced by" concept is removed: no target zeroes another.
2. Each account earns its own share (account sales x that account's rate at the tier reached by the
   combined total). A dealer target needs no single "Pays to"; each account row is its own payee.
3. Figures and the ordered/invoiced basis are correct and out of scope; this lane is the mechanism only.
