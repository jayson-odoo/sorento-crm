What a user cannot do yet

Save a view on the board itselfThe board's own state is a link (step 5), not a named saved view; named views live in the report (step 10).
Pin a view to the home pageNo dashboard / widget module exists: the home page hard-codes two SLA widgets. The sidebar star pins a page, not a figure.
Make their own measureMeasures are fixed by the dataset: RM, Qty, Customers, Documents, Products. No margin (no cost on billing lines), no new customers, no formula builder.
Schedule an email of a viewNot built anywhere in reports.
Drill into other people (salesperson without sales.reports.view)Q2 (a): own row only. Grant sales.reports.view to open it up.
