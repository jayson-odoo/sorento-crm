Dealer targets on the one target engine (v2)

v2 after the owner's rulings of 16 Oct 00:10. A dealer is a third subject on the same Targets engine as agents and teams: same list, same record, same Commission tab, same logic. What a dealer adds is the list of accounts it counts and the account it pays to. The Commission tab gains four things for every subject: threshold in % / RM / points, tiers per period, "+ Additional" rows that stack, and round to sen or ringgit. Figures and dealer names are placeholders until the masked Excel rows arrive. Pins map to the notes; each note names the component reused and its repo path. Pink notes = changed in v2.
