What is on the screen

  • One card per customer assigned to me. Customer name, contact name, phone, the last message (arrow in = customer wrote, arrow out = we wrote) and when. A card whose last message came from the customer is tinted and carries "Customer wrote last": that is the one that needs me. No unread counts: the CRM does not track reads per agent.
  • Same toolbar as Price Tag Request and Customer asks. Filter (Customer, Contact, Last message date range), Sort (Last message, Customer), Cards / List toggle; the sort is remembered per contact like Customer asks. The landing's search box matches customer name, contact name, phone and the last message text. No New button: a conversation is not something you create here.
  • Tap a card: the thread opens in the bottom sheet (the same sheet Customer asks uses), showing the CRM's chat component exactly as the ticket drawer does: WhatsApp look, date pills, bot and colleague sender labels on outgoing bubbles, delivery ticks, internal notes in amber, in-thread search with match stepping, scroll back for older messages, and the "jump to latest" button with the count of new messages that arrived while reading.
  • Empty state when none of my customers has a chat, and a "no match" state for a search.

The three open questions, with a recommendation each

1. Read-only, or reply from the portal?

Recommendation: (a) read-only for this lane. Frame C.

(a) Read, search and jump only. (b) Frame D: the ticket drawer's reply composer too, sending on the business WhatsApp number.

Why (a): a reply from the portal goes out on the company's WhatsApp number, as the CRM, not from the salesman's own phone, and the customer sees it as the business writing back. Outside the 24-hour window WhatsApp only allows a pre-approved template, so the composer has to carry the template rule and the sender identity, and the audit trail has to name the salesman behind a portal token (only some of them are CRM users). That is a second lane's worth of contract. Reading is what the owner asked for; the reply can follow once the first cut is in use and we know who wants to send. If (b) is wanted now, the composer already exists and is reused as-is.

2. How is the list sorted?

Recommendation: (a) latest message first, with the Sort button to change it.

(a) Latest message first, the way the CRM inbox and WhatsApp itself order chats; the Sort menu offers Customer A to Z too, and the choice is remembered per contact. (b) Customers who wrote last pinned on top, then latest first. (c) Customer A to Z.

Why (a): it is what every chat app does, so nothing to learn, and the tint plus "Customer wrote last" already lifts the ones waiting on us. (b) is one filter away (Filter: Customer wrote last) without reordering the rest.

3. Card or list?

Recommendation: (a) both, cards by default, one toggle. Frames A and B.

(a) The landing's existing Cards / List toggle, cards by default at every width, list as the same DataGrid the other kinds use (Customer, Contact, Last message, When). (b) Cards only. (c) A split screen with the list on the left and the thread on the right, like the CRM's Conversations page.

Why (a): it is the pattern the owner already ruled for Customer asks (reuse the Price Tag Request list), it costs nothing extra, and the portal is phone-first: a split screen would not fit 375px, and at 768px the bottom sheet still reads as one thing at a time.

What the build reuses

  • Thread: components/common/RespondChatList.tsx through useConversationThread (scroll back, search, jump), the same wiring as TicketConversationPanel.tsx, mounted in the Customer asks bottom Drawer.
  • List: LandingToolbar, LandingCardShell, the landing's DataGrid list, the per-contact remembered sort from useCustomerAsksTodo.ts.
  • Scope: the portal contact's linked sales agent (sales_agent_for_contact), that agent's customers (customers.sales_agent_id), their WhatsApp contacts (respond_contact_customers), and the last message per contact the CRM inbox already computes (conversation_inbox_service).
  • Switch: the same per-contact portal form switch Customer asks uses (Contact page, Portal forms), off by default.
