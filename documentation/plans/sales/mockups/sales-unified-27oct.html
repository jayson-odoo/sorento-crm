Owner ruling 27 Oct 16:3x: nothing is hidden; every agent and document is shown and ALL SALES is the whole company. Q1 (b): the sales type comes from the sales order.

    Columns AutoCount debtor types as they arrive on customers (no fixed list: a new type in AutoCount becomes a new column). A customer with no type lands in (blank).

    Rows Sales agents. An agent who sold both types appears in both blocks. ALL SALES = every block (owner ruling: nothing hidden). Untick No sales order / Mixed / Type not set in Channel to read the AutoCount report's own total.
  4. 4
    Year to date View = Year to date sums Jan to the period's month in one table; the Excel has one sheet per month plus a YTD sheet, like the AutoCount report.
  5. 5
    Sorting Every column header sorts server-side within each group; groups keep their order.
  6. 6
    Drill Click a cell: the billing documents behind it (number, date, type, customer, RM).
