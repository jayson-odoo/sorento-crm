Combo price tags: every combo shape, on the existing screens

Mock v5, 04/11/2026. Static. Amber dashed outline = what changes; the rest is today's screens redrawn from their source. There is no combo "type" anywhere: the combo record names its own groups, and a template points at positions in the combo.

How it fits together (one picture, no types)

  1. The combo record says what the combo is made of (existing Combos card on the product page): a main product, fixed parts, choice groups that the combo names itself ("Kitchen tap", "Tap colour"), and upgrades with a top-up.
  2. A template points at positions (existing "Product" select in the designer): Main product, Fixed part n, "group n, pick k", Upgrade n. Its needs are read from its layers, for example "3 from choice group 1".
  3. The system checks fit, people see it in words: the combo's template picker says "Needs 3 from Kitchen tap" in that combo's own names, and greys out a template the combo cannot fill, with the reason.
  4. The salesperson picks per group, exactly what the template needs: one row per group, labelled with the group's name ("Kitchen tap: pick 3"). Fixed parts need nothing.
  5. How we get the combos: the flyer re-seed reads each current flyer, proposes every combo with its groups and names, and writes nothing until you approve its report (section 1).
