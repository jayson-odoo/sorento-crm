# UAC: PRICETAG-ACC-PICTURE-PICKER

- AC-1 In the pencil dialog of an accessory with two or more pictures, every Picture choice option
  shows a small thumbnail of that picture beside its label ("Picture 1", "Picture 2").
- AC-2 The selected Picture choice shows the same thumbnail beside its label.
- AC-3 Thumbnails are the 320 px rendition from the rendition service, never the original.
- AC-4 After Save, the row in "Accessories included" shows the chosen picture at once, no reload.
- AC-5 After a reload the row still shows the chosen picture; "Use the accessory's own" + Save
  shows the first picture again.
- AC-6 Usable at 375 px and 1280 px.
