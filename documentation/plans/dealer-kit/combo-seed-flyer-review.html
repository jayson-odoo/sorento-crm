Questions for you

  1. a. A combo with the same product and name already exists (118 on the 4 Nov copy, mostly from the 2 Nov import; yours made after 4 Nov show up in the prod dry run). What should the script do with it?

    • 1. Skip it completely.
    • 2. Fill only what is empty (description, prices, free items, and parts only when it has none). Where its description has exactly the flyer's words but no bold, add the bold (39 on the 4 Nov copy). Never change anything you typed.
    • 3. Overwrite it with the flyer.

    Recommendation: 2. It never overwrites your work. If you renamed one of your combos, tell us the old and new name and the script treats them as the same combo instead of making a second one. Answered 9 Nov 2026: accepted.

  2. b. 14 codes or names on the flyer are not in the catalogue exactly as printed (31 combos). Example: the flyer prints SRTBT1835, the catalogue has SRTBT1835-16. What should happen to those combos?

    • 1. Hold the whole combo (nothing loaded) until you tell us the right code; the list below shows the catalogue codes that start the same, as hints only.
    • 2. Load the combo without the missing item.
    • 3. For the accessory names (Fixing Bolts, Flexible Bottle Trap), create them in the accessory library.

    Recommendation: 1. A half combo would print wrong on the price tag. Reply with the right code per line (or 'leave out'); we put exactly that in the seed and re-run, which is safe to repeat. Answered 9 Nov 2026: accepted.

  3. c. Water closet and urinal combos (p8, p9) print each item's own LP and one SP, with no package LP. Basin add-on and many sink combos print the price on each tap option instead. What list price should the combo get?

    • 1. Leave the combo list price blank (the tag prints only what is printed on the flyer).
    • 2. Use the sum of the printed LPs (e.g. 1,150 + 120 + 1,200 = 2,470).

    Recommendation: 1. The flyer prints no package LP, and the combo rule is that a blank price prints nothing rather than a sum. You can type one later on any combo. Answered 9 Nov 2026: accepted.

  4. d. Bathtub combos (p2-3): the flyer prints the SRTWT51012 mixer in the FREE box and 'TOP UP RM 150 for colour model' beside it. How should the mixer be set up?

    • 1. SRTWT51012 as a free item; the 4 colour mixers as upgrades with a RM 150 top-up (as printed).
    • 2. A choice of 5 mixers, with the 4 colour ones as RM 150 upgrades.

    Recommendation: 1. It matches the flyer exactly: pump, soap set and mixer free; colour mixer is an upgrade. Answered 9 Nov 2026: accepted.

  5. e.
