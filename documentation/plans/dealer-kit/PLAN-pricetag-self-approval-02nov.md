# PLAN: Price tag self-approval, edit after approval, escalate to configured assignee

Status: Plan (behaviour card in progress). Track: TBD after code read.
Lane: PRICETAG-SELF-APPROVAL. Source: "Feedback on Price Tag" meeting 2 Nov + owner rulings 2 Nov.

## Journey (owner rulings, 2 Nov)

- The salesperson fills the request, sees the generated tag, and approves it himself. Marketing leaves the normal flow.
- After approval he can edit and re-approve; each edit is a revision; no cap.
- "Escalate" sends the request, with his note, to the configured assignee (form SLA config, not a hard-coded person).
  Only then does marketing act.
